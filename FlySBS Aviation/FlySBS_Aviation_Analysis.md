## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
FlySBS Aviation is India's only DGCA-approved non-scheduled air charter (NSO permit) operator, providing bespoke private jet services to corporates, HNIs, ultra-HNWIs, celebrities, and diplomats. Headquartered in Chennai, the company operates with a global footprint across 6 continents and 18+ domestic cities.

**Business Model**
- **Asset-light charter model**: Mix of dry-leased (owned fleet) and wet-leased aircraft (external charters)
- **Direct client relationships**: In-house sales team + 12 travel agent/broker partnerships
- **Scalable unit economics**: Revenue per departing flight hour increasingly driven by dry-leased fleet (higher margins)

**Current Fleet** (*as of Mar 31, 2026*)
- 1x Embraer Legacy 600 (13-seater, dry lease) - operational since FY25
- 1x Embraer Legacy 650 (13-seater, inducted FY26)
- Wet-leased access to Dassault Falcon 2000, Bombardier Challenger 605, Global 6000
- **In-principle agreements**: 2 additional Embraer Legacy 600s (dry leases) for FY27-FY28
- **Planned inductions**: 4 Citation Cessna CJ2+ aircraft (Q4 FY26-Q1 FY27)

**Revenue Composition** *(H1FY26)*
- Dry-lease revenue: 30.13% (₹4,143.76 Lakhs) – higher margin 
- Wet-lease revenue: 69.87% (₹9,616.88 Lakhs) – lower margin but lower capex
- Corporate clients: 90.16% (stable, strong repeat revenue)
- International routes: 74.59% (hedge against domestic demand cycles)
- Repeat clients: 84.79% of revenue (high customer stickiness)

**Regulatory Achievements**
- NSOP Permit awarded by DGCA
- CAMO (Continuous Airworthiness Management Organization) approval
- CAR-M compliance (aircraft maintenance standards)
- MRO partnerships with DGCA-approved facilities
- BAOA & NBAA memberships (industry credibility)

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### Growth Catalysts (Next 18-24 Months)

1. **Fleet Expansion Acceleration** *(Q4FY26-FY27)*
   - 4 Citation Cessna CJ2+ inductions (Q4FY26-Q1FY27) will diversify revenue to smaller corporate charters
   - 2 Embraer Legacy 600s (FY27-28) will deepen penetration in large corporate/HNI segment
   - Target fleet: 8-10 dry-lease aircraft by end FY27 → revenue run-rate potential ₹35-45 Cr/year from owned fleet alone

2. **Technology-Driven Membership Model** *(Planned FY27)*
   - Proposed air-time subscription/membership tiers (loyalty programs)
   - GPS-based aircraft positioning optimization for faster quote turnaround
   - CRM system for repeat client engagement & upsell
   - Potential to shift 20-30% of revenue to recurring subscription model → improved predictability & margins

3. **International Expansion** *(Ongoing)*
   - Currently 70% international revenue; first international charter successfully completed (2023)
   - Target expansion to MENA, ASEAN, Middle East routes to diversify revenue
   - Potential partnerships with foreign MROs for maintenance support
   - **Impact**: International exposure provides hedge against India-specific demand cycles

4. **Corporate Partnerships & B2B Sales** *(Building)*
   - Travel agent network expanding (12 brokers/agents currently)
   - Corporate retainer agreements (e.g., logistics companies, consulting firms) for regular charter needs
   - Potential partnerships with corporate travel platforms (e.g., Expedia for business travel)
   - **Impact**: Shift from transactional to relationship-based revenue; higher predictability

5. **Market Share Consolidation** *(Structural)*
   - Only DGCA-approved NSO operator with fleet in India (competition limited to unregistered/informal operators)
   - Regulatory moat: High capex + operational complexity → few credible competitors likely to enter
   - HNI/ultra-HNWI client base growing 15-20% annually (India wealth creation dynamics)
   - **Impact**: Operating leverage as fixed cost base scales with fleet expansion

### DNA Shift: From Startup to Scaled Operator
- **Phase 1 (FY24-25)**: Proof-of-concept; market validation; limited fleet (1-2 aircraft)
- **Phase 2 (FY26-27)**: Fleet acceleration; professionalization; repeat revenue stabilization (79-85%)
- **Phase 3 (FY27-28 onwards)**: Scale to 8-10 aircraft; recurring revenue model; target 20%+ EBITDA margins

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

### Operational Risks

| **Aircraft Grounding** | 7/10 | Mechanical issues on dry-lease aircraft could ground fleet for weeks; no backup capacity | Contracted MRO relationships; insurance coverage; wet-lease backup agreements |
| **Repeat Client Concentration** | 5/10 | 79% repeat revenue; loss of 1-2 large clients would materially impact revenue | Diversification into new corporate verticals; broaden HNI client base |
| **Seasonal Demand Volatility** | 5/10 | Business travel demand peaks in certain quarters; summer slowdown observed | International diversification (70% existing); develop leisure/holiday charter segments |

### Market & Competitive Risks

| Risk | Severity | Description | Mitigation |
|------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Unregistered Competition** | 6/10 | Informal air charter operators operating without DGCA licensing could undercut pricing | Strong regulatory moat (DGCA approval); quality differentiation; corporate client loyalty |
| **Macro Slowdown in Corporate Travel** | 5/10 | INR depreciation, corporate cost-cutting, or recession could reduce charter demand | International revenue (70%) hedges India-specific macro risks; diversified client base |

## Investment Verdict

**RATING: BUY** (for long-term wealth creation; 18–24-month horizon)

**Fair Value (Probability-Weighted): ₹895/share**
**Terminal Value: ₹1,000/share (FY30+)**

### Why FlySBS Aviation?

1. **Unique Monopoly**: Only DGCA-licensed NSO operator with meaningful scale in India; regulatory moat is high.
2. **Strong Growth Trajectory**: 64% YoY revenue growth; fleet capex deployment has only begun (₹1.2 Cr remaining from IPO).
3. **Repeat Revenue Model**: 79% of FY26 revenue from repeat clients; provides revenue stability and pricing power.
4. **International Hedge**: 70% international revenue diversifies away from India-specific macro cycles.
5. **Margin Expansion Potential**: Dry-lease % rising from 9% to 34% → likely 200-300 bps EBITDA margin improvement by FY27-28.
6. **Strong Balance Sheet**: Debt/Equity 0.14; minimal leverage enables aggressive but sustainable capex.
7. **Capital Deployment Window**: Only 12-18 months into fleet expansion; 60% of IPO capex budget still available.

### Bull Case (25% Probability): ₹1,100/share

**Scenario**: Execution flawlessly on fleet expansion. Membership model gains traction. International revenue reaches 75% of total. Margins expand to 20%+.

**What needs to happen:**
- 4 aircraft operational by end FY27 (on track)
- Subscription model reaches 15% of revenue by FY28
- Utilization per aircraft reaches 280–300 departures/year
- EBITDA margin hits 20%+ (from 19% base case)
- Repeat client revenue stays >80%

**Time horizon**: 18–24 months

### Base Case (50% Probability): ₹1,048/share

**Scenario**: Fleet grows to 4–5 aircraft by FY27. Wet-lease segment stabilizes. Repeat revenue remains steady at 75–80%. Margins normalize to 16–17%.

**What to expect:**
- Steady quarterly revenue growth: +30–35% in FY27, +20–25% in FY28
- EBITDA margin gradually improves to 18–19% by FY28
- PAT grows from ₹60 Cr (FY26) to ₹70 Cr (FY27) to ₹91 Cr (FY28)
- Membership model launches but remains <10% of revenue

**Time horizon**: 24–36 months

### Bear Case (25% Probability): ₹384/share

**Scenario**: Fleet expansion slows due to regulatory delays. Macro slowdown in corporate travel reduces utilization and ASP. Margins compress to 12–13%. Repeat client % drops below 70%.

**What could go wrong:**
- Aircraft inductions delayed by 6–9 months
- Q3/Q4 seasonal demand weakness spills into new fiscal year
- Wet-lease pricing pressure from unregulated competitors
- Loss of 1–2 large corporate clients (concentration risk)
- Macro recession reduces HNI discretionary spending on charters

**Time horizon**: 18–24 months (mean reversion scenario)
