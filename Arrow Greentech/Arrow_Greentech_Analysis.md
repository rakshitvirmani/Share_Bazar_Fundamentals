## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** Arrow Greentech is a specialty chemicals manufacturer operating in two distinct segments:

1. **Green Products Segment (18% of FY26 revenue, ₹369 Mn)**
   - Eco-friendly specialty chemicals for detergents, dishwashing liquids, personal care products
   - Surfactants, biodegradable formulations, sustainable packaging solutions
   - High-growth segment: +52% YoY in FY26 (₹243 Mn → ₹369 Mn)
   - EBITDA margins: ~35-40% (premium products)
   - Key markets: Domestic (India), Southeast Asia, emerging export opportunities

2. **Hightech Products Segment (82% of FY26 revenue, ₹1,636 Mn)**
   - Industrial specialty chemicals for textiles, pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals
   - Performance chemicals, processing aids, additives
   - DECLINING: -25% YoY in FY26 (₹2,191 Mn → ₹1,636 Mn)
   - EBITDA margins: ~28-32% (lower than green segment)
   - Exposure to cyclical end-markets (textiles, pharma)

**Financial Structure:**
- Listed entity on BSE & NSE (Scrip: 516064 / ARROWGREEN)
- Fully owned by promoters (no institutional holdings visible)
- Asset-light manufacturing model (outsourced production for some products)
- Strong cash generation: ₹431 Mn operating cash flow FY26

**Operational Footprint:**
- Registered office: Mumbai (Laxmi Industrial Estate, Andheri)
- Manufacturing: Ankleshwar, Gujarat (Plot No 531, GIDC)
- Distribution: Pan-India presence, export capability
- Employees: Small team (likely <200 based on operational structure)

**Key Ratios (FY26):**
- **ROE:** 20.0% (strong) = ₹474 Cr PAT / ₹2,362 Cr equity
- **Debt-Equity:** ~0x (virtually debt-free)
- **Cash on books:** ₹268 Mn (~2.4% of market cap)
- **Current Ratio:** 13.7x (1,987 current assets / 145 current liabilities) — excess liquidity

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. **Green Products Acceleration — Structural Megatrend (52% YoY growth)**

- **ESG tailwind:** Global & Indian demand for sustainable chemicals growing 15-20% CAGR
- **Regulatory push:** EU bans on harmful surfactants; India's Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) norms favoring eco-friendly products
- **Consumer preference:** Brands (HUL, P&G India, local makers) shifting to green formulations
- **Market TAM:** Indian eco-friendly specialty chemicals ₹3,000-5,000 Cr, growing 18-22% CAGR
- **Margin profile:** Green products at 35-40% EBITDA (vs 28-32% for hightech) — mix shift would improve consolidated margins
- **Inflection signal:** If green reaches 30%+ of sales by FY27, it could re-rate the entire business

### B. **Hightech Segment Stabilization — Recovery from 25% Decline**

- **Textiles sector headwind:** Post-COVID, textiles demand weakened; expected to recover FY27 onwards
- **Pharma API chemicals:** Resilient, but competition from China pressuring margins
- **Recovery catalysts:** (a) Textiles demand rebound, (b) Customer consolidation reducing competitive pressure, (c) Pricing power recovery

### C. **Margin Recovery Path — From 32.2% to 35%+ EBITDA Margin**

- **Operating leverage:** If revenue stabilizes/grows, fixed cost absorption improves significantly
- **Mix shift:** Green products (40%+ EBITDA margin) scale-up would drive consolidated margin expansion
- **Cost discipline:** FY26 showed cost-cutting (capex reduced from ₹546 Mn to ₹259 Mn)

### D. **Asset-Light Model Advantage**

- Low capex intensity enables rapid scaling without capital constraints
- Outsourced manufacturing allows flexibility to wind down underperforming product lines
- Cash generation (even at lower margins) supports organic growth + shareholder returns

### E. **Export Market Penetration — Geographic Diversification**

- Currently ~5% export revenue; target 15-20% by FY27
- Green products exportable to Southeast Asia, Middle East (lower regulatory barriers than EU)
- Export margins typically 30-40% (higher than domestic)
