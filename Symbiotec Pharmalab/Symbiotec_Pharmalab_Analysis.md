## 1. Business Model Overview

Symbiotec Pharmalab is a **global leader (by volume) in corticosteroid and steroidal-hormone Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs)**, incorporated in 2002 and based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. It converted from private to public limited company in September 2025 ahead of the IPO. *(per RHP, cover page & History)*

**Three business verticals** *(per RHP, Risk Factors & Our Business)*:
1. **API Products** (the core business) — 96.07% / 99.10% / 100.00% of revenue in FY26/FY25/FY24. Portfolio of 60+ corticosteroid and steroidal-hormone APIs (sterile + non-sterile), covering ~90% of the addressable product market. Global volume market share: **38.2% in corticosteroids, 23.8% in steroidal-hormone APIs**; >50% share in Hydrocortisone (80.1%), Testosterone (76.4%) and Methylprednisolone (76.0%) *(FY26, Source: F&S Report)*.
2. **CDMO Services** — commenced FY25 (₹68.0M), still nascent (₹10.94M / 0.13% of revenue in FY26 — declined YoY even as strategic contracts were signed, incl. a 5-year insulin drug-substance agreement, a GLP-1 manufacturing agreement, and a 10-year classical-fermentation API contract).
3. **Complex Injectables** — new in FY26 (₹330.50M / 3.80% of revenue), via the newly commissioned Mhow Facility producing Double Chamber Vials (DCVs) — Methylprednisolone/Hydrocortisone Sodium Succinate injections (combined US market ~US$288M), commanding a 20–50% price premium over conventional injectables.

**Manufacturing footprint** (all in Madhya Pradesh) *(per RHP, Our Business)*:
- **Rau Facility** (own) — sterile/non-sterile steroidal API synthesis, 92 MT chemical-synthesis capacity, Gross Block ₹1,728.15M.
- **Pithampur SEZ Facility** (own) — 492.67 MT chemical synthesis + 300 KL fermentation, Gross Block ₹4,704.59M. Both US FDA / WHO-GMP / EU-GMP approved.
- **Ujjain Facility** (subsidiary Symbiotec Zenfold Pvt Ltd) — commissioned FY26, 400 KL biomanufacturing (four 100 KL fermenters), cumulative investment ₹4,794.58M; base for CDMO and future **Biologics Facility** (under construction, 14 KL / two 7KL reactors, for GLP-1 and Insulin — not yet commissioned).
- **Mhow Facility** (subsidiary Knovea Pharmaceutical Pvt Ltd) — commissioned FY26, up to 20 million DCVs/year, cumulative investment ₹3,919.19M.

**Backward integration ("farm/microbe-to-pharmacy")**: in-house fermentation of soy-derived sterols and microbial strains into corticosteroid/hormone precursors covers KSMs for >80% of the product portfolio by revenue — a key cost/supply-chain moat versus peers reliant on imported intermediates (notably from China). *(per RHP, Basis for Offer Price — Qualitative factors)*

**Revenue mix, FY26** *(per RHP, Risk Factors)*: Exports 67.04% (Europe 29.11%, US 13.12%, RoW 23.86%), Domestic India 32.96%. >200 customers across >40 countries; 43 US FDA DMFs and 23 EU CEPs (66 total API DMFs per KPI table). Top-5 products = 62.27% of revenue; top-10 customers = 57.59% of product sales.

**Promoters:** Anil Satwani (Chairman & MD), Kashish Satwani, Sushil Satwani, and Satwani Holdings LLP — collectively **28.80%** of pre-offer equity (fully diluted). *(per RHP, Capital Structure)*

**IPO structure:** 100% book-built offer, total size ₹17,570 million, comprising a **Fresh Issue of only ₹1,500 million (8.5% of the offer)** and an **Offer for Sale of ₹16,070 million (91.5% of the offer)** by the Promoter Selling Shareholder (Satwani Holdings LLP, ₹1,440M) and two PE Investor Selling Shareholders — Rosewood Investments (₹9,880M) and India Business Excellence Fund–III (₹4,750M). *(per RHP, cover page)* — see Red Flags for the implication of this OFS-heavy structure.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

*(per RHP, Our Business — Strategies; Industry Overview; Risk Factors)*

1. **Complex injectables commercialization** — Mhow Facility DCVs (Methylprednisolone/Hydrocortisone Sodium Succinate) target a combined US$288M US market at 20–50% price premiums; only 3.80% of FY26 revenue today — meaningful headroom if commercial ramp succeeds. Five DCV products under FDA's 505(b)(2) pathway in the pipeline, plus two pre-existing approved DCV products.
2. **Biologics/large-molecule pivot** — the under-construction Biologics Facility targets GLP-1 and Insulin, tapping into the global fermentation API market (large molecules segment growing from ~US$18.1B in 2020 to a projected ~US$23.6B+ by 2030, part of the broader US$100.4B→US$162.5B (2025→2030F, 10.1% CAGR) fermentation API market). This is a genuine **DNA shift from small-molecule steroid APIs toward biologics/CDMO**, diversifying away from the 96%+ API revenue concentration.
3. **CDMO scale-up** — three named long-duration contracts (5-yr insulin drug substance, GLP-1 manufacturing, 10-yr classical fermentation APIs) signed but revenue contribution is still negligible (₹10.94M FY26) — a 2–3 year monetization story, not yet proven at scale.
4. **Conjugated-estrogen (Premarin generic) program** — collaboration agreement with a global specialty pharma company for a ~US$380M (2024) global market opportunity, with milestone payments and profit-sharing already contributing to revenue in prior years.
5. **US tariff structure currently favorable** — the US-India interim trade framework (effective from Jul/Sep 2025 phase-in) explicitly **excludes generic pharmaceuticals and APIs** from reciprocal tariffs (reduced from 50% to 18% on other goods, and generics excluded from the 100% branded/patented pharma tariff announced effective Oct 1, 2025). Management does not currently expect material tariff impact, though this is an evolving policy area.
6. **Deleveraging** — ₹1,125M of IPO proceeds earmarked for debt prepayment; Net Debt/EBITDA has already improved from 2.51x (FY25, capex peak) to 1.64x (FY26).
7. **Adjusted ROCE of 30.43% vs reported 11.56%** signals a large embedded margin/return re-rating opportunity as Ujjain, Mhow, and (eventually) the Biologics Facility move from pilot to commercial utilization — the single largest value-creation lever in the story if execution succeeds.

**Bear probability ↑ if:**
- US tariff policy shifts to include generic pharma/APIs, ending the current exemption.
- Loss of a top-5 customer or a >10% revenue swing from customer concentration.
- Further impairments/write-offs in overseas subsidiaries (echoing the ₹528.13M Navisci Pte Ltd charge).

### FY30+ Terminal Value (Long-Term Anchor / Sanity Check)

Using normalized, mature-business assumptions rather than scenario-specific ones: FY27E Base Revenue (₹1,034 Cr) grown at a moderating **15% CAGR through FY30** → FY30E Revenue ≈ ₹1,573 Cr; normalized mature PAT margin **14%** → **FY30E PAT ≈ ₹220 Cr**. Applying a **terminal P/E of 18x** (per CLAUDE.md guidance for mature businesses, well below the current peer-group average of 88x) gives a Terminal Market Cap of **≈ ₹3,964 Cr**, or **≈ ₹617/share undiscounted**. Discounted back four years (FY26→FY30) at a 12% WACC: **PV ≈ ₹392/share**.

**Important caveat:** This terminal-value cross-check (₹392–617/share) sits materially **below** both the current CMP (~₹1,058) and the probability-weighted FV (~₹1,232). The gap is not an error — it reflects that the market (and this report's own Bull/Base scenarios) is pricing in **several more years of 20%+ compounding and a sustained 55–90x earnings multiple**, consistent with high-growth specialty-pharma/API peers (Concord Biotech, Laurus Labs, Divi's, Cohance) rather than a "mature" 18x multiple. If Symbiotec's growth decelerates faster than expected, or the market re-rates the whole specialty-API/CDMO peer group lower, the downside case is closer to the terminal-value anchor than to the Bear scenario above. This should be read as a risk overlay on the probability-weighted framework, not a replacement for it.

## 7. Investment Verdict

**Weighted Fair Value: ₹1,232/share** (probability-weighted, Bull 25% / Base 50% / Bear 25%) vs. **CMP ≈ ₹1,057.70** (Sept 18, 2026 — stale by ~3 days, reverify before acting) — a **~14% discount**, which on the mechanical entry-point table above screens as **BUY**.

**Bull Case (25% probability):** Biologics Facility commissions on schedule and GLP-1/Insulin CDMO contracts convert to material revenue; complex-injectables (Mhow) ramp past pilot scale toward its 20-million-DCV capacity; CDMO revenue scales past 5% of sales; reported ROCE converges toward the 30%+ "Adjusted ROCE" already embedded in the core business; US pharma tariff exemption holds. → FY27E PAT ₹165.6 Cr, justified 78x P/E, **FV ₹2,010/share**.

**Base Case (50% probability):** Execution broadly tracks management's historical cadence — steady ~19% revenue growth, gradual (not dramatic) utilization improvement at Ujjain/Mhow, EBITDA margin holding near 26–27%, no major regulatory setback from the pending FDA observations. → FY27E PAT ₹134.5 Cr, justified 58x P/E (in line with closest peer Concord Biotech), **FV ₹1,214/share**.

**Bear Case (25% probability):** US tariff/demand shock, a top-5 customer loss, further overseas-subsidiary impairments, slippage in Biologics Facility commissioning, and/or an adverse outcome on the August 2026 FDA Pithampur observations, combined with a de-rating of the whole high-multiple API/CDMO peer group. → FY27E PAT ₹82.9 Cr, justified 38x P/E, **FV ₹490/share**.

**Rating: ACCUMULATE (tranche-in bias), with the pure probability-weighted math screening BUY.**

The formulaic entry-point table outputs a BUY signal (~14% discount to weighted FV, ~16.5% expected return). However, this report assigns a **measured ACCUMULATE** rather than an outright BUY, for three qualitative reasons that the formula does not fully capture: (1) the stock has **only ~3 weeks of trading history** with zero quarterly results as a listed company; (2) **91.5% of the IPO was an OFS by the promoter and two PE investors at a rich markup** (PE WACA ₹147 vs. offer price ₹988), a structure that historically warrants some caution until post-listing execution is demonstrated; and (3) the **live, unresolved US FDA Pithampur observations** (4 observations from the August 14, 2026 inspection) create near-term binary regulatory risk that is not yet resolved. Investors comfortable with these risks, and who believe the Biologics/CDMO/injectables diversification will succeed, can build a position in tranches — adding more aggressively if the stock retraces toward ₹950–1,000 (closer to the issue price) and trimming/reassessing above ₹1,300 (near the REDUCE threshold). The FY30+ terminal-value cross-check (~₹392–617/share) is a reminder that current and projected pricing assumes sustained hyper-growth-multiple economics, not mature-business economics — re-underwrite this call once the company reports its first 1–2 quarters as a public company and once the FDA observation response is known.
