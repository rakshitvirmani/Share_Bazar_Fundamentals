## 1. Business Model Overview

Bhagyanagar India Limited (BIL) is a 40-year-old Hyderabad-based integrated copper manufacturer and recycler. Founded in 1982 by the Surana family, it has never reported a quarterly loss in its history and has zero payment defaults across 40+ years.

**What they do:** BIL sources copper scrap globally (85-90% recycled, 10-15% virgin copper), melts and refines it, then sells it either as a commodity (rods/ingots) or as value-added finished products to OEM customers.

**Revenue Mix:**
| Segment | Current Share | EBITDA Margin | End Markets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value-Added Products | ~60% | 6–12% | Transformers, auto, switchgear, solar, data centers |
| Commodity (rods/ingots) | ~40% | 1–3% | Trading, exports, commodity buyers |

**Key Products (Value-Added):**
- Bus bars — including tin/silver-plated for AI data centers (export to US)
- Enamel wires, enamel strips, transposed conductors (motor & transformer windings)
- Auto components (copper windings for motors)
- Solar interconnect wires, solar copper pipes
- Copper foil, sheets, extrusion strips
- Transformer windings

**Customer Base:** ~500 OEM customers, mostly in South India. Primarily B2B — transformer companies, switchgear companies, auto OEMs. Repeat monthly orders. Nearly zero government exposure. Write-offs <0.1% annually.

**Supply Chain:** Import copper scrap from South America, North America, Canada, Europe, UK, Middle East, Southeast Asia, Japan, Australia, New Zealand. Pricing is LME-linked with automatic pass-through, so copper price swings don't materially affect per-unit economics.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. The Three Demand Buzzwords (Structural Tailwinds)
Management explicitly built strategy around three secular demand drivers:
1. **Green Energy:** Solar and wind require 3.5x more copper vs conventional power generation. ~70% of India's new power capacity additions are renewable.
2. **Electric Vehicles:** 4x more copper per EV vs IC engine vehicle (including charging infrastructure).
3. **AI / Data Centers:** AI is a massive power guzzler. Silver/tin-plated copper bus bars for data centers are now BIL's export product. Management estimates AI will consume 10%+ of global power, translating to 10%+ additional copper demand.

India copper demand grows at ~2x GDP (~12-14% per year). BIL is targeting 20% growth — slightly above industry.

### B. Backward Integration = Structural Margin Advantage
The pivot post-2014 into scrap recycling transformed BIL from a commodity trader into an integrated recycler. Importing cable scrap from globally → processing in-house → manufacturing value-added products. This is a structural cost advantage vs competitors buying refined copper cathode.

### C. AI Data Center Bus Bars — Export Breakout
BIL has been manufacturing tin/silver-plated bus bars for 20+ years. This product is now in export demand for AI data centers in the US. Management calls this a "very big market" with limited competition (China excluded from US data center supply chains, BIL has 20-year manufacturing expertise). *Per Q3FY26 concall*

### D. Customs Duty Removal on Copper Scrap (Budget Jan 2025)
Effective January 31, 2025, customs duty on imported copper scrap was removed. This is a permanent, structural tailwind on the cost side — the savings have been flowing into EBITDA margins. *Per H2FY26 concall*

### E. Restructuring / De-Merger (Significant Corporate Action)
- **Copper business** being demerged into a new separately listed entity: **Tieramaet Limited**
  - Will carry all copper assets, liabilities, and the full consolidated balance sheet
  - Pure-play copper + recycling company post-demerger
  - 5,000 Cr revenue target (copper only) by FY28-29 (preponed from 2030)
- **Bhagyanagar India** (shell post-demerger) retains: land parcels + windmills (book value ~30 Cr, zero debt)
  - Real estate value: circle rate ~200-300 Cr; potential development value much higher
  - Upal land parcel: ~16 lakh sq ft residential potential; 4 lakh sq ft JV possible without further investment
  - Timeline: Expected demerger in FY26-27
  - Mirror shareholding: Each BIL shareholder gets 1 share of Tieramaet

This is effectively a value unlock — pure-play copper company (higher multiples) + a real estate/windmill holdco.

### F. Plastic & Lead Recycling — Next Revenue Layer
BIL already handles 600-700 tons of plastic as byproduct from cable scrap imports. Expanding to full-cycle plastic recycling (LDP granules, PVC granules, injection molding, pyrolysis fuel). Target: 500 tons by FY27. Lead recycling by end FY27. Aluminum recycling in 3-4 years. *Per Q3FY26 concall*

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

| Risk | Detail | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate Governance — CS Resignations** | Multiple Company Secretary resignations in last 3-4 years, flagged by analyst during Q3FY26 concall. Management attributed to better outside offers (comp visibility at a ~1,000 Cr company). Remains a flag. | 6/10 |
| **Low PAT Margin** | Despite EBITDA improvement to 4.26%, PAT margin is ~1.5-2% because of interest costs on working capital. As copper prices rise, WC (and interest burden) expands in absolute terms. | 5/10 |
| **Working Capital Explosion Risk** | Copper prices surging → working capital balloons. OEM receivables are 15-60 days; combined with high inventory at import-lead-times, WC can spike. One analyst noted this risk explicitly. Management comfortable at 2:1 debt-equity, but it bears watching. | 6/10 |
| **Aluminum Substitution** | Aluminum replacing copper in cables, fans, transformers. However, management notes most substitution (copper-to-aluminum ratio went 3-3.5x over 15 years) is already done. Remaining critical uses (motors, precision components) need copper for reliability. | 3/10 |
| **Family-Controlled, Promoter Concentration** | Founder-led, Surana family in all key positions (MD, founder, business dev, finance). No independent institutional shareholders. Sub-1,000 Cr market cap keeps out institutional radar. | 5/10 |
| **Copper Price Volatility** | Revenue is highly sensitive to copper LME prices. Q4FY26 will be higher in per-ton realizations due to surging copper prices, but WC also rises. Hedged on COMEX, LME, MCX. | 4/10 |
| **Demerger Execution Risk** | Tieramaet demerger is complex. Delays could keep copper business undervalued, or create confusion for minority shareholders. Not yet finalized. | 5/10 |
| **US Tariff Exposure** | Silver/tin bus bars exported to US AI data centers. US is currently a small portion, but tariff escalation could hurt this emerging export business. Management says exposure is small and not a major worry. | 3/10 |
| **Scrap Sourcing Concentration** | 100% raw material is scrap — either domestic or imported. Disruptions in global scrap availability (geopolitical, logistics) could pressure volumes. Management has built a global network to de-risk. | 4/10 |
