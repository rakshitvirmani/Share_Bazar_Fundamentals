## 1. Business Model Overview

Runwal Enterprises is the Subodh Runwal branch of the legacy "Runwal group" (founded 1978 by Subhash Runwal), which split in 2014. The other branch, run by **Sandeep Runwal**, still trades as "Runwal" in Mumbai (R City Mall and others) and is **not** part of this company *(RHP, Risk Factor 30)*. The company itself was incorporated in 2016 as Propel Developers and took its current name in 2024.

**How the business works:**
- **Integrated townships.** About 250 acres in Dombivli (Runwal Gardens + Runwal My City) planned for 100+ towers with schools, malls and offices, plus a 36-acre campus in Kanjurmarg East (Runwal Bliss / Runwal Avenue).
- **Mostly owned land, starting to go asset-light.** Only a handful of projects are JDA, DA or JV: 7 Mahalaxmi, Runwal Woods Chembur (83% revenue share), Runwal Lakeview Bandra and Girgaum Kranti Nagar.
- **Market position.** #3 in Mumbai launches and sales from Jan-2023 to Mar-2026 (2.33% / 2.46% share). **#1 in Kalyan-Dombivli launches** (11.41%) and #2 in sales. #1 in Eastern-suburbs sales (7.88%) *(RHP, JLL Report)*.
- **Revenue recognition is completed-contract.** Revenue is booked **at a point in time on receipt of the occupancy certificate (OC) and possession** *(RHP, Restated Financials, accounting policy)*. Reported revenue and PAT therefore follow OC timing, not sales. Customer collections pile up as contract liabilities: **₹5,600 Cr at Mar-26, ~3.1x FY26 revenue**.
- **Leasing / annuity seeds:**
  - **Runwal BKC** (Kurla W, 0.31 msf Grade-A office). Nishi-Nippon Railroad and Genkai Capital put ₹150 Cr of equity into the SPV.
  - **R Mall Dombivli** (0.75 msf). Nexus Select Trust invested ₹115 Cr.
  - **Runwal Commerz** (Kanjurmarg, 1.15 msf, upcoming).
  - Schools in the Dombivli townships.

  Investment property under construction stood at ₹342 Cr at Mar-26 *(RHP, Restated BS)*.

### Key Observations

- **Sales are steady but not spectacular.** Like-for-like pre-sales grew only ~11% in FY26. The headline +24% includes Runwal My City's first consolidation. Deliveries (OC) have fallen two years running, from 2.23 to 1.33 to 1.17 msf, and that is what drives near-term reported revenue.
- **Leverage is the defining financial feature.** Net D/E was 3.3x at Mar-26, against ~0–0.5x for most listed peers *(RHP peer KPI table, JLL)*.
  - Gross interest of ₹314 Cr is about **53% of adjusted EBITDA** (₹592 Cr), and net debt is ~4.7x adjusted EBITDA.
  - Most of the interest is **capitalised into inventory** (₹228 Cr in FY26), so the reported EBITDA margin (19.4%) and P&L finance cost (₹115 Cr) understate the true carrying cost.
- **Operating cash flow has been negative for three years**, even though collections exceed construction spend in most years. Land, approvals (₹223 Cr of "rates and taxes" in FY26, ₹403 Cr in FY25), interest and capex absorb the rest. Debt and ₹150 Cr of NCI equity funded the gap.
- **The profit jump is partly optical.** FY26 owners' PAT of ₹209 Cr (+167%) came from OC timing on Runwal Forests, Bliss, Avenue Retail, My City and Gardens. It also benefited from a 17% tax rate, an ₹11 Cr gain on sale of a school building, and NCI absorbing ₹23.5 Cr of subsidiary losses. FY24's ₹94 Cr included a ₹63 Cr tax credit.
- **The embedded earnings pool is real.** ₹5,600 Cr of collected-but-unrecognised advances, plus ~₹10,000 Cr of sold value in ongoing projectsᵉ, give multi-year revenue visibility. That visibility depends on OCs arriving on time, which is a problem (see Red Flags #3).

### Key Takeaways

- **The pipeline is long-dated and Dombivli-heavy.** Excluding the speculative Alibaug land, upcoming residential is ~20.5 msf, of which **71% is Dombivli affordable** at ~₹6,000/sq ft. The luxury pivot (Mahalaxmi, Girgaum) is real but small (2.4 msf, and 50% profit share).
- **Half the "upcoming" area is not yet owned.** Upcoming area doubled from 28.3 msf (FY25) to 56.4 msf (FY26) almost entirely because of the 29.5 msf Mithagar/Murud project. ## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### DNA Shift: from Dombivli/Eastern-suburbs volume builder toward a city-wide, mixed-use Mumbai developer

1. **Deleveraging through the IPO.** ₹325 Cr of the ₹500 Cr fresh issue repays debt:
   - ₹100 Cr of IndusInd loans on Runwal Pinnacle at 10.40%.
   - ₹225 Cr via subsidiaries: Evie Real Estate (IndusInd/Federal, 9.75–10.15%) and Runwal Residency (ICICI, 10.15–10.20%).

   That saves ~₹33 Cr/yr of interestᵉ. Pro forma net D/E falls from 3.3x to ~1.5–1.6xᵉ, after the HDFC Capital CCD conversion (₹217 Cr into equity) and ~₹470 Cr of net IPO proceeds *(RHP, Objects of the Issue)*. Listing also unlocks cheaper capital: the company is rated only IND BBB+ today.
2. **OC-driven earnings catch-up.** Contract liabilities of ₹5,600 Cr and ~₹6,000 Cr+ of sold value near completion can produce a step-up in recognised revenue over FY27–FY29 if OCs arrive.
3. **The luxury and western-suburb push.** 7 Mahalaxmi Ph1 sold 40% of its saleable area within a year of launch, ~₹1,239 Cr of value. Ph2 (1.89 msf), Girgaum and Bandra Lakeview move the mix up the price curve.
4. **Infrastructure tailwinds for Dombivli and Kanjurmarg.** Metro 5 (Thane–Kalyan), Metro 12 (Dombivli–Navi Mumbai), the Airoli–Katai Naka freeway, Metro lines 4/6/14 at Kanjurmarg and Navi Mumbai airport. JLL argues the Dombivli price gap to Kandivali, Ghodbunder Road and Kharghar should narrow *(RHP, Our Business/JLL)*.
5. **Annuity income.** Runwal BKC (FY29), R Mall (FY28) and Runwal Commerz could add recurring rental income from FY28–FY30. The Bliss Ph3 strata project was cancelled to re-allocate that FSI to a leased Grade-A office.
6. **The market backdrop.** Mumbai is ~24–25% of India's top-7-city housing sales. MMR sold 73,246 units in CY2025, and JLL projects Mumbai sales value of ~₹2.2 lakh Cr by CY2028. The national top-7 volume did dip 11% YoY in CY2025 *(RHP, Industry Overview)*.
7. **The IPO was priced below the last private round.** The issue price of ₹305 is **11% below** HDFC Capital's ₹342.49 CCD conversion price of Aug-2026, and the fund still holds its stake.

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

| # | Risk | Severity | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **High leverage and negative operating cash flow** | **8/10** | Net debt ₹2,778 Cr against equity ₹845 Cr (3.3x) at Mar-26. CFO negative in all three years. Gross interest ₹314 Cr ≈ 53% of adjusted EBITDA, with borrowing costs up to 17%. Even after the IPO, net D/E stays ~1.5x, among the highest in the listed peer set (only Kalpataru, at 2.1x, is higher). Promoter personal guarantees of ₹5,490 Cr underpin the debt *(RHP, Contingent Liabilities)*. |
| 2 | **Land title and regulatory probes** | **7/10** | Media report that **59.43 acres of Horizon Projects' land (Runwal My City)** is under probe in a case about 174 acres of government grazing (gauchar) land in Kalyan *(RHP, Risk Factor 6)*. No notice has been received. The 178-acre Mithagar parcel is not owned and its sellers breach land-ceiling rules. 7 Mahalaxmi has title deficiencies (unregistered agreements, inadequate stamp duty). There is no title insurance. |
| 3 | **Chronic construction delays** | **7/10** | Of 17 ongoing residential projects, most carry disclosed delays of **12–54 months**. There were 18 MahaRERA extensions post-COVID. Delays push OC-based revenue out, inflate capitalised interest and risk delay-interest claims from buyers. The "ahead of schedule" claims cover only a few completed towers. |
| 4 | **Related-party and governance concerns** | **7/10** | The auditor flagged SR Constructions partnership-firm transactions as **"prejudicial to the interest of the company"** in FY24, FY25 and FY26. There are large ICD flows with promoter-group companies: ₹121 Cr to Evie Commercial Assets, ₹124 Cr to Freeway Contractors and ₹35 Cr to Ariane Orgachem in FY26, plus a ₹121 Cr advance to Freeway for materials in FY25 *(RHP, Summary of RPTs)*. Employee statutory dues were paid late. Post-DRHP complaints alleged undisclosed proceedings and invalid land-right transfers. |
| 5 | **Litigation and contingent liabilities** | **6/10** | Proceedings against the company total ₹1,366 Cr (2 criminal, 20 tax, 6 civil, 6 real-estate). Subsidiaries face 85 tax and 61 real-estate matters. Disputed tax is ₹629 Cr *(RHP, Risk Factor 28, Contingent Liabilities)*. Developer appointment on the Bandra (W) SRA project was terminated by the SRA on 25-Aug-2026. |
| 6 | **Lumpy, OC-timed reported earnings** | **6/10** | Completed-contract accounting produced revenue of ₹2,409 Cr → ₹1,008 Cr → ₹1,799 Cr. Quarterly prints will swing with OC dates, which makes P/E-based valuation noisy and creates headline-miss risk in the first few listed quarters. |
| 7 | **Geographic and segment concentration** | **5/10** | 100% of executed projects are in Mumbai/MMR, ~71% of the Mumbai upcoming residential pipeline is Dombivli affordable, and 94% of sales are residential. A Dombivli supply glut or a Kalyan-belt slowdown hits volumes directly. |
| 8 | **Brand confusion and trademark dependence** | **4/10** | Sandeep Runwal's separate group also sells as "Runwal" in Mumbai. The "Runwal" trademark is held by Runwal Developers Ltd (a group company), which granted a no-objection. Any reputational event at the other group spills over. |
| 9 | **Low free float and future supply** | **4/10** | Post-issue public float is only ~11% (~15% including HDFC Capital). Under SCRR the company must reach 25% public shareholding within ~3 years, so **promoter selling or a QIP is an overhang**. HDFC Capital's pre-IPO lock-in expires in ~6 months. |
| 10 | **Funding mismatch at subsidiaries** | **3/10** | Several subsidiaries have fully eroded net worth with going-concern emphasis of matter (Evie Developers, Evie Holdings, Evie Infrapark, Runwal Milestone, Runwal Commercial Plaza). Evie Real Estate lost ₹11.7 Cr in FY25 and has no credit rating. |

**Net view:** Sales execution is credible and the brand is #1 in Dombivli. The risks sit in the **balance sheet (leverage), the land titles and the related-party plumbing**. The IPO only partly fixes the first and does nothing for the other two.

- **Revenue follows OCs.** FY27 includes Gardens Ph3/Ph4 towers, Bliss Ph1 Garden Heights and part of My City C5; FY28 includes Forests Ph2, Avenue J/K and My City C5 II/III. Growth is haircut for the delay track record.

- **Tax at 25%**, against FY26's 17%. NCI is assumed ~nil, so owners' PAT ≈ total PAT.

**Multiple rationale:**

Those multiples reflect low-leverage balance sheets (Godrej, Oberoi, Lodha net D/E <0.5x) or depressed-earnings optics (Keystone, Kalpataru).
- **Base 16x:** a ~25% discount to the low end of quality peers. It reflects post-IPO net D/E of ~1.5x, BBB+ credit, OC-timed earnings, auditor RPT flags and an affordable-heavy mix, offset by ~22–25% RoE.
- **Bull 20x:** in line with Sunteck/Oberoi. It requires OCs to land on schedule, the luxury mix to scale and leverage to fall below 1x.
- **Bear 12x (~1.4x post-issue book of ₹103/share):** a leveraged small-cap developer whose OCs slip, plus a title or probe overhang.

**Bull case — what has to go right (20%):**
- Gardens Ph3/Ph4, Bliss Ph1, My City C5 and Avenue J/K all get OCs in FY27 (two pulled forward from FY28).
- 7 Mahalaxmi Ph2 launches in H2FY27 with >30% sold within a year.
- FY27 pre-sales exceed ₹2,900 Cr (+25%).
- Net debt falls below ₹2,000 Cr and a rating upgrade to A- cuts funding cost.

**Base case — the most likely path (55%):**
- Pre-sales grow ~12–15%, in line with the FY26 like-for-like trend.
- Roughly two-thirds of scheduled FY27 OCs land.
- Margins tick up modestly as post-IPO interest falls.
- Leverage stabilises around 1.5x as land and approval spending absorbs collections.

**Bear case — what could go wrong (25%):**
- OCs slip another 2–3 quarters and revenue stays flat.
- The Kalyan gauchar probe escalates into notices on Runwal My City, freezing sales or approvals in the largest township.
- Dombivli pricing stalls; the FY26 realisation was already down 1–3% on some completed phases.
- Interest climbs as construction debt is redrawn.
- The market prices the stock like Sunteck/Keystone at 1–1.5x book.

### Investment Verdict

**Weighted fair value ₹299/share against CMP ₹295.35. Rating: ACCUMULATE (borderline; expected return +1.1%). Effectively fairly priced. BUY zone below ₹269.**

- **Probability-weighted outcome:**
  - **Bull ₹495 (20%):** OCs land, the luxury and leasing pivot shows up, and leverage falls below 1x.
  - **Base ₹299 (55%):** low-to-mid-teens sales growth, partial OC catch-up, and net D/E ~1.5x priced at 16x FY27E.
  - **Bear ₹141 (25%):** OC slippage, a land-probe overhang and a re-rating to ~1.4x book.
- **What is good:**
  - A 48-year-old family brand with #1 share in Dombivli.
  - ₹5,600 Cr of collected-but-unrecognised advances and ~₹10,000 Cr of sold value give earnings visibility.
  - FY26 RoNW of 27%.
  - A clean primary-only IPO that cuts net D/E roughly in half, priced 11% below a sophisticated investor's (HDFC Capital) recent entry.
  - NAV (₹309–394) is at or above the current price.
- **What holds the rating back:**
  - Leverage remains among the highest of its listed peers even after the IPO.
  - Construction delays are systemic (12–54 months).
  - The auditor has called related-party transactions "prejudicial" three years running, and there are large ICD flows with promoter-group companies.
  - A grazing-land probe touches the largest township, and half the "upcoming" pipeline is on land the company doesn't yet own.
  - Reported earnings will be lumpy.
- **Positioning:** small tranches near ₹270–285 are reasonable for investors comfortable with Mumbai real estate leverage. A full position should wait for two things:
  - **Two half-years of listed disclosures** showing OCs and deleveraging on track.
  - **No escalation** of the Kalyan land probe.

  Exit or trim above ₹314 (the REDUCE zone) unless the bull-case shifters have fired.
