## 1. Business Model Overview

### What Akums does
Akums is **India's largest pharmaceutical CDMO (contract development and manufacturing organisation)**. It makes finished formulations for **1,500+ customers**, ranging from MNCs and large Indian pharma to regional players and private labels, across **20,000+ SKUs** a year. Its share of the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM) is roughly **10–11%** (*per Q2FY27 concall*). It does not lean towards any therapy; its CDMO mix broadly mirrors the IPM by therapy and dosage form. About 90% of unit production is oral solids (*per Q2FY26 concall*). Manufacturing is concentrated in **Haridwar (Uttarakhand)**, with one plant at **Baddi (HP)**. Two cosmetics/skin-care plants at **Roorkee and Noida** came with the Oriflame India acquisition (Jul 2026).

### Pricing model
CDMO contracts are **cost-plus, with a percentage markup on input cost** (API, excipients, packaging). API is about **50% of the input transfer price** (*per Q2FY26 concall*). This means:
- When API prices fall, revenue **and absolute EBITDA** fall even if volumes grow. An 8% API price fall cuts about 4% off revenue and has a similar effect on EBITDA margin because of fixed factory overheads. That is what happened in H1FY26.
- When API prices rise, as they have since Feb–Mar 2026 on geopolitical and solvent/KSM inflation, revenue and absolute EBITDA get a tailwind. **Part of the FY27 growth is this cycle, not structure.**
- The **European CDMO contract is an exception: it is fixed-price until 2032** (*per Q3FY26 and Q4FY26 concalls*).

### Moat and positioning
- **Scale and breadth.** Akums has ~51 bn units a year of oral-solid capacity and covers almost every dosage form: tablets, capsules, oral liquids, injectables (vials, lyophilised, PFS), hormones, penems, beta-lactams, nutra and cosmetics. It also files about **30 DCGI approvals a year** (2–3 a month), which lets customers be first or second to market with new combinations (*per Q2FY27 concall*).
- **R&D:** ~₹130 Cr a year (about 3–3.5% of CDMO revenue), with **375 scientists** (*per Q2FY27 concall*).
- **Regulatory credentials:** Plant 1 (oral solids) holds EU-GMP (renewed). Plant 2 (oral liquids) received EU-GMP in Jan 2026. Plant 3 (injectables) holds EU-GMP and **ANVISA**. More plants are targeted for EU-GMP within about 18 months (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
- **Customer diversification:** with 1,500+ clients and no therapy tilt, no single customer moves the needle. The one exception will be the EU contract, which will be large and concentrated when it starts.
- **Structural tailwinds:** stricter enforcement of the revised **Schedule M** (Dec 2025 deadline for MSMEs), plus **alternate-vendor development** after the 2025 cough-syrup contamination incidents (*per Q2FY26 concall*). Management will not quantify either.

**Reading the trend:**
- **CDMO is the whole story.** Revenue went from ₹804 Cr to ₹1,115 Cr (+39%) in four quarters, and EBITDA doubled from ₹84 Cr to ₹168 Cr. Volume growth has been **~20% YoY for four straight quarters**, against IPM volume growth of 1–3%. Management attributes it to share gains from customers' in-house production and from other CDMOs, broadly across customers (*per Q4FY26 and Q2FY27 concalls*).
- In Q2FY27, **API price inflation added roughly 5–7% YoY** (*per Q2FY27 concall*). Of the ~39% CDMO revenue growth, about 20 points is volume, 5–7 points is price, and the rest is mix plus the Oriflame consolidation (management called Oriflame's PAT "negligible").
- **The QoQ dip in CDMO margin (16.9% → 15.1%)** came from Oriflame integration costs, the Baddi pre-operative build and input-cost volatility. Q1 also benefited from inventory built in Q4 ahead of the API price rise (*per Q1FY27 concall*).
- **Akumentis margin swings** with field-force investment: 200 MRs were added in Q1FY27, and PCPM is ~₹2.5 lakh vs >₹3.2 lakh in mature gynae divisions.
- **API loss has fallen from ~₹10–14 Cr a quarter to ~₹4–6 Cr a quarter.** It is not yet at breakeven.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### DNA shift: from a domestic job-worker to a regulated-market CDMO

1. **Structural domestic CDMO share gain.** Four quarters of ~20% volume growth against a 1–3% IPM is unprecedented for a player with ~10% share. Management cites two drivers: Schedule M enforcement shifting volume away from non-compliant MSMEs, and customers consolidating vendors after the cough-syrup incidents. It has stopped short of calling the trend permanent. It does have **60 days of order visibility** and says "no reason to believe volume growth will not be there" (*per Q2FY27 concall*). Even **double-digit volume growth at flat API prices** compounds the CDMO segment at 10–12%.
2. **European CDMO contract (FY28 onwards).**
   - ~€35 Mn a year (₹350 Cr+) of fixed-price, sole-source supply until 2032, from Plant 2.
   - Revenue recognition may be lumpy: management warned of "abnormal revenue" when the approval comes, because the advance and development work are recognised.
   - It also validates Akums with a global big-pharma customer. Management targets **8–10 global CDMO customers in 2–3 years** (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
   - Separately, there are 2 own-dossier products approved in Europe (Dapagliflozin to Switzerland, Rivaroxaban), 8 filed and 30+ in R&D (*per Q2FY27 concall*).
3. **Zambia.**
   - $25 Mn a year of government supply from India: FY27 slipped, so it will be booked in FY28, with a second $25 Mn round to follow.
   - The Lusaka JV plant from 2029 gives Akums local-manufacturer preference in government tenders, with a regional export hub as optionality.
4. **Operating leverage plus the Baddi expansion.** Utilisation rose from ~40% to >50% in a year. The new injectable and penem plants are still at low-teens utilisation, so they are a margin tailwind as they fill. Baddi Phase 1 removes the oral-solid capacity ceiling by Q1FY28.
5. **Loss-makers cleaned up.**
   - Trade generics has been EBITDA-positive for 3 straight quarters, after −₹28 Cr in FY25.
   - API losses are down to ~₹5 Cr a quarter, after −₹40–44 Cr a year for three years.
   - CEP/EDQM approvals open regulated markets to the cephalosporin API.
   - Full API breakeven is worth about **₹20–25 Cr of EBITDA** versus the FY26 run-rate.
   - Once loss-making entities turn profitable, the effective tax rate should fall from ~32% to ~25% (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
6. **Cash deployment.** ~₹730 Cr of free net cash plus ~₹250 Cr a year of FCF is available. Management is evaluating acquisitions that add a dosage form (MDI, oncology injectables, biologics) or a market. It looked at GLP-1 but held off because of pricing (*per Q4FY26 concall*). A value-accretive deal is upside; a poor one is a risk (see Section 5).
7. **New categories.** Cosmetics (Oriflame), nutra, and colour cosmetics are faster-growing, higher-margin adjacencies to the core pharma CDMO.

### Catalyst status over the five calls
- **Materialised:**
  - EU-GMP for Plant 2 (Jan 2026).
  - Trade generics turned EBITDA-positive.
  - CDMO volume recovered to ~20%.
  - CDMO margin moved to the upper end of guidance.
  - Oriflame acquisition closed.
  - Zambia POs received.
- **Delayed:**
  - Zambia revenue: H1FY27 → Q2/Q3 → Q3/Q4 → **Q1FY28**.
  - Zambia plant: CY2028 → 2029.
  - EU supply: Mar–Apr 2027 → "FY28", with timing to be confirmed next quarter.
  - API breakeven, repeatedly pushed out.
- **Faded:**
  - Akumentis's "double-digit growth" guidance has been cut to 8–10%.

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

## 6. Future Financial Projections

### Key assumptions

- **CDMO:**
  - FY27E: H2 run-rate ~₹1,140 Cr a quarter, giving ~₹4,350 Cr for the year (+25%).
  - FY28E base: +10% organic (≈ double-digit volume, flat price) **plus Zambia ₹230 Cr plus EU ~₹150 Cr** (partial year).
  - FY29E base: +10–12%, with a full EU year (~₹350 Cr).
- **Other segments:**
  - Akumentis: +8–10% a year.
  - Unosource: +12–15% a year.
  - API: revenue flat at ~₹130 Cr, EBITDA breakeven in FY28E.
  - Trade generics: flat at ~₹100 Cr.
- **EBITDA margin:** base 14.5% (FY27E) → 15.0% → 15.5%, helped by operating leverage, API breakeven and higher-margin export contracts.
- **Other income:** ~₹120 Cr in FY27E, falling to ~₹90 Cr as the EU advance unwinds.

## Investment Verdict & Rating

**Weighted fair value: ₹843/share** (Bull ₹1,170 × 25% + Base ₹842 × 55% + Bear ₹435 × 20%). At **CMP ₹770 (Nov 11, 2026)**, that is 8.7% below FV and implies +9.5% upside.

**Entry levels:** BUY below ₹759 · ACCUMULATE ₹759–843 · HOLD ₹843–885 · REDUCE above ₹885.

**Bull case (25%), FV ₹1,170:**
- What has to go right:
  - The ~20% CDMO volume growth proves structural (Schedule M, vendor consolidation).
  - API prices hold.
  - Zambia (₹230 Cr) and the EU contract (~€35 Mn/yr) both start in H1FY28.
  - Baddi fills to 10–15% of CDMO.
  - API turns profitable and the tax rate falls to ~25%.
- Result: FY28E PAT ₹635 Cr; the market re-rates Akums toward regulated-market CDMO peers at 29x.

**Base case (55%), FV ₹842:**
- The most likely path:
  - Volume growth normalises to ~10%.
  - API prices are flat.
  - Zambia is booked in Q1FY28 and the EU partly in H2FY28.
  - Margin edges up to 15%.
- Result: FY28E PAT ₹530 Cr (~40% two-year CAGR from FY26 core PAT of ~₹270 Cr; ~20% FY27–29E EPS CAGR), at 25x. This is consistent with management's track record: strong core delivery, late new initiatives.

**Bear case (20%), FV ₹435:**
- What could go wrong:
  - The API price tailwind reverses.
  - The volume surge proves cyclical once comparisons get tough from Q3FY27.
  - The EU start slips to FY29.
  - Zambia payments drag.
  - Margins fall back to 13%.
- Result: FY28E PAT ₹380 Cr, roughly flat on FY27E. The market reapplies the governance/cyclicality discount at 18x, close to where the stock bottomed in 2025.

### Rating: **ACCUMULATE** (CMP ₹770 vs weighted FV ₹843)

Akums is in the strongest operating phase since listing:
- PAT has risen 2.7x YoY.
- CDMO EBITDA has doubled.
- The loss-making segments are largely fixed.
- Two contracted export programmes (EU, Zambia) worth ~₹580 Cr a year begin in FY28.

The stock has re-rated from ₹409 to ₹770 and already reflects much of this. About half of the upside to FV depends on new contracts whose timelines have slipped repeatedly. The discounted FY30 terminal value (~₹614) is below the CMP. Add on dips toward the **₹759 BUY threshold or below**. Do not chase above ₹843.
