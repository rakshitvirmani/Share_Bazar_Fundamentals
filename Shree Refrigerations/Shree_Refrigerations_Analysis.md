## 1. Business Model Overview

Shree Refrigerations is a 30-year-old, niche manufacturer of **mission-critical HVAC solutions for the Indian Navy**. The company is the **only Indian firm with triple Naval certifications** (Directorate of Marine Engineering, Architecture, and Electrical Engineering), enabling end-to-end solution delivery for defence vessels.

### Core Business Segments

**Defence/Naval HVAC (70–80% of FY26 revenue)**
- Marine refrigeration and air conditioning systems for warships and submarines
- Air-cooled chillers, oil-free magnetic bearing chillers, marinised split ACs, submarine air conditioners
- Electrical control panels (design-to-deliver in-house)
- Service and spares (15–20% margin currently, 7% of revenue; expected to grow to 15–20% of revenue mix)
- Market share: **64% in defence HVAC**
- TAM: ₹3,000–3,500 Cr over next 2–2.5 years

**Non-Defence Marine (0% revenue today, emerging FY27+)**
- Merchant ship HVAC (200+ ships expected to be built in India in next 3–5 years)
- Government merchant marine initiatives
- Trickle revenue expected FY26, material revenue FY28+

**Data Centre Cooling (0% revenue today, emerging FY28+)**
- Partnership with Smardt (global leader in oil-free chiller technology)
- Targeting hyperscaler data centres (AWS, NTT, Facebook, Google)
- Smardt already approved with major hyperscalers; Shree provides local support, installation, service
- TAM: $600M globally growing to $1B+ in 3–5 years; India portion emerging
- EBITDA margin expected in line with defence business (20–22%)

### Competitive Moat

1. **Regulatory + Technical Barriers**: 5–7 year gestation for new entrants to get naval certifications
2. **Mission-Critical Positioning**: Navy can't switch once qualified; switching risk extremely high
3. **Integrated Design-to-Service**: In-house manufacturing (vs. system integrators), test facilities (only firm with controlled ambient AC test room), design office (21 people)
4. **Track Record**: Won all major recent contracts (P17A Alpha, Shallow Water Corvette, Fleet Support Ships); replaced multinational suppliers
5. **Strategic Location**: 8 coastal offices align with naval bases and future data centre cable landing sites (Mumbai, Chennai, Vizag)

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### Near-Term Catalysts (FY26–FY27)

1. **₹327 Cr Order Book Execution** (2-year visibility)
   - H2 FY26: Expected ₹100 Cr+ revenue (vs ₹50 Cr H1)
   - FY27: ₹200–240 Cr revenue (+33–60% YoY) as new orders ramp and Phase 1 facility contributes
   - Delivery schedule frontloaded into H2; FY27 expected to have more even quarterly distribution

2. **Phase 1 Manufacturing Facility Commissioning** (June 2026)
   - +50% capacity enabling volume growth without major capex bottleneck
   - Automation investment expected to improve manufacturing throughput and margins

3. **Bid Pipeline Materialization** (March 2027 horizon)
   - ₹800 Cr defence bid pipeline (Defence Acquisition Council approved 10 AOLs; 3 RFPs issued, ~₹1.52 Lakh Cr pending)
   - ₹200 Cr non-defence marine bids (Shallow Water Corvettes, Fleet Support Ships retrofits)
   - 50%+ market share historically; could win ₹400–500 Cr of bids

### Medium-Term Catalysts (FY27–FY28)

4. **Indian Navy Expansion Plan**
   - Target: 160-ship Navy by 2030 (from current ~130), expanding to 175–200 by 2035
   - Implies 60+ new ships over 9 years = ~6–7 ships/year
   - If Shree wins 50%+ market share: ₹80–100 Cr incremental annual revenue

5. **Retrofit & Spares Opportunity Ramp**
   - Existing ~80 naval ships require periodic retrofit/upgrade
   - Spares revenue currently 7% of FY26 revenue; management targets 15–20% by FY27–28
   - Higher-margin business (35–40%) driving PAT margin accretion

6. **Data Centre Cooling Traction** (FY28 onwards)
   - Trickle revenue expected FY26–27 (enquiry book exists, no orders yet)
   - Reference installations expected FY27; revenue FY28+
   - Smardt partnership provides credibility with hyperscalers (AWS, NTT already approved vendors)
   - Potential ₹20–50 Cr annual revenue by FY28 if 1–2 hyperscale projects win

### DNA Shift: Transformation from Niche Navy Supplier to Broader Cooling Platform

- **From**: Pure defence/naval dependency → **To**: Defence (70%) + Marine (15%) + Data Centre (15%) by FY28–29
- **Implication**: Reduces customer concentration risk, broadens TAM, improves visibility (merchant marine + data centre less cyclical than defence orders)
