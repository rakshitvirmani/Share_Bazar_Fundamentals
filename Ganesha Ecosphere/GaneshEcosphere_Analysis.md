## 1. Business Model Overview

Ganesha Ecosphere Limited (GEL) is India's largest organized PET recycler with 30+ years of operational history. The company operates an end-to-end value chain: collecting post-consumer PET scrap → washing & flaking → downstream conversion into two distinct product streams.

### Two-Segment Business Structure

| **Segment** | **Entity** | **Products** | **End Markets** | **Capacity (MT/yr)** | **Target Margin** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Legacy rPSF** | GEL Standalone | Recycled Polyester Staple Fiber, Filament Yarn, Non-woven Fabric | Textile mills, home furnishing, apparel, geotextiles | 112,000–120,000 | 12–15% EBITDA |
| **Food-Grade rPET** | Ganesha Ecopet (Subsidiary) | FSSAI-approved rPET granules for packaging | FMCG, beverage brands (Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Bisleri) | 42,000 (expanding to 132,000 by FY28) | 15–20% EBITDA |

**Raw Material:** 100% post-consumer PET bottle scrap (domestic + imported), procured through informal aggregator networks with no hedging.

**Key Competitive Advantages:**
- FSSAI-approved food-grade production (regulatory moat; only 13 competitors have this as of Q3FY26)
- Anchor customer relationships locked in (Coca-Cola, PepsiCo)
- Backward integration via washing lines (Tiruchirappalli operational; Chennai in progress)
- Deepest institutional knowledge in Indian PET recycling

**Legacy Segment Strategy:**
- Diversification away from commodity staple fiber: non-woven & home furnishing now >35% of Q3FY26 standalone sales
- Export expansion: UK (primary), US, Europe (targeting filament yarn qualification with global brands; UK-India FTA tailwind)
- Current standalone utilization: 95–99% of 120,000 MT capacity; no new capex planned for rPSF

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### A. PWM Amendment Rules 2026 — Structural Demand Tailwind

**Mandatory Recycled Content Requirement in Plastic Packaging (by weight):**

| **Year** | **Mandatory Recycled Content %** |
|---|---|
| FY26 | 30% |
| FY27 | 40% |
| FY28 | 50% |
| FY29 onward | 60% |

*Source: MoEFCC Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules 2026, gazetted March 31, 2026. These are legally binding mandates.*

**FY26 Carryforward Provision:** Brands failing to meet 30% in FY26 can carry forward the shortfall over 3 years, requiring makeup in FY27–FY29. This creates pent-up demand in FY27.

**Market Size Implication:** India produces ~1.2–1.5 million MT of PET packaging annually. At 50% recycled content (FY28 mandate):
- Structural demand: ~600,000–750,000 MT food-grade rPET/year
- Current organized rPET capacity: ~200,000–250,000 MT
- **Structural supply gap of 350,000–500,000 MT annually** — economically compelling

**GEL's Positioning:** 
- First-mover with anchored customers (Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Bisleri)
- FSSAI approval (regulatory moat; competitor count grew 5-6 → 13 by Q3FY26)
- Backward integration from scrap → granules improves cost & traceability

### B. Brownfield Warangal (Near-Term Volume Catalyst)

Commissioned Mar/Apr 2026: +22,500 MT rPET capacity (₹130 Cr capex) → rPET from 42,000 → 64,500 MT/year
- ~₹225–250 Cr incremental annual revenue at full utilization
- Marginal capex per tonne is low; incremental margins are higher than base case
- Telangana government provided ₹70 Cr of ₹110 Cr total incentives (~53% capex offset)

### C. Greenfield Orissa (Long-Term Scale)

₹500 Cr capex for 67,500 MT rPET; commissioning target FY27–FY28 end:
- Combined rPET capacity reaches 132,000 MT (3x current 42,000 MT)
- Sufficient to service ~10–15% of national rPET need at 50% recycled content mandate
- At 15–20% EBITDA margin on rPET: peak rPET EBITDA contribution ~₹100–170 Cr/year

### D. Legacy Business Diversification

Standalone rPSF segment transitioning from commodity staple fiber:
- Non-woven fabric & home furnishing: >35% of Q3FY26 sales (vs ~20% two years ago)
- Higher margins; less exposed to Asia import competition
- Filament yarn: pursuing UK brand qualifications (UK-India FTA tariff benefit)
- Geotextiles: emerging infrastructure-linked application

### E. Promoter Conviction Signal

**Warrant Conversion (Q1FY26):** Promoters infused ₹104 Cr at ₹800/share — significantly premium to prevailing market price in Q1FY26 — during a low-sentiment quarter (H1 scrap spike). Signals insider conviction in rPET thesis despite near-term headwinds.
