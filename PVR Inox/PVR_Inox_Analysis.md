## 1. Business Model Overview

**What They Do:** PVR Inox is India's largest multiplex cinema chain (post-PVR-Inox merger in December 2021). Core business: acquiring and operating cinema screens across India, generating revenue from ticket sales and food & beverage (F&B).

**Scale & Reach (as of Dec 31, 2025):**
- **1,791 screens** across **358 cinemas** in **112 cities** across India
- Pan-India presence: Hindi-speaking markets dominate, but growing presence in South (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam)
- Strong position in metro and Tier-2 markets

**Revenue Streams:**

1. **Cinema Box Office (60-65% of revenue)**
   - Ticket sales: ATP (Average Ticket Price) ₹293 in Q3 FY26
   - Revenue-share model: Negotiated terms with film distributors; varies by film, language, region

2. **F&B (Food & Beverage) (20-25% of revenue)**
   - In-cinema food sales: Popcorn, beverages, snacks
   - Recent strategic move: Sold premium snacking brand 4700BC to Marico for ₹226.8 Cr (Feb 2026), but continues to retail in cinemas under distribution

3. **Advertising (8-12% of revenue)**
   - Pre-show advertising to captive in-cinema audiences
   - Q3 FY26 subdued due to lack of marketable films; structural recovery expected in CY26+

4. **Ancillary (Events, Private Screenings, etc.)**
   - Small but growing segment; managed via capital-light model

**Operating Model Evolution:**

- **Pre-merger (PVR):** Asset-heavy; owned/operated majority of properties
- **Post-merger (PVR Inox, Dec 2021+):** Shifting to capital-light model
  - **FOCO:** Fully Owned, Company Operated (54 screens signed)
  - **Asset-light:** Rent-based, partner-operated (95 screens signed)
  - Traditional: Full ownership/operation (rest of portfolio)
- **Target:** 149 screens under capital-light model by end FY26; accelerating in FY27-28

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. **Calendar 2025 (CY25) Breakthrough — Structural Industry Recovery**

- **All-India gross box office:** ₹13,400 Cr (+13% YoY) — strongest year ever for Indian theatrical
- **Hindi films:** ₹5,500+ Cr (+18% YoY) — led by Dhurandhar (₹1,000 Cr), Shah Rukh Khan films
- **Hollywood:** ₹1,400 Cr (+49% YoY) — strong post-pandemic recovery
- **Regional (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam):** +4% YoY steady growth
- **Implication:** Not just recovery; structural shift to theatrical as primary entertainment medium for mass audiences

### B. **Content Pipeline Strengthening — CY26 & Beyond Visibility**

**CY26 Slate (Extremely Strong):**
- Hindi: Dhurandhar 2, Shah Rukh Khan projects, Ranbir Kapoor films, Salman Khan (Battle of Galwan), Drishyam 3, Dhamaal 4
- Regional: Toxic (Kannada/Tamil/Telugu), Padi (Tamil), Fauji (Kannada), Jailor 2 (Tamil)
- Hollywood: Avengers Doomsday, The Odyssey, Street Fighter, Mortal Kombat 2, Dune 3 (strong wide-release slate)
- **Management view:** "2026-2027 likely to surpass 2025 in quality, quantity, and box office potential"

**Structural Shift in Content:**
- Films are traveling wider: Hollywood films releasing in 2,000+ theaters in US; parallel trend in India
- Producer/studios increasing wide releases; reduces concentration risk
- Regional cinema gaining strength; South films penetrating North markets
- **For PVR Inox:** Better film rotation; longer screen hold times; higher occupancy

### C. **Merger Synergies — Full Realization Post-Integration**

- **Pre-merger (FY21-FY24):** PVR and Inox competed; high fixed costs, redundant operations
- **Merger (Dec 2021):** Consolidation began
- **Post-merger (FY26+):** Cost synergies, procurement efficiency, better capital allocation
- **Evidence:** 18% EBITDA margin at 28.5% occupancy (vs 18% at 35-40% pre-COVID) = ₹300-400 Cr of structural cost savings

### D. **Capital-Light Model — ROIC Improvement & FCF Generation**

- **Rationale:** Asset-heavy model requires high capex; capital-light (FOCO, asset-light) reduces upfront investment
- **Economics:** FOCO screens can generate ROIC of 25-30%; asset-light 20-25%; traditional 15-18%
- **Execution:** 149 screens signed by Dec 2025; target 250+ by FY27-28
- **Impact:** Lower capex, faster payback, better FCF, deleveraging acceleration

### E. **Debt Reduction Trajectory — Balance Sheet Strengthening**

- **Post-merger debt:** >₹1,000 Cr (FY22)
- **Current (Dec 2025):** ₹365 Cr net debt
- **Target:** Net debt-free by end of FY26 or Q1 FY27
- **Catalyst:** Strong cash generation + 4700BC divestment (₹226.8 Cr) accelerating paydown
- **Implication:** Once net debt-free, capital allocation flexibility for growth capex, dividends, or buybacks

### F. **Occupancy Ceiling Debate Resolution**

- **Pre-COVID occupancy:** 32-35% in good years; 28-32% normal
- **Q3 FY26 achievement:** 28.5% at strong content mix (Dhurandhar, Toxic)
- **Management view:** "28% occupancy is sustainable; trajectory upward; no reason to believe will be lower"
- **Implication:** Even at 28-32% occupancy, 18%+ EBITDA margins achievable; NOT peak profitability scenario
- **Upside:** If occupancy rises to 32-35%, EBITDA margins could reach 20%+

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### **Quarterly Tracking (Each Results Release)**

1. **Box Office & Occupancy** — Leading indicators
   - **All-India gross box office:** Track quarterly trends; target 13%+ growth
   - **Occupancy %:** Target 28%+; watch for Q2 seasonality (weak) vs Q3-Q4 (strong)
   - **Guests:** Track YoY growth; proxy for market share and pricing power
   - **Red flag:** Occupancy <27%, All-India box office growth <10% YoY

2. **ATP & F&B Metrics** — Pricing Power & Mix
   - **ATP:** Track +3-4% annual growth; if stalls, pricing power weakening
   - **F&B spend per guest:** Should grow +2-3% annually as menu mix improves
   - **F&B as % of revenue:** Should stay 20-25%; if declining, mix shift to lower-margin ticket sales
   - **Red flag:** ATP growth <2% YoY, F&B spend declining

3. **Screen Expansion & Capital-Light Progress** — Growth Execution
   - **Net screen additions:** Target 80-150 annually; track quarterly gross/exit
   - **Capital-light screens added:** Should accelerate; target 50+ annually by FY27
   - **Capital-light % of portfolio:** Target 25%+ by FY28; track progress
   - **Capex ratio (Capex/Revenue):** Should trend from 5-6% to 4-5% as capital-light scales
   - **Red flag:** Net screen additions <40 annually, capital-light <20% of target

4. **Profitability & Margin Expansion** — Unit Economics
   - **EBITDA margin:** Target 18%+ by Q2 FY26; should reach 19-20% by FY27+
   - **PAT margin:** Should improve from 5% to 7%+ by FY27-28
   - **EBITDA per screen:** Calculate as (EBITDA / Screen count); should improve as capital-light mix increases
   - **Red flag:** EBITDA margin <16%, PAT margin <4% (indicates cost/operational deterioration)

5. **Balance Sheet Strength** — Deleveraging & FCF
   - **Net debt level (₹ Cr):** Target zero by March 2026; then stay zero/negative
   - **Debt-to-EBITDA ratio:** FY25 ~1.0x; target <0.5x by FY26-27
   - **Free cash flow:** Track operating cash flow - capex; target ₹200-300 Cr annually
   - **Interest coverage ratio:** Should improve as debt declines; target >3.0x
   - **Red flag:** Net debt increasing, FCF negative, D/E >1.5x

6. **Content & Market Dynamics** — External Drivers
   - **Film slate quality:** Track wide-release count, big-budget films, regional penetration
   - **Film window trends:** Monitor day-release announcements; track if <4 weeks becoming norm
   - **Advertising revenue:** Track quarterly; should recover to 10%+ of revenue in strong quarters
   - **Unique visitors trend:** Should grow 5-8% annually; indicates market share gains
   - **Red flag:** Film slate deteriorates, 2-week windows becoming standard, ad revenue <8%

7. **Regulatory & Competitive** — External Risks
   - **Ticket price cap legislation:** Monitor state-level developments; watch Karnataka High Court ruling
   - **Competitor screen count:** Track competitor additions (Cinepolis, indie chains); PVR maintaining market share
   - **Multiplex penetration in Tier-2/Tier-3:** Track geographic expansion; early sign of TAM capture
   - **Red flag:** Ticket price caps enforced, market share losses >1-2%, over-saturation in key markets
