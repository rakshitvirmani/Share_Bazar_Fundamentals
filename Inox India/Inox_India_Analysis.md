# INOX India Limited - Equity Research Analysis

## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company:** INOX India Limited (BSE: INOXINDIA)
**Founded:** 1991 | **Headquarters:** Vadodara, Gujarat
**Market Cap (Mar 31, 2026):** ₹10,665 Cr | **Share Count:** 9.08 Cr shares (₹2 par value)
**Vision:** To be the world's best integrated cryogenic solutions enterprise with leadership across products and markets

### Core Business
INOX India is a global leader in cryogenic equipment, vacuum-insulated cryogenic vessels, and engineered systems. The company designs, manufactures, and delivers integrated turnkey solutions across the entire cryogenic value chain - from concept development through seamless execution.

### Business Segments

1. **Industrial Gas (IG)** - Largest segment
   - Vacuum-insulated cryogenic equipment for storage, distribution, and transfer of industrial gases
   - Serves oxygen, nitrogen, argon, LNG, hydrogen, and other gases
   - Global reach across 100+ countries
   - Four large-scale manufacturing facilities in India with service/distribution bases in Brazil and Netherlands

2. **LNG Division** - High-growth segment
   - LNG storage tanks, regasification systems, and marine fuel systems
   - Semi-trailers for LNG transportation
   - Satellite stations and small-scale LNG infrastructure
   - Strong positioning in emerging LNG economy for decarbonization

3. **Cryo-Scientific Division (CSD)** - High-margin niche segment
   - Advanced cryogenic research and scientific equipment
   - Aerospace, particle accelerators, fusion reactors applications
   - International collaborations (ITER, fusion programs, space exploration)
   - Specialized thermal shields, cryomodules, and high-precision systems

4. **Other Businesses**
   - Disposable cylinders (refillable containers for specialty gases)
   - Stainless steel kegs (beverage packaging, growing market with major global brewery orders)
   - Transformer tanks
   - Small industrial containers (SIC)

### Revenue Mix (FY26)
- Industrial Gas (IG): ~45-50% of revenue
- LNG Division: ~35-40% of revenue
- Cryo-Scientific Division: ~10-15% of revenue
- Others (Kegs, Cylinders, Tanks): ~5-10% of revenue

### Industries Served
Aviation & Aerospace, Construction & Cement, Cryo-Scientific Research, Dairy & Livestock, Electronics, Fertilizers & Chemical, Food & Beverages, Glass & Ceramics, Healthcare & Life Sciences, Hydrogen, Industrial Gas, LNG/LCNG, Metal, Oil & Gas/Refining/Petrochemicals, Pharmaceuticals, Power & Utilities, Rubber, Steel & Mining, Water & Treatment, Semiconductor.

### Competitive Position
- **Market Leader:** Largest manufacturer in India's cryogenic equipment field
- **30+ Years Expertise:** Deep engineering and manufacturing capabilities
- **Global Footprint:** Operations in 100+ countries, manufacturing facilities in India, Brazil, Netherlands
- **Certifications:** ISO 3824, FSSC 22000, IATF 16949, EN 13458, ASME (USA), IMO (Maritime), Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), PESO (India)
- **Technological Edge:** Strong R&D focus, partnerships with global leaders, precision engineering

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### Macro Tailwinds

1. **Global Energy Transition**
   - LNG adoption in power generation, industrial heating, maritime (IMO 2030 regulations driving LNG-powered vessels)
   - Hydrogen economy emerging: INOX positioning as critical supplier of hydrogen storage/transport equipment
   - Expected Long-term Opportunity: Hydrogen capex to be 30–40% of LNG opportunity by FY30

2. **India-Specific Drivers**
   - **Government Capex:** National ambitious LNG infrastructure expansion (₹50,000+ Cr pipeline)
   - **GAIL Expansion:** Oil & Natural Gas (ONGC) pipeline expansion driving IG demand
   - **Renewable Energy:** Cryogenic equipment for energy storage, semiconductor manufacturing (clean energy transition)
   - **Defense/Space:** ISRO, DRDO orders for space launch vehicles, satellite programs (CSD division)

3. **Industrial Demand Rebound**
   - Post-COVID normalization in food, dairy, pharmaceutical, healthcare sectors
   - Electronics manufacturing expansion in India (semicond fab push, device assembly)
   - E-commerce logistics capex (cold-chain infrastructure)

### Company-Specific DNA Shifts

1. **Margin Expansion Wave**
   - **Operating Leverage:** New capacity at Savi; breakeven on fresh capex by FY27–FY28
   - **Mix Improvement:** Shift toward higher-margin LNG, Cryo-Scientific, Kegs (30–35% gross margins vs. IG at 24–26%)
   - **Price Realization:** Specialization reducing commodity pressure; orders from Heineken, ITER, aerospace companies reflect premium pricing

2. **Global Footprint Acceleration**
   - **Brazil JV Scaling:** INOXCVA Comercio e Industria (100% subsidiary) targeting ₹150–200 Cr revenue by FY28 (vs. ~₹50 Cr now)
   - **Netherlands Distribution Hub:** Building regional capability for Europe, Middle East, Africa
   - **Target:** 40% of revenue from international markets by FY29 (vs. 25–30% now)

3. **Beverage Kegs & Stainless Steel Segment Breakout**
   - **Major Order Win:** Heineken approval for stainless steel kegs (world's second-largest brewer) — potential ₹100+ Cr opportunity over 3–5 years
   - **New Customer Base:** German company orders for semiconductor, Japanese brewery expansion orders
   - **Market Dynamics:** Global keg demand 8–10% CAGR; INOX currently <5% market share; room for 15–20% share by FY30

4. **R&D & Innovation Flywheel**
   - **Cryo-Scientific Wins:** ITER thermal shield refurbishment (₹200+ Cr), cryomodules for fusion, liquid hydrogen tanks for aerospace
   - **Patent Portfolio:** 50+ patents in cryogenic technologies; building moat
   - **Collaboration:** Active partnerships with FAIr (Germany), international research institutions

5. **Digital & Automation**
   - **Smart Manufacturing:** IoT integration at Savi, real-time production tracking, predictive maintenance
   - **Customer Integration:** Digital ordering, delivery tracking, remote diagnostics for IG equipment
   - **Expected Impact:** 5–7% cost reduction by FY28; faster time-to-market
