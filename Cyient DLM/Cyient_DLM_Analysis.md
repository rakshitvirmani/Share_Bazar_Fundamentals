> **Verdict: REDUCE** — Weighted Fair Value **₹656** vs CMP ₹866 (32% premium). The order book is real and growing (₹2,813 Cr, 6 straight quarters up), and revenue growth is back (+30% H1 FY27). ## 1. Business Model Overview

Cyient DLM is a **high-mix, low-volume, high-reliability EMS** company, carved out of Cyient Ltd (promoter) and listed in July 2023. It manufactures PCB assemblies, box builds, cable harnesses and mechanical assemblies for regulated end-markets, and increasingly co-designs products under a **Build-to-Spec (B2S)** model — now renamed the "Electronic Products" division (*per Q2 FY27 concall*).

**Facilities:** Mysuru and Hyderabad (India); **Cyient DLM Electronics Inc.** (ex-Altek Electronics, Torrington, US – acquired ~2024, ITAR-certified, integrated and renamed in Q2 FY27). ~7 SMT lines globally; utilisation 50–60% (*per Q3 FY26 concall*). Land acquired in Mysuru for an owned factory.

**Geography:** Rest of World 93%, India 7%. US ≈ 40% of revenue (*per Q2 FY26 concall*). Exports >60% of revenue on management's own framing (*Q4 FY26*).

**Key customers named on calls:** Honeywell Aerospace, Thales, Safran, BAE, Rafael (Israel), BEL (former large defence order – completed FY25), a Japanese eVTOL company (SkyDrive – B2S), Indian EV-charging OEM.

**Business model economics:** Order book = firm POs only (no TCV), executable over **18–24 months** (*Q4 FY26, Q2 FY27*). ## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### 4.1 Record order book + strong intake run-rate
- Order book ₹2,813 Cr (all-time high), +6 straight quarters; intake ₹550–600 Cr/quarter in Q1–Q2 FY27, guided to continue at ~₹600 Cr for Q3–Q4 (*Q2 FY27*). Book-to-bill guided ~1.5x for FY27.
- Unweighted sales pipeline **>$1 B** (~₹8,800 Cr; was $1.4 B earlier, $0.5 B quoted in Apr 2026 — definitions vary) with ~40% historical win ratio (*Q2 FY27*).

### 4.2 Aerospace ramp — Honeywell & Thales
- Aerospace up 40% (Q1) and 62% (Q2) YoY; now 47% of revenue. Honeywell programmes won "several years back" are in initial builds with ramp-up over ~18 months (*Q1 FY27*). Honeywell's IndiGo avionics win is build-to-print for Cyient DLM, currently in prototype/ECN stage (*Q2 FY27*).
- Aerospace is a "moat" business — hard to get in, sticky once in; Cyient DLM claims presence with nearly every major OEM/Tier-1 (*Q1 FY27*).

### 4.3 Build-to-Spec / Electronic Products — the long-dated DNA shift
- B2S was ~6–7% of FY26 revenue; guided to double digit in FY27 (*Q3 FY26*). B2S adds **250–300 bps** to consolidated EBITDA as it scales (*Q1 FY27*).
- **4 major B2S programmes, ~$1 B lifetime value over 7–9 years**, **not in the order book** — production from FY29, full ramp FY30+ (*Q2 FY27*). Includes the Japanese eVTOL programme (9-year contract) and defence/transport anchor customers.
- IP co-owned with customers; long manufacturing responsibility (*Krishna Bodanapu, Q2 FY27*).

### 4.4 New adjacencies — semicap equipment, AI data centre, robotics
- Semiconductor capital equipment: revenue started in last two quarters, expected to grow every quarter (*Q2 FY27*). Power boxes, harnesses, sub-assemblies.
- AI data centre (TAM ~$58 B EMS) and robotics: "just started exploring" — update promised by end-FY27. No revenue yet.

### 4.5 Operating leverage from fully loaded SG&A
- Management says sales/marketing build-out is complete; **no net new S&M cost until quarterly revenue roughly doubles** (*Krishna Bodanapu, Q2 FY27*). FY28 margins "10-plus percent" just from executing backlog.

### 4.6 Defence re-acceleration
- Israeli customer RFPs and order intake picking up post-ceasefire (*Q1 FY27*); European defence (Thales + unnamed) and a US ITAR defence project at the Torrington facility.

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### Investment Verdict

**Weighted Fair Value: ₹656 | CMP ₹866 | Rating: REDUCE** (BUY below ₹590, ACCUMULATE ₹590–656)

- **Bull (25%, ₹1,056):** conversion accelerates and margins hit 11.5% in FY28 on fixed-SG&A leverage. Semicap and aerospace (Honeywell/Thales) ramp hard, and the market keeps a 65x premium-EMS multiple.
- **Base (50%, ₹625):** ~30% growth in FY27 and ~25% in FY28, with EBITDA back to ~10.5% in FY28. This is a good business, but at 50x the fair value is still well below the current price.
- **Bear (25%, ₹316):** the order book keeps swelling but revenue lags (materials, approvals, geopolitics). Margins stay at ~9%, and a de-rating follows a fourth guidance miss.

**Bottom line:** Operationally this is the best set-up Cyient DLM has had since listing: a record book, 30% growth, a strong pipeline and an asset-light model with net cash. But at ₹866 investors are paying for FY29–FY30 earnings today, and management's near-term guidance has been unreliable. Existing holders should trim. New money should wait for ₹590–656, or for proof (Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28) that margins are back in double digits at a much higher revenue base.
