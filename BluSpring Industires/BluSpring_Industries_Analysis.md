## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- BluSpring Industries Limited (formerly BluSpring Enterprises Limited)
- Ticker: BLUSPRING (NSE), 544414 (BSE)
- Headquarters: 3/3/2, Bellandur Gate, Sarjapur Main Road, Bengaluru – 560103, Karnataka
- Established: Multi-decade heritage in infrastructure services
- Listed: Recent years as public company
- Workforce: 93,000+ employees across all segments
- Management: Kamal Pal Hoda (Executive Director & CEO), Prapul Sridhar (Chief Financial Officer)

**Business Model: Diversified Infrastructure Services Platform**

1. **Facility & Food Services** (60%+ of FY26 revenue, ₹2,031 Cr)
   - Largest segment providing facility management, housekeeping, hard services (maintenance, repairs)
   - Food services & catering operations for institutions, corporates, educational buildings
   - Client base: Education, commercial, healthcare institutions
   - Revenue model: Long-term contracts with recurring revenue streams
   - Contract dynamics: Low churn, high switching costs once integrated into client operations
   - Quality focus: Recently added 24 new clients with total ₹124 Cr ACV (annual contract value)
   - Growth drivers: Volume addition of new contracts at better margins, age collections improvement

2. **Telecom & Industrial Services** (₹615 Cr, 19% of FY26 revenue)
   - **Telecom Vertical**: Tower maintenance, infrastructure support for telecom operators
   - Growth slowed due to capex delays from telecom operators (temporary headwind)
   - **Industrial Vertical**: Plant operations & maintenance, engineering services for metal/manufacturing facilities
   - Metal space showing strong growth: new high-margin industrial contracts
   - Vertical EBITDA: ₹57 Cr (11% growth YoY)
   - Margin trajectory: Double-digit EBITDA margins (improving from operational discipline)

3. **Security Services** (₹659 Cr, 20% of FY26 revenue)
   - Leading provider of manned guarding and security solutions
   - Headcount: 24,000+ security personnel (added 2,900 guards in FY26, record addition)
   - Business model: High-volume, recurring contracts with large enterprise clients
   - Quality focus: Zero on-job fatalities achieved in Q4 FY26 (25,000+ health & safety training hours)
   - Growth dynamics: New client additions + headcount mobilization
   - Q4 FY26: ₹169 Cr revenue (+15% YoY)
   - EBITDA growth: 23% YoY (₹19 Cr in Q4)
   - Margin profile: Mid-single digit, improving with operational efficiency

4. **Foundit** (Job Search Platform, Investment Vertical)
   - Online recruitment platform (founded 2001, founded by naukri.com era)
   - Loss-making investment, but showing turnaround in FY26
   - Q4 FY26: ₹19 Cr revenue, losses reduced to ₹9 Cr (from ₹12 Cr in Q3)
   - Sales jumped 50% in Q4 (₹26 Cr in bookings from average ₹17 Cr)
   - Strategic focus: Pathway to EBITDA breakeven by end of FY26 + moving toward PAT positive
   - Management commitment: Investing heavily in product, tech, sales capabilities
   - Medium-term: View as investment asset (potential for sale, scale, or continued growth)

**Strategic Positioning:**
- Transitioning from **labor-intensive services provider** → **integrated infrastructure services platform**
- **Asset-light model**: Most business is human capital-intensive with long-term contracts (3-5 years recurring)
- **Scale advantage**: 93,000+ workforce creates operational leverage and client retention stickiness
- **M&A strategy**: Acquiring high-margin specialty businesses (STEAG Energy, LSG Sky Chefs) to pull up blended margins

**Strategic Acquisitions & Integration**

**1. STEAG Energy Services (India) Private Limited** (Announced March 2026)

- **Enterprise Value**: ₹180 Cr (₹140 Cr cash in business)
- **Business Scale**: ₹700 Cr revenue, high single-digit EBITDA margins (₹60-65 Cr)
- **Founded**: 2001 by STEAG Power GmbH (Germany, 90-year-old company, €2B+ energy major)
- **Business Model**: 
  - Asset-light power plant operations & maintenance (O&M)
  - Manages both captive and non-captive power plants
  - Services: Plant operations, maintenance, control room operations, overhauling services
  - 90% of revenue from long-term SLA-based contracts (3-5 years, high stickiness)
  - 7 gigawatts of power assets under management
  - Overseas presence: 600 MW contracts in Africa, Middle East, Vietnam
  - 2,000+ professionals in India operations
- **Strategic Rationale for Bluespring**:
  - Immediate ₹60-65 Cr EBITDA accretion (9x multiple on ₹180 Cr EV)
  - Addition of 90-100 bps to blended EBITDA margins
  - Cross-sell opportunities: Bluespring's industrial services into STEAG client base (power plants need security, facilities, HR solutions)
  - Established long-term contract base reduces execution risk
  - German quality/operations expertise brings discipline to Bluespring operations
- **Close Timeline**: Expected to close within the week from May 20, 2026
- **Integration Plan**: 
  - Q1 FY27: Close transaction
  - Q2-Q3 FY27: Consolidation, synergy realization
  - Target: Leverage operating leverage + cross-sell motion

**2. LSG Sky Chefs (India) Private Limited** (Announced April 2026)

- **Business Scale**: ₹110 Cr revenue annually
- **Business Model**: 
  - In-flight catering and allied aviation services
  - Multi-year contracts with leading airline companies (IndiGo, Lufthansa, Etihad, Qatar Airways)
  - Long-term concession agreement with Bangalore airport (catering facilities operating until 2039)
  - 400+ employees
  - Mid-to-high teens EBITDA margins (₹17-20 Cr EBITDA)
- **Strategic Rationale**:
  - Immediate ₹20 Cr EBITDA accretion
  - High-margin aviation/hospitality segment expansion
  - Bangalore airport concession provides stable platform for growth
  - Synergies with Bluespring's food & facilities capabilities
  - Capital-light model aligns with Bluespring strategy
- **Close Timeline**: 30-45 days from announcement (mid-June 2026 expected)
