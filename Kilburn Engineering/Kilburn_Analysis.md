## 1. BUSINESS MODEL OVERVIEW

### **Core Business Positioning**

Kilburn Engineering manufactures customized **thermal processing equipment** (dryers, calciners, heat exchangers, reactors) serving industrial end-markets across **12+ sectors globally**. The company has evolved from a legacy tea-drying specialist to a diversified industrial solutions provider, powered by organic growth and strategic acquisitions.

**Product Suite** *(per AR FY25)*
- **Rotary Dryers** — Core product; bread-and-butter for Carbon Black, Fertilizers, Chemicals
- **Paddle Dryers** — Growing segment; sewage sludge, specialty chemicals
- **Spray Dryers** — Specialty applications
- **Fluid Bed Dryers** — Newer capability; emerging uptake
- **Heat Exchangers & Reactors** — High-value, bespoke packages for Oil & Gas, Nuclear
- **Ancillary Equipment** — Conveyors, Silos, Fans, Blowers

**End-Market Segmentation** *(per AR FY25)*
- **Carbon Black & Specialty Chemicals** — Dominant segment; pricing power, repeat customers
- **Petrochemicals & Oil & Gas** — Growing exposure; high-value projects
- **Fertilizers & Soda Ash** — Government policy-supported (PMKSY); largest order (₹126 Cr OCP Morocco)
- **Tea Processing** — Legacy strength; high margins, niche market
- **Nuclear Power** — Emerging growth driver (40% of Q4 FY25 order intake)
- **Pharmaceuticals** — Expanding segment
- **Food, Textiles, FMCG, Fiberglass** — Via Monga Strayfield RF heating platform

### **Acquisition-Driven Expansion (2024-25)**

**M.E. Energy Private Limited** (Feb 2024)
- Waste Heat Recovery specialist; FY25 contribution ~₹89 Cr
- Expands addressable market into ferroalloy, cement, steel waste energy recovery
- Phase 2 expansion underway (Pune facility); ₹10-15 Cr capex

**Monga Strayfield Private Limited** (Jan 27, 2025 — ₹123 Cr acquisition)
- 50+ year legacy in Radio Frequency (RF) heating & drying
- Proprietary filtered oscillator IP; global footprint (6 continents)
- FY26 contribution: ₹90-110 Cr (partial year)
- New market access: Packaged foods, textiles, composites, advanced materials
- Synergy potential: Cross-selling RF + thermal dryers to Monga's customer base

## 4. KEY CATALYSTS / DNA SHIFT

### **Structural Growth Drivers**

**1. Sectoral Diversification Away from Tea**
- **Tea** (legacy): Declining 2-3% YoY; niche, low-growth market
- **Emerging Segments**: Nuclear, Carbon Black, Fertilizer, Waste Recovery, Pharmaceuticals = 60%+ of new orders
- **Management Target**: Tea <15% of revenue mix by FY28 (was 20%+ historically)
- **Implication**: Pricing power improves; cyclical exposure reduced

**2. Nuclear Power Sector Revival**
- **Order Intake Evidence:** 40.4% of Q4 FY25 orders from Nuclear (₹199+ Cr in order book)
- **NPCIL Projects in Pipeline:** Heavy Water Board reactor exchangers, pump room coolers; multiple new unit proposals (Kaiga, etc.)
- **Execution Lag:** 12-18 months government approval → 6-9 month fabrication = 24-month order-to-revenue cycle
- **Opportunity Size:** ₹10-15 Cr order increments; 3-4 projects in negotiation phase as of Aug 2026 concall
- **Risk:** Government capex cycles; political approval delays; tender competition (Lloyd's Engineering identified as main competitor)

**3. Large-Ticket Order Acceleration**
- **Avg. Order Size:** ₹30-40 Cr (FY25-26) → ₹50-60 Cr target (FY27+)
- **OCP Morocco Project:** ₹126 Cr order (largest ever) validates Kilburn's execution capability for mega-projects
- **Follow-on Inquiries:** OCP discussing additional projects; Coromandel placement trials underway
- **Project Concentration Risk:** Few large orders drive >50% of revenue; any delay impacts annual guidance

**4. Waste-to-Energy & Ferroalloy Expansion via M.E. Energy**
- **Ferroalloy Waste Heat Recovery:** New segment entered via M.E. Energy Phase 2; ₹10-15 Cr order increments
- **Cement & Steel:** Waste heat recovery boiler inquiries emerging; Shree Cement breakthrough order achieved
- **TAM Expansion:** Global waste recovery market + steel/ferroalloy addressable base 3-4x current Kilburn revenue
- **Margin Profile:** Lower than core dryers (18-20% EBITDA margin vs. 26% for rotary dryers), but volume & geography expansion offsets

**5. Data Center Infrastructure & RF Heating (via Monga Strayfield)**
- **Global Trend:** Data centers require RF drying for server housing, power equipment; Monga has proprietary filtered oscillator design
- **Customer Base Expansion:** Monga serves tech giants; cross-sell thermal dryers for cooling systems
- **Emerging Market:** Post-COVID AI/GPU capacity ramp driving data center expansion (India focus)
- **Synergy Potential:** Combine Kilburn's large-scale industrial dryers + Monga's RF miniaturization = unique market position

**6. Export Ramp (20-30% of Revenue Target)**
- **Current State:** ₹97 Cr exports (23% of FY25; ₹335 Cr standalone)
- **Target:** 30-40% of FY27 revenue from exports (implied ₹210-280 Cr export revenue)
- **Execution Progress:** Tech tie-ups with Japan (Nara), USA (Komline), Italy (IDERICOE) underway
- **Regulatory Tailwinds:** India-EU, India-US trade agreements expected to benefit industrial capital goods; 12-18 month lag to materialization
- **Competitive Advantage:** Indian manufacturers gaining share from declining Europe manufacturing; Kilburn poised to capture export growth

### **Management DNA Evolution**

- **From Project-Centric → Platform Provider:** Building Kilburn as hub for thermal, RF, waste recovery solutions across sectors
- **Acquisition Strategy:** Bolt-on M&A (M.E. Energy, Monga) to accelerate sectoral & geographic reach
- **Operational Scaling:** Capex-ahead-of-revenue mindset (FY27 capex ₹40-45 Cr to enable 1,000+ Cr vision)
- **Risk Appetite:** Willing to dilute near-term margins (invest in people, infrastructure) for long-term positioning
