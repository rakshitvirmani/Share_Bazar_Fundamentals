## 1. Business Model Overview

Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited ("GWS", CIN: U74999MH2010PLC207187), headquartered in Lower Parel, Mumbai, is a **premium façade engineering and fenestration solutions provider**, verified from the RHP as a manufacturer/EPC contractor of curtain-wall façade systems (*not* a decorative glass or interior-partition company as the name might suggest). *Per RHP, Our Business — Overview*: GWS is India's **second-largest façade solutions provider by revenue** in Fiscal 2025 and Fiscal 2024, and **India's largest façade exporter in 2024 by revenue** (source: Ken Research industry report commissioned by the company).

The company traces its roots to a partnership firm founded in 2002, converted to a private limited company in 2010, and became a public limited company in April 2025 ahead of listing.

**Three business verticals** (*per RHP, MD&A*):
1. **Domestic Façade Solutions** (48.88% of FY26 revenue) — end-to-end EPC: design, engineering, fabrication, manufacturing, supply and installation of curtain-wall, storefront, unitized/semi-unitized and frameless façades for real estate developers, hospitals, airports and corporate clients in India.
2. **International Façade Products Supply** (45.20% of FY26 revenue) — design, engineering, manufacturing and export supply (ex-installation) of façade panels/products to façade contractors in the **USA and Australia**. Higher realizations, lower working capital, no on-site installation risk.
3. **Fenestration Solutions** (5.92% of FY26 revenue) — ultra-luxury windows, doors, skylights under the acquired subsidiary **Yes Systems Private Limited** (brand "ORIA"), targeting HNI/UHNWI residential buyers; acquired Aug 21, 2025 for a 100% equity stake.

**Manufacturing:** Single facility at Vile Bhagad, Raigad, Maharashtra (leasehold, MIDC land, lease valid to 2108), 32,415 sq. m developed area, ~100 km from Nhava Sheva port, "Designated Export Place" status for direct export clearance. ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 and ISO 14001:2015 certified. Four production lines, 130 panels/day post-expansion capacity (Oct 2025 expansion).

**Key clients:** Bagmane, K Raheja, Prestige, Embassy, Lodha (domestic, 8–12 year relationships); Reflection Window + Wall LLC / Winpro International LLC (USA, affiliated entities); SRG Global Facades (Australia). *Per RHP, Risk Factors*: top 10 clients = 86.40% (FY26), 78.13% (FY25), 88.56% (FY24) of revenue — very high concentration. Within the USA vertical, **Winpro International alone = 80.26% of USA revenue in FY26**, i.e., roughly **32% of total company revenue from a single overseas client relationship**.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Backward integration into glass processing (GPU)** — direct margin-accretion lever; company explicitly expects depreciation to be "substantially offset" by cost savings from eliminating third-party glass processing margins (*per RHP, Objects of the Offer*).
2. **Export mix shift** — international façade product supply revenue CAGR of 25.08% (FY24→FY26), carrying higher realizations and lower working-capital intensity than domestic EPC; company positions itself as a "China+1" beneficiary for global façade component sourcing (US, Australia, and emerging EU/Canada geographies flagged as expansion targets).
3. **Fenestration/luxury vertical via Yes Systems** — entry into India's ultra-luxury residential fenestration market, which the Ken Report estimates grew from ₹2.6 Bn (FY20) to ₹9.7 Bn (FY26), a 24.4% CAGR; positions GWS to capture a structurally higher-margin, less cyclical adjacent market. Yes Systems FY26 PAT margin was ~29% (₹7.89 Cr PAT / ₹27.06 Cr revenue) vs. the group's blended 18.34% — accretive if scaled.
4. **Industry tailwind** — Indian façade market grew at 15.9% CAGR (FY20–FY26, ₹37.3 Bn → ₹90.6 Bn) and is projected by the Ken Report to grow at 12.3% CAGR to ₹144.1 Bn by FY30, with Smart Cities Phase-II (Tier 2/3 cities) cited as a FY27 demand driver.
5. **Deep, sticky client relationships** — 8–12 year relationships with marquee developers (Bagmane, K Raheja, Prestige); 30/32/36 repeat customers in FY26/25/24.
6. **Debt-light balance sheet** — Debt/Equity of just 0.03x (FY26), net cash positive (Net Debt/Equity −0.30x), giving headroom for further capex or M&A without leverage strain.

## 6. Future Financial Projections

### Valuation Basis Disclosure

*Per RHP, Basis for Offer Price*, GWS disclosed exactly **one listed industry peer**: **Innovator Façade Systems Limited** (BSE SME), with FY26 P/E of **16.54x** (closing price ₹118.90 on Aug 13, 2026, EPS ₹7.19, RoNW 8.65%, revenue ₹227.52 Cr, PAT ₹13.57 Cr). This is the only direct peer-multiple anchor the company itself disclosed; GWS's own FY26 metrics (RoNW 32.03%, EBITDA margin 23.02%, PAT margin 18.34%) are materially stronger than this peer's (RoNW 8.65%, EBITDA margin 13.90%, PAT margin 5.96%), which is the qualitative justification for GWS trading at a premium P/E — but a ~1.7–2x peer-multiple premium (implied by the current CMP, see below) is a large gap for a single-facility, IPO-stage company with only 3 years of comparable financial history.

Because the RHP itself left the final Offer Price P/E as "[●]" (unpriced at filing), and the stock has *since* listed and is trading actively, this valuation section anchors Bull/Base/Bear scenarios off **(a)** the disclosed peer P/E (16.54x) as the low/bear anchor, **(b)** GWS's own three-year P/E-implied re-rating trajectory and KPI trend as the base/bull anchors, and **(c)** the current post-listing CMP (₹263.5, Sept 21, 2026) as the actual market-clearing reference point. **This is a newly-listed stock (5 trading days of history) — treat all forward valuation output here as high-uncertainty.**

Market cap and per-share figures below use the live market cap (~₹2,370 Cr at CMP ₹263.5) to back out an implied fully-diluted share count of **~8.99 Cr shares** (external market-data convention; the RHP's own pre-Offer count was 7.59 Cr shares plus ~33 lakh fresh-issue shares ≈ 7.92 Cr shares — a minor reconciliation gap likely reflecting rounding/ESOP-pool treatment by data vendors, flagged for transparency).

### Probability Shifters

**Bull probability rises if:**
- Winpro/RWW (USA) order flow visibly diversifies beyond the current single-relationship dependency (e.g., new large international clients signed).
- GPU project commissions on/ahead of schedule (Aug 2027) and demonstrably lifts EBITDA margin.
- Order book (₹982 Cr as of Jul-26) continues converting to revenue without slippage, and FY27 revenue growth prints >30% with EBITDA margin sustained >22%.
- Yes Systems fenestration vertical scales past ₹50–60 Cr revenue at >25% PAT margin.

**Bear probability rises if:**
- Any disruption to the Winpro/Reflection Window + Wall relationship (loss, price renegotiation, order deferral) — given it is ~32% of group revenue.
- Revenue growth reverts to flat/negative as in FY25 (US election-cycle-style order deferral risk recurs, e.g., around US trade-policy shifts or tariffs).
- Receivable days continue rising past 90+ and working-capital stress emerges.
- MVAT High Court litigation resolves unfavourably, crystallizing a material cash outflow (~₹43.65 Cr disputed).
- Post-listing price momentum fades sharply (typical for thinly-floated, richly-run-up small-cap IPOs) independent of fundamentals.

### FY30+ Terminal Value

Using the Base-case FY27E revenue of ₹548 Cr as the anchor, growing at a normalized **15% CAGR** through FY30 (broadly in line with the Ken Report's projected 12.3% industry CAGR plus modest market-share gains):

- FY30E Revenue ≈ ₹548 Cr × (1.15)³ ≈ **₹833 Cr**
- Normalized PAT Margin (mature, post-GPU-integration): **15%** → FY30E PAT ≈ **₹125 Cr**
- Terminal P/E multiple (mature façade/building-materials business): **16x** (in line with the one disclosed listed peer's multiple, reflecting multiple compression as growth normalizes)
- Terminal Implied Market Cap ≈ **₹2,000 Cr** → ≈ **₹222/share** (undiscounted)
- Discounted to present value at a 12% cost-of-equity over 3 years (FY27→FY30): PV factor 0.712 → **≈ ₹158/share (PV terminal fair value)**

This FY30+ terminal value (₹158–222/share range) sits **below** both the current CMP (₹263.5) and even the near-term weighted fair value (₹224), reinforcing that the current market price already embeds a fairly optimistic long-run trajectory.

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

1. **Revenue from Winpro International / Reflection Window + Wall (USA)** — track disclosure of USA client concentration each quarter; any signal of diversification or, conversely, order deferral is the single highest-impact variable.
2. **Order book conversion rate** — order book was ₹982 Cr (Jul-26) vs. FY26 revenue of ₹457 Cr (2.1x book-to-bill); watch whether FY27 revenue growth tracks this order book or lags (as happened in FY25).
3. **EBITDA margin trend** — FY26 margin (23.02%) was below FY25's 26.23% despite the revenue surge; confirm margin doesn't structurally erode as export mix normalizes.
4. **GPU project execution** — civil work commenced Sept 2026, commissioning targeted Aug 2027; watch for capex overruns, delay announcements, and post-commissioning margin impact.
5. **Trade receivable days & retention money release** — rising from 67 (FY25) to 88 (FY26) days; a further rise would signal working-capital stress.
6. **Unitized panel capacity utilization** — was already at 87% in FY26; watch for announcement of a *further* panel-capacity expansion (beyond the glass-processing GPU) to support order-book growth.
7. **Yes Systems (fenestration/ORIA) standalone revenue and margin** — track whether this high-margin vertical scales as a % of consolidated revenue (5.92% in FY26).
8. **MVAT/tax litigation outcome** — High Court petition filed Aug 2026 by tax department; any adverse ruling could crystallize a portion of the ₹43.65 Cr disputed liability.
9. **Promoter and PE-investor (India Business Excellence Fund IIA) post-listing lock-in and further stake-sale behavior** once lock-in periods expire.
10. **Post-listing price/volume action** — given the steep ~45% run-up in the first 5 trading days, monitor for a mean-reversion / cool-off phase typical of newly-listed small-caps, independent of fundamentals.
