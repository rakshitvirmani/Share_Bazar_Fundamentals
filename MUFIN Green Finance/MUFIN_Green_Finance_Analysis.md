## 1. Business Model Overview

### Company Profile
MUFIN Green Finance Limited is a fintech-enabled NBFC specializing in climate and green finance, with recent strategic pivot toward high-yielding tech-based lending products. The company has evolved from a climate-financing pioneer (EV/Solar) to a multi-product, asset-light platform with near-zero operational overhead.

### Lending Segments & Revenue Mix (FY26)

**Four Core Product Lines:**

1. **Mediclaim Insurance Premium Financing (39% of AUM)**
   - Converts insurance premiums into EMI solutions via tech-enabled integration with 10+ insurance companies
   - Entirely digital, zero branch footprint; customers finalize loans at checkout point
   - ~2–3 lakh policies financed in FY26 alone
   - Customer acquisition cost: Near-zero (embedded in insurance platform)
   - Average ticket: ₹55,000; tenure: 2–36 months
   - Credit quality: Near-zero NPA, negligible delinquencies
   - CRISIL A+ (SO) securitization rating confirms portfolio quality

2. **EV & Solar Financing – B2C & B2B (30% of AUM)**
   - Legacy flagship product; still 2–2.5% ROA
   - B2C book: ₹166.04 Cr; B2B book: ₹293 Cr
   - Backed by marquee global development finance institutions (FinFund, IDFC, Symbiotics, BlueOrchard)
   - Market leadership position in India's green lending space

3. **Government Salary-Backed Lending ("Salary Saathi") (2% of AUM)**
   - Direct integration with state HRMS salary systems (Rajasthan, Assam deployed; expansion to Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Meghalaya underway)
   - ~18 lakh government employees accessible via single-click loan origination
   - EMI deducted directly from state treasury before salary disbursal (near-zero credit risk)
   - Avg ticket: ₹4 lakh; max tenure: 60 months; yield: 12–20%
   - ROA: 4–5%

4. **Other Loans (29% of AUM)**
   - Includes unsecured personal loans and other smaller segments
   - Transitional portfolio; declining as company optimizes toward high-ROA products

### Market Position & Competitive Moat
- Pioneer in mediclaim premium financing at scale; established deep integrations with major insurance companies
- Tech-first strategy reduces dependence on branch infrastructure and headcount (target: 300 employees in FY27, down from 420 in Q4 FY26)
- Backed by global development finance institutions, reflecting ESG/impact credibility
- Operating leverage evident: Disbursement per employee reached ₹4.21 Cr, with shrinking headcount

**Revenue Model:** Interest margin on disbursements, fee income from insurance partners, and securitization gains.

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### Strategic Transformation: From Climate Lender to Tech-First NBFC

**Historic Position (FY24–FY25):**
- Narrow focus on EV and Solar financing with limited scale
- Dependence on traditional credit assessment and branch-based origination
- Lower profitability due to high operational costs

**Current Transformation (FY26 onwards):**
1. **Tech-Enabled Product Innovation:**
   - Mediclaim Insurance Premium Financing: Integrated at checkout point of insurance platforms; near-zero manual touchpoints
   - Salary Saathi: Automated EMI deduction from state salary systems; eliminated credit assessment risk
   - These two products represent 41% of FY26 AUM but have far higher ROA (7–8% and 4–5% vs. 2–2.5% for EV/Solar)

2. **Monetization of Data & Scale:**
   - Pre-authorization and collection efficiency data used to optimize credit risk models
   - Deep insurance company integrations create switching costs and recurring revenue streams
   - Government salary system tie-ups create multi-year lock-in agreements

3. **Margin Expansion Path:**
   - Current weighted-average ROA: 2.31% (FY26)
   - Management target: 10–11% ROA over medium term
   - Driver: Mediclaim mix shift to 50–60% of AUM in FY27 (7–8% ROA product)

### Key Catalysts for Next 12–24 Months

| Catalyst | Timeline | Potential Impact |

### ESG & Regulatory Tailwinds

- **Financial Inclusion:** Government salary systems represent captive underserved population; regulatory support for digital finance
- **Insurance Premium Finance:** Largely unregulated segment with massive TAM; potential future regulatory framework could further validate category
