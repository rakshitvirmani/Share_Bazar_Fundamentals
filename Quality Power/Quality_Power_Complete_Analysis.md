## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** Quality Power is a specialized electrical equipment manufacturer operating at the intersection of global energy transition, HVDC infrastructure, and power quality solutions. The company operates through a diversified platform of subsidiaries and strategic partnerships.

**Business Segments:**

1. **Quality Power Standalone (Parent Company)**
   - High-voltage coils, reactors, converter transformers for HVDC/FACTS applications
   - Highly specialized, oligopolistic market (fewer than 6 credible non-Chinese global players)
   - Customers: Hitachi Energy, Siemens Energy, GE Vernova, Tier-1 global OEMs
   - Equipment design life: 30 years (reliability paramount)
   - Approvals: 120+ countries
   - Margins: 34-35% EBITDA (proprietary technology)
   - Revenue model: ~98% fixed-price contracts, 12-15 month execution cycles
   - Capacity: Operating at 100% utilization; Sangli factory (9x expansion) coming live

2. **Endoks (Turkey) — Step-down Subsidiary (51%)**
   - Power electronics: SVCs (Static VAR Compensators), STATCOMs
   - EBITDA margins: 27-30%
   - Benefits: Near-zero tax (R&D incentives), access to Europe/Middle East/Central Asia
   - Q3 FY26 revenue: ~₹149 Cr (largest single-quarter contribution)
   - Note: FY26 Q4 faced Turkish currency inflation (₹257.39 Mn loss recognized in FY26)

3. **Mehru Electrical & Mechanical Engineers (51% owned subsidiary)**
   - India's largest instrument transformer manufacturer
   - Margin trajectory: 9.5% (Q1 FY26) → 16.4% (Q3 FY26) → ~20% (Q4 FY26 guidance)
   - Capex: ₹17.2 Cr planned for FY27 (GIS equipment, high-voltage testing)
   - Products: 765 kV GIS equipment development (major new product)
   - Turkish facility evaluation: $6M initial → $25-35M over 2-3 years
   - Revenue contribution: ₹369-500 Cr annually

4. **S&S Transformers & Accessories (100% subsidiary)**
   - Transformer components: core, tank, assembly
   - Backward integration play for group
   - Merger planned with parent for operational efficiency

5. **Sukrut Electric (50% associate — acquired Jan 2026)**
   - Transformer components (backward integration)
   - Core, tank, assembly (not yet consolidated in FY26 results)

6. **Strategic Partnerships**
   - **Hyosung Partnership (GIS Manufacturing):** $17B South Korean conglomerate providing GIS technology
   - Trial product targeted June/July 2026
   - Represents fundamental DNA shift: from pure coil/reactor player to full-spectrum HV equipment platform

**Geographic Footprint:**
- Domestic: ~50% of revenue (India)
- International: ~50% (Europe, Middle East, Asia, Australia)
- 3,225+ branches through subsidiary network
- 76,000+ employees (group-wide)

**Financial Structure:**
- Listed on BSE & NSE (Scrip: 511218 / SHRIRAMFIN for Shriram; 517030 for Quality Power)
- Post-MUFG deal: Shriram became 20% stake holder (separate company context)
- Quality Power: Founder-led, strategic vision concentrated
