## 1. Business Model Overview

Cyient (formerly Infotech Enterprises, Hyderabad, founded 1991) is a 30-year-old **engineering services firm**. It has added a manufacturing arm (DLM) and, since 2025, a semiconductor product company. The group reports **four segments**: DET, DLM, Semiconductors and Others. From FY26, DET excludes the semiconductor business *(per Q2FY26 concall)*.

### DET: the core cash engine

- **Lifecycle positioning.** Management has moved the pitch from "ER&D outsourcing" (a ~$80–100 bn market) to "product lifecycle partner" across design, manufacturing, supply chain, aftermarket and MRO. They say this is a ~20× larger opportunity *(per Q1FY27 concall)*. Investor Day put it at **$2.4–3.2 tn** *(press coverage of 25-Aug-2026 Investor Day)*.
- **Domain moat.** Cyient contributes to over half of a jet engine's outcome. It has designed full-scope power plants across 12 categories, including nuclear, and its network solutions serve "half the world's population" *(per Q2FY26 concall)*. More than 90% of the workforce are local nationals, and **offshoring is below 50%**. Management treats that as a buffer against localization pressure and as a margin lever it has not yet used *(per Q3FY26 concall)*.
- **Built-to-Spec (B2S) with DLM.** Cyient designs a product and DLM manufactures it. This combination wins deals pure-play ER&D peers cannot, for example a multi-year flight-control B2S deal with a Japanese eVTOL maker and high-value electronics for an aerospace OEM *(per Q2FY26 concall)*.
- **AI stance.** Cyient calls its approach "Embracing Intelligence", meaning domain first and AI second. It has an agentic Engineering Intelligence Platform and an AI MRO platform launched at Farnborough in July 2026 *(per Q3FY26, Q4FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls)*.
- **TAO Digital Solutions acquisition.** SPA signed 30-May-2026 for 100% of a California-based "AI-native" data and product-engineering firm. Price is **~US$218 mn**: $130 mn upfront plus an earn-out. Closing was expected around August 2026, and the deal is debt-funded at SOFR plus a spread *(Q1FY27 results Note 3; Q1FY27 concall)*. Management has indicated revenue of **~$40–50 mn**.

### Cyient Semiconductors: the option value

- Carved out in early 2025. Management's stated mission is to become "India's first and largest fabless semiconductor company" focused on **power semiconductors** for AI data centers, electrification and industrial uses *(per Dec-2025 Kinetic call)*.
- **Kinetic Technologies acquisition** closed 8-Apr-2026 for **US$85 mn** (net US$74 mn). It is a power-management ASSP maker with about $40 mn revenue *(Dec-2025 call; Q1FY27 results Note 2)*. The deal is funded by **~$80 mn of Singapore debt at below 3%**. Goodwill is $50 mn, and minority shareholders hold a put option worth $26 mn that can be exercised after 4 years.
- **External funding** came in May 2026: ₹300 Cr (₹200 Cr debt plus ₹100 Cr equity) from EAAA Alternatives (formerly Edelweiss Alternatives) at an **equity valuation of ₹4,650 Cr (~US$500 mn post-money)**. That is ~6–7× revenue *(per May-2026 financing call)*. Cyient has committed up to $100 mn in total and has invested ~$30 mn so far.
- **Revenue mix after Kinetic:** ASSP 50–60%, with the rest split between turnkey ASIC and services *(per May-2026 call)*. Management's long-term target mix is ASSP 50%+, ASIC 30–35% and services ~15% *(per Dec-2025 call)*.

### Earnings-quality observations

1. **Other income is volatile and driven by forex.** Consolidated other income fell from ₹69.7 Cr (Q1FY26) to **₹6.9 Cr** (Q1FY27). Net forex swung from a **₹46.5 Cr gain to a ₹23.4 Cr loss** *(Q1FY27 results Note 4)*. FY26 normalized PAT growth (+7.2%) relied on record treasury gains.
2. **Every quarter has a "normalization".** Q2FY26 had ~200 bps of restructuring. Q3FY26 had ₹40 Cr of gratuity. Q4FY26 had ₹71 Cr of Astro diligence and a $3 mn tooling impairment. Q1FY27 had ₹14 Cr of TAO deal costs. Reported group EPS for FY26 was ₹38.78 against normalized ₹48.42, a **20% gap**.
3. **The tax rate is rising**: 29.2% in Q1FY27 (+350 bps YoY), because profit mix is shifting to higher-tax geographies. Management guides 27–28% for the full year *(per Q1FY27 concall)*.
4. **Cash conversion is strong.** DET FCF/PAT was 114–163% across Q2–Q4FY26. DET net cash was **₹1,434 Cr at Q3FY26**, a 9-quarter high *(per Q3FY26 concall)*.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### DNA shift: from a flat ER&D services firm to a services holdco with two growth subsidiaries

Cyient's identity is being pulled three ways. **DET** is a mature, cash-generating, low-growth services business that is being turned around under a new CEO. **DLM** is a listed electronics manufacturer that has re-rated. **Semiconductors** is a cash-burning chip venture with a private valuation above its earnings. Over the next 2–3 years, investors will judge Cyient less by DET's P/E and more as a **sum of the parts**. That is why management is setting up separate capital structures for each piece. On the semiconductor raise, Krishna Bodanapu said: "we want to make sure that the Cyient shareholders are not getting penalized for the performance of Cyient Semiconductor" *(Q4FY26 concall)*.

### Catalysts

1. **DET growth turns back up.** New-business order intake is +64% YoY, and the large-deal pipeline is at an all-time high with $300 mn+ added in Q1FY27. T&M is growing at 13–15% YoY CC. If Strategic Units merely stabilize, DET can return to positive mid-single-digit CC growth. Watch Q2/Q3FY27.
2. **Margin path to 15%.** EBIT reached 13.2% in Q1FY27 despite flat revenue. The levers are customer price increases (some clients accepted hikes), AI-led productivity, G&A cuts and a weak rupee. Each 100 bps of DET EBIT adds roughly ₹50 Cr to PAT (about ₹5 of EPS).
3. **TAO accretion and the move to data and AI.** TAO brings cloud-native, data-engineering and AIOps skills to Cyient's aerospace, energy and industrial clients. It answers the main concern that Cyient is "mechanical-engineering heavy" *(Q3FY26 concall Q&A)*.
4. **Semiconductor value recognized.** The ₹4,650 Cr round sets a reference value worth about **₹435 per Cyient share**, roughly 37% of the CMP. Further funding rounds, a strategic investor or a future spin-off/IPO (Ramya Mohan mentioned "potentially a future spin-off path") could make that value visible.
5. **DLM re-rating.** Cyient's ~52% stake in DLM is a listed, marked-to-market asset. DLM traded around ₹290–315 in March–April 2026 and was at a reported **all-time high (₹870 or more)** on 13-Oct-2026. That moved the stake's value from ~₹1,300 Cr to an estimated **~₹3,600 Cr**.
6. **Capital return.** The ₹720 Cr buyback (5.76% of equity at ₹1,125) adds roughly 6% to EPS from FY27 onward. The board keeps a **40–50% payout** through dividends plus buybacks.
7. **Nuclear and the energy turnaround.** Management expects significant nuclear engineering wins within 1–2 quarters (North America and Europe) and is cross-selling the Citec energy capability into North America *(Q1FY27 concall)*.
8. **India semiconductor policy tailwind.** ISM/MeitY programs (SCL), indigenous smart-meter chips and GaN localization all help.

### Catalysts that have faded

- **Project Astro** (transformative DET acquisition) is paused, at a cost of ₹71 Cr in sunk diligence.
- **Semi breakeven in FY27** has moved to FY28.
- **15% DET EBIT in Q4FY27** has moved to H1FY28.

### What to track each earnings call

1. **Q2FY27 (22-Oct-2026), the immediate test.** Did DET return to positive CC growth? Did Strategic Units at least stay flat, as management targeted? Did TAO close, and with what confirmed revenue and EBITDA?
2. **Large-deal conversion.** How much of the $300 mn+ qualified in Q1FY27 has been won, and how quickly are the five won deals ramping?
3. **Margin bridge to 15%.** Separate price increases, productivity and forex. A margin gain that comes only from a weak rupee is low quality.
4. **Semiconductors.** Organic revenue growth (management claimed five quarters above 5% QoQ), Kinetic growth (target +15–20%), SCL revenue start, the size of the high-voltage ASSP R&D spend, and any next funding round or valuation mark.
5. **Normalization items.** Count them. Another quarter with a large "one-off" erodes credibility.
6. **Capital allocation.** Project Astro status, total group debt (expected around ~$250 mn), and whether the 40–50% payout holds while M&A is debt-funded.
7. **AI impact.** Pricing on renewals, the share of revenue from software development, and how much of the pipeline is tech/AI.
8. **Kinetic ownership reconciliation** (74% vs 82.9%) and the accounting for the put-option liability.

### Investment verdict

**Rating: BUY** (probability-weighted FV **₹1,429** vs CMP **₹1,158**, a 19% discount, expected return +23%). This is a **moderate-conviction**, valuation-led call, not a growth call.

- **Bull case (20%), ₹2,143.** Large deals convert, and DET returns to high-single-digit growth with 15.5% EBIT in FY28. TAO turns Cyient into a credible data and AI lifecycle player, and the sector re-rates DET to ~19×. Cyient Semiconductors raises its next round at or above $500 mn on the back of SCL execution and Kinetic growth. DLM holds its re-rating. Everything has to go right, including the macro.
- **Base case (50%), ₹1,491.** DET recovers slowly: flat FY27, +5% in FY28, 15% EBIT reached in H1FY28 as revised. TAO adds scale but little net profit after interest and amortization. Semiconductors break even in FY28 and are valued at a 40% discount to the round. DLM is valued at a 25% holdco discount. Most of the upside comes from the **sum of the parts**, while core DET is priced at a trough ~10× multiple.
- **Bear case (30%), ₹850.** DET stays ex-growth because AI deflation and energy weakness offset aerospace, and EBIT is stuck at ~12.5%. Debt-funded M&A (TAO, possibly Astro) dilutes returns. Semiconductor burn runs past FY28, and the private valuation is marked down. DLM gives back its rally. On a 12× multiple the stock falls about 27%.

**Positioning:** buy in **tranches**. Start at the CMP and add more aggressively **below ₹1,050**, where the margin of safety holds even if the subsidiary valuations deflate. The **earnings-only DCF (₹1,229)** says the upside depends on the DLM and semiconductor valuations holding. Q2FY27 (22-Oct-2026) is the near-term proof point: if DET is negative in CC again or Strategic Units fall further, shift weight to the bear case and downgrade to **HOLD**.
