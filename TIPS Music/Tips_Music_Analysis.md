## 1. Business Model Overview

### What Tips does
Tips is India's **third/fourth-largest listed music label** after T-Series (unlisted), Saregama and Sony. Management puts its **revenue market share at ~8–10%** of the ~₹3,500–4,000 Cr Indian recorded-music industry (*per Q3FY26 and Q2FY27 concalls*).

It owns a catalogue of roughly **38,000 songs**: ~34,000 earlier plus ~4,000 Gujarati/Kutchi songs from the Studio Radha acquisition (Jul–Aug 2025), which were digitised and added by Q4FY26 (*per Q2FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls*). The catalogue is concentrated in **1988–2020 Hindi film music**: 90s hits from *Raja Hindustani*, *Kya Kehna*, *Soldier*, *Taal*, *Dil Ka Rishta*, *Ajnabee* and *Race*. It also includes regional labels (Tips Punjabi, Bhojpuri, Haryanvi, Rajasthani, Sindhi, Telugu, Gujarati).

**The business is a catalogue-annuity model:**
- ~**85% of revenue comes from content more than 3 years old** and ~15% from releases of the last 3 years (*per Q2FY25, Q3FY26, Q1FY27 and Q2FY27 concalls*).
- Fixed costs are tiny: ~98 employees (*per Q4FY26 concall*), negligible capex and depreciation of ~₹2–3 Cr a year.
- **EBITDA before content cost is a steady ~90% of revenue** (see Section 2).
- Reported EBITDA margin is therefore set almost entirely by how much is spent on new content.

**Domestic vs international.**
- ~70–75% of **consumption** is in India.
- The annual report shows ~75% of revenue as foreign-currency receipts. That is only because YouTube and Warner bill in USD from overseas entities (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
- True international revenue comes from SMP publishing, TikTok and diaspora streams.

### Industry structure: the shift to paid streaming
- **Monetisation gap.** India is comparable to the US in stream volume but only ~2% of the ~USD 29 Bn global label revenue pool (*per Q4FY25 concall*). Indian labels earn **₹0.04–0.10 per stream vs ₹0.50–0.90 globally**. The gap is almost entirely subscription: paid users are 50–60% of revenue globally vs ~15% in India (*per Q1FY27 concall*).
- **Paid subscriptions** are **~10–15% of Tips' digital revenue**, up from 10–12% a year earlier (*per Q4FY26 and Q2FY27 concalls*). Paid subscribers are growing **30–50% a year**, and Spotify's India paid base grew >50% YoY in CY2025 (*per Q3FY26 concall*).
- **Platform consolidation (2024–25):**
  - **Wynk (Airtel) shut down in Q3FY25.** Tips had a long dispute with Airtel and received a **₹12 Cr settlement in H1FY25**, a one-off.
  - Resso exited earlier. Gaana and JioSaavn pushed free users behind paywalls and restricted free replays.
  - In the short term this cut free-tier streams. Management called it "a temporary phase" of 3–7 quarters (*per Q2FY26 concall*).
- **Price hikes:**
  - YouTube Premium raised prices ~40% in 2024 (*per Q2FY25 concall*).
  - **Spotify raised India prices for the first time in H2CY2025** (~20–25%), and Spotify and YouTube both raised prices again around end-CY2025 (*per Q2FY26, Q3FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls*).
  - Spotify revised plan pricing in May 2026, which management called "not material" (*per Q1FY27 concall*).
  - Amazon Music launched a 3-tier plan for non-Prime users (*per Q1FY27 concall*).
  - Labels get a fixed ~55% share, so higher ARPU passes straight through.
- **Bundling** through telecom packs pays labels less, and Tips prefers pure-play subscriptions (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
- **Industry growth is slow.** Tips' own deck shows ~8% industry growth. One analyst cited a 2025 industry report showing digital licensing +2% and **YouTube −8%** (*per Q4FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls*). **Tips has outgrown the industry 2–3x** thanks to catalogue virality (Instagram reels driving Spotify and YouTube streams). Management says Q2FY27 growth was **"primarily volume-driven", with streams up ~30–35% YoY** and per-stream rates flat at ~₹0.05 (*per Q2FY27 concall*).

### Content sourcing model
Content comes in **roughly equal thirds** (*per Q1FY27 concall*):

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Tips Films** | Promoter-owned sister company. Music rights are bought "at arm's length", approved by the board and auditors |
| **Outside producers** | Third-party films |
| **In-house non-film / regional** | Includes recreations of catalogue songs (e.g. *Aankhon Se Tune 2.0*) |

- Management **refuses to join bidding wars**. Kumar Taurani's example: he offered ₹3–5 Cr for a film's music, the producer had a ₹17 Cr rival offer, and Tips walked away (*per Q2FY26 concall*).
- **Target payback is 4–5 years**, and management says the new-release hit rate is only 10–15% (*per Q4FY25 and Q2FY26 concalls*).

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### DNA shift: from a "30% compounder" to a paid-streaming option on a mature catalogue
Tips went from ₹91 Cr revenue (FY21) to ₹376 Cr (FY26) on three things:
- YouTube growth;
- the switch to Warner (which put Tips on Meta and Apple for the first time);
- the virality of its 90s catalogue on Instagram reels.

Growth has now **normalised to ~20–25%**. The next step-up depends on India's **ad-to-subscription transition**, where per-stream economics are 5–10x better.

## Investment Verdict & Rating

**Weighted fair value: ₹843/share** (Bull ₹1,160 × 25% + Base ₹855 × 50% + Bear ₹501 × 25%). At **CMP ₹784 (Nov 12, 2026)**, that is 7.0% below FV and implies +7.5% upside, plus a ~2% annual payout.

**Entry levels:** BUY below ₹759 · ACCUMULATE ₹759–843 · HOLD ₹843–885 · REDUCE above ₹885.

**Bull case (25%), FV ₹1,160:**
- What has to go right:
  - India's paid-streaming transition accelerates: paid share passes 20% of digital, with further Spotify/YouTube price hikes.
  - The YouTube Shorts renewal is a material step-up and moves toward revenue share.
  - Warner consumption beats the MG, delivering an FY28 overflow and a richer renewal.
  - The FY27 film slate builds durable new catalogue.
- Result: revenue compounds at 25–27% and FY28E PAT reaches ₹328 Cr. The market pays 45x, as in 2024–25.

**Base case (50%), FV ₹855:**
- The most likely path:
  - ~20% revenue growth, driven by catalogue volume and a gradual subscription mix-shift.
  - Content at ~22% of revenue and EBITDA margin ~70%.
  - 100% payout continues.
- Result: FY28E PAT ₹302 Cr (~18% CAGR from FY26) at 36x. This matches management's track record: steady core delivery, slower-than-promised new monetisation.

**Bear case (25%), FV ₹501:**
- What could go wrong:
  - Catalogue virality fades and growth reverts toward the ~8–10% industry rate.
  - Shorts renews flat and the Warner MG resets lower in FY28.
  - Competition for film rights pushes content to 25% of revenue.
- Result: FY28E PAT ₹255 Cr, flat on FY27E. The market treats Tips as an ex-growth annuity at 25x, below the 52-week low's multiple.

### Rating: **ACCUMULATE** (CMP ₹784 vs weighted FV ₹843)

Tips is a high-quality, cash-returning catalogue business:
- ~90% EBITDA before content;
- zero debt and ~₹280 Cr net cash;
- 100% of prior-year PAT returned to shareholders.

Revenue growth has re-accelerated to 28% in H1FY27 and 35% in Q2. FY27 PAT growth will lag because of the deliberate content catch-up.

The stock has moved from its 52-week low of ₹481 to ₹784 and already discounts most of the base case (33x FY28E). The discounted FY30 terminal value (~₹687 including payouts) is below the CMP. The two key 2027–28 renegotiations (YouTube Shorts, then Warner) are binary. **Add gradually, preferably near or below the ₹759 BUY threshold. Do not chase above ₹843.** Re-rate to BUY only on a signed Shorts step-up plus evidence that paid subscriptions are inflecting.
