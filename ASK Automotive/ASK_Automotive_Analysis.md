## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- ASK Automotive Limited – Leading safety systems manufacturer for automotive (2W, 3W, 4W segments)
- Listed on NSE (ASKAUTOLTD) and BSE (544022) on November 15, 2023 (IPO ~7 months ago at time of this analysis)
- Headquarters: Gurgaon/Manesar, Haryana
- Manufacturing: 18 state-of-the-art facilities across India (9 plants in Haryana, 2 in Uttarakhand, 1 in Rajasthan, 1 in Himachal Pradesh, 2 in Karnataka, 3 in Gujarat)
- Leadership: Kuldip Singh Rathee (Chairman & MD), Prashant Rathee (Joint MD), Aman Rathee (Joint MD)
- Workforce: 9,000+ employees
- Est. operating: 35+ years (founded as ASK Automotive Private Limited)
- AHSAAS: CSR arm focused on education, healthcare, women safety, sports talent, conservation, skill development

**Business Model: Diversified Automotive Components Supplier**

1. **Advanced Braking Systems (ABS) – 37% of FY26 revenue, ₹1,457 Cr**
   - Largest manufacturer of 2W ABS in India with ~50% market share
   - Also supplies to 3W, 4W segments
   - Products: Brake shoes, brake pads, brake panels, case missions for 2W; brake shoes/pads for 3W/4W
   - Customer base: Hero (33 years), Honda, Bajaj, Yamaha, Suzuki, TVS, Royal Enfield, etc.
   - EV & Non-EV powertrain agnostic (regen braking compatible)
   - Technology collaborations: Japanese manufacturer (2001, non-asbestos), NUCAP Canada (2016, patented retention systems)

2. **Aluminium Lightweighting Precision Solutions (ALPS) – 51% of FY26 revenue, ₹2,134 Cr**
   - Alloy wheels, engine covers, crankcase, pillions grip, throttle body, filter housings (2W/PV segment)
   - High-pressure die casting technology
   - Growing EV opportunity (lightweight components for battery vehicles)
   - Recent breakthrough: High Pressure Die Casted Alloy Wheel with Kyushu Yanagawa (Japan), first supply to leading Japanese customer
   - Strategic JV: AISIN, Japan (2024) – OE auto components supplier

3. **Safety Control Cables (SCC) – 4% of FY26 revenue, ₹167 Cr**
   - Front/rear brake cables, throttle cables, speedometer cables, gear-shift cables (2W/3W)
   - Smaller contribution but growing (focused on regulated markets)
   - Technical collaboration: HSH Safety Control Cable (2016), GEMO Germany (2025) for sunroof cables (3W)

4. **Wheel Assembly (Closed FY26)**
   - Strategic closure: Low-value-added business, diverted resources to higher-margin products
   - ₹47 Cr FY26 → NIL FY27 (managed exit to optimize product mix)

5. **Other Segments & Exports**
   - Export segment: 2.9% of revenue, growing to 14 countries
   - Joint Venture: FRAS-LE Brazil (2018, brake linings for CV segment)
   - Aftermarket sales: Independent after-market channel growing (IAM 7% of revenue)
   - Products for exports: ECU bodies, ECU housings, demolition hammers, fan covers, base adapters

**Market Position & Moat**
- Largest 2W ABS manufacturer in India (~50% market share)
- Long-standing customer relationships (25-33 years with top OEMs)
- Technical collaborations with world-class players (Japanese, Canadian, Brazilian)
- Powertrain-agnostic product portfolio (EV & Non-EV ready)
- Proprietary material formulations, in-house engineering & design capabilities
- High-entry barriers due to:
  - Regulatory approvals (OEM certification cycles)
  - Technical expertise & manufacturing know-how
  - Established customer relationships (switching costs)
  - Capital intensity of manufacturing facilities
