## 1. Business Model Overview

**What They Do:** Landmark Cars is India's largest used car retailer and aftersales service provider. Core business: acquiring, reconditioning, and retailing pre-owned premium vehicles from OEM partners, plus ancillary aftersales services (workshops, parts, insurance).

**Scale & Reach (as of FY26 end, March 31, 2026):**
- **Estimated 100+ outlets** across major metros and Tier-2 cities
- **47 lakh passenger vehicles sold in FY26** (13% YoY growth; outpaced industry 13% growth)
- **Strong OEM partnership base:** Mercedes-Benz (40% of revenue), BYD, Mahindra & Mahindra, MG Motors, Renault, Honda, Jeep, Ashok Leyland (commercial)

**Revenue Streams:**

1. **Used Car Sales (New Car Sales Channel) (75-80% of revenue)**
   - Sells certified pre-owned (CPO) vehicles from OEM partners
   - Average Selling Price (ASP): ₹23 lakh (FY26 full year; ₹21.96 lakh FY25)
   - ASP increased to ₹23 lakh in FY26 from ₹20.79 lakh in FY25 (+10.8% YoY)
   - Mercedes ASP ₹73 lakhs (Q4 FY26, up from ₹69 lakhs Q3 FY26)
   - Revenue for new car sales: ₹5,668 crores (FY26, +21% YoY)

2. **Aftersales Services (20-25% of revenue)**
   - Workshops, parts sales, vehicle servicing, insurance
   - **Milestone achieved:** Aftersales revenue crossed ₹1,000 crore mark in FY26
   - FY26 aftersales revenue: ₹1,051 crores (+12% YoY)
   - Gross margin on aftersales: 20%+ (highest in business; 10-year track record)
   - Average revenue per vehicle service: ₹27,148 (FY26 vs ₹27,420 Q4 FY25)
   - Workshops: Progressively ramping up (18 months into expansion, still in ramp-up phase; historic growth being demonstrated)

**Operating Model Evolution:**

- **Pre-FY24:** Asset-light model; partnerships with OEMs for used car sales
- **FY24-25:** Rapid expansion of workshops and aftersales footprint; new market entry (Pune, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Mumbai)
- **FY26:** Consolidation phase after 18 months of rapid expansion; focus on profitability optimization and asset sweating
- **FY27+:** "Disciplined expansion" — will grow profitably, not chase volume

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. **India's Auto Retail Market Inflection — Used Car TAM Expansion**

- **Market size:** India is world's 3rd largest auto market (behind China, US), with long growth runway
- **Used car penetration:** Historically low vs developed markets (20-30% in India vs 50%+ in US/Europe)
- **Structural driver:** Rising income, vehicle affordability, financing availability → increased replacement cycle and used car acceptance
- **For Landmark:** Well-positioned as largest player; can capture disproportionate share of TAM growth

### B. **Premium & Luxury Segment Resilience + Growth**

- **Mercedes-Benz strategy:** Recently launched all-new V-Class (₹1.4 crores), new electric CLA (₹55 lakhs)
- **Mercedes volume:** 40% of Landmark revenue; launching 40 new models globally this year
- **Industry tailwind:** EU FTA signed (Feb 2026) → multiple OEMs (Mercedes, Renault, Volkswagen, Stellantis) positioning India at center of growth strategy
- **For Landmark:** Over 50% of volume from OEMs who will benefit from FTA; new model launches support higher ASP
- **Top-end vehicle contribution:** ~20% of Mercedes sales; driving premiumization

### C. **EV Penetration & OEM Diversification**

- **EV % of Landmark sales:** Grew from <5% (FY25) to 21%+ (FY26)
- **Key OEM partners in EVs:**
  - **BYD:** 80% volume growth in CY2025; homologated 3 new models (Sealing 7, Atto 3, eMAX); strong momentum
  - **Mahindra & Mahindra:** Strong launches (XEV 9S, XUV 7XO); expected to scale significantly
  - **MG Motors:** MG Windsor becoming highest-selling EV in India; multiple launches planned
- **For Landmark:** Exposure to fastest-growing segment; aftersales margin structure proven (12-14% revenue drop per Goldman Sachs, but offset by value-added services)

### D. **Aftersales Business Reaching Inflection Point**

- **FY26 milestone:** ₹1,000+ crore aftersales revenue on annual basis
- **Margin profile:** 20%+ gross margin (2x used car sales business); EBITDA margin 14%+
- **Growth driver:** As vehicle parc grows and warranty periods mature, aftersales revenue accelerates
- **Workshops:** 10-year track record of 20%+ gross margin; currently in ramp-up phase but demonstrating historical growth rates
- **For Landmark:** Aftersales can become 30-40% of revenue by FY28; materially improve overall profitability

### E. **OEM Portfolio Diversification & Stability**

- **Geographic + Brand diversification:** Mercedes (40%), BYD, Mahindra, MG, Renault, Honda, Jeep, Ashok Leyland (commercial)
- **Risk mitigation:** No single OEM >40%; multiple growth drivers; hedge against brand-level slowdowns
- **New launches:** Renault Duster re-launch (started mid-April, positive response); Jeep new SUV (in collaboration with Stellantis); Honda new strategy (10 launches planned)

### F. **Cost Optimization & Operating Leverage**

- **Margins improving sequentially:** Q3 FY26 EBITDA margin 5.9% → FY26 full year 5.8% (maintained despite new outlet ramp-up)
- **Employee cost & OpEx discipline:** Kept below 4.4% of proforma revenue; workforce efficiency improving
- **GST 2.0 rationalization (CY25):** One-time benefit; ownership costs down for customers; stimulated demand
- **For Landmark:** Consolidation phase allowing margin expansion; 100-200 bps EBITDA margin upside possible by FY28

   - **Gross margin:** Target 17%+ (from 16.7% in FY26)
   - **EBITDA margin:** Target 7%+ by Q2 FY27 (from 5.8% FY26 full year)
   - **PAT margin:** Should improve from 0.8% to 1.1%+ as aftersales mix improves
   - **Red flag:** EBITDA margin <6%, PAT margin declines, gross margin <16%

3. **Aftersales & Workshops**
   - **Aftersales revenue growth:** Target 15-18% annually
   - **Aftersales as % of revenue:** Should grow from 21% to 25-28% by FY28
   - **Workshop contribution:** Track gross margin; target 20%+ (historical 10-year benchmark)
   - **Outlet count:** Should add 5-15 net outlets annually in consolidation phase
   - **Red flag:** Aftersales growth <10%, workshop margins <15%, outlet count stalling

4. **Operating Efficiency**
   - **Employee cost & OpEx:** Should stay below 4.4% of proforma revenue (management's discipline)
   - **Inventory velocity:** Watch for buildup; should match sales growth
   - **Receivables days:** Aftersales receivables should stay <60 days

   - **EBITDA per outlet:** Tract whether each outlet is reaching profitability
   - **New outlet ramp timing:** Monitor how quickly new workshops achieve 15%+ EBITDA margin
   - **Outlet closures:** Track if any unprofitable locations are being shut (this is management's strategy; acceptable)
   - **Red flag:** Outlet EBITDA margins declining, new outlets taking >24 months to profitability

### Detailed P/E Bridge: Why 57.8x is INDEFENSIBLE

**Current Valuation Basis (Implied ₹39 Cr PAT):**
- Implied PAT from 57.8x multiple on ₹544: ₹39 Cr
- FY26 actual PAT: ₹38 Cr
- **Issue:** Valuation is literally on FY26 actual results, which were FY26 peak profitability (highest-ever PAT)
- **What this means:** Market is pricing in assumption that FY26 was NOT peak; that FY27-28 will show 40%+ PAT CAGR to justify 57.8x

**Forward P/E Reality Check:**
- On FY27E PAT ₹65 Cr (base): P/E = 2,255 / 65 = **34.7x FY27E** (still very high)
- On FY28E PAT ₹115 Cr (base): P/E = 2,255 / 115 = **19.6x FY28E** (reasonable)
- **BUT:** To get FY27 ₹65 Cr PAT, requires:
  1. Revenue growth 15%+ YoY (₹4,896 → ₹5,600+ Cr)
  2. EBITDA margin 7%+ (from 5.8% FY26)
  3. No one-time charges/exceptional items (like workshops write-off noted in Q4 FY26)
  4. Aftersales scaling 15-18% YoY with consistent margins

**Why 57.8x is Indefensible:**

1. **Priced at peak execution:** Only justified if FY27-28 delivers flawless 40%+ PAT CAGR (rare for retail)
2. **PAT base tiny (₹38 Cr):** Any miss on aftersales ramp or vehicle growth = multiple compression
3. **Zero margin for error:** FY27 growth needs to be 60%+ to justify 57.8x; if only 30-40%, multiple compresses to 30-35x
4. **New outlets unproven:** 18-month expansion just completed; profitability of new locations not yet proven at scale
5. **Multiple compression scenario:** Even if FY27 PAT hits ₹60 Cr (+58%), at 30x multiple = ₹1,800 Cr cap = ₹433/share = -20% downside
