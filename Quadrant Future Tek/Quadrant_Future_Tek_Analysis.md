## 1. Business Model Overview

### Core Business Segments

Quadrant Future Tek operates in two high-growth, infrastructure-critical segments:

**A. Specialty Cables Division (~70% Revenue, FY26)**

- **Product Range**: E-beam irradiated specialty cables for safety-critical, high-performance applications
  - Railways: 300V–6.0KV irradiated cables for locomotive traction, signalling, auxiliary power
  - Defence: EED-certified cables for Naval & Army platforms (submarines, warships, defence vehicles)
  - Renewables: Solar & wind installation cables (BIS-approved, CEC-certified)
  - EV Charging: Fast-charging cables for EV infrastructure (green hydrogen applications)
  - Aerospace: DGQA & international-standard certified submarine/undersea cables
  
- **Technology**: Proprietary electron beam irradiation using 2.5 MeV AERB-licensed accelerator facility
  - Delivers superior thermal, mechanical, flame-resistance properties vs. conventional cables
  - Creates durable, high-reliability products ideal for safety-critical infrastructure
  
- **Revenue FY26**: ₹1,530 Mn (vs. ₹1,506 Mn FY25; +1.6% modest growth)
- **Order Book**: ₹407–558 Mn *per FY26 results*

**B. Train Control & Signalling (TCAS) / KAVACH Division (~0% Revenue Currently, Strategic Asset)**

- **Flagship Product**: KAVACH 4.0 Automatic Train Protection System
  - Trackside equipment: RFID-based stationary signalling units, towers, radio antennas, ISM towers
  - Onboard equipment: Locomotive-mounted TCAS computer, RFID reader, radio unit, Driver Machine Interface (DMI), brake interface
  - Electronic Interlocking Systems (EI) for railway stations and junctions
  - Digital Axle Counter technology for train detection
  
- **Status**: Pre-commercial; RDSO (Rail India Technical Supervisory Organisation) approval in final stages
  - Received Interim Independent Safety Assessment (ISA) clearance confirming CENELEC SIL-4 compliance *per Feb2026*
  - RDSO approved commencement of passenger trials as of May 2026
  - **Final commercial deployment approval expected post-trial phase completion** (timeline not contractually fixed)
  
- **Order Book**: ₹8,054–8,287 Mn (large, multi-year visibility across CLW, ICF, BLW, PLW locomotive plants)
  - Exclusive MoU with RailTel for joint marketing & implementation in India and export markets
  - Position as preferred tech partner reduces sales/execution risk
  
- **Production Capacity**: 2,200 locomotive units + 4,500 station units per annum

### Market Context & Government Tailwinds

- **Railway Modernization**: Union Budget allocated ₹1.2 lakh Crore for railway safety initiatives (FY2026-27)
- **Signalling Allocation**: ₹7,500 Crore specifically for Rail Signalling & Telecom modernization (FY2026-27)
- **KAVACH Network Target**: 15,512 km of track planned for KAVACH deployment; full network coverage within 6–8 years via phased rollout
- **Make in India Push**: Government focus on indigenous rail tech; Quadrant positioned as elite vendor (only 5 RDSO-approved KAVACH suppliers nationwide)

### Manufacturing & R&D Infrastructure

- **Basma Facility (Mohali, Punjab)**: 4.8 acres, 60,000 sq. ft., fully backward-integrated
  - In-house electron beam irradiation, compounding, testing labs, simulation center
  - Capacity: 1,900 MT/annum Specialty Cables; 2,200 loco + 4,500 station TCAS units/year
  
- **R&D & Software Centers**: Bengaluru (Karnataka) & Hyderabad (Telangana)
  - ~50 domain specialists across TCAS, embedded systems, electronics design
  - RDSO-compliant design & validation protocols
