## 1. Business Model Overview

AESL started in 1992 as a seismic data acquisition company. It is now an energy and minerals services platform, and its parent Oilmax Energy (owned by the Garg family) is being folded into it. After the merger, the company will report **three segments** *(per Q4FY26 concall)*:

| Segment (post-merger) | What it does | FY26 scale | Margin profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AESL services (India)** | **Oil & gas:** seismic surveys, integrated field development (the Vedanta model), O&M of oil and gas fields, EPC of process facilities, an AGCL BOOT project. **Minerals & other energy:** turnkey coal handling plants (CHP) for Coal India subsidiaries and state gencos, and bulk material handling | ~₹495 Cr revenue (analyst estimate: consolidated ₹791 Cr less ~₹295 Cr from Kuiper) | ~16% EBITDA *(per Q4FY26 concall)* |
| **Kuiper Group (UAE)** | Offshore and onshore oilfield manpower and technical staffing, mainly crews for drilling rigs. Clients are EPCI and drilling contractors across the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Africa. Acquired 01-Sep-2025 | ~₹530–540 Cr annualized; **US$60–65 mn guided for FY27** | ~7% EBITDA in FY26 (gross margin 14–15%), ~6% PAT because tax is low *(per Q2FY26 concall)* |
| **Oilmax E&P (owned fields)** | Five oil and gas blocks plus one CBM block, a quartzite mine, and new preferred-bidder awards (a DSF-IV offshore block and the Pakro vanadium/graphite mine). Strategy is **discovered, low-risk, low-cost fields only**, with no frontier exploration | ~₹130 Cr revenue (unverified); ~2,500 boepd | **>50% EBITDA**, ~US$5/bbl production cost *(per Q1FY27 deck)*. Management says new fields run at 70–75% *(per Q2FY26 concall)* |

### The "closed-loop" thesis

Management's pitch is that the combined company will be the **only listed Indian energy-services company that can deliver across the whole upstream value chain itself**: subsurface work and drilling from Oilmax, facilities and O&M from AESL, and international manpower from Kuiper *(per Q4FY26 concall)*. The proof point is the **Vedanta integrated field development contract** (₹865 Cr incl. GST, 57 months). AESL drills new wells, builds the facilities and then operates the field. Capex is reimbursed at a bid price, and opex is paid in US$/bbl on baseline plus incremental production, so **output risk is minimal** and the contract is crude-neutral *(per Sept-2025 call and Q2FY26 concall)*. ONGC is Vedanta's partner in the field and has since floated IFD-style tenders *(per Q4FY26 concall)*.

### Revenue mix

- **FY26 segments (consolidated):** Oil & Gas ₹633 Cr revenue / ₹102 Cr segment profit (80%), Minerals ₹158 Cr / ₹32 Cr (20%) *(per Q4FY26 concall)*. Kuiper sits inside Oil & Gas.
- **Q1FY27:** Oil & Gas ₹244.8 Cr / ₹33.3 Cr; Minerals only ₹26.4 Cr / ₹4.1 Cr *(per Q1FY27 deck)*.
- **Contract types:** CHP work is pure construction, so revenue does not depend on output but is hit hard by monsoons. O&M runs on 4–5-year contracts. Kuiper works under long-tenure MSAs. Oilmax crude is sold to IOC on a monthly dated-Brent formula on cash-and-carry terms. Gas is billed every 15 days and paid within 7, so **the E&P business runs on negative working capital** *(per Sept-2025 and Q4FY26 calls)*.

### Key clients

Vedanta (Cairn), ONGC, Oil India, Coal India subsidiaries (MCL, others), GSECL, Assam Gas Company (AGCL), Hardy and IOC (crude offtake). Kuiper serves global EPCI and drilling contractors.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### DNA shift: from a lumpy seismic contractor to an integrated services company that owns its own fields

1. **FY24–FY25:** seismic and CHP projects, episodic revenue, ~₹465 Cr revenue.
2. **FY26:** the Kuiper acquisition adds ~₹530 Cr of recurring international manpower revenue. The Vedanta IFD win creates a multi-year template.
3. **FY27 onwards:** the Oilmax merger adds high-margin, cash-generative E&P with negative working capital. Management targets group PAT of **₹450–500 Cr by FY29** (AGM 2025 guidance, **reaffirmed in the May-2026 concall**) against ₹60 Cr of adjusted PAT in FY26.

### Catalysts

| # | Catalyst | Evidence | Expected timing | Value impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Merger completion and full Oilmax consolidation** | SEBI, shareholder and creditor approvals done; final NCLT hearing 28-Aug-26 | Oct–Nov 2026 | Removes the holdco structure; the guarantees fall away; segment disclosure for E&P starts |

Size the position small, build it on weakness below ₹425, and re-rate it after the Duarmara test results and the first post-merger quarter with audited Oilmax numbers.
