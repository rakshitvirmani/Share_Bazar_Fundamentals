## 1. Business Model Overview

Tatva Chintan Pharma Chem is a 30-year-old (completed 3 decades in FY26) Vadodara-headquartered specialty chemicals manufacturer with plants at **Ankleshwar** (legacy, small) and **Dahej** (main manufacturing hub), plus a new **greenfield site at Jolva** (~6–7 km from Dahej) under construction. The company is organized into four reporting segments, with a fifth (semiconductor chemicals) emerging as a distinct long-gestation growth vertical:

1. **Phase Transfer Catalysts (PTC):** The company's founding chemistry (since 1996). Single-step, relatively easy-to-replicate organic chemistry — management notes India now has 9–10 PTC producers versus 2 when Tatva started *(per Q2FY26 concall, Oct-2025)*. PTC is increasingly consumed captively (forward-integrated into SDA and Electrolyte Salts) rather than sold externally as demand for downstream products rises *(per Q1FY27 concall, Jul-2026)*.

2. **Structure Directing Agents (SDA):** Raw materials/templating agents used by zeolite manufacturers for automotive catalytic converters (emission purification) and petrochemical applications. Tatva has been in SDA since 2015 and faces effectively a **duopoly market structure** — competitor Sarchem (US) holds an estimated ~85% share versus Tatva's ~15%, though management expects share gains as Euro 7 rolls out globally *(per Q4FY26 concall, May-2026)*. SDA demand is explicitly **campaign-based/lumpy** — management repeatedly cautions analysts to track it annually, not sequentially *(per Q3FY26 concall, Jan-2026)*.

3. **Electrolyte Salts & Solutions (ESS):** A "sunrise" vertical supplying salts (and, for hybrid-vehicle batteries, full electrolytes) into two applications: (a) stationary energy storage systems (ESS/grid batteries) and (b) hybrid-vehicle/supercapacitor batteries. Tatva is fully backward-integrated (PTC → SDA → salt → electrolyte) and claims to be the sole Indian supplier of electrolyte for hybrid/supercapacitor batteries *(per Q2FY26 concall, Oct-2025)*. Explicitly **not** exposed to lithium-ion batteries *(per Q1FY27 concall, Jul-2026)*.

4. **Pharma, Agro & Specialty Chemicals (PASC):** Multi-step, catalytic/electrolytic-chemistry-driven intermediates — agro intermediates (herbicide, insecticide, including one green-chemistry/photochlorination import-substitute product) and pharma intermediates (anti-cancer related, mid-chain dossier products supplied to Stage-2 API manufacturers). This is the newest and most capital-intensive segment, requiring multi-stage synthesis versus the single-step PTC/SDA chemistry.

5. **Semiconductor Chemicals (emerging, pre-commercial):** Ultra-high-purity chemicals (impurity profiles below 1 part per billion) for etching, cleaning, and as key starting materials for chip manufacturing, built on ~8–9 years of R&D. First commercial plant-scale batch was delivered and **successfully qualified by the customer in Q1FY27** *(per Q1FY27 concall, Jul-2026)* — a major technical milestone, though full commercialization is not expected before late CY2028/early CY2029.

**Exports** constitute the majority of revenue (~71% in Q1FY26, up from 61% in the year-ago quarter), with major markets including Japan, USA, Germany, Malaysia, Switzerland, South Korea and China *(per Q1FY26 concall, Jul-2025)*. Roughly 40–45% of raw material content is imported (key inputs: tertiary amines and alkyl halides, largely ammonia/crude-derivative based) *(per Q2FY26 concall, Oct-2025)*.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Moat migration — from commodity PTC to hard-to-replicate multi-step/continuous-flow chemistry.** Management's stated philosophy across all five calls: PTC was "easy to replicate" (9–10 Indian competitors today); SDA took ~8 years of R&D (2010 first development → 2018 first commercial sale) and has seen **zero domestic competition** in 10 years; the same playbook is now being applied to Electrolyte Salts and semiconductor chemicals.

2. **Euro 7 emission-norms rollout (Europe first, 2027; then US/Japan/China/India through 2030)** is the single most quantifiable SDA demand driver. Tatva says it is "well in time" for Euro 7 (unlike being a late entrant for Euro 6) and expects a structural, non-transient step-up in catalyst volumes per vehicle rather than a one-time demand surge *(per Q3FY26 concall, Jan-2026)*. SDA revenue guided to ₹250–300cr for FY27 (~20% growth on FY26 base) *(per Q4FY26 concall, May-2026)*.

3. **Full backward integration into Electrolyte Salts & Solutions** — positioned as the company's second SDA-like long-duration bet. Guided to contribute **8–10% of FY27 revenue (~₹40–60cr)** *(reiterated across Q3FY26, Q4FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls)*, with a second leg (hybrid/supercapacitor battery electrolytes) contingent on a single OEM customer's vehicle launch timeline (commercial ramp guided to begin Oct/Nov-2026, full-scale commercialization not before **late CY2027**, aligned to a mid-2028 vehicle launch).

4. **PASC transitioning from one-off commercialization to repeat orders** — flagged explicitly as a positive inflection in Q1FY27 ("transition from initial commercialization to recurring procurement is a significant milestone"). Pharma intermediates guided to add ₹70–80cr in FY27, scaling toward ~₹200cr at full utilization across three molecules.

5. **Two new capacity investments (~₹400–475cr combined, evolving figures) approved within 12 months** — Jolva Phase 1 and the newly announced ~₹200cr multipurpose facility — represent a large capital allocation bet (roughly 10% of current market cap) on the multi-step chemistry pivot, explicitly framed by management as "building capabilities ahead of demand."

6. **Semiconductor chemicals customer qualification (Q1FY27)** — the first tangible proof point after 8–9 years of R&D spend, unlocking (per management, speculatively) a large but entirely unquantified multi-year opportunity.

7. **30-year anniversary / inflection-point narrative** — management explicitly frames FY26 (crossing ₹500cr revenue) as the year multiple initiatives "begin to converge" into a "more structured growth cycle" *(per Q4FY26 concall, May-2026)*.
