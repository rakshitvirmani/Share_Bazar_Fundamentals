# Shera Energy Limited: Complete 7-Section Equity Analysis

## Section 1: Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**: Shera Energy Limited is an integrated non-ferrous metals manufacturer headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Established in 2009 (evolved from "Shera Metals and Engineers" proprietorship founded in 2002), the company manufactures and processes copper, aluminum, and brass products serving transformers, motors, electrical cables, and power infrastructure sectors. **Leadership**: Mr. Sheikh Naseem (Chairman & Managing Director, 23+ years in metals industry) and Ms. Shivani Sheikh (Whole-Time Director).

**Revenue Streams**:
- **Primary**: Winding wires, strips, rods, tubes, conductors in copper, aluminum, and brass
- **Secondary**: Nickel-based alloys, CTC conductors (for EHV transmission), solar ribbons
- **Emerging**: Copper cathode production (Zambia), backward integration recycling

**Capacity & Scale** (as of FY26, *per Investor Presentation FY26*):
- **Consolidated installed capacity**: 46,750 MT per annum
  - Aluminum: 21,200 MT (78% utilized in FY26)
  - Copper: 17,150 MT (80% utilized in FY26)
  - Brass: 8,400 MT (77% utilized in FY26)
- **Customer base**: ~300 customers; B2B model with no dealer/distributor network (direct sales)
- **Export presence**: 7-10% of revenue to 15+ countries; major markets are Middle East and South Africa (US exposure minimal at <1%)

**Subsidiary Structure**:
1. **Rajputana Industries Limited** (51.01% holding): Recycling operations of non-ferrous metals; 13,150 MTPA capacity; publicly listed on NSE Emerge (Aug 2024)
2. **Shera Metal Private Limited** (85.55% holding): Manufacturing of bus bars, rounds, flats, tubes; 13,000 MTPA capacity
3. **Shera Zambia Limited** (99.5% holding): Copper cathode refining in Central Africa; initial 1,200 MTPA, targeting 5,000 MTPA

**Competitive Advantages**:
- Fully integrated value chain (melting → casting → extrusion → drawing → finishing)
- Backward integration ensures cost control and supply security via recycling and Zambia cathode facility
- Multi-metal flexibility (can switch production between copper/aluminum/brass based on market prices and customer demand)
- 23+ years operational excellence with strong track record
- Vendor approvals from PGCIL & GSECL; ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 certified

## Section 4: Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### 1. **Backward Integration into Copper Cathode Refining (Game Changer)**

**The Opportunity**:
- Current copper prices: $10,600-11,000/ton (LME quoted; historically grew from $1,300-1,400 five years ago)
- Cost advantage from in-house cathode: Eliminate middleman margin, reduce import volatility, lock in cost structure
- India's copper consumption: ~800-900 tons/month for Shera; Zambia facility initially targets 100 tons/month, scaling to 5,000 MT/year (>50% of requirement)

**Margin Impact** (*per Nov2025 concall guidance*):
- Phase 1 (1,200 MT, 15%+ EBITDA): Add 2-3% to consolidated EBITDA by FY27
- Phase 3 (5,000 MT, 15%+ EBITDA): Add 10-15% to consolidated EBITDA by FY28-29

**Timeline**: Commercial production by Q2 FY27; scaling through FY27-28

### 2. **Forward Integration into High-Margin Products**

**Solar Ribbon & Conductors**:
- Global solar panel manufacturing shift to India (NITI Aayog targets 500 GW solar by 2030)
- Solar ribbon demand to grow 20%+ CAGR through 2030
- **Shera's play**: PV ribbon manufacturing (expected EBITDA margin: 8-10% vs 5.6% average)

**CTC Conductors (765 kV transmission)**:
- India's power transmission network undergoing continuous upgrade (14% transmission loss target being pursued annually)
- 765 kV and higher voltage lines require CTC (continuously transposed conductor) technology
- **Shera's play**: Newly acquired capability by Q1-Q2 FY27
- **Margin uplift**: EHV-grade conductors command 10-15% EBITDA premiums over standard winding wires

**Expected impact**: Additional ₹100-150 Cr revenue opportunity by FY28-29; EBITDA margin uplift of 2-3%

### 3. **International Geographic Expansion (Africa TAM)**

**Zambia & Ethiopia markets**:
- Zambia: Initial ₹5 Cr orders received; large infrastructure pipeline funded by World Bank
- Ethiopia: Potential to capture 5-10% of East African wire/cable demand
- **Shera's advantage**: Early entrant into Central Africa; capacity to supply standards-compliant products (ISO, PGCIL approvals recognized globally)

**Export mix evolution**:
- Current: 7-10% of revenue (₹115-164 Cr in FY26)
- Target FY27: 10-12% as Zambia ramps
- Target FY28: 15-20% as Ethiopia operational

### 4. **NSE Main Board Migration (Catalyst for Institutional Participation)**

**Status** (*per Feb2026 concall*):
- Listing date: 17 Feb 2023 on SME Emerge platform
- Eligibility for migration: 3 years completed by 17 Feb 2026 ✓
- **Expected migration**: H2 FY26 / Q1 FY27 (formalities in process)
- **Impact**: Access to larger institutional investor base; improved liquidity; brand recognition uplift

### 5. **Operational Leverage from Capacity Utilization**

**Current vs. Target**:
- FY26: 78% utilization (only 20% headroom left in existing 46.75k MT capacity)
- Management plan: Reach 90%+ utilization before major capex
- **Implication**: Every 1% utilization improvement = ₹15-20 Cr incremental revenue at current price levels
- Fixed cost absorption will drive EBITDA margin expansion as volumes grow
