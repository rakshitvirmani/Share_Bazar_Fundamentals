# Wockhardt Limited: 7-Section Equity Analysis

## Section 1: Business Model Overview

**Wockhardt Limited** is a global, research-driven pharmaceutical and biotechnology company with operations across three core platforms: pharmaceuticals (75% of business), specialty biologics/biosimilars, and novel antibiotics (NCE - New Chemical Entity). The company operates 11 manufacturing facilities and 2 R&D centers globally, with geographies spanning India, UK, Ireland, US, and emerging markets.

### Business Segments:

**Pharmaceutical Division (75% of Revenue):**
- India: Portfolio in diabetes, orthopedics, pain, and regenerative medicine (Emrok, Miqnaf brands)
- UK/Ireland: Market leadership in specialty injectables (18% market share in covered UK market, top-5 position). Focus on hospital, pharmacy, export, OTC, and branded generic channels.
- Emerging Markets: 28% of turnover, growing 35% YoY, driven by partnerships in Brazil, Thailand, Algeria, Malaysia

**Biosimilar & Biotech Division (13% of Revenue):**
- Focus exclusively on diabetes space with integrated capabilities (research to API to manufacturing to sales)
- Products: Human Insulin (capacity scaled 2x), Glargine (scaled 1.5x), launched in 30+ emerging markets
- Pipeline: 5 products (Aspart, RN 30/70, Degludec, Degludec-Aspart, Semaglutide)
- Growth: +27% YoY in FY26 *(per June 2026 investor concall)*

**Novel Antibiotics Division (NCE):**
- Lead asset: **Zaynich** (cefepime-zidebactam combination) — **first Indian drug approved by US FDA** (received June 2026)
- Indication: Complicated Urinary Tract Infections (cUTI); label covers Enterobacterales and Pseudomonas (95% of carbapenem-resistant cases)
- Clinical profile: 96.8% composite cure rate vs. Meropenem; 20% superiority in micro-cure rate (eradication)
- Compassionate use: 85 patients across India, US, Malaysia, France (patients for whom all other treatments failed)
- Patent: Expires 2038; QIDP status grants additional 5 years in US (min. 10-year exclusivity)
- Peak sales guidance: $1.5–$2 billion globally *(per Dr. Habil Khorakiwala, June 2026 concall)*
- Pricing: $1,200–$1,500/day in US; 75–80% discount in India (access-first philosophy)

### Competitive Advantages:

1. **First-mover advantage in India** for novel antibiotic with US FDA approval
2. **Integrated R&D capability** spanning chemistry, microbiology, toxicology, pharmacokinetics (25-30 year continuous investment)
3. **Unique manufacturing strategy:** Internal manufacturing in India; European FDA-approved facility for US market (de-risked GMP/quality)
4. **Unmatched QIDP status portfolio:** 6 molecules with QIDP status (only company globally with >1); indicates FDA confidence in R&D pipeline
5. **No dependency on partnerships** — building global organization in-house for Zaynich launch (vs. licensing to pharma majors)
