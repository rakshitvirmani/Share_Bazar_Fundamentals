## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Stallion India Fluorochemicals Limited (specialty chemicals manufacturer)
- Listed on NSE (STALLION) and BSE (544342)
- CEO: Shazad Rustomji (MD & CEO)
- Headquarters: Khalapur, Maharashtra

**Business Segments (Specialty Gases & Fluorochemicals)**

1. **Refrigerants** (~60% of revenue, ₹260 Cr FY26)
   - R-32 (current, ₹110-150 Cr annual capacity, high-margin)
   - HFO blends (next-gen low-GWP refrigerants, launching FY27-28)
   - HFC-32 de-growth phase (CFC phaseout regulations)
   - Pricing power: R-32 prices ₹500-900/kg depending on volumes; current ₹900/kg
   - Export opportunity: India becoming net exporter of refrigerants (phasing out imports)

2. **High-Purity Gases & Specialty Chemicals** (~40% revenue, ₹174 Cr FY26)
   - Helium (semiconductor, healthcare, aerospace applications)
   - HFO (Hydrofluoroolefin) blending and distribution
   - Specialty gas handling for semiconductor/electronics
   - Margin profile: 70-80% gross margin (asset-light for helium blending)

**Geographic Footprint**
- India-focused (90% domestic, 10% exports currently)
- 4 strategic facilities:
  - Khalapur (Mumbai): Helium facility (going live Aug 2026)
  - Bhilwara (Rajasthan): R-32 manufacturing (10,000 MT capacity, production Oct 2026)
  - Mumbattu (Andhra Pradesh): HFO blending and specialty gas hub
  - Mindesspace, Malad (Mumbai): Corporate head office

**Key Strengths**
- Backward integration: Manufacturing own refrigerants (proprietary R-32 plant design = cost advantage)
- Import substitution tailwind: India transitioning from CFC/HFC to domestic R-32 manufacturing
- Specialty gas moat: Helium and semiconductor gas handling expertise rare in India
- Strong management credibility: Executed 30-35% CAGR historically, clear capital allocation roadmap
- Contracted revenue visibility: Member commitments ₹3,363+ Cr forward (27 months visible)
- Government support: India's Make in India push for refrigerant manufacturing + emission regulations driving R-32 demand
- Asset-light expansion capability: Specialty gas segment (80% EBITDA margins) scalable with minimal capex

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

**1. R-32 Manufacturing Inflection (Oct 2026)**
- Company currently sources R-32; October launch enables backward integration (cost savings ₹50-100/kg on ₹900/kg product = 5-11% EBITDA uplift)
- FY27E PAT guidance ₹100 Cr (+127% from FY26's ₹43.84 Cr) directly tied to 6-month R-32 production ramping
- de facto moat: First-mover in organized R-32 manufacturing in India; competitors (Balaji, others) 18-24 months behind

**2. HFO Blending & Next-Gen Refrigerant Opportunity**
- HFO (hydrofluoroolefin) = regulatory replacement for R-32 post-2030
- Margin profile: 15-20% higher than R-32 (blending vs commodity manufacturing)
- Global players (Chemours, etc.) already in HFO; India still importing
- Stallion's FY27-28 HFO launch targets 5-10% of revenue; path to 20% by FY30

**3. Helium & Semiconductor Gas Expansion**
- Helium facility (Khalapur) going live Aug 2026; initially for medical/industrial/aerospace
- Semiconductor opportunity: India's chip manufacturing ambitions (ISMC, Micron, etc.) creating demand for ultra-high purity gases
- Stallion positioning itself as sole integrated supplier (manufacturing + distribution)
- Margin profile: 70-80% EBITDA on helium/specialty gases (vs 40-50% on refrigerants)

**4. Import Substitution & Export Opportunity**
- India currently imports 15-20% of refrigerant needs; Stallion's R-32 + HFO plants will flip this to net exports
- Global refrigerant market: $6B+; India share rising as China capacity constraints tighten
- Management explicitly targeting exports by FY28-29 (post-stabilization)

**5. Margin Expansion Path (Key Leverage)**
- FY26 PAT margin: 10.1%
- FY27E PAT margin: 10.1% (flat, weighted by capex burden on profitability)
- FY28E PAT margin: 10.9% (new plants stabilizing)
- FY30 target: 16%+ (as specialty gas mix grows and scale kicks in)
- This 600 bps margin expansion over 4 years = largest upside lever on valuation
