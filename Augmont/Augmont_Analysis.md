## 1. Business Model Overview

Augmont is an integrated gold and silver platform in India spanning procurement, refining, bullion trading, digital gold, jewellery manufacturing, international sales and gold-backed financial services, with a presence across **24 states** as of March 31, 2026 *(per Prospectus, "Our Business")*. Management describes it as one of the few Indian players with both online and offline gold/silver channels, and the company was recognised as India's **"Number 1 Gold Platform of the year 2024-25"** by the India Gold Conference *(Technopak Report)*.

The business runs on **two online platforms**, complemented by physical infrastructure:

- **Augmont SPOT (Enterprise/B2B):** A fully electronic, delivery-based OTC bullion platform (live since 2012) that lets GST-registered jewellers, bullion dealers and manufacturers buy gold/silver bars with assured physical delivery within 1–2 working days, via 20 spot delivery centres across 13 states (9 company-operated, 11 franchised). SPOT generated **₹8,175.06 Cr, or 86.80% of FY26 revenue from operations** (down from 91.17% in FY24 — see DNA shift below).
- **Augmont Gold For All (Consumer/B2C):** Digital gold/silver SIPs, buy/sell/store, EMI jewellery, coin sales, sell-old-gold and technology support for gold loans (provided to group company **Finkurve Financial Services**, which operates 106 "Gold-For-All" physical centres on Augmont's behalf). Served **49.62 million registered digital-gold customers** as of March 31, 2026. Generated **₹301.21 Cr (3.20% of revenue)** directly, but the broader "customer-focused offerings" (including EMI jewellery, coins) totalled **₹668.72 Cr (7.10% of revenue)**, up from 3.37% in FY24.
- **International sales:** Jewellery (primarily chains) manufactured at a **13.80 MTPA** unit in Sitapur SEZ, Jaipur, sold to traders in Hong Kong, Turkey and the UAE. Started FY23; contributed **₹570.15 Cr (6.05% of revenue)** in FY26.
- **Refining:** Two NABL/ISO-accredited refining units (Rudrapur, Uttarakhand — 144 MTPA; Mumbai — 140 MTPA), India Good Delivery-certified, authorised to deliver refined bullion on BSE/MCX. Refines doré bars (lower import duty than refined gold) and scrap/recycled jewellery gold.
- **Lab-grown diamonds:** Trading facilitated on the SPOT platform starting FY26 — a new, unproven revenue line.

**Corporate structure:** Incorporated 2012 as "RSBL Spot Trading Private Limited," renamed Augmont in 2015, converted to a public company in May 2025 ahead of listing. Material subsidiaries: **Ideal Fiscal Services Limited (IFSL)** — operates the Jaipur jewellery unit; **Augmont Goldtech Private Limited (AGTPL)** — operates the digital gold platform; plus Augmont International Limited, Augmont Trading Limited (formerly Arvog Enterprises Limited), and Augmont IFSC Private Limited (GIFT City subsidiary for direct bullion import via the India International Bullion Exchange, IIBX).

**Critical structural point for valuation:** *"There are no listed companies in India or globally that engage in a business similar to that of our Company"* — stated by Augmont itself in the "Basis for Offer Price" section. This means there is **no peer-multiple benchmark**; the IPO price itself, and its own quantitative KPIs, are the only market-set reference points available *(per Prospectus, "Basis for Offer Price," p.145)*.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

- **Mix shift from pure B2B trading toward higher-value consumer and international lines.** SPOT's share of revenue fell from 91.17% (FY24) to 86.80% (FY26) while consumer-focused offerings rose from 3.37% to 7.10% and international sales from 4.67% to 6.05%. If this trend continues, blended margins should structurally improve, since B2C/EMI/digital-gold economics are inherently richer than pass-through bullion trading.
- **Technology/price-discovery moat.** Proprietary real-time pricing engine (AI + human-in-the-loop hedging) that reportedly updates every second and monetises via embedded spread rather than brokerage/terminal fees — a genuine differentiator versus the unorganised bullion trade.
- **GIFT City direct-import subsidiary (Augmont IFSC)** cuts out intermediary brokerage/commission on bullion imports via IIBX — a structural cost advantage as volumes scale.
- **Regulatory formalization tailwind.** BIS hallmarking mandates, AEO-T2 customs status, and India's broader push to formalize gold trade favour scaled, compliant, technology-enabled players like Augmont over the large unorganised bullion market.
- **New optionality lines:** lab-grown diamond trading (launched FY26, unproven), gold-backed lending as a technology-and-brand partner to Finkurve (asset-light fee/royalty model), and continued Tier 3/4 geographic expansion.
- **Structural demand backdrop:** jewellery's share of India's retail consumption basket is projected to rise from 9.33% (FY25) to 14.64% (FY30) *(Technopak Report)*, and India's underlying consumption/GDP growth (~10% nominal GDP CAGR estimated FY26–30) provides a large addressable market.
- **Brand recognition**: multiple 2024–26 awards (IBJA "Best Bullion Dealer – Gold," India Silver Conference "No.1 Silver Platform," "Best OTT Platform" three years running) support the "trust" premise central to a business where counterparties must trust a bullion seller's purity/weight claims.

## 6. Future Financial Projections & Fair Value Analysis

### ⚠️ Pre-Listing Valuation — Read This First

Augmont has **no listed trading history**. There is no CMP (current market price) to anchor this valuation — only the **finalized IPO Offer Price of ₹788/share** (top of the ₹750–788 band). **This entire valuation framework is therefore built around the IPO offer price, not a market price**, and is explicitly subject to how the stock actually trades once listed. Listing-day price discovery, broader market conditions, and grey-market sentiment at the time of listing can move the actual price meaningfully away from both the offer price and the fair-value estimates below. Treat the "BUY/ACCUMULATE/HOLD/REDUCE" framework in this section as a pre-listing reference to revisit against the actual listing price once the stock opens for trading.

**IPO's own quantitative pricing basis** *(per Prospectus, "Basis for Offer Price," p.144–146)*:
- FY26 Basic/Diluted EPS: ₹40.45 → **P/E at Offer Price (₹788) = 19.48x**; P/E at floor (₹750) = 18.54x
- 3-year weighted-average EPS: ₹30.70 → implied weighted P/E ≈ 25.7x
- RoNW FY26: 49.52%; 3-year weighted average RoNW: 55.25%
- NAV/share post-offer at Offer Price: ₹169.29 → **Price/Book ≈ 4.66x**
- Implied market capitalization at Offer Price (9.14 Cr post-offer shares × ₹788) ≈ **₹7,200 Cr**
- Market Cap/Revenue (FY26) ≈ 0.08x — extremely low, but expected for a high-volume, thin-margin trading business (comparable to how bullion/commodity traders are typically valued on earnings, not revenue)

### Probability Shifters

**Bull probability rises if:**
- FY27 revenue growth holds above 35% (vs. the ~30% base case)
- RBL/related-party revenue share declines materially (e.g., below 15% of revenue) post-listing, reducing the governance discount
- PAT margin expands past 0.45%, evidencing genuine mix-driven operating leverage rather than a one-off
- Tier 3/4 centre rollout and lab-grown diamond/gold-lending lines contribute a meaningfully rising share of revenue

**Bear probability rises if:**
- FY27 revenue growth falls below 20% (government anti-gold campaign and customs duty hike bite harder than expected)
- Top-10 customer concentration stays above 50% with RBL above 25%, and any adverse RBL-linked development (DGFT case outcome, fresh SEBI/customs scrutiny) disrupts the relationship
- Operating cash flow stays structurally negative beyond FY27, forcing a dilutive follow-on raise
- Employee attrition remains above 50%

### FY30+ Terminal Value

Extending the base case: FY30E revenue growth slows further to ~15% as the business matures, with PAT margin stabilizing around 0.48% on continued mix shift.

```
FY30E Revenue  ≈ ₹1,83,663 Cr (base FY29E) × 1.15 ≈ ₹2,11,213 Cr
FY30E PAT      ≈ ₹2,11,213 Cr × 0.48%            ≈ ₹1,013.8 Cr
Terminal P/E (mature, lower-growth platform business): 16x
Terminal Market Cap ≈ ₹1,013.8 Cr × 16           ≈ ₹16,221 Cr
Terminal Fair Value/Share ≈ ₹16,221 Cr / 9.14 Cr shares ≈ ₹1,775/share

Discounted to present value (4 years, 14% cost of equity):
PV ≈ ₹1,775 / (1.14)^4 ≈ ₹1,775 / 1.689 ≈ ₹1,051/share
```

The DCF-style terminal check (₹1,051/share) lands close to the probability-weighted FY27-based fair value (₹999/share), lending some cross-validation to the range — though both numbers share the same core uncertainty (no peer group, and heavy reliance on continued margin-mix improvement).

## Investment Verdict

**Lead with the number:** On a probability-weighted basis, Fair Value is estimated at **~₹999–1,000/share**, against a finalized IPO Offer Price of **₹788/share** — implying **~27% upside** at the offer price. This places the IPO offer, on paper, in **BUY** territory per the entry-point framework above.

**However — treat this with real caution**, for three specific reasons this note flags explicitly: (1) there is **no listed peer** to sanity-check the assumed forward P/E re-rating (19–25x) against — the entire multiple assumption is self-referential to the company's own disclosed 19.48x trailing multiple; (2) **~27–48% of FY26 revenue runs through related-party transactions**, concentrated in a single promoter-linked entity (RBL) whose own regulatory history includes a pending ₹100 Cr DGFT matter — this is a governance risk that public-market investors have not yet had the chance to price; and (3) **operating cash flow turned negative in FY26** and standalone working capital needs are projected to nearly triple by FY28E, meaning further capital raises (dilutive or debt-like) are plausible even after this IPO's ₹572.76 Cr net proceeds are deployed.

**Bull case (25% probability, ₹1,570/share):** Related-party revenue concentration declines post-listing as governance scrutiny increases; Tier 3/4 expansion and digital-gold/EMI mix shift drive both volume and margin higher; the market re-rates Augmont from a "bullion trader" multiple toward a "gold-tech platform" multiple given its technology moat and #1 brand positioning.

**Base case (50% probability, ₹1,019/share):** Growth continues decelerating in line with the FY25→FY26 pattern, settling into a mid-20s-to-30% growth band; margin improves modestly on mix shift; the stock broadly holds its IPO-implied multiple.

**Bear case (25% probability, ₹391/share):** Government demand headwinds (anti-gold messaging, customs duty hike) and RBI funding restrictions bite harder than expected; related-party concentration proves sticky or triggers an adverse event; the market applies a commodity-trading-house discount multiple (8–12x) to a business that, at its core, still earns sub-0.5% net margins on pass-through bullion volumes.

**Rating: BUY (at Offer Price ₹788), with an explicit governance and concentration caveat.** Investors comfortable underwriting related-party concentration risk and the absence of peer benchmarks can view the ₹788 offer price as attractively set relative to the company's own growth and return profile (ROE 51%, ROCE 40% in FY26). Investors uncomfortable with those two specific risks — or unwilling to hold through the 18-month promoter lock-in period without a clear de-concentration catalyst — should treat this as a **HOLD/watch** situation post-listing rather than a Day-1 allocation, and revisit the entry-point table once an actual market price is established.
