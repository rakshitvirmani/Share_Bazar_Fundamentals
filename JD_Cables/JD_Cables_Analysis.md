# JD Cables Limited — Equity Analysis

## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** JD Cables is an ISO and BIS-certified manufacturer of low-tension (LT) power cables, control cables, instrumentation cables, and aluminum conductors (AAC, AAAC, ACSR). The company serves the power transmission and distribution (T&D), infrastructure, industrial, and electrification sectors across India.

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Two production units in West Bengal:
- **Unit I:** 6,000 km/annum capacity, 82.4% utilization (FY26)
- **Unit II:** 22,000 km/annum capacity, 84.6% utilization (FY26)
- Total combined capacity: ~28,000 km/annum

**Facility Expansion:** Recently acquired 1.18 lakh sq ft facility in Dankuni, West Bengal (commissioned during FY26, phased ramp-up starting December 2025).

**Customer Base:** Approved vendor for state electricity boards across 10+ states. Primary customers are EPC contractors on government transmission/distribution projects, with growing direct-to-government tender participation.

**Product Portfolio:**
- Power cables (LT XLPE and PVC)
- Control cables
- Instrumentation cables
- Aluminum conductors (AAC, AAAC, ACSR)
- New products added: MVCC, AL59 conductors, HTLS conductors, HE cables
- EPC business (infrastructure & electrical works) — newly launched

**Strategic Division:** EPC business headed by Mr. Rajesh Jhunjhunwala (Whole-Time Director, 30+ years EPC experience). Provides forward integration advantage for cable-based projects.

## 3. Capacity Roadmap

**Current State (End FY26):**
- Installed capacity: 28,000 km/annum
- Utilization: 82.4% (Unit I), 84.6% (Unit II) — near optimal
- Operating at high capacity with strong demand signals

**Phase 1 Expansion (Dankuni facility):**
- **Size:** 1.18 lakh sq ft facility acquired in FY26
- **Timeline:** Phased commissioning Dec 2025 – Jan 2026
  - Conductor division: Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 (machinery order ₹5.72 Cr placed)
  - Cable division: Follow-up in Feb-Mar 2026
- **Expected capacity:** Double current capacity (56,000 km/annum target by Sept 2026)
- **FY27 utilization:** Expected 70-80% of total combined capacity by Sept 2026
- **Product focus:** New high-margin products — HTLS connectors, AL59 conductors, MVCC, HE cables

**Phase 2+ (Long-term):**
- Land acquisition in progress adjacent to Dankuni facility
- Target: 3x to 4x capacity expansion within 2-3 years (to 84,000–112,000 km/annum)
- Capex requirements: ₹160-170 Cr to reach ₹1,000 Cr revenue target by FY28
- FY27 capex guidance: ₹20-30 Cr (dependent on order intake)

**Revenue Potential:** Management expects new facility to contribute ₹50-60 Cr in FY26, scaling to ₹200+ Cr by FY27 across both new and existing units.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. **Structural Demand Drivers**
1. **Government Power Investment:** India's aggressive T&D capex (RDSS, grid expansion, electrification programs)
2. **State-Level Approvals:** New vendor approvals from Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Rajasthan unlock pan-India presence (previously West Bengal-centric)
3. **Order Book Scaling:** ₹515 Cr order book (1.8x FY26 revenue) provides 18-24 months visibility

### B. **EPC Business Pivot (Major Strategic Shift)**
- **Launch:** Entered EPC segment in Q4 FY26 (₹30 Cr revenue)
- **FY27 Guidance:** ₹200 Cr+ (from ₹30 Cr, 6.7x growth)
- **Long-term:** ₹500-700 Cr EPC revenue by FY28 (targeting ₹1,000 Cr order book in EPC)
- **Margin Profile:** 8-9% EBITDA vs 13-15% in cables/conductors (dilutive initially, but provides forward integration and infrastructure multiplier)
- **Competitive Advantage:** Internal cable/conductor manufacturing eliminates supply chain dependency for EPC projects

### C. **Capacity Expansion Timing**
- New capacity (Dankuni) coming online during high-growth phase (50-60% revenue growth FY27-FY28 guidance)
- Capacity constraints easing; current 82-84% utilization limits growth
- 2x capacity by Sept 2026 enables 50-60% revenue growth without bottleneck

### D. **Product Portfolio Expansion**
- New products (HTLS, AL59, MVCC, HE cables) entering higher-margin, specialized segments
- Management expects "better margins" from new products vs legacy offerings (double-digit EBITDA)
- Data center and solar cable opportunities identified (future expansion)

### E. **IPO Catalyst (Completed Nov 2025)**
- Listing on BSE SME platform followed strong IPO (128x subscribed, ₹8,100 Cr subscription vs ₹96 Cr issue)
- Institutional credibility and capital access strengthens tender participation and project execution
- Working capital optimization using IPO proceeds mentioned

| **Cables & Conductors (₹ Cr)** | 350–400 | 600–700 | ~50% |
| **EPC Revenue (₹ Cr)** | 200 | 400–500 | NM (new segment) |
| **EBITDA Margin %** | 12–13% | 12–13% | - |
| **EBITDA (₹ Cr)** | 66–78 | 120–130 | ~60% |
| **Order Book (₹ Cr)** | 700–800 | - | - |

**Key Assumptions:**
1. **Revenue Mix Shift:** Cable/conductor growth 30-40% + EPC growth from ₹30 Cr → ₹200+ Cr (6.7x)
2. **Margins:** EBITDA margins held at 12-13% despite EPC dilution (8-9%) — implies cable segment margins improve to offset
3. **Capacity Utilization:** 50-60% revenue growth enabled by Dankuni doubling capacity; by FY28, 2x facility expected to reach 3-4x planned total
4. **Order Book Conversion:** ₹700-800 Cr order book by March 2027 supports ₹1,000 Cr FY28 revenue target (assumes 18-24 month execution and repeat order capture)
5. **Capex:** ₹160-170 Cr cumulative capex (FY26–FY28) to support ₹1,000 Cr revenue; implies ₹15-20% asset-light leverage post-ramp

**Implied Returns (FY28 Base Case):**
- PAT (at 9% margin): ₹90 Cr
- ROIC (assuming ₹300 Cr invested capital): ~30%
- Revenue run-rate supports ₹25-30 Cr annual free capex post-FY28 (self-funding phase)

**Scenario Analysis:**

**Bull Case (+10% upside):**
- EPC order book wins ₹500+ Cr (vs ₹400-500 Cr base); reaches ₹1,200+ Cr total revenue by FY28
- New product margins hit 15%+ (HTLS, AL59 premium pricing)
- Margin floor improves to 14-15% combined EBITDA
- FY28 PAT potential: ₹105+ Cr

**Bear Case (-15% downside):**
- EPC execution delays or margin compression to 6-7% (competitive pricing pressures)
- Large tender losses; order book growth stalls at ₹500 Cr
- Capacity underutilization in H2 FY27 (80% utilization vs 100% guided)
- FY28 revenue plateaus at ₹800 Cr; PAT ₹65 Cr

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### **Quarterly Tracking (Each Results/Concall):**

1. **Revenue Composition**
   - Cables & Conductors segment revenue (vs management guidance of 30-40% growth)
   - EPC revenue (tracking towards ₹200 Cr FY27E milestone)
   - Geo mix (monitoring North/North-East expansion post vendor approvals)

2. **Operational Efficiency**
   - EBITDA margins (watch for H1 FY27 margin recovery to 15%+; H2 FY26 was 11.5%)
   - Capacity utilization (Dankuni facility ramp; target 70-80% by Sept 2026)
   - Conductor vs Cable revenue mix (new products contribution)

3. **Order Book Health**
   - Order book value and 2.0x+ revenue visibility ratio
   - Tender participation (tracking ₹1,000+ Cr tender base) and conversion win rate
   - Average contract value and execution timeline (watch for scope creep in EPC)

4. **Working Capital & Cash Flow**
   - Operating cash flow (critical given ₹-74 Cr FY26 burn)
   - Days inventory, receivables, payables (target: reduce DIO, DSO; stretch DPO)
   - Debt levels (watch debt-to-EBITDA; above 3.0x signals refinancing risk)

5. **Capex & Capacity**
   - Dankumi facility commissioning status (conductor division online by Dec 2025, cable by Jan-Feb 2026)
   - Utilization ramp trajectory (should reach 70-80% by Sept 2026)
   - New product certifications and BIS approvals (HTLS, AL59, MVCC) — critical for margin uplift

6. **EPC Segment Metrics**
   - Project completion % (currently 10% on first large contract; watch execution velocity)
   - Order backlog and revenue guidance updates for EPC (tracking ₹1,000 Cr order book target by FY27)
   - Margin realization (confirm 8-9% EBITDA achieved; watch cost overruns)

### **Annual Strategic Milestones (FY27–FY28):**

- **By Sept 2026:** Dankuni facility 70-80% utilization; new conductor division producing
- **By March 2027:** ₹700-800 Cr order book; ₹500-600 Cr revenue (vs ₹365 Cr FY26)
- **By March 2028:** ₹1,000 Cr revenue run-rate; ₹400-500 Cr EPC business; 3x+ capacity installed

### **Red Flag Early Warnings:**

- Dankumi commissioning delays beyond Q3 FY27 → revenue guidance miss
- EPC project completion falls below 50% by Q4 FY27 → execution concerns, margin risk
- Order book conversion slows (new orders <₹100 Cr/quarter) → demand pullback
- Debt-to-EBITDA exceeds 3.5x for consecutive quarters → liquidity stress
- New product margins miss double-digit target in H1 FY27 → pricing power weakening
