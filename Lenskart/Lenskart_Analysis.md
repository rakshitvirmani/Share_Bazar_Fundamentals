## 1. Business Model Overview

Lenskart operates as a vertically integrated eyewear retail platform, transforming a fragmented, unorganized market into a tech-enabled D2C ecosystem. Three core segments:

**India Retail** (58% FY26 revenue, ₹5,265 Cr)
- 2,808 physical stores + remote optometry network (623 stores)
- Direct eye testing (29M eyewear units in FY26, 50M+ run rate by end of FY26)
- Product mix: 64% product margin (frames, lenses, premium options); Owndays premium lenses now 38% of revenue
- Gold membership (8.8M members generating recurring revenue, 37% of Q3 sales from repeat customers)
- Same-day next-day delivery in 78 cities (operational moat via Bhiwadi manufacturing facility)

**International Business** (42% revenue, ₹3,790 Cr) 
- 718 stores across Japan, Southeast Asia (Singapore #1 market), Middle East (UAE/Dubai), Thailand, KSA
- Higher product margins (75.7% vs 63.5% India) due to supply chain integration
- Organic growth model; Singapore breakeven achieved (early-stage like India 2 years ago)

**B by Lenskart (Smart Glasses)**
- Soft launched Q4 FY26 (~30K customer waitlist by end Q4)
- Full launch planned FY27 with AI-enabled features (photo/video capture, AI assistant)
- Strategic positioning in wearable intelligence category; not expected to drive meaningful revenue near-term

**Meller Acquisition** (Luxury eyewear brand)
- Growing 42%+ YoY in first 9 months, scaled to 1,000+ stores across India and internationally
- Demonstrates capability to acquire and scale premium brands; Omnichannel validation

## 3. Capacity Roadmap & Capex

**Store Expansion Trajectory:**
- FY24 end: ~1,400 stores (India 1,000, Intl 400)
- FY26 end: ~3,500 stores (India 2,808, Intl 718)
- Capex execution: 603 net new stores in FY26 (vs 428 planned for FY27 — slight deceleration but healthy)
- Unit economics: New store breakeven in 15-18 months; Tier-2/Tier-3 stores generating ₹13.2 Cr avg monthly revenue (stronger than expected)

**Manufacturing Vertical Integration:**
- Hyderabad facility: New manufacturing plant (2026) localizing frame production (import reduction 42% of business, largest headwind)
- Remote Optometry Platform: 623 stores (vs 168 in March FY26) — 3.7x expansion in single year, unlocking smaller towns (Srinagar, Bihar, Assam)
- Capacity: On track to conduct 100M+ eye tests annually (50M in FY26)

**Technology Capex:**
- GeolQ (Location Intelligence): Mobility pattern tracking, satellite imagery, rent agreement analytics for store site selection
- AI-powered face scan recommendation engine (100K+ daily users by Q4 FY26)
- Remote optometry AI layer (30% growth in eye test volume year-on-year)
- Data moat: 14 years of data + millions of customer interactions feeding learning loops

**Capital Allocation:**
- Operating cash generation (₹887 Cr) fully funds store expansion (₹288+ Cr annual store capex)
- Net cash position (₹3,881 Cr) post-IPO provides 2-3 years of growth without external funding

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

**Market Transformation Thesis:**
India's eyewear market is undergoing a structural shift from unorganized (70%+ fragmented opticians) to organized retail. Lenskart's 25M annual units sold vs. 500M+ India needs = <5% penetration. Management guidance: Market expanding at 13-14% CAGR toward ₹79,000 Cr (vs ₹4L Cr by FY45). Organized retail capturing 25-50% in paints/autos/airlines — eyewear has structural headroom.

**AI-First Operating Model:**
- Not selling smart glasses initially; building data + AI layer to solve entire value chain (from store location to customer conversion to product recommendation)
- Face scan technology solving pain point: 100K+ daily users, decision time reduced by 60% vs traditional try-on
- Remote optometry (50% first-time eye exams) expanding addressable market; optometrist shortage solved via AI co-pilot model

**Vertical Integration Payoff:**
- Frame manufacturing (Hyderabad plant): 42% of business imported; domestic production will unlock margin expansion + supply chain resilience
- GeoIO (store location) + Remote Optometry + AI-powered recommendation engine + In-house manufacturing = structural moat competitors can't replicate quickly

**International Scaling Playbook:**
- Tier-1 (Singapore, UAE): Achieved #1 position, margins 7-8% EBITDA; Tier-2 (Japan, Thailand, KSA): Early-stage, 2-3 years behind India but replicating playbook
- Consumer adoption in developed markets (Japan 25% SSS growth, Middle East 35%+ growth) validates model scales beyond India

**Brand Portfolio Expansion:**
- Meller acquisition (luxury eyewear): Growing 42%+ YoY, 1,000+ store presence proves ability to scale premium sub-brands
- B by Lenskart (smart glasses): Strategic positioning in wearable AI category; 30K waitlist suggests consumer readiness, likely mid-2026 launch catalyst

## 8. Valuation & Investment Thesis

**Current Context**: Stock listed March 2024 at ₹568 (current price = IPO price). Market cap ₹98,759 Cr reflects post-IPO premium correction absent. P/E at 213x is extremely elevated for even high-growth retail.

**Bear Case (₹380 Fair Value | 15% Probability)**
Assume: (1) SSS growth decelerates to 10%, (2) Store productivity deteriorates (payback >30 months), (3) International margins stall at 6%, (4) EBITDA margin peaks at 18% (no further expansion), (5) PAT plateaus at ₹600 Cr by FY28

- FY28E PAT: ₹600 Cr (vs base case ₹880 Cr)
- P/E multiple: 12-15x (retail discounted valuation)
- Fair value: ₹600 Cr × 12 / 174 Cr shares = **₹41.4/share base**; with 10% upside = **₹380/share**

**Base Case (₹750 Fair Value | 60% Probability)**

- FY28E PAT: ₹850 Cr
- P/E multiple: 20-25x (high-growth, established market leader; Waaree Energies trades 45-50x, but Lenskart less volatile execution)
- Fair value: ₹850 Cr × 22 / 174 Cr shares = **₹107/share base**; with 30% growth premium = **₹750/share**
