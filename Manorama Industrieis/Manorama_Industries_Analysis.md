## 1. Business Model Overview

**What They Do:** Manorama Industries is India's leading specialty fats and cocoa butter manufacturer. Core business: sourcing exotic seeds (Shea, Sal, Palm, Mango) from West Africa, fractionating oils via solvent extraction, and manufacturing specialty fats (CBE, cocoa butter alternatives, FSOS) for global food & cosmetics industries.

**Scale & Reach (as of March 31, 2026):**
- **₹1,357 crore revenue** in FY26 (+76.1% YoY)
- **Domestic + Global operations:** India-based manufacturing; West Africa (Burkina Faso) raw material sourcing
- **Customer base:** Global food manufacturers, cosmetics companies (indirect B2B model)
- **Product mix:** 30% specialty products (CBE, cocoa butter alternatives), 70%+ value-added products (FSOS, fractionated oils)

**Revenue Streams:**

1. **Specialty Fats & Cocoa Butter Equivalents (CBE) (30% of revenue)**
   - Cocoa butter replacements for confectionery industry
   - Growing demand as cocoa prices rise globally
   - Higher margins than commodity fats (50%+ gross margin vs 40%+ for commodity)

2. **Fractionated Specialty Oils (FSOS) (40-50% of revenue)**
   - Shea butter, Palm stearin, Sal fats, Mango kernel fats
   - Used in bakery, confectionery, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals
   - Premium pricing due to specialty applications

3. **Commodity Fats & Oils (15-20% of revenue)**
   - Refined oils, RBD (Refined, Bleached, Deodorized) oils
   - Lower margin but volume driver

**Operating Model:**

- **Fully integrated value chain:** Raw material sourcing (West Africa) → Solvent fractionation (India) → Value-added processing → Global distribution
- **Capacity structure (March 2026):**
  - Solvent Fractionation Plant 1: 15,000 tons per annum (MTPA)
  - Solvent Fractionation Plant 2: 25,000 MTPA (debottlenecked in FY26 from 25k baseline)
  - Total effective capacity after debottlenecking: ~47,500 MTPA
  - New capacity expansion: Adding 52,000 MTPA (no incremental cost; included in capex programs)
  - Utilization: Currently ~85% (targeting 85-90%)

- **Strategic advantage:** Backward integration into West Africa sourcing (Burkina Faso) reduces raw material cost by eliminating middlemen; long-term supply chain security

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. **Structural Growth in Global Specialty Fats Market**

- **Cocoa price inflation:** Global cocoa prices at multi-year highs (₹300+ per kg in recent volatility); CBE becomes economic substitute
- **Confectionery industry tailwind:** Growing demand from chocolate manufacturers shifting to cocoa butter equivalents (cheaper alternative to natural cocoa butter)
- **Cosmetics & personal care:** Increasing use of specialty fats in skincare, haircare (shea butter, palm stearins)
- **For Manorama:** Well-positioned as CBE/FSOS supplier; capacity expansion enables capturing this growth

### B. **Capacity Utilization & Operational Leverage**

- **Current utilization:** ~85% of 47,500-ton capacity = running at ~40,000 MTPA
- **Incremental capacity:** Adding 52,000 MTPA of virtually no-cost debottlenecking
- **Full-year capacity target:** 52,000 MTPA by FY27-28 end
- **Operating leverage:** Fixed cost spread over higher volumes → EBITDA margin expansion from 27% to 28-30% possible
- **For Manorama:** Significant runway for growth with minimal capex intensity per additional ton

### C. **Backward Integration into Africa (Burkina Faso Project)**

- **Raw material sourcing:** Shea, Sal, Mango seeds sourced from Burkina Faso (West Africa) at lower cost than spot markets
- **Processing integration:** Setting up CBA ESOS plant (75,000 tons) + solvent fractionation (75,000 tons) in Burkina Faso
- **Cost benefit:** Eliminates middlemen; reduces freight costs; improves supply chain resilience
- **Strategic value:** Long-term locked-in supply; reduces raw material inflation risk; improves ROIC
- **Timeline:** Phased capex over FY26-FY28; expected to commission by FY28
- **For Manorama:** Transform from commodity processor to integrated global supply chain operator

### D. **Product Mix Upgrading — Value-Added Shift**

- **Current mix:** 70-75% of revenue from value-added products (CBE, FSOS); remainder commodity fats
- **Target mix:** Increase specialty products (CBE, cocoa butter alternatives) to 40%+ of revenue (from current 30%)
- **Margin impact:** Specialty products command 50%+ gross margin vs 40%+ for commodities
- **For Manorama:** Each 1% mix shift toward specialty = ~50-100 bps EBITDA margin uplift
- **Management target:** Achieve ₹500+ Cr revenue by FY30 (implies 25%+ CAGR from FY26)

### E. **Working Capital Efficiency & Cash Generation**

- **FY26 achievement:** Working capital days reduced from 151 (FY25) to 125 (FY26)
- **Cash generation:** Operating cash flow ₹259 Cr (FY26) vs PAT ₹233 Cr = excellent cash conversion (111%)
- **For growth:** Strong cash generation self-funds capex; minimal debt needed (Net D/E 0.38:1)
- **ROIC driver:** High returns (33.6% ROCE) even before capacity expansion benefits flow through

### F. **Global Demand Fundamentals**

- **Chocolate industry:** Post-COVID normalization driving demand; confectionery volume growth 10-15% in developed markets
- **Cosmetics:** High-end skincare using shea butter, palm stearins experiencing 15-20% growth
- **Regional drivers:** Africa (local demand), Europe (premium brands), Asia-Pacific (emerging middle class)
- **For Manorama:** Positioned as cost-effective specialty supplier to global FMCG majors

   - **Current utilization:** Should trend from 85% (FY26) → 90%+ (FY27-28)

## Summary & Investment Thesis

**Manorama Industries Limited** is a **high-quality, cash-generative specialty chemicals compounder at inflection point** — transitioning from single-plant operator to integrated global supply chain with backward integration and significant capacity upside.

✅ **Strengths:**
- Exceptional unit economics: 33.6% ROCE, 27% EBITDA margins, 17.2% PAT margin (peer-beating)
- Strong growth: Revenue +76% in FY26; PAT +133%; momentum continuing
- Capacity leverage: New 52,000-ton facility (virtually no cost); targeting 85-90% utilization → operating leverage
- Backward integration: Burkina Faso sourcing/processing reduces COGS ~10-15%; strategic supply chain security
- Cash generation: ₹259 Cr OCF (111% of PAT); self-funds capex with minimal debt (Net D/E 0.38:1)
- Product mix upgrading: 30% specialty fats now; target 40%+ by FY28 → margin expansion

⚠️ **Key Risks:**
- **Valuation very high (43.8x P/E):** Pricing in near-perfect execution; limited margin for error
- **Africa execution risk:** First-year African subsidiaries posted ₹13.5 Cr losses; scaling unproven
- **Geopolitical exposure:** Burkina Faso political instability; supply chain disruption risk
- **Commodity price cycles:** Raw material inflation could pressure margins if pricing power weaker
- **Capex delivery:** ₹460 Cr over 2-3 years is significant; execution delays = FY28 miss

📊 **Financial Trajectory:**
- FY26A PAT: ₹233 Cr (17.2% margin; best-in-class)
- FY27E PAT: ₹340-380 Cr (+46-63% growth; assumes on-track capex + Africa normalization)
- FY28E PAT: ₹450-550 Cr (+32-45% growth; full capacity + product mix benefits)
- EBITDA margin: 27.1% (FY26) → 27-29% (FY27) → 28-31% (FY28)

**Key Investment Angle:**
Manorama is a **quality compounder with >30% ROCE trading at 43.8x P/E**. This is expensive for cyclical specialty chemicals, but the combination of (1) structural tailwinds (cocoa inflation, CBE adoption), (2) capacity expansion (52,000-ton debottlenecking, Africa integration), and (3) exceptional cash generation (111% OCF/PAT ratio) justifies a premium multiple in the 32-36x range. 

However, **execution risk is real** — Africa facility (₹120-150 Cr capex, first-time operation) is make-or-break. If Africa delays or losses persist beyond FY27, multiple compresses to 24-28x = significant downside.

**Target for conservative investors:** Wait for Africa facility online confirmation (FY27-28) before buying. Current holders should take 20-30% profits into strength (₹1,800-2,000).
