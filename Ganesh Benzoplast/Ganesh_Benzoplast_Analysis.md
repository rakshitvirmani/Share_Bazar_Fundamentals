# Ganesh Benzoplast Limited – Comprehensive Equity Analysis

## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Publicly listed mid-cap company engaged in liquid logistics and terminal operations
- Headquarters: Mumbai (Dina Building, Marine Lines)
- Stock ticker: BSE (GANESBE)
- Chairman & Managing Director: Rishi Pilani

**Business Segments**

1. **Liquid Storage Tank (LST) Division** – Core business (~55-60% of revenue)
   - Operates storage tanks for petroleum products, chemicals, and specialty liquids
   - Primary facilities: JNPT (Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust, Mumbai), Kochi, Goa
   - Terminal types: Class A (premium products), Class B, specialized tanks
   - Revenue model: Rental income from tank capacity leased to customers
   - Utilization: JNPT ~100%, Cochin ~80-85%, Goa ~5-10%

2. **Chemical Division** – Growing segment (~25-30% of revenue)
   - Manufactures and trades specialty chemicals
   - High volatility historically; improved significantly from FY25 onwards under new management
   - Revenue drivers: Seasonal demand, commodity pricing
   - Turnaround story: New management (under Rishi Pilani's direct control post-FY24)

3. **EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Contracts) / GBL Infra** – Emerging
   - Subsidiary: GBL Infra (wholly-owned)
   - Focuses on port-related tank infrastructure projects
   - Recent win: INR51.33 crore order from Reliance Industries for carbon fiber project
   - Margins: 5-10% typically

**Key Catalysts & Strategic Positioning**

The company is at an inflection point with multiple growth vectors:

- **JNPT Land Utilization (~11 acres total; ~4.5 acres new acquisition)**
  - Phase 1: 30,000-ton Class A petroleum tank facility commissioned in Q2-Q3 FY26
  - Expansion: 50,000 KL incremental capacity planned for JNPT
  - Timeline: First 40-50% ready by Q1 FY27; completion by FY27-28
  - Expected incremental revenue: INR45-50 crore once fully commissioned
  - EBITDA margin on new capacity: 65-75% (significantly higher than existing ~50-51% blended)

- **LPG Terminal Opportunity (BW Venture)**
  - Original JV with BW LPG (largest LPG handler globally) approved by High Court (Aug 2025)
  - Timeline: 2-3 years to operationalize; requires capital
  - Status: Land retained by Ganesh; now evaluating liquid storage vs. LPG vs. ammonia/cryogenic options
  - All options earmarked for JNPT land; decision expected by end of FY26

- **Chemical Division Stabilization**
  - Turnaround begun in Q1 FY26 following operational improvements and management restructuring
  - Efficiency gains: Better procurement policies, system upgrades, improved yields
  - Not driven by commodity price movements; sustainable improvements
  - Stabilized at ~15% EBITDA margin (vs. highly volatile 2-10% previously)

- **Strategic rationale**: Build client relationships; generate 5-10% margin projects; leverage existing infrastructure
- **Recent wins**: INR51.33 crore Reliance order (carbon fiber project)

### 5. **LPG / Ammonia Terminal Optionality**
- **Binary outcome**: Resolves by end-FY26 (management will announce final product mix)

- **Base case**: Ammonia/cryogenic storage; aligns with India's green hydrogen transition narrative

## Investment Summary

**Thesis**: Mid-cap logistics company executing disciplined capacity expansion in a supply-constrained storage terminal market. Chemical division turnaround adds earnings visibility. Debt-free balance sheet + organic capex funding = low financial risk.

**Key Strengths**
- Sticky customer base (long-term lease contracts; switching costs high)
- Pricing power (annual escalations negotiated; tied to inflation)
- High ROIC (18%+ on LST core business)
- Capex flexibility (can self-fund expansion; no dilution)

**Key Risks**
- Execution risk on JNPT expansion (infrastructure delays common)
- Chemical margin sustainability (still in early turnaround phase)
- Regulatory tail risk (port authorities could cap tariffs; low probability)

**Valuation Considerations** *(for investor reference)*
- Current FY26E EPS: ~INR10.2 → If market P/E is 20-25x, fair value ~INR200-250
- FY28E EPS: ~INR19.2 → At 22x, fair value ~INR420
- 3-year CAGR upside: 18-22% annually (if capex executes)
