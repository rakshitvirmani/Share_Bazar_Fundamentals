## 1. Business Model Overview

Vinyas (founded 2001, Mysuru) is a **high-mix, low-to-medium-volume, high-reliability EMS** provider focused on **defence and aerospace**. It is almost entirely **build-to-print**: the customer owns the IP and design, and Vinyas manufactures to specification (*per Nov 2025 concall*). It is moving up the value chain:

**PCBA → cable harness → system-level manufacturing → subsystem integration → lifecycle support/MRO** (*H1 FY27 presentation*)

**Customers:** an "equal mix" across three groups (*Nov 2025*):
- Defence PSUs: BEL, HAL. These include Israeli transfer-of-technology programmes, and Vinyas is often the sole approved vendor.
- Indian private defence primes.
- Foreign OEMs, via exports and offset programmes.

The business also includes medical (Fresenius Kabi Make-in-India devices plus one more customer ramping), industrial and transportation work, and an entry into commercial aerospace through a Tier-1 qualification.

**Programmes:** airborne platforms (helicopters, aircraft, HUD/IFFU), ground-based communications, imaging and fire-control systems, and naval radars (*Nov 2025*). A new 5-year long-term agreement with **HAL Korwa** covers HUD and IFFU electronic systems (*H1 FY27 presentation*).

**Footprint:**
- Mysuru: 1.5 lakh sq ft, with a further 25,000 sq ft Class-3 integration facility under construction.
- USA: **United Vinyas Technologies Inc.**, an 18,000 sq ft facility for PCBA and box build.
- Israel: a JV with an Israeli defence prime for manufacturing, system integration and MRO in India.

**Promoter holding:** only ~30%, and there are no plans to raise it (*May 2026*).

**Key observations:**
1. **Growth has been consistent:** +25% in FY25, +30% in FY26 and +37% in H1 FY27, against guidance of 25–35%. Vinyas is one of the few small-cap defence EMS names beating its own guidance.

4. **Working capital rotated, not improved:** in H1 FY27 receivables fell ₹62 Cr, but inventory rose ₹50 Cr and other current assets rose ₹22 Cr.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### 4.1 Defence indigenisation with sole-vendor positions
- Vinyas is the sole approved vendor on several PSU programmes after their full 24–36-month gestation. Those programmes give repeat, single-tender orders and "very clear visibility for the next 5 years" to fund 30–35% growth (*Nov 2025*).
- The HAL Korwa 5-year LTA for HUD/IFFU systems (H1 FY27) adds to the defence base.

### 4.2 Exports and Nadcap: access to global aerospace and defence
- Exports are already about 50% of FY26 revenue, mainly Europe and then the US. Nadcap AC7120 is a prerequisite for regulated commercial aerospace and export-controlled defence work, and it shortens the qualification cycle with Tier-1s (*Nov 2025*).
- The company is targeting 15% of revenue from global programmes by FY29 (*H1 FY27 deck*).

### 4.3 System integration: the margin lever
- Moving from PCBA to tested subsystems raises realisation per programme by **2–2.5x**. Management targets a **~300 bps EBITDA uplift within about 4 years** (*Nov 2025*). FY27 guidance is 11–12% EBITDA, with system integration pushing it "slightly up" (*May 2026*).

### 4.4 Israel JV and US subsidiary: optionality
- The Israeli defence-prime JV covers manufacturing, integration and MRO of defence systems in India. In the US, two programmes are shortlisted for local manufacturing. Neither is in the numbers yet.

### 4.5 Opportunity map (*H1 FY27 deck*)

The company presents this pipeline (unchanged since June 2025):

| Lever | 5-yr opportunity |
|---|---|
| Programme pipeline (existing defence) | ₹3,000 Cr |
| System integration | ₹2,000 Cr |
| Global expansion | ₹800 Cr |
| Vertical expansion (medical, commercial aero, rail) | ₹800 Cr |
| Technology-transfer enablement | ₹600 Cr |
| Total identified (incl. others) | **~₹10,000 Cr** |

Management itself says programme delays of 2–3 years are possible and are not factored in (*Jun 2025*).

### 4.6 Diversification target
- Within 5 years, management wants defence and aerospace at about 60% of revenue, with industrial, medical, commercial aerospace and rail making up about 40% (*Nov 2025*).

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### Investment Verdict

**Weighted Fair Value: ₹1,301 | CMP ₹1,496 | Rating: REDUCE.** ACCUMULATE between ₹1,171 and ₹1,301; BUY below ₹1,171.

**Bull (25%, ₹2,104).** Vinyas becomes a scaled Indian defence and aerospace EMS platform. Conditions:
- Large H2 defence orders land.
- Exports and Nadcap-led aerospace keep compounding.
- System integration pushes EBITDA to 13%.
- The Israel JV and US unit add new revenue streams.
- Working capital normalises.

**Base (50%, ₹1,300).** About 30% growth with a ~12% EBITDA margin, but heavy working capital keeps the PAT margin near 5.5–6% and leaves cash flow thin. On this path, the price is already fair to slightly rich.

**Bear (25%, ₹500).** Defence push-outs hit a business that is 85–90% defence. Leverage and interest squeeze PAT, warrant dilution lands, and a 25x multiple is applied to a ~₹22 FY28 EPS.

**Bottom line.** This is a good operator: it has beaten guidance on both revenue and margin and holds a real certification moat. The price is the problem. The only blemish in the P&L is poor cash conversion, so before paying a premium, watch for:
- H2 FY27 order inflows of at least ₹800 Cr.
- FY27 operating cash flow turning positive.

Wait for a pullback toward ₹1,170–1,300 (the stock has traded between ₹809 and ₹1,730 over 52 weeks), or for proof of the cash-conversion turn.
