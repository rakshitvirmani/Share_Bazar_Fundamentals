## 1. Business Model Overview

TARIL makes transformers in Ahmedabad (Gujarat). It says it is India's second-largest transformer maker by capacity, with ~1,200 employees *(company boilerplate, Jan 2025 / Aug 2025 filings)*. Its range covers 33 kV up to 1,200 kV *(per Q1FY27 concall, Jul 2026)*.

**Product portfolio**
- **Power transformers & auto-transformers** (66 kV–765 kV, up to 1,200 kV). These are the core business. Around 55–60% of the order book is 400 kV and above *(per Q2FY27 concall, Oct 2026)*. In April 2025, ~70% of the order book was 220 kV+ *(per Q4FY25 concall)*.
- **Shunt reactors**: recent GETCO wins of 80 MVAr / 125 MVAr reactors *(Q2FY27)*.
- **Special-duty transformers** (furnace, rectifier, specialty): ~35% of turnover *(per Q1FY26 concall, Aug 2025)*. Management says only TARIL and one Italian competitor are qualified for certain specialty furnace transformers. It sizes the niche at ₹200–300 Cr *(Q3FY26)*, with an enquiry pipeline of ₹400–500 Cr *(Q2FY26)*.
- **Distribution, IDT (inverter-duty) and renewable transformers**, made at Changodar and Odhav. Distribution orders are short-cycle ("cash and carry") and mostly not counted in the order book *(Q3FY25, Q1FY26)*.
- **New areas**: a first nuclear order (NPCIL Kaiga 5&6 generator transformers, 400 kV+) *(Q2FY27)*; regular supply to data centres in India and abroad *(Q2FY27)*; an HVDC repair order from PGCIL, the first ever for an Indian-origin company *(Q3FY26)*. BESS transformers are at the enquiry stage only *(Q2FY27)*. Green-hydrogen transformers are still in R&D *(Q1FY26)*.

**Plants**
| Plant | Product focus | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moraiya | EHV/UHV large power transformers, reactors | 57% utilisation in Q1FY27 *(Q1FY27)*. A +22,000 MVA EHV expansion is planned after Changodar. ~30% more floor space frees up once the insulation shop moves to the new pressboard plant *(Q2FY27)* |
| Changodar | Distribution, IDT, RE, small power transformers up to 132 kV, large power transformers up to 220 kV | Expanding from 12,000 to 35,000 MVA. Commercial production around Diwali 2026 *(Q2FY27)*. Ran at 27% utilisation in Q1FY27 during construction *(Q1FY27)* |
| Odhav | Distribution transformers | ~1,200 MVA *(Q3FY26)*. 100% utilised *(Q1FY27)* |

**Customer and geography mix**
- Utilities ~45% and EPC/others ~55% of the order book *(Q4FY25)*. Key customers: PGCIL (one order above ₹1,000 Cr in Q1FY27), GETCO (₹740 Cr in Mar-2025; ₹228 Cr in Q1FY27), RRVPNL, APTRANSCO, DVC, NPCIL and Megha Engineering (MEIL).
- **Exports**: 8% of Q2FY27 revenue and ~15% of the order book *(Q2FY27)*. Management caps exports at 10–15% *(Q1FY27)*. TARIL is present in 40+ countries. It won its first US order (PDC AK LPIV LLC, ~₹150 Cr) in Q1FY27 and set up a UK subsidiary in Sept 2026 *(Q2FY27)*.
- **Subsidiaries**: they include a 51% stake in a CRGO processing unit (Posco Poggenamp Electrical Pvt Ltd) *(Q3FY25, Q4FY25)*. Subsidiaries earn higher margins: consolidated EBITDA margin was 19.2% in Q1FY27 against 15.6% standalone *(Q1FY27)*.

**Pricing model**: Most orders carry an IEEMA price-variation clause. PV billing added ~7–8% to Q2FY27 revenue growth *(Q2FY27)*. Average realisation is ~₹7 lakh/MVA. It varies by product: smaller units fetch ~₹15 lakh/MVA and a 500 MVA/400 kV three-phase unit about ₹32 Cr *(Q4FY25, Q2FY27)*.

**Capacity disclosures conflict (flag):**
- Q4FY25 described Changodar as a +15,000 MVA expansion, taking the total to 75,000 MVA once Moraiya's +22,000 was included.
- Q3FY25 said the total would be ~55,000 MVA after "16,000 MVA".
- The Jul-2026 investor presentation listed "current" capacity as Moraiya 40,000 / Changodar 35,000 / Odhav 22,000 *(Q1FY27 Q&A)*.
- The Oct-2026 call gives Changodar +23,000 to reach 35,000, for a 75,000 MVA total.
- The Kanohar RHP shows FY26 installed capacity of 75,000 MVA, against 40,200 in FY25.

We use **~75,000 MVA from Q3FY27** (Changodar live) as the working figure. Even that is aggressive: **Q2FY27 output was ~9,500 MVA**, a ~38,000 MVA annualised run-rate *(Q2FY27)*.

**Revenue capacity:** Management says ₹5,000–6,000 Cr from transformers, plus ₹800–1,000 Cr of third-party backward-integration (BI) sales. That would give ₹7,000–8,000 Cr by "late FY29 / early FY30" *(Q1FY27, Q2FY27)*. Our cross-check: 75,000 MVA × 90% × ₹7 lakh/MVA ≈ ₹4,725 Cr. Adding Moraiya's 22,000 MVA (97,000 × 85% × ₹7 lakh) gives ≈ ₹5,770 Cr.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Order-intake inflection (strongest datapoint).** After a weak FY26 (~₹2,300 Cr of inflow), H1FY27 inflow reached ₹3,283 Cr. That included TARIL's largest-ever single order: PGCIL, above ₹1,000 Cr. The order book is at a record ₹7,237 Cr (+32% YoY) and the enquiry pipeline at ₹21,000 Cr. Historical win rate is 10–15% *(Q1FY27, Q2FY27)*.
2. **Changodar ramp: from capacity-constrained to capacity-available.** Changodar triples to 35,000 MVA from Q3FY27. Moraiya utilisation can rise from 57% toward 80–85% once raw-material supply is secured *(Q1FY27)*. Q2FY27 already shows operating leverage: revenue +66% YoY, EBITDA +203% YoY.
3. **Backward-integration margin lever.** CTC, pressboard, bushings and fabrication ramp during FY28. Management claims +200–300 bps of EBITDA and lower supply risk. Bushings are described as "a large bottleneck" *(Q1FY27)*. Third-party sales could add ₹800–1,000 Cr of revenue over time.
4. **Moving up the technology ladder.**
   - Dynamic short-circuit testing was completed on 500 MVA/400 kV (Q4FY25), 4 units (Q1FY27) and 3 units of 400/220 kV (Q2FY27).
   - First nuclear order (NPCIL Kaiga). Management expects it to recur and says transformers are 15–18% of a greenfield nuclear project's cost *(Q2FY27; interpretation of the remark ambiguous)*.
   - HVDC: qualification needs repair completion plus 6–7 months of field running. The earliest commercial HVDC/converter orders are ~18 months away *(Q2FY27)*.
5. **Export optionality.** First US order (~₹150 Cr), a UK subsidiary, and Americas/Australia targeted. Exports make up 20% of the enquiry pipeline. TARIL is a registered supplier to large data-centre operators *(Q1FY27, Q2FY27)*.
6. **Valuation-gap catalyst.** On 13-Aug-2026 TARIL traded at **32x FY26 EPS and 20.8x EV/EBITDA**. That is the lowest in the listed T&D peer set, where the average is 107x P/E and 83x EV/EBITDA *(Kanohar RHP)*. A few quarters of delivering on guidance could re-rate the stock from its credibility discount.

## 7. Investment Verdict & Key Metrics to Monitor

### Weighted Fair Value & Entry Recommendation
**Probability-weighted fair value: ₹297/share. CMP (13-Aug-2026 reference): ₹292, a 1.6% discount.**
**Rating: ACCUMULATE**, at the low end of the band. Prefer adding below ₹267 (BUY zone), which gives a margin of safety for the credibility discount.

### Bull Case (20%) – What Needs to Go Right → ₹456
Changodar and the BI plants come in on the latest dates. FY27 revenue beats ₹3,400 Cr. The 75,000 MVA base reaches ~80% utilisation in FY28. BI adds the full 200–300 bps, taking EBITDA toward 18–19.5%. PGCIL and state utility "mega orders" keep coming at ₹1,000+ Cr per quarter, and nuclear and data-centre orders recur. Working capital falls to 120 days, so FCF turns positive and the stock re-rates toward 35x as the governance discount fades.

### Base Case (55%) – Most Likely Path → ₹302
FY27 guidance of ~₹3,200 Cr and 16–16.5% EBITDA is broadly met, helped by the strong H1 and the Changodar start. FY28–29 grow 24–28% a year, and BI margin benefit arrives gradually. Working capital stays heavy at 130–150 days, so debt is flat to slightly higher. The multiple holds near today's level (~27x FY27E / ~20x FY28E). Returns would track earnings growth, not re-rating.

### Bear Case (25%) – What Could Go Wrong → ₹157
History repeats. Changodar and the BI plants slip again, and FY27 revenue lands at ~₹2,900 Cr (a third straight guidance miss). EBITDA is held near 14.5% by mix, LDs and input disruption (CRGO duty, bushings). Debt climbs past ₹700 Cr on inventory and capex, and governance overhangs (World Bank, management churn) persist. The stock de-rates to ~18x.

### Rating: **ACCUMULATE** (weighted FV ₹297 vs CMP ₹292)
The order book (₹7,237 Cr; 2.6x TTM revenue) and Q2FY27's operating leverage are real. Management's record on timelines, debt and order-book targets is poor, though, and the current price already discounts the base case. **Size positions modestly. Add on dips below ₹267, or after Q3FY27 confirms Changodar output and the ₹900 Cr+ quarterly run-rate.**
