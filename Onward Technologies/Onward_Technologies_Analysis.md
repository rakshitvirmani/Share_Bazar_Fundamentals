## 1. Business Model Overview

### What Onward does
Onward is a **pure-play engineering R&D (ER&D) services company**, about 35 years old and run by MD **Jigar Mehta** (MD since 2016). It works as an extension of the R&D departments of large **North American and European** OEMs. Its work covers mechanical and manufacturing engineering, robotics, embedded/electronics and controls, and increasingly digital engineering (data, analytics, AI-enabled workflows). It has **no Indian customers**: it exited the domestic business in 2019. India-billed revenue comes entirely from the **GCCs (global capability centres) of US and European companies**, plus the UK branch (*per Q2FY26, Q3FY26 and Q2FY27 concalls*).

### "3x3" strategy: three verticals and three horizontals

| Vertical | What it is | Status (Q2FY27) |
|---|---|---|
| **IEHM (Industrial Equipment & Heavy Machinery)** | Largest and oldest vertical (15+ years). Construction/mining equipment, off-highway, agriculture, power generation, power management and cooling for data centres. Most client relationships are 10+ years old. | Growing ~20% a year for 8 quarters (*analyst estimate, Q2FY27 concall*). Runs at "industry operating margin" (*per Q2FY26 concall*). Focus is North America; Europe is new territory for this vertical (*per Q3FY26 concall*). |
| **T&M (Transportation & Mobility)** | Automotive OEMs (US/EU, plus their India GCCs) and rail. Tier-1/Tier-2 clients were exited around FY25; ~90–95% is now OEM (*per Q2FY26 concall*). | **Declined ~1% in FY26** because of the Tier-1 exit and European auto furloughs (*per Q4FY26 concall*). Turning positive in FY27 (auto rebound, rail growing; *per Q1FY27 and Q2FY27 concalls*). Single-digit margins, investment mode. No SDV capability; management admits it is "2–3 years late" (*per Q4FY26 concall*). |
| **HCLS (Healthcare & Life Sciences)** | Medical device engineering. Started about 2 years ago. | **~₹5 Cr a quarter** (~3% of revenue; *analyst estimate, Q2FY27 concall*). No client above $1M yet (*per Q1FY27 concall*). Management expects 20–50% annual growth. Single-digit margins. |

**Horizontals / service lines:** mechanical engineering is now **~50% of revenue and software (embedded + digital) ~50%** (*per Q4FY26 concall*). Embedded is expected at 18–30% of revenue "in the next couple of years". Management's long-term aim is to move the company towards digital/AI.

### Geography
- **By client headquarters:** **~70% North America (US + Canada), ~30% Europe**, annual basis (*per Q4FY26 concall*). Management also says that **Germany is "still very small"** within the hedged export book (*per Q3FY26 concall*). European exposure is mainly UK and Germany-based auto/industrial OEMs. Europe was "slow" in Q2FY26 (*per Q2FY26 concall*), and the US is the growth engine. **Germany/European auto dependence is moderate rather than high.**
- **By billing entity (Q1FY27):** India ₹77.1 Cr (52%; GCC clients plus the UK branch billed through India), USA ₹50.4 Cr (34%), Europe ₹3.8 Cr (3%), others ₹18.2 Cr (12%) (*Q1FY27 results via multibagg*). About 50% of revenue is foreign-currency invoiced and hedged (*per Q3FY26 concall*).

### Client concentration
- **~70–75 active clients**, down from ~250 four years ago. The tail was pruned deliberately, and management sees ~50 clients as the "sweet spot" until revenue reaches ₹1,000 Cr (*per Q2FY26 and Q4FY26 concalls*). There were 73 live clients in Q1FY27, and 6 net new clients were added in FY26 (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
- **Top 25 clients = 87–88% of revenue** (*per Q3FY26, Q4FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls*). It was 80% when revenue was ₹250 Cr.
- **Top 10 ≈ 70%** (from <50% in FY22), and **top 5 ≈ ₹50–55 Cr each, i.e., ~45–50% of revenue** (*analyst estimates on the Q3FY26 concall; management did not dispute them*). **The top client is not disclosed.** 2–3 of the top 5 have been clients for 10+ years (*per Q4FY26 concall*).
- **$1M+ clients: 18** (16 → 18 in Q1FY27; the Q2FY27 call repeated "16 to 18"). The aspiration is **$10M a year per client**.
- **Contract model:** **~88–90% time-and-material (T&M)**, the rest fixed price (fixed price was ~16% in Q1FY27). Management intends to stay around 90% T&M until ~₹1,000 Cr revenue (*per Q3FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls*).

### Moat and positioning
- **Sticky, domain-heavy work.** Onward hires laterals from the shop floor rather than freshers; engineers typically have 8–20 years' experience. Becoming a qualified supplier takes 3 months to 3 years of pilots, POCs and audits (*per Q3FY26 and Q4FY26 concalls*). Rate cards are standard across the top suppliers of each OEM.
- **Land-and-expand to ODCs.** Work starts onsite, moves offshore, then becomes a dedicated offshore development centre (ODC) of 200–500 engineers (*per Q1FY27 concall*).
- **Tiny wallet share.** Onward has 0.5–1% of each client's outsourcing budget, and clients' ER&D outsourcing budgets are $100M–$1B each (*per Q1FY27 concall*).
- **Weaknesses:** small scale (~$65–75M revenue run-rate), late to SDV, only now building digital/AI capability, revenue/employee of ₹22–24 lakh vs ₹35–40 lakh at best-in-class peers, and limited disclosure (no USD revenue, vertical margins, utilization or order book).

## 3. Capacity Roadmap (Delivery Capacity)

For a services company, "capacity" means billable engineers, seats, and the onsite/offshore leverage mix.

### Headcount and productivity

|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY22–FY26 | ~2,500 flat | ~0 | 8–9 → ~22 | Mix shift from India clients to export, tail pruning |
| Q2FY26 | 2,525 | — | ~22 | Management guided headcount "relatively stable, +10–20% over a couple of years" |
| Q3FY26 | 2,491 | −34 | ~22 | Annual bottom-5% exit cycle |
| Q4FY26 | 2,485 | −6 | ~22 | ~125 employees outside India; target 200–300 |
| Q1FY27 | ~2,670 | ~+180 | ~22–23 | Pune centre #4 planned, Chennai full |
| **Q2FY27** | **2,917** | **~+250** | **~23–24** | **432 net adds in H1, the first large hiring wave in years** |
| Q3FY27E | ~2,950 | slow | — | Holiday period; hiring senior SMEs for 2027–28 |
| Q4FY27E–Q1FY28E | ~3,200–3,300 | "a few hundred" net | — | *per Q2FY27 concall* |

- Management said in Jan 2026 that it would reach **~3,000 employees by $100M revenue** (*per Q3FY26 concall*). It is already at 2,917 with revenue running at roughly $75M annualised (INR rate assumed). The plan has shifted from "productivity-led" to "capacity-led" growth.
- **Revenue/employee aspiration ₹30–40 lakh** (Board aspiration; no timeline). Management cautions that this needs a blended 10% onsite / 90% offshore mix, and that US visa restrictions lower the achievable ceiling (*per Q2FY27 concall*).
- **Utilization:** not disclosed numerically. Management cited "higher utilization for our offshore engineers" as the main Q2FY27 margin driver.
- **Attrition:** at a record low of 14.7–15.5% LTM through FY26. Q2FY27 is not disclosed, but management says "the market is getting competitive" for talent.
- **Hiring model:** no freshers or campus hiring. Laterals come from the manufacturing shop floor through **90+ in-house recruiters**, with no agencies (*per Q2FY27 concall*). This limits how fast Onward can ramp.

### Onsite/offshore mix
- New engagements start at **~10% onsite / 90% offshore or remote** (*per Q2FY26 concall*). The tightening US visa regime (H-1B changes and the new PERM regulation) has pushed clients to **ramp offshore faster without depending on onsite staff**, which was a key Q2FY27 margin lever (*per Q2FY27 concall*). Offshore rates are "double-digit" margin while onsite is "low single-digit" (*per Q3FY26 concall*).
- Q1FY27 offshore mix "moderated slightly" (*per Q1FY27 concall*). Some onsite subcontracting (₹25 Cr → ₹34 Cr YoY) was used because staff could not travel during the Middle East conflict. These roles are to be backfilled with Onward's own staff (*per Q1FY27 concall*).

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### DNA shift: from an India-centric mechanical "body shop" to a global, offshore-led digital engineering partner
1. **Client mix (FY19–FY26):** exited all Indian clients, pruned ~250 clients to ~73, and moved to direct US/EU OEMs and their GCCs.
2. **Margins (FY25 → FY26):** EBITDA margin 9% → 13.2% on offshore leverage, simplification and automation. Fixed G&A and account management costs stay flat as revenue grows ("for 70 clients, we will only need 70 account managers", *per Q1FY27 concall*).
3. **Growth (FY27):** after four years of flat headcount, Onward is hiring hundreds a quarter. It raised guidance for the first time in its history (*per Q2FY27 concall*).

## Investment Verdict & Rating

### Weighted fair value ₹519 vs CMP ₹367: 41% upside. **Rating: BUY** (CMP is 29% below FV, beyond the 10% BUY threshold)

**Bull case (25%) → ₹810:** the AI/data-centre industrial boom turns Onward's ODC model into a multi-year compounder. The power-management client scales 5x, renewals keep coming at +50–70%, all three verticals grow, and margins reach 16%+ by FY28 on offshore leverage and fixed G&A. **Base case (50%) → ₹508:** FY27 revenue of ~₹650 Cr (+19.5%) at 14.2% margin, then 15–16% growth with 30–50 bps of margin expansion a year. Management keeps doing what it has done for six quarters: beating vague guidance. FY28E PAT ₹66 Cr at 17x, broadly today's trailing multiple applied one year forward.

**Bear case (25%) → ₹249:** the hiring wave of ~700 engineers meets a programme delay or data-centre capex pause at a concentrated client base. Revenue growth falls to ~6% in FY28 and margins revert to 12.5%. AI-driven pressure on hourly billing appears, and the stock de-rates to 12x on FY28E PAT of ₹46 Cr. Net cash of ~₹44/share and the dividend cushion the downside.

**Why BUY despite the re-rating from ₹250 to ₹367:** the stock still trades at ~12x base FY28E earnings and ~7.8x FY27E EV/EBITDA for a debt-free business with ~20% ROE that is growing EPS at ~20%. The risk/reward is asymmetric: +41% to the weighted FV and +38% to the base case, against −32% to the bear case.
