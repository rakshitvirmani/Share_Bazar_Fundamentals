# Harsh Special 2 - Nifty 2026 Outlook & Market Cycle Analysis

This is a summarized version of Harsh's video covering his Nifty-focused 2026 outlook, market cycle rules, and the technical + fundamental framework he uses to predict bull and bear phases.

---

## USD-INR as a Bull Rally Predictor

The USD-INR weekly chart follows a clear trendline channel. The upper trendline acts as resistance for the dollar. Every time USD-INR touches this resistance, Nifty's bottom forms almost simultaneously, and a bull rally starts soon after.

Historical backtests:

- Feb-March 2025: USD-INR near resistance, Nifty rallied ~15-16% in 133 days (4-5 months)
- October 2022: USD-INR at resistance, Nifty rallied 50% in 721 days for anyone who invested at that point
- March-April 2020 (COVID): USD-INR at resistance, Nifty rallied 121% in 588 days

Currently USD-INR is at Rs.90 per dollar, hovering near the resistance trendline. It may stay in this range for 1-3 months, but after that it should cool down, and as soon as it does, market rallies. This is not something to fear — it is historically a bullish signal.

## FII Selling is a Contrarian Buy Signal

FII net selling years: 2011, 2018, 2022, and 2024-2025. In every case, within 1-2 years of FII selling, very strong bull rallies followed.

- After 2011 FII selling: 84% rally in Nifty over 730 days
- After 2018 FII selling: 118% rally in 1800 days
- After 2022 FII selling: 62% return in 731 days, then another massive green yearly candle

2025 has seen Rs.1,55,000 crore of FII net selling. Based on historical patterns, this sets up the market for strong FII buying and bull runs in the next 2-3 years. FII selling should not be feared — it should be seen as a setup.

## ROC (Rate of Change) Indicator — The Bottom Signal

Setup: ROC indicator with length 18 on a monthly timeframe, applied to Nifty. This tells you what return Nifty has given in the last 18 months.

The rule: when ROC drops to zero (meaning Nifty's last 18-month return is 0%), the market is near its bottom. When ROC crosses from below zero back above zero, the bull run starts.

Currently ROC is at ~16% (Nifty has given 16% return in last 18 months). If Nifty stays in the 25,000-26,000 range for the next 2-3 months, ROC will hit zero by March. It may dip slightly below zero for 1-3 months max, then cross back above. That crossing is the perfect timing signal for the new bull rally.

Backtests: March 2023 — ROC hit zero and crossed back up, triggering a 53% rally in 560 days. June 2022 — same signal, same result. The pattern is highly reliable.

ROC also signals market tops (when it goes too high), which Harsh has covered in a separate video. When ROC reaches 100, that is the exit signal.

## 400 EMA — The Hard Floor for Nifty

On the daily chart, the 400-day Exponential Moving Average acts as the ultimate support level for Nifty. The market does not stay below this level for long.

Backtests:

- March 2025: Nifty touched 400 EMA, bounced with a good rally after
- March 2023: Same — touched 400 EMA, strong rally followed
- June 2022: After falling from Oct 2021, Nifty found support at 400 EMA and rallied

Current 400 EMA level is around 24,000 but since this is a moving average, by March it will be around 24,500-25,000 levels. So if bad news triggers a temporary dip, Nifty can come to this level, but that is the floor. After a second retest of 400 EMA, a very strong rally is expected.

The current pattern closely mirrors the Oct 2021 to March 2023 period: fall from highs, recovery to near ATH, rejection, and then a second test of 400 EMA before the real bull rally begins.

## NSE Net New High Indicator — Pinpointing the Exact Week to Deploy

Setup on TradingView: "NSE Net New High" indicator with 52-week duration, on a weekly timeframe. Add a reference line at -20.

What it does: it tracks the change in the number of NSE-listed stocks making 52-week highs. When this drops below -20, it signals extreme panic in the market — these are the best weeks to deploy money in Nifty 50 stocks, ETFs, or largecap mutual funds.

Backtests:

- Feb 17 and March 2025 weeks: indicator went below -20 on both occasions. Deploying at the average level yielded ~14-15% return in just 126 days on the Nifty index itself
- March 27, 2023 week: indicator hit -20, which was the exact market bottom. Rally of 53% in 560 days followed
- June 2022: same signal, same excellent result
- Tariff announcement week: also breached -25, signaling peak panic

When the other cycle indicators (ROC near zero, USD-INR at resistance) are already in place, these panic weeks identified by NSE Net High are the exact deployment windows.

## Valuation Check — PE Ratio Confirms the Setup

Nifty current PE: 22.5. Historical pattern shows that whenever Nifty PE drops to 19-20 levels, strong bull rallies follow (June 2022 at 18.9 PE, March 2023 at 20.4 PE, March 2025 at 20 PE).

To get from 22.5 to 20 PE, two things need to happen: a ~5-6% price correction (bringing Nifty to the 400 EMA around 24,500-25,000) plus ~5-6% earnings growth over the next two quarters (December and March quarter results). Both together will bring the PE to approximately 20 by March-April. That means proper bull market conditions will be in place by March-April for Nifty and largecaps.

## Nifty Target: 30,000 by December 2026

Using a cup-with-handle pattern analysis: Nifty is forming a large cup, and if it retests 400 EMA, a handle will complete. The breakout target (cup depth extrapolated upward) comes to approximately 30,000.

Backtest of this method: in the 2021-2023 consolidation, the same cup-with-handle approach gave a target that was achieved within 245 weeks (less than 1 year). An even earlier instance completed in 336 days.

Based on this, 30,000 on Nifty is achievable by December 2026. That represents roughly 16% upside from current levels, or about 22% from the 400 EMA level.

## Key Takeaway: All Indicators Are Converging

For Nifty and largecaps, 2026 is shaping up to be a bull rally year. The convergence of signals is strong:

1. USD-INR at resistance trendline = historically bullish for Nifty
2. FII selling year = historically followed by 2-3 years of buying and bull runs
3. ROC approaching zero = bottom formation signal, bull rally expected from March-May
4. 400 EMA acting as hard floor = max downside 5-6% from current levels
5. NSE Net High indicator providing exact weekly deployment windows during panic
6. PE ratio on track to reach 19-20 by March-April = valuation-friendly for a sustained rally
7. Cup-with-handle pattern targeting 30,000 by December 2026

Smallcap and microcap analysis will diverge from Nifty (there is a time lag between the two), and that is covered in the companion video (smallcapAnalysis).
