## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL) – Since 1994
- Listed on BSE (Script: 530305)
- Headquarters: Indri, Haryana (distillery location)
- Founded as sugar mill (1994), diversified into alcohol (2007, 2010, 2017 onwards)
- Workforce: Multi-hundred employees across distilleries, sugar operations
- Management: Harvindar Chopra (Managing Director), Natwar Aggarwal (CFO)

**Business Model: Premium Alcohol-Beverage Company (transitioning away from Sugar)**

Piccadily operates across **two strategic pillars**:

### Pillar 1: Distillery / Alcohol-Beverage (IMFL – Indian Made Foreign Liquor) [≈43% revenue, growing to 60%+ target]

**Premium Single Malt Whisky – "Indri"** (Flagship brand)
- India's largest independent producer of malt spirits
- Unique climate advantage: Indri region (Haryana) with temps from 0°C to 50°C enable distinct flavor profiles
- Indri captured **55% of Indian Single Malt export market** – dominant positioning
- FY25 volumes: 100,000+ cases; FY26: +37% growth
- Sales realized: ₹150 Cr → ₹250 Cr (2-year trajectory)
- Export focus: 25% of total revenue; major markets: Europe, APAC, Americas
- EBITDA margins: **45-50%** (premium luxury spirits category)
- New launches FY25: Indri Game of Thrones, Indri City Series Edition, Indri Founders Reserve 11YO, Indri Diwali Collectors Edition 2024
- Distribution: Indri Experience Centre at distillery; curated tastings, golf greens, premium positioning

**Premium Sugarcane Rum – "Camikara"** (Fast-growing, newer brand)
- Bold innovation redefining rum category
- FY25 growth: **310% YoY** (on smaller base, ₹21 Cr → ₹92 Cr)
- Products: Camikara 12-Year-Old (for international markets), Camikara 3YO, Camikara mixers
- Experiential events: Camikara Millionaire Bartenders Challenge (Jan-Apr 2025, winner prize ₹1 Cr)
- Distribution: Building premium positioning in on-trade (bars, hotels, nightclubs)

**Blended Malt Whisky – "Whistler"** (Acquired/inherited portfolio)
- Barrel-aged blended whisky with barrel maturation
- FY25 growth: **54% YoY**
- Appeal: Sophisticated blend of tradition and modernity
- Positioning: Mid-premium segment vs Indri's ultra-premium

**Other IMFL Products**: Cane juice rum, specialty expressions, limited editions

### Pillar 2: Sugar (Refined Sugar Production) [≈28% revenue, declining]

**Traditional Sugar Refining Business**
- 5,000 TCD (Tonnes per Calendar Day) capacity
- White crystal sugar production; collaborates with 5,000+ sugarcane farmers for sustainable sourcing
- FY25 revenue: ₹249 Cr
- FY25 growth: **-9.4% YoY** (headwind from commodity sugar pricing pressure)
- EBITDA margin: **-1.1%** (negative, loss-making in FY25)
- Strategic shift: **Management intends to demerge** sugar business into separate entity
- Purpose: Focus capital and human resources on high-margin IMFL portfolio

### Pillar 3: Other Distillery (Ethanol / ENA / Country Liquor) [≈29% revenue, bulk/B2B focused]

**Extra Neutral Alcohol (ENA) & Ethanol Production**
- 78 KLPD (Kilo Liters Per Day) capacity in Indri; 200 KLPD in Chhattisgarh (commissioned Dec 2025)
- Revenue: ₹257 Cr (FY25)
- B2B focused: Supplying ethanol for fuel, industrial use, country liquor manufacturing
- Chhattisgarh expansion: 200 KLPD distillery targeting ₹250-300 Cr → ₹300-400 Cr revenue (FY26-27)
- EBITDA margins: Lower than IMFL, mid-single digit

## 4. Key Catalysts & Growth Drivers

**1. IMFL Volume & Revenue Explosion – FY27 Guidance of 60-70% (BUT HIGH EXECUTION RISK)**

**FY26 Actual Performance vs Guidance:**
- FY26 overall IMFL growth: **42% YoY** (₹639 Cr → ₹908 Cr) ✓ Strong
- **BUT Indri brand (flagship) only grew 16% in FY26** ✗ MISSED 25-30% prior guidance by 40%
- Camikara rum: Growing 310% (from tiny base) ✓ Momentum
- Whistler: Growing 54% ✓ Momentum
- **Implication**: Indri growth gap was offset by Camikara/Whistler. At current trajectory, Indri will NOT drive 60-70% FY27 growth alone.

**FY27 Management Guidance: 60-70% Overall Alco-Bev Growth**
- Management claims: "We see growth of 60% to 70% in our Alco-Bev business, keeping in mind the growth of our current portfolio there are a lot of new product pipeline which will add to it."
- Reality check: If Indri (core, ₹250 Cr revenue) grows 20%, Camikara (₹100 Cr) grows 50%, Whistler (₹60 Cr) grows 40%, blended growth = ~30-35%, NOT 60-70%
- **Only way to hit 60-70%**: Chhattisgarh IMFL production (requires malt aging 3-5+ years) would need to contribute significantly in FY27 = **unrealistic** (new malt won't be ready for premium IMFL until FY27-28)
- **Likely scenario**: FY27 Alco-Bev growth closer to 30-40% (still strong, but 30-50% miss vs guidance)

**Near-term Drivers (Conservative View):**
- Indri brand: Likely 15-25% growth (supply-constrained by maturation timeline; capacity expansion still ramping)
- Camikara rum: 50-100% growth (leverage new capacity; penetrating premium on-trade)
- Whistler: 40-50% growth
- New product launches: Kashmir Vodka (May 2025 launch), Game of Thrones, City Series = incremental revenue

**Structural Drivers:**
- **Premiumisation in India**: Younger consumers preferring quality over quantity; single malts growing 236% (FY22-FY25 CAGR)
- **Indri's unique positioning**: Only large-scale malt distillery in India with climate advantage + award-winning quality (Indri Diwali 2023 won World's Best Whisky)
- **Export momentum**: 25% of revenue from exports; international demand for Indian single malts surging
- **Distribution expansion**: 16,000+ retail outlets (vs 6,700 FY24); CSD network; HORECA channel
- **IMFL EBITDA margins 45-50%**: 2x the company average → as IMFL grows to 60%+ revenue, blended margins expand to 25-26%+

**2. Margin Expansion** (21.4% → 25%+ by FY27)

Path to margin expansion:
- FY25: IMFL 42.9%, Sugar 28.1%, ENA 29%
- FY27: Target IMFL 60%+, Sugar 10-15% (post-demerger), ENA 25-30%
- IMFL at 45-50% margins pulling blended company margin from 21% → 25%+
- Sugar demerger removes negative margin drag (-1.1% in FY25)

**3. Sugar Demerger Value Unlock**

- Standalone sugar company: ₹249 Cr revenue, negative margins = distressed valuation (2-3x P/E)
- Post-demerge benefit: 
  - Piccadily IMFL becomes pure-play spirits company → re-rating to 20-25x forward P/E
  - Sugar subsidiary becomes turnaround opportunity for specialists → potential M&A target

**4. International Expansion (Scotland Distillery)**

- Scotland distillery will produce Scotch whisky under Piccadily brand
- Global single malt market: $100B+ TAM
- Indri already exporting to 28 countries + 11 international duty-free outlets
- Scotland distillery: 3x revenue potential by FY28-29 once operational

**5. Acquisition Opportunities**

- Management mentioned exploring brand-side acquisitions (domestic or international)
- Potential to acquire established spirits brands → leverage Piccadily's production + distribution capabilities
- Examples: Import established rum, vodka, gin brands → distribute across 16,000+ outlets
