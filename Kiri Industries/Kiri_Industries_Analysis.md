# Kiri Industries Limited – Equity Research Analysis

**CRITICAL DISCLAIMER:** This is a **TRANSFORMATION & EXECUTION-RISK play**. Core business is modest; 90% of upside tied to ₹12,000 Cr integrated copper-phosphate complex ramp-up (2027-2030). This is NOT a traditional dividend stock.

## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company:** Kiri Industries Limited – Specialty chemicals producer transitioning into integrated copper & fertilizer manufacturer.

**Current Business (Dyes, Dyes Intermediates, Basic Chemicals):**
- Headquarters: Jaipur, Rajasthan
- Products: H-Acid (7,200 MT capacity), Vinyl Sulphone (12-15k MT), specialty dyes, intermediates
- Customers: Textile dyers globally; specialty chemical compounders
- Revenue FY26: ~₹1,000 Cr (chemistry-only, mature business)
- Market Position: Regional player in commodity dyes; not a global powerhouse

**Joint Venture (Lonsen Kiri – 40% stake):**
- Separate dye/chemical manufacturer; NOT consolidated
- Strong standalone profitability: ₹71 Cr EBITDA, ₹51 Cr PAT (Q1 FY27)
- Kiri's 40% share contributes ₹21 Cr PAT to consolidated results
- Serves as "proof of concept" for profitability at scale

**TRANSFORMATIONAL PIVOT: Integrated Copper Complex**

**Why Copper?**
- India imports ~1.8 million tons copper annually; supply gap widening
- EV battery boom driving 20%+ annual copper demand growth globally
- Backward integration opportunity: Can source raw materials at scale, process in-house
- Long-term margin opportunity: Refining (highest value-add), foils, rods all profitable

**Project Structure (Ipapav Port, Gujarat):**

| Phase | Facility | Capacity | Timeline | Investment | Status |
|-------|----------|----------|----------|------------|--------|
| **Phase 1** | Tube Plant (Cu tubes) | 35 KT | June 2027 | ₹500 Cr | Civil works 50% done |
| | Rod Plant (CCR) | 2.25 Lakh tons | Aug-Sep 2027 | ₹2,000 Cr | Design finalized |
| | Scrap Furnace | Scrap melting | Dec 2027-Jan 2028 | ₹1,000 Cr | Procurement ongoing |
| | Copper Foil | 5,000 KT (pilot) | Mar 2028 | ₹500 Cr | R&D stage |
| **Phase 2** | Refinery | 1.75 Lakh tons | Q3-Q4 FY29 | ₹3,000 Cr | EPC tender stage |
| | Sulfuric Acid | Integrated | Q4 FY29 | ₹800 Cr | Engineering |

| | Ammonia Plant | Integrated | Q1 FY29 | ₹1,500 Cr | Vendor engagement |
| **Phase 3** | Steel Recycling | TBD | Post-FY30 | TBD | Concept stage |

**Total Capex Through FY29:** ₹12,000 Cr (~$1.44 billion)

**Financing Strategy (CRITICAL FOR VALUATION):**
- Structured to AVOID shareholder dilution >50%
- >50% committed via debt (structured with moratorium until 2029)
- Equity funding: ₹230 Cr already raised from Indo Asia Copper Ltd (subsidiary?)
- Ongoing equity discussions at ₹500-700/share (below current market) to minimize dilution
- No external debt currently; all equity-funded existing business

**Supply Chain Strategy:**
- Securing 1 million tons copper concentrate via MoUs (3-4 month visibility currently)
- Target 1.5 million tons annual procurement by FY28-29
- Setting up own procurement yards in US, EU (reducing intermediary costs)
- Long-term contracts at LME indices (commodity hedged, not speculative)

**Regulatory/Strategic:**
- MCB Copper-Gold Mining Project: 70% off-take agreement
  - Litigation with Celsius (shareholder) ongoing 11+ years
  - Kiri position: Structured as lender (secured cash recovery if Celsius wins court case)
  - Upside: Access to copper ore if Kiri's ore rights upheld
  - Status: Matter now in court; resolution expected by year-end (per management commentary)

## 3. Capacity Roadmap

### Existing Business (Dyes & Chemicals)

**Current State:**
- Multiple plants in Rajasthan, Western India
- 13-14 major products with cumulative capacity for 1+ lakh tons/year
- **Current Utilization:** 60% (40% unused capacity)
- Products with high unused capacity: H-Acid (underdeveloped at 7.2K MT), Vinyl Sulphone (12-15K MT), others

**Expansion Path (Q1 FY27 onwards):**
- Management being cautious: Ramp up utilization to 70-75% before capex
- Focus on margin (not volume) given price/raw material volatility
- If demand supports: Possible debottlenecking of existing plants (low capex, high return)
- **No major new capex planned for existing business** (all resources going to copper project)

### Integrated Copper Complex – PHASED COMMISSIONING

**Phase 1A (CY2027, Q4 FY28):**
- **35 KT Tube Plant**: Operational June 2027
  - Products: Copper tubes for heat exchangers, automotive, HVAC
  - Steady-state: ~₹50-60 Cr annual revenue at full utilization
  - Margins: 8-10% (value-add of 2-3%)
  - Investment deployed: ₹500 Cr
  - Timeline RISK: HIGH (first plant, learning curve)

**Phase 1B (CY2027-2028, Q2-Q4 FY28):**
- **2.25 Lakh Ton Continuous Rod Plant (8mm)**: Aug-Sep 2027
  - Products: Copper rods for wiring, cable, industrial uses
  - Steady-state: ₹1,000-1,200 Cr annual revenue at 70% utilization (100k tons)
  - Margins: 6-8% EBITDA (₹60-100 Cr per 100k tons at current prices)
  - Investment deployed: ₹2,000 Cr
  - Sourcing: Mix of scrap furnace output + procured cathodes

**Phase 1C (CY2027-2028, Q1-Q2 FY29):**
- **Scrap Melting Furnace**: Operational Dec 2027-Jan 2028
  - Capacity: Produces anodes from scrap copper (Ingot, blister, etc.)
  - Steady-state: ~50-60k tons/year anode production
  - Margins: 6-8% (value-add minimal, mainly collection/melting)
  - Investment deployed: ₹1,000 Cr
  - Key Enabler: Necessary for recycled copper pathway; reduces cathode sourcing costs

- **Copper Foil (Pilot)**: ₹5,000 KT pilot by Mar 2028 (Q4 FY29)
  - Products: Rolled copper foil for PCB, flex circuits, batteries
  - **Highest margin product** (15-20% EBITDA potential)
  - Steady-state (Phase 3, post-2029): ₹500-1,000 Cr revenue
  - Investment: ₹500 Cr (pilot + scale-up)
  - Technology: Importing cathodes from Japan initially (LME-grade, premium-priced)

**Phase 2 (CY2028-2029, Q3-Q4 FY29):**
- **Refinery (LME Cathode Production)**: 1.75 Lakh tons/year
  - Most capital-intensive (₹3,000 Cr)
  - Process: Anodes → Electro-refining → LME-grade cathodes
  - Margins: 8-12% EBITDA (₹80-120 Cr per 100k tons)
  - Revenue impact: Adds ₹1,400-1,800 Cr annual revenue
  - Timeline RISK: HIGH (technology transfer, vendor dependencies)

- **Sulfuric Acid Plant** (Integrated)
  - Byproduct from copper refining
  - Revenue: ₹200-300 Cr annually
  - Margins: 10-12%
  - Strategic: Supplies phosphoric acid plant

- **Phosphoric Acid + Ammonia Plants**
  - Phosphoric acid: Supplies fertilizer downstream
  - Ammonia: Imported currently; own plant reduces cost
  - Combined revenue: ₹800-1,200 Cr
  - Margins: 8-10%
  - Timeline: Q1 FY29 onwards

**Phase 3 (Post-FY30):**
- Steel recycling (secondary consideration, concept stage)

## Investment Thesis Summary

**Kiri Industries is a HIGH-RISK, HIGH-REWARD TRANSFORMATION PLAY.**

### Why Consider It

1. **Secular Tailwind:** India's copper import dependency (1.8M tons/year) + EV battery boom creating 15-20% annual demand growth
2. **Execution Visibility:** Management has delivered Phase 1 capex (₹1,400 Cr spent, on budget); proof of capability
3. **Supply Chain Advantage:** Backward integrated copper + fertilizer complex reduces cost, improves margins
4. **Valuation Upside:** At ₹600-700/share, weighted fair value ₹869 implies 24-45% upside even in Base case
5. **Import Substitution Play:** India wants domestic copper/fertilizer capacity; Kiri is one of few making it happen

### Why Avoid It

1. **Execution Risk is MASSIVE:** ₹12,000 Cr capex, 3+ year timeline, technology transfer, supply chain dependencies
2. **Shareholder Dilution:** Will likely require equity raise at ₹400-700/share; current holders face >20-30% dilution
3. **No Dividends for 3+ Years:** All cash flow reinvested in capex; shareholders get returns only post-FY30
4. **Litigation Overhang:** 11-year MCB mining dispute still pending; court ruling could go either way
5. **Commodity Exposure:** Copper, fertilizer prices volatile; refinery margins may not reach 8-10% if global capacity adds

### Best Suited For

- Growth-focused investors (2-3 year+ horizon)
- Investors bullish on import substitution / commodities
- High-risk tolerance
- **NOT suitable for:** Dividend seekers, risk-averse investors, short-term traders
