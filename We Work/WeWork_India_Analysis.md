## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- WeWork India Management Limited (formerly WeWork India Solutions Private Limited)
- Operator of flexible co-working and shared office spaces in India
- Listed on NSE (WEWORK) and BSE (544570) in October 2024 (IPO)
- CEO: Karan Virwani | CFO: Clifford Lobo

**Business Segments**

1. **Core Workspace** (87% of revenue, ₹603 Cr Q1 FY27)
   - Monthly membership hot-desks and dedicated desks
   - Private offices for small teams and enterprises
   - Revenue model: Per-desk/per-month subscription with flexible tenure (3-27 months average)
   - Occupancy: 84.9% across portfolio (mature centers 87.5%, growth centers ~65%)
   - Members: 113,000 (30% YoY growth, growing faster than capacity additions)
   - NPS: 78 (strong customer satisfaction)
   - Membership mix: 77% enterprise, 65% global companies, 46% North America riding GCC wave

2. **Value-Added Services (VAS)** (11-12% target revenue, currently tracking 10%)
   - Parking, facilities, customization, late fees
   - Customization revenue: Transitioning from one-time to recurring (amortized over contract term)
   - Q1 FY27 customization: ₹9.5 Cr (down from ₹47 Cr Q4 FY26 due to large one-time deals)
   - Recurring VAS: ₹10-15 Cr per quarter expected

3. **Digital Services & Tech** (3-4% of revenue, ₹26 Cr Q1 FY27, +27% YoY)
   - 80% EBITDA margins on digital revenue (high-margin stack)
   - In-app bookings, billing, facility management
   - Member Services platform (launched July 15, 2026): Bundled business services (HR, hiring, insurance, transport)
   - Expected material contribution to revenue over next 2-3 years

**Geographic Footprint**
- 79 centers across 8 cities (Bangalore 30 centers home market; Hyderabad +68%, Chennai +66%, Delhi >3x)
- Operational space: 9.1 million sq ft
- Contracted LOIs: 2.9 million additional sq ft (12-month development pipeline)
- Contracted capacity: 12 million sq ft total (44,000 desks locked in, 179,000 desks already committed for FY27-28)

**Key Strengths**
- Dominant market position in India's flex workspace (55% market share, growing fastest; 27% of office leasing now flex)
- Strong member stickiness (52% of desk sales from existing members, 84% renewal rate)
- Operating leverage (revenue up 28%, opex per sq ft flat → margin expansion)
- Contracted revenue visibility (₹3,363 Cr member commitments = 27 months forward revenue visibility)
- Enterprise/GCC customer base (66% from global companies; North America alone 46% of revenue; stable, high-margin)
- Rapid profitability inflection (ROCE 9.1% → 28.6% in 1 year)
- Asset-light model (no real estate ownership, leases buildings → capital efficiency)

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

**1. Profitability Inflection (Achieved)**
- Company has crossed profitability threshold (Q1 PAT ₹53.2 Cr suggests sustainable run-rate of ₹150-170 Cr annually by end-FY27)
- Margin expansion visible: 19.8% EBITDA in Q1, targeting 20%+ sustainably
- Catalyst: Maturation of first 79 centers to break-even and positive unit economics (mature centers at 87.5% occupancy)

**2. Market Consolidation & Competitive Moat**
- WeWork India is category leader (55% market share in branded flex space)
- Competitors (Regus, Awfis, others) fragmented; none have density/brand reach of WeWork
- Switching costs high: Enterprise customers integrate WeWork into real estate strategy
- 84% renewal rate = customer stickiness = predictable recurring revenue

**3. GCC/Global Expansion Opportunity**
- 46% of revenue from North America (remote Indian offices for US companies)
- India's cost arbitrage + English-speaking workforce = structural tailwind
- As India's tax incentives for GCCs improve (especially Bengaluru, Hyderabad), WeWork is positioned as preferred operator
- Target: Maintain 40%+ global company mix; potentially grow to 50%+ as international demand stabilizes

**4. Member Services Ecosystem (Multi-Homing)**
- New platform (launched Jul 15) bundles services members already buy scattered (HR, insurance, hiring, transport)
- Network effects: More services → more engagement → higher stickiness → ability to command premium
- TAM: 113,000 members × ₹10K annual additional services spend = ₹113 Cr potential annual revenue
- Margin profile: 70-80% (e-commerce/SaaS-like, vs 70-80% on core workspace)

**5. Capex Cycle Visibility**
- FY27 capex: ₹500-600 Cr; already 44,000 desks "sold" in advance (LOIs signed)
- FY27 revenue growth: +20% (conservative given 30% member growth)
- By FY28: New centers ramping to profitability, EBITDA margins to 23%+ (from 19.8%)
- This creates compounding: More spaces → more revenue → higher margins → more capex capacity

## Investment Recommendation

**HOLD / SELECTIVE SELL above ₹700 | Fair Value ₹520-562 | Risk/Reward UNFAVORABLE**

**Rationale:**

1. **Valuation Disconnect**: At ₹737, stock trades at **118x P/E on FY26 earnings** (₹6.25 EPS). Even assuming 30% EPS CAGR to FY30 (₹6.25 → ₹30 EPS in 4 years), fair P/E should be 20-22x = ₹660. Currently overvalued by 11% at best case.

2. **Execution on Hypergrowth**: Company is in heavy capex cycle (₹500-600 Cr FY27). While contracted revenue (₹3,363 Cr) provides visibility, actual profitability depends on:
   - Growth centers ramping to 70%+ occupancy (currently 65%)
   - Member Services adoption (only launched Jul 2026, unproven)
   - Maintaining 20%+ EBITDA growth (guided, but capex-intensive quarters can see margin dips)

3. **Business Quality Excellent, Price Terrible**: 
   - ✓ 30% member growth outpacing 17% capacity growth = pricing power
   - ✓ 87.5% occupancy in mature centers = near-peak utilization
   - ✓ 52% revenue from renewals = recurring revenue base
   - ✓ ROCE 28.6% = capital-efficient business
   - ✓ Free cash generation ₹141.9 Cr in Q1 = self-funding growth
   - **✗ BUT P/E 118x leaves zero margin for error**

4. **Triggers for Downside:**
   - **Q2-Q3 execution miss**: If capex spending creates lower profitability in any quarter (normal for growth companies), stock could correct 20-30%
   - **Member Services flops**: If adoption <5% by end-FY27, story shifts to "still unproven one-trick pony"
   - **Occupancy stalls**: If growth centers don't ramp above 70%, or mature centers tick down to 85%, margin guidance at risk
   - **Macro headwind**: GCC hiring pause → occupancy pressure → revenue miss

5. **Better Entry Points Ahead:**
   - ₹550-600 range (on any temporary market weakness or Q2 margin dip) → fair value
   - ₹400-450 range (if macro slows or Member Services adoption disappoints) → bargain entry
   - Current ₹737 = peak enthusiasm pricing; risk/reward asymmetric to downside
