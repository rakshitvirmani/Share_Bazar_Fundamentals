# Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited – Comprehensive Equity Analysis

## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Small Finance Bank (SFB) with focus on financial inclusion
- Headquartered: Bengaluru (Grape Garden, 18th Main, 6th Block, Koramangala)
- Listed on NSE (UJJIVANSFB) and BSE (542904)
- Stock Brokers/Exchanges: NSE, BSE
- Recent management leadership: Sanjeev Nautiyal (MD & CEO), Carol Furtado (Executive Director)

**Business Segments**

1. **Affordable Housing & Micro Mortgages** (~25-30% of GLB)
   - Gross Loan Book (GLB): INR8.231 Cr in secured portfolio (Q3 FY26)
   - Growth: 40.3% Y-o-Y to INR8.231 Cr (Q3 FY26)
   - Geographic reach: 581 branches for affordable housing
   - Loan ticket size: INR16 lakh to INR20 lakh average
   - Loan-to-value (LTV) underwriting: Calibrated approach (56%+ securitized)
   - Yield profile: 12.5% (competitive vs. market)

2. **Micro Banking** (~50% of secured portfolio)
   - Loan portfolio: INR20.709 Cr in Q3 FY26
   - Growth: 6.9% Q-o-Q, 10.9% Y-o-Y
   - Disbursements: INR5.245 Cr in Q3 FY26 (all-time high)
   - Collection efficiency: 99.8% (March 26)
   - Bucket-X collection efficiency: 99.7%+ for 7 consecutive months
   - New customer addition: 1.4-1.5 lakh per quarter

3. **MSME (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises)** (~12-15% of book)
   - Loan portfolio: INR3.470 Cr with 54% Y-o-Y growth
   - PAR (Provision for Doubtful Assets): Stable at 1.2% and 0.6% (GNPA)
   - Key drivers: Working capital and supply chain finance
   - Product mix: ~28% of MSME book

4. **Gold Loans** (~7% of overall secured portfolio)
   - Gross loan book: INR1.020 Cr (+248.6% Y-o-Y)
   - New branches: 106 branches added in Q3 FY26 (455 total sourcing branches)
   - Disbursements: Growing at 183.9% Y-o-Y
   - Origination LTV: Comfortable at 75%
   - Yield profile: High yield driver (19.5%-odd range)

5. **Vehicle Finance** (~3-4% of new business lines)
   - Gross loan book: INR1.036 Cr (+85.1% Y-o-Y)
   - New two-wheeler segment: Growing at 120% Y-o-Y; INR823 Cr
   - Product launches: Pre-owned cars (pilot), Commercial vehicle (ADI), LCV/HCV in pipeline
   - GNPA: 1.8%; FIG book: Growing 6.9% Q-o-Q, 17.9% Y-o-Y
   - Growth focus: Mid-premium and EV segments

6. **Deposits & Liabilities**
   - Total deposits: INR45.668 Cr (Q3 FY26)
   - Growth: 21.4% Y-o-Y, 8.2% Q-o-Q
   - CASA deposits: INR12.930 Cr (+37.8% Y-o-Y)
   - Retail TD + CASA: INR31.995 Cr (70% of deposits)
   - CD ratio: 89% (liquidity management)
   - Deposit mobilization: 25% growth guidance (FY27)

**Key Strengths**
- Diversified loan portfolio (6 product lines reducing concentration)
- High asset quality with improving collection efficiency
- Consistent deposit mobilization (25% YoY growth)
- Strong management team with technology focus
- Emerging digital infrastructure (AI, analytics, cloud-led platform)

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### 1. **Universal Bank Transition (DNA Shift)**
- **Old story**: Small Finance Bank restricted to lending; deposit cap, liability limitations
- **New story**: Full banking license would unlock wholesale funding, investment banking, payment settlement
- **Timeline**: RBI decision expected within 12-24 months
- **Impact**: Addressable market expands 5-10x; liability structure more resilient
- **Probability**: Medium-high (management executing well on compliance, capital adequacy)

### 2. **Deposit Franchise Quality & Stability**
- **Catalyst**: CASA ratio improving (27% → 28%+ targeted); retail TD mobilization strong
- **Key metric**: Deposits growing 25% guidance vs. GLB growing 25% — balanced growth
- **Cost of funds**: Stabilizing at 6.8-7% level; future rate cuts will accrue to margin
- **Upside**: If CoF drops 50 bps, NII margin expands by 30-50 bps

### 3. **Affordable Housing Penetration**
- **Opportunity**: 580+ branch network; semi-urban focus (underserved market)
- **Ticket size**: INR16-20 lakh average; competitive vs. MFI, housing finance players
- **Growth**: 40% YoY evident in Q3 FY26; momentum likely to sustain
- **Risk**: Competition intensifying; market may saturate in core markets

### 4. **Gold Loan Business Inflection**
- **Catalyst**: Activating 100+ branches in Q3-Q4; new customer additions accelerating
- **Economics**: High-yield business (19.5%+); LTV-controlled growth (75% origination)
- **Scale potential**: From INR1,020 Cr (Q3 FY26) → INR2,000-2,500 Cr by FY28
- **Market backdrop**: Gold prices stable; strong retail participation

### 5. **Vehicle Finance as 3rd Pillar**
- **Opportunity**: Two-wheeler (core pilot successful), pre-owned cars (new vertical), commercial vehicles (planning)
- **Growth**: Two-wheeler at 120% YoY growth; portfolio now INR823 Cr
- **Profitability**: Expected to turn profitable post-scale; breakeven infrastructure in place
- **Timeline**: Meaningful contribution to P&L by FY28

### 6. **Credit Cost Normalization**
- **Current status**: At 1% (Q1 FY27) vs. 0.9% (Q3 FY26); guidance to 1.4% by end-FY27
- **Driver**: New business growth (higher slippage initially) + labor code provision
- **Upside**: Once normalized and stabilized, credit cost may improve to 1.2-1.3% range (mid-cycle)
- **Watch**: Asset quality trends in new portfolios (gold, vehicle)

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor Each Quarter

### Leading Indicators (Watch Every Call)

1. **Deposit Growth Rate %** (Quarterly)
   - Expected: 5-6% Q-o-Q (aligned with 25% YoY guidance)
   - Signal: <4% Q-o-Q suggests deposit mobilization stress; >7% suggests unsustainable pace

2. **Gross Loan Book Growth %**
   - Expected: 5-6% Q-o-Q (18-20% YoY for FY26, then 25% FY27+)
   - Signal: Acceleration = strong demand; deceleration = capacity constraint or demand slowdown

3. **CASA Ratio %**
   - Target: 28%+ (from 27% current)
   - Signal: Improving ratio = lower cost of deposits; declining = pressure on NII margin

4. **Asset Quality Metrics (GNPA %)**
   - Watch: Overall GNPA trend (target <2% in mid-cycle)
   - Segment-wise: Monitor gold loan GNPA (target >99% collection efficiency), vehicle GNPA (target >98%)
   - Signal: GNPA trending up = credit stress; down = portfolio quality improving

5. **Net Interest Margin (NIM %)**
   - Expected: 8.2-8.5% (stable to slight improvement)
   - Signal: >8.6% = favorable rate environment; <8.0% = competitive pressure or deposit cost inflation

6. **Cost-to-Income Ratio %**
   - Expected: 65-66% (gradual improvement as scale grows)
   - Signal: <65% = operational leverage kicking in; >67% = branch expansion cost not yielding expected ROA

7. **Credit Cost Ratio % (to Advances)**
   - Expected: 1.4% by FY27 (from 0.9-1.0% current)
   - Signal: <1.2% = early recovery phase; >1.6% = stress emerging

### Lagging Indicators (Track Quarterly)

1. **Profit After Tax (PAT) Growth %**
   - Expected: 50%+ for FY26, 48% for FY27
   - Red flag: <20% growth = guidance miss on profitability

2. **Return on Assets (ROA %)**
   - Expected: 1.8% FY26, 2.2% FY27
   - Watch: Trend towards 2.0%+; if <1.5%, indicates margin compression or cost overrun

3. **Return on Equity (ROE %)**
   - Expected: 16% FY26, 19% FY27
   - Target: 20%+ by FY28 (peer competitive)

4. **Dividend Payout Ratio %**
   - Expected: 20-25% of PAT initially (building capital for universal bank transition)
   - Watch: If <15%, cash accumulating for M&A or capex; if >30%, return of capital signals confidence

### Management Commentary Red Flags

- **Deposit costs rising faster than guided** (>15 bps per quarter) = margin pressure incoming
- **Slippages in gold/vehicle portfolios** accelerating (GNPA >2% in either segment)
- **Delays in universal bank approval** past FY27-end = growth caps at 20%
- **Competitive yield pressure** on new originations (<11% on affordable housing vs. 12.5% current)
- **Capex overruns** on branch infrastructure (>INR1 Cr per branch)
- **Talent attrition** in collections or credit teams = quality degradation risk

## Investment Summary

**Thesis**: Small Finance Bank executing well on asset diversification (6 business lines) and deposit franchise building. Universal bank transition is binary catalyst; even in base case (SFB structure), ROE trajectory to 19-20% is attractive. Asset quality stable; management credible on credit cycle management.

**Key Strengths**
- Diversified loan portfolio (reduces single-product risk)
- Strong deposit mobilization discipline (CASA improving)
- Proven credit management in micro-banking (99.8% collection efficiency)
- Management track record on execution (branch expansion, product launches on time)
- Regulatory tailwind (SFB mandate to grow financial inclusion; RBI supportive on universal bank transition)

**Key Risks**
- Universal bank approval uncertain (binary outcome)
- New products (gold, vehicle) need 2-3 more quarters of performance validation
- Deposit franchise rate-sensitive; if rates stay high, cost of deposits remains sticky
- Competitive intensity in affordable housing and micro-lending segments

**Valuation Considerations** *(for investor reference)*
- Current FY26E EPS: ~22 → At 25x P/E, fair value ~INR550 per share
- FY27E EPS: ~32.5 → At 23x P/E (growth premium), fair value ~INR750
- FY28E EPS: ~48 → At 22x P/E (mature SFB), fair value ~INR1,050
- 3-year CAGR upside: 28-35% annually (if universal bank approved and execution on track)
