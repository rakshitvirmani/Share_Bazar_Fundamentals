## 1. Business Model Overview

CSM Technologies Limited (formerly CSM Technologies Private Limited), incorporated in 1998 and headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, is a **GovTech-led digital transformation company** — it designs, builds and operates mission-critical digital platforms for governments, public-sector undertakings (PSUs), development agencies (World Bank, ADB, AfDB, UN bodies) and enterprises. *per AR FY26, MD&A*

**Seven industry verticals** (FY26 revenue mix, *per AR FY26 MD&A*):

|---|---|---|
| Mining & Allied Services | 29.83% | 24.74% |
| Government & Public Services | 18.41% | 25.75% |
| Agriculture & Allied Services | 23.69% | 16.10% |
| Industry & Trade Facilitation | 16.49% | 11.13% |
| Education | 6.29% | 14.82% |
| Healthcare | 5.16% | 7.32% |
| Tourism | 0.13% | 0.14% |

**Revenue mix by geography:** India 92.21% / Outside India 7.79% (FY26); international revenue is growing off a small base, with operations spread across Africa (Kenya, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Malawi, Cabo Verde, Gambia, Gabon — Nairobi is the Africa Delivery Centre hub), UAE, USA and Canada. *per AR FY26 MD&A*

**Revenue mix by client type (FY26):** Government 61.33%, Enterprise 23.98%, PSU 14.23%, Development Agencies 0.46%. Government dependence has actually fallen from 74.15% in FY25 as Enterprise/PSU mix has grown. *per AR FY26 MD&A*

**Differentiation:** Domain-led technology built around statute/approval-hierarchy requirements of the public sector (not adapted commercial software); integrated cross-department digital platforms; emerging tech (AI/ML, GIS, IoT, satellite imagery, AR) deployed in live population-scale government systems; proprietary/patented IP — including a patented **AR-based ore-sampling technology** used in mining royalty systems (i3MS), an in-house **Low Code No Code (LCNC) framework**, an **AI Model Orchestration Platform**, and a proprietary Document Management System. Certifications: **CMMI Maturity Level 5 v3.0, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 9001/14001/20000-1/27001/30414.** *per AR FY26, Section I & MD&A*

**Scale (as stated in AR):** 28+ years of operating history, ~1,327 employees as at 31 March 2026 (standalone; the "About" section separately cites "1,700+ employees" and 900+ implementations as a current/group-wide figure), 180+ active projects, presence across 14 countries and 20 Indian states/UTs, 90-95% of revenue historically from repeat/long-standing customers (per management, *Q4 & FY26 concall, 28-Jul-2026*).

**Listing history:** CSM completed its Mainboard IPO — a **pure fresh issue of 1,29,01,000 equity shares at ₹113/share** (₹103 premium to ₹10 face value), raising **₹145.78 Cr** — and commenced trading on NSE and BSE on **2nd July 2026**. Paid-up capital rose from 3.87 Cr to **5.16 Cr shares**. A 5:1 bonus issue (June 2025) preceded the IPO. Promoter holding stood at 94.90% as of 31 March 2026 (pre-IPO), of which founder-Chairman **Priyadarshi Pany alone held 93.60%**; post-IPO (fresh-issue-only, no OFS), promoter holding dilutes to an estimated **~71%** — still an extremely high single-promoter concentration. *per AR FY26, Directors' Report*

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Order book of ₹357.63 Cr as at 31-Mar-2026 — 1.58x FY26 revenue.** Contracts typically run 3–5 years, giving revenue visibility beyond 24 months. *per AR FY26 "FY26 at a Glance" & concall*

2. **Margin re-rating already underway.** EBITDA margin expanded 571 bps (14.69%→21.0%) and PAT margin 347 bps (7.03%→10.5%) in FY26 on operating leverage and delivery efficiency — management explicitly guided margins "on an upside" for FY27. *per AR FY26 MD&A & concall*

3. **AI/Agentic-AI as an efficiency + monetisation lever.** Management frames AI both as an internal delivery-efficiency tool and as a growing category of government demand (AI-enabled land approvals, exam systems, LLMs trained on government process/historical data). ₹10.25 Cr was invested in FY26 specifically in an "AI wrapper/layer" on existing platforms. *per concall & MD&A*

4. **Government digital-spend tailwind.** Indian government IT/digital spend is projected to grow at a 7.2% CAGR FY26E–FY31P; global IT-ITeS industry is projected to grow at 4.7% CAGR CY26E–CY30P with digital/AI spend growing faster (6.8% CAGR). E-governance/public-sector digitalisation (Digital India, state IT policies in Odisha/Telangana/Karnataka/Maharashtra) is a structural growth driver for CSM's core addressable market. *per AR FY26 MD&A, Section A*

5. **Geographic and customer diversification reducing single-market/single-client dependence.** Government share of revenue fell from 74.15% (FY25) to 61.33% (FY26) as Enterprise/PSU mix grew; two new African geographies (Malawi, Cabo Verde) added in FY26; management specifically flagged reducing Odisha concentration by winning orders in Chhattisgarh (Khanij 2.0 mining), Delhi (NAFED ERP), Rajasthan, Assam (Adani), Ethiopia and Kenya.

6. **High entry-barrier positioning.** CMMI Level 5 + SOC 2 Type II certification and multi-state/PSU empanelments create structural barriers to new entrants in the tender-based GovTech niche — a moat the MD&A explicitly calls out as favouring established players like CSM.

7. **First year as a listed entity — governance/capital-discipline reset.** Successful mainboard listing (July 2026) raised ₹145.78 Cr of fresh growth capital, and management has signalled selective M&A (post-Kwantify) as a strategy to deepen verticals/geographies.

## 6. Future Financial Projections & Fair Value Analysis

### Assumptions

- **Share base used for all per-share figures: 5,16,03,472 shares (~5.16 Cr)** — the fully post-IPO paid-up capital as of 30-Jun-2026 (note: FY26 reported EPS of ₹6.10 used the pre-IPO weighted-average share count, so it is *not* directly comparable to the FY27E+ figures below).
- **CMP used for valuation: ~₹83/share** (mid-September 2026 trading range of ₹76–95; market cap ~₹430 Cr; down ~27% from the ₹113 IPO price).
- Base case anchors to management's own FY27 guidance ("revenue on an upside... margins on a similar or upside" per concall) plus the ₹357.63 Cr order book providing >24-month visibility.
- Bull/Bear cases stress the two swing factors evident in the FY26 numbers: (a) receivables/cash-conversion trajectory, and (b) bid-win-ratio-driven order intake.

### Probability-Weighted Fair Value Calculation (FY27E basis)

- Bull: ₹137/share × 25% = ₹34.25
- Base: ₹90/share × 50% = ₹45.00
- Bear: ₹45/share × 25% = ₹11.25
- **Weighted Fair Value ≈ ₹90.5/share**

At CMP ~₹83, this implies the stock trades at an **~8.3% discount** to probability-weighted fair value (expected return to FV ≈ **+9%**).

### Probability Shifters: What Moves Bull/Base/Bear Odds

**Bull probability ↑ if:**
- Operating cash flow turns solidly positive for two consecutive quarters (DSO reverts below ~90 days)
- Order-book conversion runs ahead of the 24-month visibility implied by the ₹357.63 Cr book
- International (Africa/N. America) revenue share crosses ~15% of total (from ~7.8% in FY26)
- Bid-win ratio recovers above 0.55–0.60
- EBITDA margin sustains above 22% for two consecutive quarters

**Bear probability ↑ if:**
- DSO stays above 120 days through FY27 or worsens further
- Net Debt/Equity crosses 1.0x
- Revenue growth undershoots high-single digits in FY27 (order intake stalls, bid-win ratio stays <0.50)
- Any further, more material cyber/security or governance disclosure surfaces
- Promoter stake is reduced meaningfully via further dilution/pledging (none currently disclosed, but a risk given the concentration)
- A large customer/segment (e.g., a state mining department) is lost or delayed materially

### FY30+ Terminal Value

Using the Base Case FY29E as the launch point: FY30E revenue ≈ ₹340.7 Cr × 1.13 ≈ **₹385.0 Cr**, with PAT margin normalising at a mature **11.2%** → **FY30E PAT ≈ ₹43.1 Cr**.

Applying a **terminal P/E of 16x** (a mature, lower-growth multiple appropriate once the order-book-driven growth phase matures) → Terminal Market Cap ≈ ₹689.6 Cr → **Undiscounted FY30 fair value ≈ ₹133.6/share**.

Discounting back 3 years (FY27→FY30) at a 13% cost-of-equity appropriate for a small-cap, single-promoter, recently-listed stock: PV ≈ ₹133.6 / (1.13)³ ≈ **₹92.6/share** — reassuringly close to the FY27E-anchored weighted fair value of ₹90.5/share, providing a cross-check on the valuation.
