## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Krishana Phoschem Limited (KPL) – Integrated phosphatic fertilizer producer
- Listed on NSE (KRISHANA) and BSE (532681)
- Headquarters: Bhilwara, Rajasthan (AKVM Industrial Area)
- Managing Director: Praveen Ostwal
- Group company: Madhya Bharat Agro Products (parent/sister company)

**Business Segments**

1. **Phosphatic Fertilizer Manufacturing (~67% of FY26 revenue, ₹1,300 Cr)**
   - **NPK-DAP Complex**: 495,000 MTPA capacity (post-expansion, completed FY26)
     - Old capacity: 330,000 MTPA
     - New capacity added: 165,000 MTPA (just commissioned in FY26)
     - Product range: NPK complexes (12-32-16, 16-20-13, 15-15-15, 8-21, 9-24-24), DAP
   - **Single Super Phosphate (SSP)**: 120,000 MTPA capacity (operating >100% utilization)
   - **Total phosphatic capacity**: 615,000 MTPA

2. **Backward Integration (~30% revenue contribution)**
   - **Sulfuric Acid Plant**: 99,000 MTPA capacity (own production, 50-75% utilization)
   - **Phosphoric Acid**: Produced in-house for DAP/SSP manufacturing
   - **Rock Phosphate Beneficiation**: 200,000 MTPA capacity (50-75% utilization currently)
     - Low-grade domestic rock phosphate sourced from MP State Mining Corp
     - Converts to high-grade phosphate for manufacturing

3. **Trading Business (~33% of FY26 revenue, ₹550 Cr)**
   - Import/trading of various fertilizer types to complement manufactured products
   - EBITDA margin: 5-6% (lower-margin business, facilitates farmer reach)
   - Strategic purpose: Provide complete nutrient solutions to farmers

**Integrated Manufacturing Advantage**
- Backward integration into sulfuric acid, phosphoric acid, and rock phosphate beneficiation
- Provides cost advantage vs. peers (estimated ₹500-1,000/ton margin cushion vs. competitors like Paradeep Phosphates, Coromandel)
- Manufacturing EBITDA per ton: ₹5,200-5,500 (FY26, down from ₹6,000 in FY25 due to input cost inflation)
- Diversified product portfolio (NPK, SSP, DAP) reduces exposure to single-product cycles

**Market Position**
- One of only 3-4 integrated rock phosphate beneficiators in India (along with RSMM, Madhya Bharat Agro Products, others)
- Rock phosphate reserves: Limited in India; only RSMM and domestic MP mines available
- Duopoly-like structure in beneficiation capability provides competitive moat

## 4. Key Catalysts & Growth Drivers

**1. NPK-DAP Capacity Ramp-Up (FY27-FY28)**
- **Key catalyst**: Steady production ramp from 43% utilization (Q1 FY27) toward 60-70% by FY27-end
- **Revenue uplift**: +₹200-250 Cr annually from capacity utilization improvement
- **Execution risk**: Low (plant commissioned, production ongoing; just demand/sales execution)
- **Margin accretion**: +₹25-30 Cr EBITDA annually as utilization scales

**2. New NPK Grades & Product Mix Enhancement**
- **Introduced Q1 FY27**: 12-32-16, 16-20-13, 15-15-15, 8-21, 9-24-24 (higher-margin variants)
- **Q1 FY27 impact**: New grades contributed to EBITDA margin improvement (16.7% in Q1 vs. 12.3% FY26 blended)
- **Market opportunity**: Indian farmers shifting toward balanced nutrient applications; KPL positioned as price-maker with complex offerings
- **Upside**: Margin expansion potential to 13-14% as new products scale (vs. 12.3% FY26)

**3. Government Support Framework**
- **Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS)**: Enhanced rates announced for Kharif 2026 season
- **MSP (Minimum Support Price)**: Hiked for crops (soybean, groundnut, cotton, pulses) → increased fertilizer demand
- **Cost pass-through**: Government support for farmer affordability → prices held while subsidies cushion farmer economics
- **Benefit to KPL**: Volume growth protected; margin pressure moderated

**4. Industry Structural Shift**
- **Supply constraints**: Industry production down 28% YoY (supply-side constraints), but KPL sales +33-35%
- **Market share gains**: KPL taking share via backward integration advantage and distributed product range
- **Pricing power**: Complex fertilizers (NPK) increasingly favored over DAP; KPL volume growing faster than peers

**5. Green Ammonia Transition (FY29+)**
- **70K MTPA SECI agreement**: Starts FY29, reduces ammonia cost inflation exposure
- **Long-term strategic benefit**: Supply security, cost stability, ESG alignment
- **Timeline**: Material contribution post-FY29; not relevant to near-term (FY27-28) returns
