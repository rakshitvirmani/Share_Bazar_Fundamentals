## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Supriya Lifescience Limited (SLL) – Specialty pharmaceutical/CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) company
- Listed on BSE (543434) and NSE (SUPRIYA)
- Headquarters: Mumbai (Corporate office at Gorregaon East)
- Manufacturing: Lote Parshuram (Ratnagiri, Maharashtra); Amberath facility (new, operational FY26); Patalganga (expansion site)
- Leadership: Dr. Satish Wagh (Executive Chairman & Whole-Time Director), Dr. Saloni Wagh (Managing Director)
- USFDA-approved supplier; Government Recognised Export House
- Est. 1995, established pharmaceutical company (47+ years of experience)

**Business Model: Export-Focused CDMO + Specialty Pharma**

1. **Export Segment (~82% of FY26 revenue, ₹679 Cr)**
   - Primary markets: Europe (40% growth), Asia (growing), LATAM (emerging)
   - Focus: Regulated markets only (strict margin-focused strategy)
   - Customers: Large global pharma companies, CMOs, CDMO partnerships
   - Revenue drivers: API (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients) + advanced intermediates + finished dosages
   - Growth model: Launch 3-4 new products annually; full backward integration into APIs

2. **Domestic Segment (~18% of FY26 revenue, ₹149 Cr)**
   - Branded generics sold through Indian distribution network
   - Focus: Niche therapeutic areas (anesthetics, cardiovascular, ADHD, antibiotics, vitamins, anti-hypertensives)
   - Not aggressively pursuing domestic market (strategy: regulated markets prioritized for better margins)
   - Domestic mix growing but intentionally capped

**Key Therapeutic Focus**
- **Anesthetics**: 91% of revenue, 6-7 products, fast-growing in regulated markets
- **Cardiovascular**: New advanced intermediate launched Q4 FY26, scaling up
- **ADHD**: Launched FY26, strong demand in LATAM and Europe
- **Antibiotics**: Stable growth
- **Vitamins & Minerals**: DSM contract (Ribo flavin/B2) stabilized at ~3 tons/month
- **Anti-hypertensives**: Growing traction
- **Anti-allergic**: Smaller category, emerging

**Strategic Advantages**
1. **Backward Integration**: Fully backward integrated into APIs, advanced intermediates, and finished formulations
   - Own CVS (Cardiovascular API), liquid anesthetic API, etc.
   - Rare in India; provides cost/margin advantage over competitors
   - Enables better quality control, IP protection, and higher margins

2. **Regulatory Compliance**: USFDA-approved facilities (Lote, Amberath); EU-GMP certified; multiple global approvals
   - Only 1 minor observation in surprise USFDA audit (Q4 FY26)
   - Positions as USFDA-approved alternative to China-sourced APIs
   - Strategic advantage in regulated markets (Europe, US seeking diversification from China)

3. **R&D Capability**: Doubled R&D spend over last decade
   - Two R&D labs (Lote, Amberath), new formulation facility
   - 3-4 new launches annually; focus on differentiated molecules
   - R&D focused on CMO/CDMO opportunities (large-volume pharma partnerships)

4. **Quality & Scale**: 47+ years of operation; GMP standards; large pharma trust
   - Scaling existing API volumes as demand increases
   - DSM contract shows ability to manage large-scale batch operations
