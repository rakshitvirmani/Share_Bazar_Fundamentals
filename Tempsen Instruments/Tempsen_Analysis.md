## 1. BUSINESS MODEL OVERVIEW

### **Core Business Positioning**

Tempsen Instruments manufactures and sells **temperature sensing solutions, electrical heating solutions, and specialised cable solutions** to diverse industrial end-markets globally. The company has evolved from a niche temperature sensors player to a diversified thermal solutions provider through organic growth and strategic acquisitions (Marathon Heater, Tempsen Instruments GmbH, Tempsen Polska).

| **Temperature Sensing Solutions** | Thermocouples, RTDs, Temperature Gauges, Infrared Pyrometers, Data Loggers, Temperature Transmitters | Chemicals, Metals, Oil & Gas, Power, Semiconductors, Food Processing |
| **Electrical Heating Solutions** | Immersion Heaters, Panel Heaters, Heating Tapes, Industrial Heating Elements | Industrial process heating, OEMs, HVAC, Water heating |
| **Specialised Cable Solutions** | Fire-resistant cables, Low-smoke cables, LSZH cables, specialty sensor cables | Infrastructure, power, defense, aerospace |

## 4. KEY CATALYSTS / DNA SHIFT

### **Structural Growth Drivers**

**1. Industrial Automation & IoT-Enabled Sensing (Secular Tailwind)**
- **Market Trend:** Global temperature sensors market growing at 7-8% CAGR (CY2020-30E per F&S Report)
- **India Opportunity:** Manufacturing-led growth, PLI schemes, semiconductor/EV investment → demand for precision temperature monitoring
- **Tempsen Positioning:** Core competency in temperature sensing; opportunity to pivot towards IoT/wireless sensors (limited current offering)
- **Growth Opportunity:** Estimated ₹100-150 Cr incremental revenue if IoT penetration reaches 30% of temperature sensing mix by FY2028

**2. Energy Efficiency & Green Transition (Medium-term Tailwind)**
- **Regulatory Drivers:** EU energy efficiency directives, India's HVAC/cooling standards, EV thermal management
- **Tempsen Exposure:** Electrical heating solutions for industrial process optimization; thermal sensors critical for battery management (EVs)
- **Growth Opportunity:** EV thermal management sensors (growing 15%+ CAGR) could add ₹50-100 Cr revenue by FY2029

**3. Acquisition Consolidation & Margin Expansion (Near-term Catalyst)**
- **Marathon Heater Integration:** FY2025-26 saw initial contribution; FY2027-28 should see margin improvement as integration costs normalize
- **Expected Margin Lift:** 50-100 bps EBITDA margin expansion as Marathon (18-20% margin) benefits from Tempsen distribution network
- **Synergy Potential:** Cross-selling heating solutions to temperature sensing customer base could add ₹50-75 Cr revenue with 200+ bps margin uplift

**4. Export Ramp & Geographic Diversification (Medium-term Tailwind)**
- **Current State:** 28.52% of FY2026 revenue from outside India
- **Target:** 35-40% of FY2028 revenue from exports (implied 40-50% export revenue growth)
- **Execution Levers:** GmbH/Polska footprint provides European distribution; JVs in Korea/Indonesia support APAC growth
- **Regulatory Support:** RoDTEP, Duty Drawback, GST export refunds provide cost advantage for international sales

**5. Specialised Cable Expansion (Niche Tailwind)**
- **Market Trend:** Fire-resistant & Low-smoke cables growing 10-12% globally (defense, aerospace, data centers)
- **Tempsen Offering:** Established supplier of specialty cables (₹1,531 Cr FY2026; 34.71% of revenue)
- **Growth Runway:** Infrastructure buildout (PM Gati Shakti, Railway modernization) + data center boom could add ₹150-200 Cr revenue by FY2028

**6. Government Sector & Defense/Aerospace (Emerging Tailwind)**
- **Market Opportunity:** Government contracts (defense, space, railways) valued at ₹100+ Cr opportunity (per RHP disclosures)
- **Tempsen Capability:** Established supplier to government entities (ISRO, Defense, Power generation)
- **Growth Potential:** Government spending on infrastructure/defense expected to grow 8-10% CAGR through FY2028

### **Management DNA Evolution**

- **From Niche Supplier → Integrated Solutions Provider:** Marathon Heater + GmbH/Polska acquisitions show intent to build vertically integrated platform
- **Strategic Orientation:** Bolt-on M&A strategy (asset-light; leverage existing customer base + distribution)
- **Capital Allocation:** Conservative capex (₹40-50 Cr annual); focus on organic growth + selective acquisitions
- **Innovation Focus:** R&D for IoT/smart sensors, wireless monitoring systems (emerging product lines)
