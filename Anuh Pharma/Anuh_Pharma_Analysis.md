## 1. Business Model Overview

Anuh Pharma Ltd., incorporated in 1960 and manufacturing since 1989, is one of India's largest producers of Macrolide (Erythromycin family) and Anti-TB Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), and a major player in Anti-bacterials, Anti-malarials, Anti-hypertensives, Corticosteroids, Anti-diabetics and Expectorants. *(per Nov2025/Aug2026 Investor Presentation)*

- **Group structure:** Part of the INR 8.5bn SK Group (~2,200 employees), which also runs pharma formulations, trading, distribution and logistics businesses, largely for large MNC brands *(per Aug2026 concall)*.
- **Manufacturing:** Single facility at Tarapur (Boisar, Palghar dist., Maharashtra), spread over 11,400 sq. meters, comprising 9 API blocks + 2 intermediate blocks, with total installed capacity of **2,400 MTPA** as of FY26 (up from 2,200 MTPA a year earlier). The site is USFDA / EU GMP / WHO pre-qualified, with **zero Form 483 observations** at both its 2019 USFDA inspection and its February 2026 USFDA re-inspection, and again at the EU GMP inspection completed in FY26 *(per May2026 & Aug2026 concalls)*.
- **R&D:** Dedicated 10,000 sq. ft. DSIR-approved R&D centre at Mahape, Navi Mumbai, staffed by 16 scientists; R&D assets of Invent Pharma (Spain) were acquired in 2012.
- **Revenue mix:** ~46% of FY26 revenue came from exports (down from ~55% in FY25), sold to 360 marketing partners/customers across 72+ countries (up from 59 countries a year earlier) including Europe, Mexico and South Africa *(per May2026 & Aug2026 concalls)*.
- **Product portfolio (12 therapeutic categories):** Macrolides (Erythromycin salts), Higher Macrolides (Azithromycin), Corticosteroids (15 molecules), Anti-TB (Pyrazinamide, Isoniazid), Anti-diabetic (Gliclazide, Vildagliptin, Dapagliflozin), Anti-malarial (Sulfadoxine, Pyrimethamine, Amodiaquine), Anti-bacterial (Chloramphenicol), Antibiotic (Sulfadimethoxine), Anti-gout (Allopurinol), Quinolones (Moxifloxacin), Expectorant (Ambroxol HCL), Anti-histamine, Anti-asthmatic.
- **Product mix (Q1FY27 revenue, Rs Cr):** Erythromycin 49.1 (vs 63.6 Q1FY26), Higher Macrolides 39.9 (vs 19.7), Sulphadoxine 37.0 (vs 11.0), Corticosteroids 20.1 (vs 20.9), Pyrazinamide 12.2 (vs 10.3), Gliclazide 11.9 (vs 5.1), Ambroxol HCL 7.9 (vs 8.4), Chloramphenicol 4.0 (vs 12.2), Other items 10.9 (vs 34.7) *(per Aug2026 concall)*. This shows a clear mix shift away from legacy Erythromycin/Chloramphenicol volumes toward Higher Macrolides and Sulphadoxine.
- **Balance sheet:** Debt-free (Debt/Equity 0.00–0.03x across FY22–FY26), current ratio 2.22x (FY26), consistent dividend payer (Rs 15.03 Cr FY26, ~37% payout ratio) *(per May2026/Aug2026 concalls)*.
- **Management:** Chairman Arun Todarwal, Vice Chairman Bipin N. Shah, Joint MDs Ritesh Shah (marketing) and Vivek Shah (manufacturing/R&D), CFO Darshan Rampariya. Promoter & Promoter Group holding steady at 71.80–71.82% through the year; Public holding 28.18–28.20%.

**Trajectory read-through:**
- Margins bottomed in Q1–Q2 FY26 (EBITDA ~7%, PAT ~4%) on management's own admission of "lower sales of certain high-margin products," "non-recurring maintenance expenses," and "adverse currency movements" *(per Nov2025 concall)*.
- A sharp recovery followed in Q3FY26 (EBITDA margin 10.60%) coinciding with the USFDA re-inspection (zero 483s, Feb 2026) and improving pricing.
- Q4FY26 saw a partial margin give-back (9.22% EBITDA) attributed to "global market challenges during Q4" *(per May2026 concall)*.
- Q1FY27 shows renewed and broader-based margin recovery: EBITDA margin 9.99%, PAT margin 5.89%, PAT +37.63% YoY and PBT +54.55% YoY — even as revenue grew just **+3.93% YoY** (Rs 193.81 Cr vs Rs 186.48 Cr). Management explicitly frames this as a "deliberate shift towards higher-value products in regulated markets," where "profit growth [is] running well ahead of revenue" *(per Aug2026 concall — Message from the Joint MDs)*.
- Production volumes: FY26 total production 1,733 MT (+24.99% YoY) against 2,400 MTPA capacity (~72% utilisation); Q1FY27 production 491 MT (+19.46% YoY), an annualised run-rate of ~1,964 MT (~82% utilisation) — evidence of rising operating leverage behind the margin recovery.

No new brownfield/greenfield site has been announced; growth strategy is explicitly volume/mix-led plus "selective inorganic growth opportunities" *(per May2026 concall)*, not major fresh capex.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Regulated-market pivot.** Exports fell from ~55% of revenue (FY25) to ~46% (FY26), and export customer concentration eased sharply — the top-5 export customers' share of sales fell from 55% (Q1FY26) to 30% (Q1FY27), while Europe's share of export revenue rose from 19% (FY25) to ~24–30% across recent quarters. Management now states the "regulated-market pipeline is now our primary engine of growth, with returns strengthening without added leverage" *(per Aug2026 concall)*.
2. **Product-mix upgrade.** Revenue is shifting from legacy, lower-margin volume drivers (Erythromycin base, Chloramphenicol) toward higher-value Higher Macrolides (Azithromycin) and Sulphadoxine — visible in the Q1FY27 product-mix table above — a mix shift management credits for margin expansion even as headline revenue growth has moderated.
3. **Pipeline graduating into commercial molecules.** The "Products Under Development" list has shrunk from 10 molecules (Nov2025: Ticagrelor, Linagliptin, Empagliflozin, Pyronaridine Tetraphosphate, Vonoprazan Fumarate, Ethambutol, Piperaquine Phosphate, Dapagliflozin Amorphous, Pretomanid, Bedaquiline Fumarate) to just 4 by Aug2026 (Vonoprazan Fumarate, Pretomanid, Edoxaban Tosylate, Bisoprolol Fumarate), with several of the dropped molecules (Vildagliptin, Dapagliflozin, Amodiaquine) now appearing in the core commercial Product Portfolio — indicating successful pipeline-to-commercial conversion, and continued addition of new complex-chemistry molecules (Edoxaban Tosylate — anti-coagulant; Bisoprolol Fumarate — beta-blocker/anti-hypertensive, added in Q1FY27).
4. **Regulatory track record.** Zero Form 483 observations across USFDA (2019, Feb 2026) and EU GMP (FY26) inspections is a differentiator that supports the regulated-market pivot and customer-acquisition story.
5. **Debt-free balance sheet with dry powder for inorganic growth.** Management has flagged pursuit of "selective inorganic growth opportunities alongside disciplined capital expenditure" *(per Feb2026 concall)* — an underused lever given zero net debt.
6. **Guidance has been raised, not cut**, despite margin volatility: steady growth guidance moved from "15–16% per annum" (Nov2025/Feb2026 decks) to **"15–20% per annum"** (May2026/Aug2026 decks) — even though Q1FY27 revenue growth (+3.93% YoY) is running below this range, which bears watching (see Red Flags).

### Probability Shifters

**Bull probability ↑ if:**
- Full-year FY27 revenue growth re-accelerates into the guided 15–20% range (i.e., H2FY27 makes up for the soft Q1)
- EBITDA margin sustains above 10.5% for 2+ consecutive quarters (vs. Q1FY27's 9.99%)
- Regulated-market (US/EU) export share climbs further and top-5 customer concentration keeps easing
- New pipeline molecules (Edoxaban Tosylate, Pretomanid, Vonoprazan Fumarate, Bisoprolol Fumarate) commercialize on schedule
- A value-accretive inorganic acquisition is executed using the debt-free balance sheet

**Bear probability ↑ if:**
- Revenue growth stays below 5% YoY for two or more consecutive quarters
- EBITDA margin slips back below 8% (FY26 full-year level)
- Cash conversion cycle continues lengthening beyond 86 days, straining free cash flow
- INR volatility / adverse forex swings recur, as flagged in the Q2FY26 concall
- Loss of a top-5 customer or an adverse regulatory (Form 483) finding at a future inspection

### Investment Verdict

**Rating: BUY (at last disclosed CMP of ~Rs 78/share), reverting to ACCUMULATE/HOLD above ~Rs 85–90**

The probability-weighted fair value of **~Rs 90/share** sits ~13% above the last disclosed market price of Rs 78 (10-Aug-2026), placing the stock in BUY territory on this framework. The call is finely balanced, however — a 13% discount is only modestly above the 10% BUY threshold, and the thesis rests heavily on the Base Case (50% probability) assumption that FY27 revenue growth reaccelerates from Q1's soft +3.93% YoY print toward the company's own 15–20% guidance, even as margin gains (EBITDA 9.99%, PAT margin 5.89% in Q1FY27) prove durable rather than another false dawn like the Q3FY26 → Q4FY26 sequence.

- **Bull Case (25% probability, Rs 124.8/share):** Requires the regulated-market/higher-margin mix shift to broaden into genuine volume acceleration — i.e., FY27 revenue growth actually lands in the guided 15–20% band while EBITDA margin holds above 10.5%. New pipeline molecules (Edoxaban Tosylate, Bisoprolol Fumarate) would need to start contributing, and export customer diversification would need to continue. This would justify a re-rating toward 20x earnings.
- **Base Case (50% probability, Rs 87.0/share):** Most likely path — moderate revenue growth (~10%) as H2FY27 partially offsets the soft Q1, with margins holding in the 9–10% EBITDA range (roughly Q1FY27's level, well below the FY24 peak of 13.48% but clearly above the FY26 trough of 8.56%). This scenario broadly matches the stock's current trading multiple.
- **Bear Case (25% probability, Rs 59.9/share):** Revenue growth stalls near mid-single digits (extrapolating Q1FY27), FX/global-market headwinds re-emerge as they did in Q4FY26, and the cash-conversion-cycle deterioration (86 days and rising) starts to pressure free cash flow — triggering a de-rating to a "commodity API" multiple of ~14x.

Given the closeness to the BUY/ACCUMULATE boundary, investors should treat Rs 78 as an attractive entry only with conviction that Q1FY27's margin recovery is structural (regulated-market mix shift) rather than transient (as the Q3→Q4 FY26 pattern suggests is possible) — and should re-underwrite the call once FY27 Q2 results and updated CMP data are available.

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

1. **Quarterly revenue growth rate** vs. the company's own 15–20% p.a. guidance — Q1FY27's +3.93% YoY is the key data point to watch for a reversal.
2. **EBITDA margin trajectory** — watch for a sustained move back above 10% (vs. Q1FY27's 9.99%, FY26's 8.56%, and the FY24 peak of 13.48%).
3. **Cash conversion cycle / working-capital days** — currently 86 days (FY26) and rising each year since FY23; a further increase would be a genuine cash-flow red flag.
4. **Export vs. domestic mix and geographic mix** — track continued Europe/regulated-market share gains vs. Asia/Africa within exports, and the ~46% export share for further decline (currency-risk reduction).
5. **Top-5 customer concentration** (both export and domestic) — currently ~34–36% of total sales; watch for further easing (diversification) or re-concentration (risk).
6. **Pipeline-to-commercial conversion** — progress of Edoxaban Tosylate (anti-coagulant), Pretomanid (Anti-TB), Vonoprazan Fumarate (anti-ulcerative) and Bisoprolol Fumarate (beta-blocker) toward commercial launch.
7. **Regulatory inspection outcomes** — track record is zero Form 483 observations to date (USFDA 2019 & Feb 2026, EU GMP FY26); any adverse finding would be a major red flag.
8. **ROE / ROCE trend** — FY26 lows of 12.10%/15.89% vs. FY24 peaks of 22.50%/26.82%; Q1FY27 ROCE recovered to 19.18% (+326 bps vs FY26) — watch for continuation.
9. **Capacity utilisation** — production volumes (FY26: 1,733 MT, ~72% utilisation of 2,400 MTPA; Q1FY27 annualised: ~82%) — further utilisation gains support operating-leverage-driven margin expansion without new capex.
10. **Any inorganic (M&A) announcements** — management has flagged pursuit of "selective inorganic growth opportunities" using the debt-free balance sheet.
11. **Updated market cap / CMP** — this analysis's CMP reference (Rs 78/share, 10-Aug-2026) is dated; refresh before acting on the entry-point table.
