## 1. Business Model Overview

**Company Profile**
- Inventurus Knowledge Solutions Limited (trading as IKS Health)
- Listed on NSE (IKS) and BSE (544309) in December 2024 (~7 months post-IPO at time of analysis)
- Headquarters: Thane, Belapur Road, Navi Mumbai
- Registered office: 801, Building No. 5 & 6, 8th Floor, Mindspace Business Park (SEZ)
- Workforce: 13,331+ employees (5% growth YoY), ~1,981 clinically trained staff, 550+ technologists
- Leadership: Sachin Gupta (Founder & Global CEO), Nithya Balasubramanian (Whole-Time Director & CFO)

**Business Model: AI-Native Healthcare Services Platform**

1. **Core Mission**: Delegating chore tasks from US healthcare providers to IKS platform
   - TAM: $260 billion US healthcare market
   - Outsourced Services TAM: ~$35 billion (growing at 12% annually)
   - IKS growing faster than 12% = gaining market share
   - Revenue model: Outcome-based (percentage of customer revenue improvement)

2. **Three Genres of Competition in $260 Bn TAM**:
   - **System of Record Companies** (Legacy EHR vendors): Epic, Cerner/Oracle, Athena, Allscripts, NextGen, eClinicalWorks – handling patient data storage & core workflows
   - **Point Solution Systems of Action**: Ambient AI scribing (Abridge, DAX, Ambience Healthcare), revenue cycle focused (Med-Metrix, CorroHealth) – solving 1-3 specific tasks
   - **Comprehensive Platform Systems of Action** (IKS position): Integrated solutions addressing 16+ interconnected workflow tasks, targeting full platform deployment model

3. **16-Odd Features of IKS Platform** (tracks autonomy achievement for each):
   - Clinical documentation (Scribble, Scribble Select, Scribble Now)
   - Medical coding (autonomous coding, Scribble Select variants)
   - Revenue cycle management (RCM platform system of action)
   - Prior authorization
   - Denials management
   - Patient access & engagement (MyCare Hub)
   - Scheduling optimization
   - Patient financial clearance
   - Claims processing
   - Eligibility verification
   - Payer interactions
   - Referral management
   - Care coordination
   - Predictive analytics
   - Performance reporting
   - Custom workflow integrations

4. **Customer Segmentation Strategy**:
   - **Large Health Systems** (>$5B revenue): Full platform play, land with RCM/coding/documentation features, expand to other capabilities over time (Palomar, WWMG, top 5 health systems)
   - **Mid-Sized Health Systems** ($1-5B): Point solution entrees (RCM features, coding), gradually move to fuller platform
   - **Independent/Single Specialty Groups**: Point solution focused (coding, RCM), limited platform scope
   - **Rural Healthcare** (TruBridge): Platform system of action for 0-50 bed hospitals, special capability set

5. **Outcome-Based Economics Model**:
   - Pay structure: Percentage of customer revenue or EBITDA improvement achieved
   - First deal (Palomar Health): $16.5M net economic value add in first full year from platform implementation
   - Customer benefits: 700-1,000 EBITDA basis point improvement potential (~6-10% EBITDA uplift)
   - Value creation: Reduced cost of chore tasks, improved quality, freeing provider time for patient care

6. **Technology Stack**:
   - AI Platform: Generative AI + Neuro-Symbolic AI (glass-box AI with explainability)
   - Agentic AI: Multi-agent orchestration for complex workflows (MyCare Hub, scheduling, patient engagement)
   - LLMs: Internal proprietary models built with ARAI team, reduced reliance on external LLMs
   - Knowledge Graphs: Ontologies for clinical reasoning
   - Integration: Epic Connection Hub, native EHR integration (not API-based; direct workflow orchestration)

7. **Five Key Strategic Pillars**:
   - **Agentic AI Platform**: Evolution from human-led tech-in-loop (2010s) → tech-led human-in-loop (2015-2020) → AI-native agentic platform (2022-2026+)
   - **AQuity Integration**: Transform human-led US RCM delivery model to tech-led, offshore-enabled model (acquisition Nov 2023, integration ~complete by Q3 FY26)
   - **Differentiated Go-to-Market**: Large systems (platform), mid-size (point + eventual platform), independent groups (point solutions), rural healthcare (platform via TruBridge)
   - **AI-Driven RCM**: Black Book #1 in AI-driven RCM, KLAS top performer in ambulatory RCM, recognized by Google as one of best AI users in healthcare
   - **True North Vision**: Rural healthcare market opportunity via TruBridge acquisition ($162B market, 2,200 hospitals, 30% US population, lower competition)

## 4. Key Catalysts & Growth Drivers

**1. Platform Adoption Acceleration** (6-12 month runway)
- **Historical Challenge**: Large health systems prefer point solutions → IKS forced to go-to-market with best-in-class features in each category (RCM, coding, documentation)
- **Q3-Q4 FY26 Turning Point**: After AQuity integration + product maturity, seeing first significant platform deals (Palomar, WWMG, top 5 health system deal announced Q4)
- **Evidence**: Palomar implementation delivered $16.5M net economic value in first year; customer now expanding to full platform
- **Opportunity**: $260B TAM; if 30% can be served by integrated platform (vs point solutions), that's $78B TAM expansion opportunity
- **Timeline**: 2026-2027 will see inflection point where platform deals > point solution deals

**2. AQuity Cross-Sell Completion** (Q1-Q3 FY27 runway)
- **Legacy AQuity Base**: ~600 RCM-only customers
- **Tail Cutting**: Exited small, unprofitable customers (inherited at acquisition) → base now ~450 large enterprise customers
- **Cross-Sell Motion**: Started Q3 FY26, accelerating Q4-Q1 FY27
- **Realistic TAM per Customer**: $700-1,000 EBITDA basis point opportunity per customer (based on Palomar model)
- **Revenue Potential**: If cross-sell to 50% of 450 customers with average $16-20M contract value = $3.6-4.5B additional TAM over 3 years
- **Execution Risk**: Moderate (proven model, customer relationship foundation exists, product-market fit demonstrated with Palomar)

**3. TruBridge Integration & Scale** (FY27-FY30 multi-year)
- **Acquisition Close Expected**: Q2 FY27 (90-120 days from April 24 announcement)
- **Year 1 (FY27)**: Finalize close, begin EHR modernization, start RCM platform cross-sell pilot to 50-100 hospitals
- **Year 2-3 (FY28-29)**: Accelerate RCM platform adoption across TruBridge base (200-300 hospitals), expand other IKS platform features
- **Year 4-5 (FY30-31)**: Mature integration, achieve platform system of action model across majority of TruBridge customer base
- **Financial Impact**:
  - TruBridge standalone: $347M revenue, $69M EBITDA (20% margin)
  - Post-integration target: $500M+ revenue, $150M+ EBITDA by FY30 (30% margin)
  - IKS earnings accretion: Immediately EPS accretive at close (internally estimated 5x interest coverage, nearly 5x debt paydown interest charge from internal accruals)

**4. Agentic AI Monetization** (FY27-FY28 runway)
- **Product Evolution**: Moving from ambient AI scribing (Scribble) to fully agentic workflows (MyCare Hub, autonomous coding, scheduling)
- **Customer Willingness**: Early indication from pilots that autonomous agents reduce manual tasks by 40-60% (vs scribing's 30-40% manual reduction)
- **Pricing Power**: Outcome-based model allows scaling revenue with value delivered (not headcount)
- **Timeline**: By FY28, expect 40-50% of new deals to include agentic workflows; by FY30, 80%+ adoption
- **TAM Expansion**: Agentic orchestration opens adjacencies (patient engagement, payer operations, supply chain) historically outside IKS scope

**5. Rural Healthcare Market Penetration** (FY27-FY30 multi-year)
- **Market Size**: $162 billion, growing 12% annually, highly fragmented (2,200 hospitals, mostly 0-50 beds)
- **Competitive Position**: TruBridge already #1 EHR player in segment (40% share); adding IKS platform system of action = near-monopoly position
- **Willingness to Pay**: Rural hospitals under severe margin pressure (reimbursement rates lower, operations less efficient); value proposition of 700 basis point EBITDA lift is compelling
- **Runway**: Barely scratched surface (30% penetration on RCM alone); room for 3-5x revenue expansion in segment over 5 years

**6. AI Competitive Moat Deepening** (Ongoing FY27-28)
- **Proprietary Training Corpus**: Building AI training data from 150,000+ provider workflows, 15M+ TruBridge patient records
- **Neuro-Symbolic Models**: ARAI-developed models combining generative + symbolic AI for clinical accuracy (vs GenAI-only solutions prone to hallucination)
- **Small Language Models (SLMs)**: Building proprietary SLMs tuned to healthcare tasks (cheaper to run, faster inference than external LLMs)
- **Competitive Advantage vs Claude/ChatGPT**: IKS SLMs will be domain-specialized, require no external API calls (data stays in-house), lower cost per transaction
- **Timeline**: SLMs ready for production by late FY27; expect cost per task to drop 30-50% by FY28

**7. Regulatory Tailwinds & Adoption Acceleration** (External FY27+)
- **Medicare Advantage Pressure**: Biden administration re-calibrating MA rates; providers need efficiency tools to maintain margins → demand for IKS platform accelerates
- **AI in Healthcare Regulation**: FDA guidelines on AI in healthcare just emerging; IKS positioned as leader (Black Book #1 in AI-driven RCM, KLAS top performer)
- **Interoperability Push**: ONC (Office of National Coordinator) pushing EHR interoperability; IKS well-positioned as orchestrator between systems
