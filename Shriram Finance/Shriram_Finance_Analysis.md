# Shriram Finance Limited — Equity Analysis

## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** Shriram Finance is India's leading diversified non-bank financial company (NBFC), providing retail credit across vehicle financing, farm equipment, MSME lending, and gold loans. Post-2022 merger of Shriram Transport Finance Company (STFC) and Shriram City Union Finance (SCUF), the company operates as India's largest vehicle and asset-backed NBFC by AUM.

**Business Segments (FY27 Q1 Disbursement Mix):**
- **Commercial Vehicles (CV):** ₹19,556 Cr (39% of disbursements) — M&HCV, LCV financing to operators
- **Passenger Vehicles (PV):** ₹11,018 Cr (22%) — Increasing focus on new vehicle loans to existing customers
- **MSME:** ₹6,184 Cr (12%) — Working capital & equipment financing for small businesses
- **Gold Loans:** ₹5,153 Cr (10%) — Collateralized lending against jewelry
- **2-Wheelers:** ₹3,548 Cr (7%) — Retail two-wheeler financing
- **Farm Equipment & Tractors:** ₹947 Cr (2%) — Mechanization financing
- **Construction Equipment:** ₹792 Cr (2%)
- **Personal Loans:** ₹2,773 Cr (6%) — Unsecured lending to existing customers

**Geographic & Operational Footprint:**
- **3,225 branches** across India with pan-national reach
- **76,000 employees** (FY26)
- Post-merger integration complete with multi-product activation across branches
- Strong presence in auto financing (CV, PV, 2W) with deep penetration in used vehicle market

**Strategic Alliance (Transformative):**
- **MUFG Bank Investment (April 2026):** 20% stake acquisition for ₹396.18 Cr
  - Issue price: ₹840.93 per share (47.11 Cr shares allotted)
  - Total capital infusion: ~$4.4 billion USD (~₹36,500 Cr equivalent)
  - Elevates Shriram to institutional-grade capitalization
  - 2 board seats for MUFG; access to treasury, capital market, funding solutions

**Financial Structure:**
- **Listed entity on BSE & NSE** (Scrip: 511218 / SHRIRAMFIN)
- Strong balance sheet with healthy leverage and capital ratios post-MUFG deal

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. **MUFG Capital Infusion (Transformative Structural Catalyst)**
- **₹396 Cr capital at ₹840.93/share** provides 100 bps reduction in cost of funds over 2 years
- **Strategic access:** MUFG's digital platform (used in Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia), treasury solutions, capital markets support
- **Credibility signal:** Institutional investor validation elevates Shriram's credit rating (expected upgrade)
- **Growth acceleration:** Enables shift from 16-17% to 18-20% AUM growth

### B. **NIM Expansion & Cost of Funds Arbitrage**
- Current NIM 9.04% (Q1 FY27) — highest in 3-year trajectory
- Post-MUFG, borrowing cost expected to fall 100 bps over 2 years → enables NIM to stabilize at 8.5%+ medium-term
- **Flywheel effect:** Lower cost → can match competitor rates on new vehicles → retain customers → higher volumes → leverage improves ROE

### C. **Vehicle Financing Market Shift (GST Rate Cuts)**
- **Post-GST reduction (28% → 18%):** Vehicle prices down ~10% for OEMs, creating demand surge
- **Q1 FY27 OEM sales:** CV +14.1%, PV +25.9%, 2W +20.3%, 3W +29.7%, Tractors +21.6%
- **New vehicle market opportunity:** Shriram targeting 6% of new vehicle market (from 3% currently) → 2x growth from existing customer base upgrade

### D. **Portfolio Mix Optimization**
- **Gold loans:** Fastest-growing segment; targeting 2.5% → 5% of AUM over 3 years (from 2,200 activated branches)
- **MSME expansion:** New vehicle-like opportunity; regional network now complete post-merger; expect MSME to shift from 15% → 20% of AUM
- **Personal loans:** Growing within existing customer base (non-predatory model); opportunity to cross-sell to gold & MSME customers

### E. **Asset Quality & Credit Cost Advantage**
- **Gross Stage 3: 4.58-4.64%** — stable despite retail-heavy portfolio (CV operators, individual borrowers)
- **Net Stage 3: 2.33%** — best-in-class due to collateral (vehicles, gold, property)
- **Credit cost guidance: <2%** — sustainable as company has deep penetration, field executives, recovery infrastructure
- **Competitive moat:** Small operators prefer Shriram's human touch + flexible repayment over impersonal banks

### F. **EV Transition & Two-Wheeler Growth**
- **EV sales surge:** PV EVs +94.8%, 2W EVs +69.3%, 3W EVs +13.2% in Q1 FY27
- **Opportunity:** Shriram positioned to finance EV buyers (existing customer base + new entrants)
- **Risk:** Lower residual values on used EVs (not yet quantified)

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### **Quarterly Tracking (Each Results Release)**

1. **AUM Growth & Mix**
   - Overall AUM CAGR (target: 18% FY27, 15-18% FY28)
   - Segment-wise: CV (target: 14-18%), PV (20-25%), MSME (15-20%), Gold (30%+), 2-wheeler (10-15%)
   - New vehicle % of PV book (target: 20-25% by FY27-end, 30%+ by FY28-end)
   - Watch for: PV or 2-wheeler softness; MSME growth stalling

2. **Profitability & Margins**
   - **NII growth %:** Target 15%+ CAGR FY27-28
   - **NIM %:** Watch quarterly trajectory (currently 9.04%, expect normalized 8.5-8.7% by FY27-end)
   - **Cost-to-Income ratio:** Target 25-27% (currently 25.48% in Q1 FY27)
   - **PAT growth:** Should match NII growth + cost efficiency
   - Red flag: NIM compression > 50 bps sequentially or CI ratio >28%

3. **Asset Quality**
   - **Gross Stage 3:** Target stay <4.7% (currently 4.64%)
   - **Net Stage 3:** Target stay <2.5% (currently 2.33%)
   - **Credit cost ratio:** Maintain <2% annual guidance (currently 1.66%)
   - **Slippage rate:** Monitor Stage 1→2→3 flows (high slippages = early warning)
   - Watch for: CV slippages (if operator stress), 2-wheeler slippages (if discretionary spending falls), MSME slippages (if US tariff impact spreads)

4. **Capital & Leverage**
   - **Leverage ratio:** Monitor quarterly (target: 2.5-3.5x; currently 2.14x post-MUFG)
   - **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** Maintain >30% (currently 34.1%)
   - **Cost of funds:** Track quarterly decline toward 100 bps reduction by FY28-end
   - **Incremental cost of borrowing:** Target sub-7.5% (currently 7.77% in Q1)

5. **Customer & Operational Metrics**
   - **Number of customers:** Currently 9.66 Cr; expect 10-11 Cr by FY27-end (from 3,225 branches)
   - **Branches:** Track net additions (target: 300-500 annually)
   - **Employees:** Currently 76,000; expect 80,000+ for expansion
   - **DSA (Direct Selling Agents):** Track quality and compensation inflation

6. **Funding & Liquidity**
   - **Liability profile:** Retail deposits (target: 26%+), ECB (13-15%), Securitization (16%+)
   - **Liquidity coverage ratio:** Monitor (currently 262.54%; target: 200-250%)
   - **Access to capital markets:** Monitor ECB/bond market receptiveness
   - **Refinancing risk:** Low given diverse sources, but monitor if borrowing costs spike

### **Annual Strategic Milestones (FY27-FY28)**

- **By Q3 FY27 (Dec 2026):** AUM should reach ₹335-345 Cr (on track for 18% FY27 growth)
- **By Q4 FY27 (Mar 2027):** Confirm FY27 guidance met; provide FY28 outlook
- **By Q1 FY28 (Jun 2027):** NIM normalizes to 8.7-8.9%; new vehicle % of book visible

### **Red Flag Early Warnings**

- **Q-o-Q AUM growth falls below 10%** (vs. 15%+ guidance) → demand slowdown
- **NIM compression >75 bps from peak** → cost of funds not falling as expected
- **Gross Stage 3 exceeds 5.0%** → credit stress emerging
- **Quarterly credit cost >2.2%** → deterioration acceleration
- **Debt growth >10% QoQ without corresponding AUM growth** → refinancing pressure
- **CV slippages spike (>5% annual rate)** → operator stress real
- **Cost-to-Income ratio >28%** → operational efficiency deteriorating
- **Major customer defection or OEM partnership loss** → competitive positioning weakening

## Summary

**Shriram Finance** is a **high-growth, capital-efficient financial services company** at an inflection point:

**Catalysts & Tailwinds:**
- MUFG capital infusion (structural competitive advantage)
- 18% AUM growth targeting (achievable given strong Q1 start)
- NIM expansion (9%+) in near-term
- New vehicle market opportunity (GST cuts driving demand)
- Gold loan growth (multi-year 30%+ opportunity)

**Headwinds & Risks:**
- Monsoon deficit (rural portfolio exposure)
- Geopolitical tensions (fuel costs, consumer sentiment)
- New vehicle execution risk (competitive market)
- Portfolio mix shift diluting margins medium-term

**Recommendation:** Best suited for **growth-oriented investors** seeking 18%+ AUM growth, margin expansion, and capital efficiency improvement. Strong capital position post-MUFG removes financing constraints. Monitor quarterly execution against 18% FY27 guidance and credit quality (target <4.7% gross Stage 3). Fair value estimate ~₹900-950/share for FY27-end earnings with 15x P/E multiple = **8-13% upside** from ₹840.93 MUFG entry price.
