## 1. Business Model Overviex`w

Fractal Analytics Limited is a 26-year-old pure-play enterprise AI company. It helps large global corporations make better decisions by embedding AI into their business workflows — pricing, supply chain, inventory, logistics, product strategy, and customer experience. This is not IT staffing or generic consulting; Fractal is explicitly positioned as an AI-first firm that has been doing this before "AI" was a buzzword.

**Vision:** "Power every human decision in the enterprise."

**What they do:** Fractal connects AI directly to enterprise data and workflows, helping companies automate and augment decisions at scale. Revenue comes from two distinct streams:

### Two-Segment Structure

| Segment | Description | Revenue Mix | Gross Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fractal.ai (Core Services) | Bespoke AI solutions built for enterprise clients — data science, ML, agentic AI deployments | ~90%+ | ~47% (blended) |
| Fractal Alpha (Products) | Proprietary AI platforms and software — Asper, Analytics Vidhya, Cogentiq | ~10%+ | Growing; segment at slight loss, rapidly closing |

**Must Win Client (MWC) Strategy:**
Fractal targets a specific tier of mega-enterprises that meet one of: >$10B annual revenue, >$20B market cap, or >30M end customers. These are companies like PepsiCo, Coca-Cola, global banks, pharma companies, and technology giants.
- MWCs as of Dec 2025: **127** (up from 113 in March 2025)
- MWC revenue share: **83%** of total revenue (up from 81% in FY25)
- Clients >$1M annual revenue: 58 (up from 53)
- Clients >$20M annual revenue: 6 (up from 5)
- Target: generate at least $1B of measurable impact for each MWC client

*Per Q3FY26 concall*

Fractal Alpha grew 51% YoY in 9M FY26. Gross margin expanded 276 bps. *Per Q3FY26 concall.*

### Cogentiq — Agentic AI Platform (Key Strategic Bet)

Cogentiq is Fractal's answer to Palantir — an ontological + agentic layer on top of frontier AI models (GPT-5, Claude Opus, Fathom, etc.) that reimagines enterprise workflows. Key characteristics:
- Model-agnostic: works with any underlying foundation model
- Every new underlying model improvement automatically improves Cogentiq performance (no model-layer competition risk)
- Priced on license/output basis — not token consumption
- Human-in-the-loop architecture to solve the agent accuracy/drift problem that limits enterprise AI adoption
- Two "preferred supplier" statuses with Magnificent Seven companies recently secured

### R&D Investment Framework (Three Pillars)

1. **Fractal.ai productivity**: PiEvolve (ML agent), internal tools like Pitch Dark (sales AI) and Iqigai (AI hiring) — reduces SG&A cost structurally
2. **Cogentiq platform**: Best-in-class agentic AI for enterprise workflow reimagination
3. **Vaidya / India AI Mission**: Healthcare foundation model; if selected for India AI mission deployment, access to 1.4B population feedback loop — potential frontier AI research value

R&D spend: ~6-8% of revenue total; ~4.1% expensed directly through P&L (creates 4 percentage point EBITDA drag vs. peers who have no AI R&D).

### Microsoft Partnership
Won Microsoft Partner of the Year 2025 (Retail & Consumer Goods category) — secured preferred supplier status with 2 of the Magnificent Seven (large US tech companies). Validates enterprise AI positioning. *Per Q3FY26 concall.*

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### A. Enterprise AI Adoption — Secular Tailwind

Enterprise AI adoption is still "somewhat moderate" per management because AI accuracy doesn't yet consistently exceed human performance in every workflow. As AI model quality improves (GPT-5, Claude Opus etc.), the cost-benefit for enterprise deployment dramatically improves. Fractal — positioned at the intersection of domain expertise + AI research + enterprise data access — is uniquely placed to capture this acceleration. Every improvement in frontier models benefits Fractal's clients without Fractal needing to rebuild its stack.

### B. Healthcare & Life Sciences — Fastest Growing Vertical at 78% YoY

Healthcare and life sciences grew 78% YoY in Q3FY26. This is driven by:
- Vaidya 2.0: first model to score 50+ on OpenAI HealthBench (Hard) — gives Fractal technical credibility in a regulated, high-stakes domain where generic AI players cannot compete
- Strategic investments in healthcare capabilities over prior years now converting to revenue
- India AI Mission alignment: if Vaidya becomes part of the national healthcare AI backbone, it creates a massive data and distribution advantage

### C. Output/Outcome Contracts — Structural Margin Expansion

The shift from time-and-material (T&M) → output-based → outcome-based contracts is the most important structural margin driver:
- Output contracts: Fractal controls the full project; internal productivity tools (PiEvolve, Pitch Dark, etc.) improve output quality without increasing cost — directly expands gross margin
- Management is actively converting existing T&M relationships to output/outcome models
- Gross margin expanded 17 bps in Q3 (115 bps attributable to engagement mix shift) — this is an ongoing structural trend
- The only companies that can underwrite output-based contracts are those with genuine AI capability and domain expertise — raises barriers to entry

### D. Fractal Alpha Approaching Profitability

Fractal Alpha (Asper + Analytics Vidhya + Cogentiq products) has been a drag for years. With segment losses falling from ₹54 Cr → ₹44 Cr → ₹26 Cr → ₹10 Cr (9M FY26), Alpha is near breakeven. When Alpha crosses to profitability:
- The loss drag disappears from blended P&L
- These are high-gross-margin software revenues
- Analytics Vidhya (69% growth) could emerge as a significant recurring revenue stream
- Asper is growing 43% and already serving CPG/retail brands directly

### E. SG&A Leverage + AI-Led Cost Reduction

Fractal is aggressively deploying its own AI tools to reduce selling and G&A costs:
- **Pitch Dark**: Sales preparation AI — reduces meeting prep from days to minutes; improves win rates
- **Iqigai**: AI-driven end-to-end recruiting — job description, candidate screening, 360-degree assessment, matching — automates hiring process
- SG&A has already declined significantly as % of revenue over last 3 years. As revenue scales, operating leverage further improves. *Per Q3FY26 concall.*

### F. ESOP Charges Declining — Direct PAT Tailwind

ESOP charges (stock-based compensation) have fallen from 9.9% of revenue in FY23 to 2.3% in 9M FY26. As a recently listed company with a mature ESOP program, this drag continues to decline. Every 1% improvement in ESOP as % of revenue = ~₹32-40 Cr PAT improvement at current scale. This is a mechanical PAT margin expansion catalyst.

### G. New Client Additions Accelerating

8 percentage points of Q3FY26 revenue growth came from new clients added in the last 12 months. New client engine is healthy. NRR of 114-115% means existing clients are expanding spend — every new client adds to a compounding base.

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

| Risk | Detail | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **CPG/Retail Tariff Exposure** | Largest vertical (36% revenue) directly hit by US tariff uncertainty starting April 2025 ("Liberation Day"). CPG clients paused spending, reducing growth to 14% vs 25%+ in other verticals. As long as US trade policy is uncertain, this headwind persists. Without CPG drag, overall growth would have been ~25-26%. | 7/10 |
| **Qure.ai Associate Losses** | Fractal owns 31.5% of Qure.ai, an AI healthcare startup. Under IndAS, Fractal must proportionately book Qure losses. In Q3FY26, Qure losses cost Fractal ₹19 Cr (2.2% of revenue) vs ₹3 Cr (0.4%) in Q3FY25 — driven by USAID funding cuts. This directly suppresses reported PAT and is outside Fractal's control. | 7/10 |
| **Below-Historical Growth Rate** | Fractal has grown at 29-30% CAGR for last 5-10 years. Current run rate is 20-21% YoY. Management attributes this primarily to CPG tariff headwinds, but if the deceleration is structural (rising competition from IT services players pivoting to AI, or from Palantir-type platforms), the premium valuation is at risk. | 6/10 |
| **Client Concentration Risk** | 83% of revenue from 127 MWC clients. While large enterprises are sticky, individual client restructurings directly show up in revenue (Australia telecom, US tech client restructuring contributed to -2% in TMT segment). Only ~1% overall churn per year, but individual client events can be material for a specific quarter. | 5/10 |
| **R&D Spend Drag on EBITDA** | ~4.1% of revenue is expensed as R&D (total budget 6-8%). This makes Fractal's reported EBITDA look ~4 pts lower than pure services peers. If AI commoditizes rapidly and R&D does not translate into revenue acceleration, this is wasted expense. The bet is that R&D builds moats (Cogentiq, Vaidya, PiEvolve). | 5/10 |
| **Frontier Model Competition** | OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft are all building enterprise AI capabilities directly. If they develop plug-and-play enterprise deployment that doesn't require Fractal's services, the addressable market shrinks. Management argues Cogentiq is complementary (ontological layer), not competitive, and compares to Palantir — but this risk must be monitored. | 6/10 |
| **New IPO — Limited Public Track Record** | This was Fractal's FIRST earnings call as a public company. Investors have only one quarter of disclosed public data. Valuation is based on historical growth promises and management credibility. If execution stumbles in the first few public quarters, the stock could re-rate significantly. | 6/10 |
| **Forex Volatility** | Revenues are substantially in USD/EUR; costs are substantially in INR. Rupee depreciation helps revenue, but exceptional forex items (₹22 Cr loss in Q3FY26) can swing PAT materially. Fractal appears partially hedged but still exposed. | 4/10 |
| **Talent/Headcount Risk** | Enterprise AI quality depends on top-tier data scientists and AI researchers. Competition for talent from US tech firms (direct India hiring), Indian startups, and domestic IT giants is intense. Revenue per head growing slowly (+2% in USD) could reflect pricing pressure or workforce mix dilution. | 5/10 |
| **Fractal Alpha Execution** | Cogentiq, Asper, Analytics Vidhya, Vaidya — all simultaneously being scaled. If any product fails to gain traction, it represents sunk R&D. Asper in particular depends on CPG clients — same vertical that's under tariff headwinds. | 4/10 |
