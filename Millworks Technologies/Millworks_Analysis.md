## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** Millworks Technologies manufactures **precision-engineered metal fabricated components** for critical defence, aerospace, and industrial sectors. The company is a **B2B supplier to OEMs and defence PSUs**, operating under NIC codes 25999 (metal fabrication), 32909 (miscellaneous manufacturing), and 30305 (aircraft/spacecraft parts).

**Primary End Markets:**
- **Aerospace & Defence** (~75% estimated): Aircraft parts, UAVs/drones, defence systems (primary growth driver)
- **Railways & Metro Rail** (~15% estimated): Precision components for Indian railway infrastructure
- **Allied Engineering & Industrial** (~10% estimated): General precision engineering for automotive, industrial equipment

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Unit 1:** No.458/1, 10th A Cross, Peenya Phase-4 (Registered Office + Assembly)
- **Unit 2:** B-165, 4th Main Road, Peenya 2nd Stage (Manufacturing Facility)
- **Unit 3:** Plot 270/2, 3rd Main Road, Peenya Phase-4 (Manufacturing Facility)
- **Location Advantage:** Peenya Industrial Area (Bangalore) = proximity to Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (HAL), Bangalore-based defence contractors, and IT hubs

**Business Model Characteristics:**
- **Project-based revenue model** with multi-year contracts
- **High precision, low-volume production** (contrasts with mass-manufacturing economics)
- **Vertically integrated:** In-house CNC machining, assembly, quality testing
- **Quality certifications likely:** ISO 9001 (implied); defence/aerospace likely requires AS9100 or equivalent
- **Technology focus:** Modern manufacturing practices, CNC machines, process efficiency

**Revenue Concentration Risk:** Disclosed related-party transaction with **V3 Technologies** (loan of ₹55 Cr at 8% pa with Vidwan Aeronautics); suggests **customer concentration** among defence/aerospace PSUs.
