## 1. Business Model Overview

Smarten was founded in 2014 by four ex-Su-Kam/Delta power-electronics engineers. It **designs and assembles** power backup and off-grid solar electronics under the "Smarten" brand and sells them through a distributor → dealer channel that is concentrated in North India. Smarten also **manufactures lead-acid batteries** (Baddi, HP plant acquired FY26) and **trades white-labelled solar panels**. Management calls the core proposition a "solar power generating system": inverter/PCU + battery + panel (*Jan-2026 call*).

### Product segments and revenue mix

| Segment | Products | Share of revenue | Source / comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Power electronics** (core, highest margin) | Home UPS (Bravo, Nova, Everon; 500 VA–20 kVA), solar PCUs (MPPT/PWM), solar charge controllers, on-grid inverters (3–25 kW), new hybrid / 2-in-1 grid-tie inverters, smart RMS inverters | **~50–60%** | 50–55% *(Jan-2026)*, 60% *(Mar-2026)*. Management says electronics carry the best margins. |
| **Batteries** | Tubular and solar tall-tubular lead-acid (20–350 Ah, made at Baddi); lithium-ion packs (12.8–51.2 V, assembled/traded) | **29.1%** (₹59.43 Cr in H1FY27) | FY24 25.3% → FY25 31.7% → FY26 30.1% → H1FY27 29.1% *(Nov-2026 PPT)*. ~90% of batteries sold are lead-acid. |
| **Solar panels** (pure trading) | Mono-PERC / poly, 40–600 W, bought in and white-labelled | **~10–20%** | "15–20%, will continue" *(Jan-2026)*. 10% *(Mar-2026)*. |
| Services | AMC / service income | <1% | ₹0.99 Cr FY26 *(AR FY26)* |

- **Manufacturing vs trading:** **60% assembled / 40% traded** in H1FY27 *(Nov-2026 PPT)*. Of the 60% manufactured, ~70% is electronics and ~30% is batteries. Management expects this to move to ~70:30 as Baddi ramps up *(Nov-2026 call)*. Trading margins are ~7–8%. In-house batteries earn ~12–15% *(Nov-2026 call; "margin" is undefined, probably contribution)*.
- **Asset-light assembly model:** PCB assembly (SMT), injection moulding and sheet metal are **outsourced**. Smarten keeps design, BOM, PCB testing, final assembly, QC and after-sales in-house. This explains how it generates ~₹200 Cr of revenue on ~₹1 Cr of inverter plant and machinery *(Mar-2026 call)*. It also means low entry barriers (see Red Flags).

### Channel / distribution, B2B vs B2C

- **~99% B2B through the channel** (company → area distributor → dealer → consumer). The online D2C subsidiary **Smart Store International Pvt Ltd** had FY26 turnover of ₹16.15 Cr (6.65% of consolidated), down from ₹22.73 Cr in FY25, with PAT of ₹0.53 Cr *(AR FY26)*.
- **Network:** 427 domestic distributors in 23–24 states and 2 UTs, 32 international distributors, 44 service points, 109 field service engineers, 345 FTEs *(Nov-2026 PPT)*. BRSR data shows only **281 distributors/dealers were actually billed in FY26**, with the **top-10 at 39.4% of sales** *(AR FY26)*.
- **Geographic concentration:** **~80% of domestic sales come from Haryana + half of UP** *(Jan & Mar-2026 calls)*. ~95% of domestic revenue is from 8 North Indian states *(Nov-2026 PPT)*. Smarten claims it is "No. 1 in solar in Haryana". It will not sell where it has no 24-hour service coverage (e.g., Maharashtra).
- **Exports collapsing:** standalone exports fell from **₹25.75 Cr in FY25 to ₹11.62 Cr in FY26** *(AR FY26)*. H1FY27 exports were 5% of sales (~₹10 Cr). Smarten was "earlier in 30+ countries" and is now in 9 *(Nov-2026 call)*. A **new China subsidiary** has been set up for merchant-export (China → Africa shipments) and component sourcing *(Nov-2026 call)*.
- **Institutional / projects (nascent):** one ₹1.75 Cr government tender for 4,000 solar PCUs (Odisha Har Ghar Jal, executed through a third party). An Exide Technologies (Europe) India channel-partner agreement covers telecom, defence and data-centre batteries, with no revenue yet. Smarten is also targeting private C&I rooftop EPC (50–500 kW).

### Solar rooftop / PM Surya Ghar exposure

Exposure is **indirect**. Smarten's portfolio is skewed to **off-grid and backup solar** (PCUs, charge controllers, tubular batteries). PM Surya Ghar is a grid-tied scheme. Smarten sells 3–25 kW on-grid inverters and a 2-in-1 grid-tie inverter (grid-tie + backup without a battery), and the deck leans heavily on PM Surya Ghar (50 lakh+ homes, 5.06 lakh installs in Jul-2026). However, **no revenue is disclosed from PM Surya Ghar** and management did not quantify it on any call. The GST cut on solar systems and panels (12% → 5%) and on lead-acid batteries (28% → 18%) in FY26 is a more tangible tailwind *(Jan-2026 call)*.

**Read-through:**
- **H1FY27 is a step-change.** Revenue nearly doubled and management says **volume growth was ~80%** *(Nov-2026 call)*. About 20% of H1 revenue came from distributors added in the last year. Battery revenue alone was ₹59.4 Cr in H1, vs ₹73.0 Cr for all of FY26.
- **H2FY26 was the trough.** Gross margin fell to 19.1% and EBITDA margin to 4.0%. Management blamed a lead-price spike in Jan–Mar 2026 (Middle-East war), IPO costs and a 3-month (vs peers' 6-month) procurement horizon.
- **The opex base is scaling slower than revenue.** Employee cost +45% and other expenses +58% against revenue +78% gave **~240 bps of operating leverage**. Gross margin only returned to its historical ~22%. **Margin improvement so far is operating leverage, not a mix or backward-integration gain.**
- **Seasonality:** the Feb–Sep summer and stocking season is strongest and Oct–Dec is weak *(Jan-2026 call)*. Management repeatedly claims "H2 ≥ H1". That held in FY26 (127 vs 115) but not in FY25 (~94 vs ~108). Its own FY27 guidance of ₹375 Cr implies **H2FY27 of only ~₹170 Cr (below H1's ₹205 Cr)**.

**Capex:** FY26 capex was ₹18.8 Cr (Baddi plant, land and Jhajjar building). Remaining spend is ~₹2–3 Cr of Jhajjar payables plus ₹0.5–0.6 Cr at Baddi, about **₹3.5 Cr in total for the next year**. **No further major capex** is planned *(Nov-2026 call)*. Depreciation was ~₹1.1 Cr/half and should rise to ~₹3–3.5 Cr/yr once Jhajjar is capitalised.

**Backward integration:**
- **Batteries:** Baddi plant + Su-Urja supply line. 40% of the company's revenue is still traded. The "40% → 100% in-house" slogan in the PPT refers to electronics procurement of semi-finished goods.
- **Transformers:** in-house winding has started.
- **Lithium:** Smarten will only *assemble* cell packs (cells imported). It is easy to enter but offers no moat.
- **Not planned:** panel manufacturing, PCB SMT and plastics.

**Implied revenue capacity:** at 1,200 units/day in electronics plus 11,000 batteries/month, the asset base can support the ₹500+ Cr FY28 ambition on a single shift. **Demand and channel expansion, not capacity, is the binding constraint**, as management itself says.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **From a regional Haryana/UP brand to a multi-state player.** 80% of domestic sales still come from ~1.5 states. Punjab, Rajasthan, J&K, Uttarakhand, South and East India are in the "introduction phase", with service engineers hired ahead of sales. Distributors grew from 327 to 427. New distributors contribute ~20% of their potential in year 1 and mature in year 2, so **FY28 should benefit from the FY27 distributor cohort.**
2. **Battery backward integration.** Battery is ~30% of revenue and was historically traded at ~7–8% margin. Owning Baddi (12–15% margin) and raising capacity from 8,000 to 11,000 units/month should lift consolidated gross margin by ~100–200 bps over FY27–FY28. **Not yet visible:** H1FY27 gross margin was flat YoY at 22.3%.
3. **Jhajjar plant (2x capacity, owned).** It removes ~₹2.4 Cr/yr of rent plus multi-site logistics and admin costs, and adds automation. Management guides **+1–3 pp EBITDA margin in FY28** *(Nov-2026 call)*.
4. **Product shift to grid-tie, hybrid and smart RMS.** In-house hybrid inverters are in field trials, with mass production in "1–2 months". The 2-in-1 grid-tie inverter (runs loads without a battery when the grid fails) and IoT/RMS inverters enable AMC/service revenue. This positions Smarten for time-of-day tariffs and storage-mandated rooftop schemes. New products earn margin before commoditisation.
5. **Tailwinds:** GST cuts on lead-acid batteries (28% → 18%) and solar systems and panels (12% → 5%). PM Surya Ghar rooftop momentum (record 5.06 lakh installs in Jul-2026). PM-KUSUM farm solar. Rising evening peak loads.
6. **Institutional optionality.** This covers government PCU tenders (Odisha repeat inquiries of 10–15k units), the Exide Technologies (Europe) distributorship for telecom, defence and data centres (₹15–20 Cr first-year target), and private C&I rooftop EPC. None of these contributes meaningfully yet.

## 7. Investment Verdict & Key Metrics to Monitor

### Weighted Fair Value & Entry Recommendation

**Weighted FV: ₹146/share (₹145.7) vs CMP ₹85.8 (12-Nov-2026) → 41% discount → BUY** (BUY zone < ₹131; ACCUMULATE ₹131–146; HOLD ₹146–153; REDUCE > ₹153).

### Bull Case (20%): what needs to go right → ₹274.5
- The H1FY27 surge proves structural: FY27 ≥ ₹410 Cr and FY28 ≥ ₹540 Cr as Punjab, Rajasthan, J&K and South/East states scale and the FY27 distributor cohort matures.
- Jhajjar starts in Q4FY27 on schedule. Rent and logistics savings plus Baddi at 11k/month lift EBITDA to 10%.
- Hybrid, 2-in-1 grid-tie and RMS inverters win share in the storage-mandated rooftop market. Exide/projects add a second leg.
- Cash conversion turns consistently positive and the market re-rates the stock to ~14x.

### Base Case (55%): most likely path → ₹143.7
- Management's ₹375–380 Cr FY27 is achieved. FY28 grows ~20% to ₹460 Cr (below management's ₹525 Cr) as normal seasonality returns.
- EBITDA margin creeps up from 8.0% to 8.75%. PAT ₹27 Cr in FY28.
- Working capital stays heavy but self-funded. No dilution.
- The stock is valued at ~10x forward earnings, a discount kept in place by governance and cash-conversion concerns.

### Bear Case (25%): what could go wrong → ₹47.2
- H1FY27 was channel stocking and a GST/season bulge. H2FY27 and FY28 revert to ~5–10% growth.
- Another commodity shock plus competition from Luminous/Microtek/lithium all-in-ones pushes margins back to ~6%. PAT falls to ~₹13 Cr.
- Jhajjar adds fixed costs before volumes arrive. CFO turns negative again. The multiple compresses to ~7x.

### Rating: **BUY** (weighted FV ₹146 vs CMP ₹85.8; expected return +70%)

Valuation is undemanding: ~11x TTM and ~8x base FY27E, with net cash/no debt and the first year of post-IPO earnings power visible. That said, this is a **high-risk, small-position SME BUY**:
- The bear case is −45%.
- Cash conversion has been poor for four years.
- The company has a record of loose disclosures (IPO-use claim, Jhajjar progress, Exide naming, distributor and export counts).
- The stock has doubled from its lows and sits at a 52-week high.

Size accordingly, and preferably build the position in tranches around the H2FY27 print (May-2027), which is the real test of whether the H1 surge was structural.
