## 1. Business Model Overview

### Company Profile

Modisons Limited is a leading manufacturer of **electrical contacts and contact materials** catering to low-voltage (LV), medium-voltage (MV), high-voltage (HV), How and extra-high-voltage (EHV) switchgear industries. Founded in 1965 as a trading enterprise for tool steels and engineering goods, the company pivoted into silver refining (1975) and then electrical contacts manufacturing (1977), establishing its core business for nearly five decades.

### Business Segments

**Primary Product Lines:**
- Silver and silver alloy wires, plates, and strips
- Solid and bimetal contacts for switchgear applications
- Copper-based and tungsten-based contact materials
- Specialty contacts for automotive, aerospace, railway, and marine applications

**Revenue Mix (FY26):**
- Domestic switchgear OEMs: ~65-70% (primary customer base)
- Export markets: ~11.5% of revenue (₹8,216 Lakhs in FY26, up 5.79% YoY)
- Industrial and power transmission: ~20-25%

### Customer Base

The company supplies to a diversified but concentrated customer base:
- Power distribution utilities and transmission companies
- OEM switchgear manufacturers (ABB, Siemens, Schneider, domestic manufacturers)
- Automotive and EV infrastructure manufacturers
- Railways, aerospace, marine, defense, and engineering sectors
- Pharmaceutical and industrial automation sectors

*Key Risk:* Significant customer concentration—a limited number of OEM customers drive majority of revenue (*per AR FY26*).

### Raw Materials & Supply Chain

The company relies on **precious and base metals:**
- **Silver** (~62% of global electrical contacts market by value)
- **Copper** (~28% of market)
- **Tungsten and specialty alloys** (remaining share)

Manufacturing capability includes precision metallurgy, alloy formulation, coating technologies, and quality control for high-performance, arc-resistant contacts.

### Geographical Footprint

- **Manufacturing:** Vapi, Gujarat (GIDC, Phase-I; multiple plots: 85A, B, D, E, 2923, 2924 A&B)
- **Corporate Office:** 33 Nariman Bhavan, 227 Nariman Point, Mumbai
- **Export Markets:** Europe (historically strong), US, select Asian markets

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### Structural Industry Tailwinds

**1. India's "Decadal Upcycle" in Power Equipment (JP Morgan Report, Apr 2026)**
- India transitioning to renewable energy with 500+ GW targets by 2030
- Transmission infrastructure modernization and smart grid deployment accelerating
- Estimated ₹3-5 lakh crores capex in power sector over next 5-7 years
- Switchgear market growing at 5.88% CAGR through 2034 (*IMARC Group Report*)

**2. Electrification & Urbanization**
- Rural electrification programs (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana)
- Urban infrastructure (metro rail, smart cities, data centers) requiring advanced switchgear
- Electricity demand expected to grow 5-6% CAGR through 2030

**3. Electric Vehicle (EV) Adoption**
- EV infrastructure surge creating demand for high-performance contacts in battery management, charging stations, power relays
- EV segment alone contributed 51% growth in electrical contact materials demand (Precision Reports, Apr 2026)
- Expected 30%+ CAGR in EV-related contact demand through 2035

**4. Global Supply Chain Shift & Make-in-India**
- Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes encouraging domestic electrical equipment manufacturing
- International OEMs (ABB, Siemens) investing in India to reduce China dependency
- Export opportunity: Modison already has established presence in Europe and US; room for 3-5x export growth

**5. Technological Advancement in Contact Materials**
- Rising adoption of silver alloys and nano-coated contact materials for superior performance
- Premium pricing power for advanced materials (15-20% price premium over commodity contacts)
- Modison positioned as quality leader; opportunity to shift mix toward high-end applications

### Company-Specific Catalysts

**1. Post-Fire Operational Optimization**
- Insurance recovery (₹1,632.95 Cr outstanding) will improve FY27 margins by 2-3%
- Facility rebuild with modern equipment → 15-20% productivity gain

**2. Export Market Expansion**
- Currently 11.5% of revenue; target to reach 20% over next 3 years
- Europe & US switchgear market larger and more lucrative than domestic
- Capacity to absorb 30-40% export growth without major capex

**3. Margin Expansion Through Mix Shift**
- Shift from commodity silver contacts (8-10% EBIT margin) to specialty/automotive contacts (15-18% EBIT margin)
- Automotive and EV OEMs willing to pay 10-15% premium for certified contacts
- Each 100 bps mix shift toward specialty = 50-60 bps EBITDA margin expansion

**4. Working Capital Optimization**
- Current cash conversion cycle: ~45-50 days (reasonable for industry)
- Inventory turnover: 3.41x (FY26); opportunity to improve to 4x+ through supply chain optimization
- Receivables turnover: 5.77x; opportunity to tighten credit terms with smaller OEMs

**5. Vertical Integration with Modison Copper**
- Related party Modison Copper manufactures precision copper components
- Integration opportunity: 10-15% cost reduction on raw material through backward integration
- Transaction limit ₹8,000 Cr approved for FY26-27; suggests 3-5 year strategic roadmap
