## 1. Business Model Overview

Cryogenic OGS designs, makes and tests equipment used at **custody-transfer points**: the points where ownership of fuel or gas passes from one party to another, so the metered quantity becomes the basis for invoicing, duties and royalties. These measurements are regulated under legal-metrology rules and standards such as API MPMS and OIML R117 (*per H1FY27 presentation, Nov 2026*). The company started in 1997 doing job work for ISRO and the Institute for Plasma Research, which is where the "Cryogenic" name comes from, and moved into oil and gas in 2008-09 (*per Jan2026 Valueportal call*).

### How the company sells

- **Customers are mostly automation and EPC contractors, not oil companies directly.** Cryogenic is on the approved vendor lists of IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, ONGC and EIL, but orders come through the 5–6 terminal-automation integrators in India: Honeywell, Emerson, Endress+Hauser, Yokogawa, ABB and Advanced Systek. Direct end-user revenue is "very less" (*per Jan2026 Valueportal call*).
- **Top-10 customers make up 89% of revenue** (*per Jan2026 Valueportal call, citing the AR*).
- **Catalogue products** (air eliminators, basket strainers) are installed at more than 200 IOCL, BPCL and HPCL terminals, and management claims an **80–90% domestic share** in them. They are replaced roughly every 10 years (*per Jan2026 Valueportal call*).
- **The turnkey shift is the core of the thesis.** Earlier, clients supplied the high-value parts (flow meters, valves, transmitters) free of cost, so Cryogenic billed only about ₹30 of every ₹100 skid. In the new model it buys and integrates everything and bills the full value. Honeywell Nigeria was the first full-scope project and was completed in FY26 (*per Jan2026 Valueportal call; FY26 presentation, May 2026*). Management says margins hold because it chooses the specifications during design and gets better OEM prices than MNC integrators do.
- **Exports:** Egypt (143 truck-loading skids for an end user via Emerson/Honeywell), Libya (11 metering skids for Emerson), Nigeria (Honeywell), and an LNG metering skid for Honeywell USA. Earlier end users include Schlumberger, Dangote Refinery and ConocoPhillips.

ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001; DNV fabrication certificate; **EIL approval for piping spools (Jan 2026)**; **ADNOC vendor approval for metering skids and strainers (25 May 2026)**; IBR workshop approval (valid to Sep 2028); ASME U-stamp still "advanced", not granted (*per H1FY27 presentation, Nov 2026*).

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Fabricator → turnkey systems integrator.** Billing the full skid value (meters, valves, instruments) instead of only fabrication could lift revenue per skid by up to about 3x (management's ₹30-of-₹100 example). This is the main reason revenue grew 44% in H1FY27 while margins held. The risk is that absolute margin per job rises but the percentage margin may fall as the bought-out content grows.
2. **Export and GCC access.** ADNOC approval (May 2026) and the Dubai FZE allow direct bidding in the GCC, where management says margins are better than domestic. The ₹19.36 Cr export piping-spool order and the Egypt, Libya, Nigeria and USA projects show a growing export base.
3. **New product verticals on existing assets:**
   - **Piping spools:** EIL-approved, no capex, export order already won. This could be a large addressable market (refinery and petchem projects).
   - **aDENS density probes:** import substitution. Management says no Indian maker exists, and Cryogenic holds the PSU approval in its own name.
   - **Meter runs for ultrasonic-meter OEMs:** a catalogue-like product that recurs with every meter sold and has better payment terms.
   - **H₂ / propane / methane and LNG skids:** ₹5.08 Cr order (Sep 2026). Earlier LNG skids went to Konkan LNG, giving a first-mover position in small-scale LNG truck loading.
4. **Infravolt (51%):** diversification into solar-inverter and railway-traction busbars. It is fast-scaling (₹9.74 Cr in about 5 months) but margin-dilutive (~9% EBITDA) and so far depends on a single customer (Fimer).
5. **Domestic refining and gas capex cycle.** Refining capacity is expanding from 258 MMTPA to about 310 MMTPA by 2030, gas's share of the energy mix is targeted at 15%, and CGD and LNG-for-transport are being built out (*FY26 presentation, May 2026*). New terminals need metering skids, strainers and air eliminators.
6. **Operating leverage before the capex.** Utilisation is about 35–40% on the existing plant, so revenue can roughly double before the big capex. Depreciation is only about ₹0.9 Cr a year.

## 7. Investment Verdict

### Weighted Fair Value & Entry Recommendation

**Weighted FV ₹364 vs CMP ₹374 (+2.8% premium) → HOLD.** For fresh money, a better entry is below **₹328 (BUY)** or ₹328–364 (ACCUMULATE). Above **₹382** it moves into REDUCE, and the stock is only about 2% below that level.

### Bull Case (25%): What Needs to Go Right (FV ₹630)

The turnkey model becomes the default for Honeywell, Emerson and E+H skids. Piping spools become a second engine on both EIL-floated domestic projects and export EPC work. ADNOC approval converts into GCC orders at better margins. Infravolt broadens beyond Fimer. Revenue reaches about ₹125 Cr in FY28 with an attributable PAT margin of about 18%, giving ₹22.5 Cr PAT and EPS of about ₹15.8. The market pays 40x for a debt-free, high-ROE energy-transition equipment play, and phase 1 of the new plant is built from internal cash.

### Base Case (50%): Most Likely Path (FV ₹347)

O&G grows about 40% in FY27 and about 20% after that, with H1-heavy and lumpy halves. Infravolt adds volume at thin margins. Consolidated EBITDA margin settles around 25% and PAT reaches ₹16.5 Cr in FY28 (EPS ₹11.55). The capex need in FY28–29 absorbs much of the IPO cash. A 30x multiple, still a premium to fabricator peers, gives about ₹347.

### Bear Case (25%): What Could Go Wrong (FV ₹132)

After the Egypt and spool orders, H2FY27–FY28 sees an order gap as H2FY26 did. Fimer volumes normalise, receivables and advances tie up cash, and margins compress with pass-through content. PAT stagnates at ₹10–11.5 Cr. As SME momentum reverses, the multiple de-rates to about 18x, giving about ₹132 (−65%).

### Rating: **HOLD** (CMP ₹374; weighted FV ₹364; BUY below ₹328)

This is a good small business: ~30% operating margins, net cash, 20%+ ROE, PSU and ADNOC approvals, and a credible fabricator-to-integrator story. It is also **fully priced**: ~44x TTM and ~32x base-case FY28E. Management has a record of over-promising on near-term revenue (H2FY26), and disclosure is thin (no concall, no cash flow, no segment margins). Hold existing positions, and add only on dips toward ₹300–330 or after a clean H2FY27 (revenue of ₹32 Cr or more with receivables normalised).
