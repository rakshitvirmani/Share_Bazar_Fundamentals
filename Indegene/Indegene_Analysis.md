## 1. Business Model Overview

**What Indegene does:** It is a specialist commercialisation and medical-services partner for life-sciences companies. Its clients are large and mid-size pharma and biotech companies, mostly in the US and Europe. It sells to Chief Marketing Officers, Chief Medical Officers and brand teams, not to IT departments. The work covers content creation and production, omnichannel physician engagement, medical information and medical writing, regulatory and safety documentation, and clinical operations support. Delivery runs largely on proprietary GenAI-enabled platforms (Cortex, Invisage, Tandem/Audience Intelligence).

**Segment Mix (Q2 FY27)** *(per Q2 FY27 concall)*

| Segment | % of Revenue | What it covers | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise Commercial Solutions (ECS)** | ~71% | Content (Tectonic, Agentic AOR, production), omnichannel orchestration, brand activation, BioPharm (data-driven HCP promotion) | Fastest-growing. Most of the deal wins |
| **Enterprise Medical Solutions (EMS)** | ~25% | Medical information, medical writing, regulatory and safety | Slower in the next few quarters after a strong base (+16% in FY26) |
| **Others (incl. Clinical)** | ~4% | Clinical operations, consulting | Jumped on a large clinical deal that ramped from Aug 2026 |

**Geography:** North America 74%, Europe 23%, Rest of World 3%. BioPharm's acquisition increased the US share.

**Pricing model:** About **60%+ of revenue is output- or outcome-based**, not FTE/time-and-materials *(per Q1 FY27 concall)*. All but one of the Q2 FY27 wins were output/outcome-priced. FTE contracts are being converted to hybrid structures at renewal.

**Productivity:** TTM revenue per employee was **US$79,600**, up from ~$69,000 a year earlier and ~$51,000 a few years ago. Management calls this industry-leading. Implied headcount is about 5,500–5,700, our estimate from TTM revenue of ~$445M.

**Competitive Landscape:** Indegene competes mainly with **pharma creative agencies and CROs**, not IT services companies. Management "doesn't see" TCS and similar firms in its deals. Its edge is domain depth, embedded workflows, proprietary physician data (3.6M+ HCP profiles after Tandem) and GenAI tools that turn agency work taking months into days. Frontier AI labs are seen as partners who need a "last mile" provider, not as competitors *(per Q2 FY27 concall)*.

**Acquisitions (FY26):** BioPharm (US, ~$65M plus earn-out, effective 1 Oct 2025, "sub-10x EBITDA"), CultHealth, MJL and Cake (agency and creative capabilities). FY26 acquisition outflows totalled ₹725 Cr *(per Q4 FY26 concall)*.

### Key FY26 Facts *(per Q4 FY26 concall)*

### What H1 FY27 Tells Us
1. **Growth has stepped up.** Five straight quarters of 5%+ QoQ growth in INR. Q2 USD growth was +27.5% YoY. Taking out BioPharm (~$10–11.5M a quarter, fully inorganic until Q2 FY27), **organic USD growth is roughly 15%**, ahead of FY26's ~12% organic. *(Our estimate; management no longer splits out BioPharm.)*
2. **Margins are at the bottom of the cycle, as flagged.** EBITDA was 16.7–16.9%, against the historical ~19–20%. Reasons: (a) go-to-market and technology investments announced in Aug 2025 (~150 bps), (b) costs of the $10M outcome-based omnichannel deal and Tectonic incurred ahead of revenue, (c) a Q2 wage hike (~150 bps), offset by productivity (~85 bps) and favourable FX.
3. **PAT growth is lagging revenue.** Q2 PAT was +10% YoY against revenue +39.5%. The gap comes from acquisition amortisation (D&A rose from ₹23 Cr to ₹40 Cr a quarter after the acquisitions) and lower treasury income. Interest income fell from ₹23.7 Cr to ₹13.4 Cr QoQ because mutual-fund NAVs were marked down when rates rose.
4. **Working capital is slightly worse.** Net DSO went 63 → 67 → 69 days, and unbilled revenue jumped. Management blames summer confirmation delays and milestone billing on outcome contracts.
5. **Cash has been drifting lower:** ₹1,539 Cr, then ₹1,460 Cr, then ₹1,390 Cr. Likely causes are the dividend, the BioPharm earn-out and working capital. This needs watching.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

**A. Industry demand is turning up *(per Q2 FY27 concall)*.** Pharma and biotech funding is up ~85% YoY. Biopharma M&A roughly doubled. FDA approvals are at a record pace (45 novel approvals YTD), and priority vouchers shorten reviews to 1.5–2 months. There are about 12,700 molecules in development (2.5x 2015), with late-stage and pre-approval up ~30%. Over $400B of revenue faces patent expiry in the next decade. Large pharma is moving from fixed in-house costs to flexible partners and consolidating vendors, which favours scale players.

**B. Margin recovery in H2 FY27 (the near-term catalyst).** Target is **19%+ quarterly EBITDA by Q4 FY27**, against 16.7% in Q2. Drivers:
- Wage hike already absorbed in the base
- **$10M ACV outcome-based omnichannel deal**: costs have been running since Q4 FY26, and revenue arrives mostly in **Q4 FY27**, so most of it "flows straight to the bottom line"
- Workforce transformation and productivity (~85 bps a quarter)
- About 75% of the next two quarters' FX exposure is hedged at favourable rates
- Below EBITDA: amortisation down ₹5 Cr a quarter from Q3 FY27 (another ₹2.5 Cr a quarter from Q3 FY28), and treasury yields recovering by moving to held-to-maturity instruments. Together that is **+90–95 bps at PBT**, so PAT margin should expand faster than EBITDA margin.

**C. Moving upstream: Agentic AOR displacing creative agencies.** A pilot with a top-20 pharma company, where Indegene ran a 3-day agentic concepting workshop against the incumbent agency, became a full engagement worth over $1M. Indegene is now the **primary agency** for that launch, with enterprise-level talks underway. Management says "first in the industry, nobody is close". If this scales, Indegene moves from production vendor to strategic agency, which is a much larger pool of client spend.

**D. Large-deal momentum** *(per Q2 FY27 concall)*:
- **9 deals above $1M ACV** in Q2. These include one $5M+ deal: a top-5 client's content-platform migration outside the US, extending the Q1 US mandate and ramping in Q4. There were two $3–5M deals: a top-20 pharma global content factory that grew out of a sub-$1M change-management engagement, and a top-30 pharma global digital content deal **replacing a large incumbent**.
- An emerging US pharma launch client added $3M+ of work orders.
- Q4 FY26 had 1 deal at $3M+ and 7 at $1M+. Q3 FY26 brought the $10M+ multi-year outcome-based omnichannel deal.

**E. Client pyramid compounding.** $1M+ clients rose from 41 to 59 in about 18 months, and active clients from 73 to 107. Management describes the land-and-expand model as working "one tier down" and faster with GenAI. The $25M+ client that slipped should recover in 2–3 quarters on the $5M+ win.

**F. BioPharm as an enabler.** Its data and targeting capability is now an edge in the largest omnichannel pursuits in the pipeline. BioPharm is growing more than 15% (earn-out trigger), against flat growth before the acquisition.

**G. Shift to outcome pricing.** In a GenAI world where hourly billing is breaking down, Indegene's 60%+ outcome/output-based book means productivity gains stay with Indegene instead of being passed on as price cuts.

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

| Risk | Severity (1–10) | Description & Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **GenAI Deflation / Business-Model Disruption** | **7** | AI cuts the cost of content and medical writing sharply. Clients may demand price cuts: in the CY26 renewals Indegene **gave "certain benefits on price in lieu of volume or scope expansion"** *(Q3 FY26)*. Frontier labs (e.g. Anthropic pharma partnerships) and clients' in-house AI could disintermediate parts of the work. **Mitigation:** outcome-based pricing, proprietary data, workflow embedding, a "last-mile" partnership stance. **Watchpoint:** revenue growth from top-20 clients and renewal pricing. |
| **Client Concentration & Largest-Client Stall** | **6** | Top 10 is 49% of revenue. The largest client (7.8%) was flat in FY26 and **eased in Q2 FY27**. The FY27 hope of a first $50M+ client looks unlikely. One $25M+ client slipped below. **Mitigation:** active clients 107 and $1M+ clients 59. Revenue outside the top 20 is about one-third and doubled YoY. |
| **Margin-Recovery Execution / Back-Ended H2** | **6** | The 19%+ Q4 target depends heavily on a single $10M outcome-based contract whose revenue needs a **full-year outcome threshold** met and client data lock in Feb/Mar. A miss would push recovery into FY28 and undercut credibility. The target has already moved from "~20%" to "19%+", and "sequential improvement from Q1" was not delivered. |
| **Tectonic Adoption Lag** | **5** | Contracts are signed but not used at the largest client because of change-management friction between central and local brand teams. Costs are carried ahead of revenue. Management: "will take its own time". |
| **Acquisition & Integration Risk** | **5** | Four acquisitions in FY26 (BioPharm, CultHealth, MJL, Cake). Amortisation weighs on PAT through FY28. More M&A is likely given the cash. **Mitigation:** BioPharm integrated early, synergies delivered, bought at sub-10x EBITDA. |
| **Pharma Industry Restructuring / US Policy** | **5** | Large-pharma cost programmes, US drug-pricing policy and patent cliffs can cut discretionary marketing spend. The flip side is vendor consolidation and outsourcing, which favour Indegene. North America is 74% of revenue. |
| **Working Capital / Unbilled Revenue** | **4** | Net DSO rose from 63 to 69 days and unbilled revenue jumped in Q2. Outcome contracts bring milestone billing and timing swings. FY26 cash conversion was excellent (OCF 162% of PAT). |
| **Treasury Income Volatility** | **3** | Mutual-fund MTM hit Q2 PAT by ~70 bps. Mitigated by shifting to held-to-maturity instruments. |
| **FX** | **3** | 97% of revenue is in USD/EUR, so INR depreciation helps. About 75% of exposure two quarters ahead is hedged. Hedge accounting adopted from Jan 2026. Q4 FY26 had a ₹24 Cr MTM charge before adoption. |
| **Legal / Compliance** | **2** | The 2020 TCPA class action has been settled (₹20.3 Cr provided), and fax outreach is no longer used. No other material exposures disclosed. |
| **Segment Slowdown: Medical** | **3** | Commercial will outgrow Medical "for the next few quarters". Medical faces higher automation risk in writing and med-info. |

**Summary Risk Profile:** A high-quality, cash-rich, debt-free compounder in a margin trough it chose to enter. The main near-term risk is **execution of the H2 margin recovery**, which leans on one outcome-based contract. The main long-term debate is **GenAI deflation versus GenAI share gain**.

## 7. Investment Verdict & Key Metrics to Monitor

### Verdict: **ACCUMULATE** at ₹615 (Weighted FV ₹662, +7.6%; BUY below ₹596)

**Bull Case (25%): ₹929.** The pharma upcycle (funding, approvals, launches) meets Indegene's GenAI-led move upstream into agency work. The $10M outcome deal and Tectonic scale, the largest client becomes a $50M account, and margins go above 20%. EPS grows about 55% from FY27E to FY28E, and the stock re-rates to 30x. What needs to go right: Q4 FY27 delivers clearly and Agentic AOR becomes a repeatable enterprise product.

**Base Case (50%): ₹670.** Management hits its two FY27 goals. Revenue grows about 17% in FY28 and margins recover to ~19%. PAT grows about 35% in FY28 on top of EBITDA growth, helped by lower amortisation and better treasury yields. This matches the company's record of taking margin hits to invest and then restoring margins within 6–8 quarters.

**Bear Case (25%): ₹379.** Recovery slips, the largest relationships keep shrinking, and the GenAI-deflation argument takes hold in the market. The stock de-rates to about 18x. Net cash and a 52-week low of ₹414 provide support.

**Bottom line:** Indegene is a high-quality franchise: debt-free, ~₹1,390 Cr of cash, OCF above PAT, 107 clients, rising revenue per employee and a credible GenAI strategy. It is priced close to fair value after a strong run, just 4% below its 52-week high. The **Q4 FY27 margin print (reported in May 2027) is the key test**. Accumulate gradually, and buy more decisively on dips below ₹596 or after a clean Q4.
