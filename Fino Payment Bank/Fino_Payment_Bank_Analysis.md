## 1. Business Model Overview

**Fino Payments Bank (Established 2010)** is a digital-first, merchant-centric payments bank transitioning to a **Small Finance Bank (SFB)** following RBI's in-principle approval in December 2025. The bank operates a unique **asset-light, liability-first business model** with three revenue streams:

### Core Business Segments (FY26):

**1. CASA (Current Account Savings Account) – 38% of revenue, high margin**
- 1.75 Cr CASA customers (+22% YoY)
- Strong renewal income growth: FY26 ₹237 Cr (+25% YoY)
- Average balance per account: ₹1,314 (+9% YoY)
- Cost of funds: <2% (structural advantage)
- Subscription-based model; customers pay for banking services

**2. Digital Payments – 19% of revenue, declining**
- UPI P2M (Point-to-Merchant) through 20+ lakh merchant network
- Program managers and payment aggregators as distribution
- Digital throughput 9M FY26: ₹1.96 Lakh Cr (+31% YoY)
- **Challenged**: RMG ban (Aug 2025), regulatory scrutiny, digital payment merchant onboarding delays

**3. Transaction Business (Legacy) – 18% of revenue, structural decline**
- Cash Management Services (CMS): throughput ₹18,850 Cr (pricing pressure, take rate 0.16%)
- Remittance (AEPS/Micro ATM): declining as customers shift to digital UPI
- Once core business, now commoditized

**4. Lending Referral (Emerging, non-revenue accretive pre-SFB)**
- FY26 disbursals: ₹1,300 Cr (Q4 alone: ₹592 Cr)
- Products: Gold loans, affordable housing, LAP, MSME
- Proof-of-concept for future SFB lending book
- 95%+ gold loans; nascent affordable housing pilot

### Strategic Moats:

1. **Large Merchant Network**: 20+ lakh merchants across India (98% PIN code coverage)
2. **Low-Cost CASA Deposits**: ₹2,957 Cr at <2% cost (300 bps advantage vs traditional SFBs)
3. **Merchant-Led Sourcing**: Asset-light model through existing merchant relationships
4. **Technology**: Finacle core banking migration (₹200 Cr investment); hollow-the-core architecture; in-house AI/ML for lending
5. **Regulatory Credibility**: First payments bank to receive SFB approval; compliance-focused culture
