## 1. Business Model Overview

Anthem Biosciences is a Contract Research, Development & Manufacturing Organization (CRDMO) operating in India's biotech and pharma supply chain. The company works with global innovators, biotech firms, and large pharma to develop and manufacture active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), advanced intermediates, and specialty ingredients.

### Revenue Structure (FY26 Actuals)

**Two primary business segments:**

1. **CRDMO Business (83% of revenue, Rs. 1,773 Cr in FY26)**
   - Custom synthesis for small molecules and advanced medicinal chemistry
   - Fermentation-based biologics (biosimilars, peptides, fermentation derivatives)
   - R&D services (discovery, process optimization, scale-up support)
   - Specialty APIs (peptides, ADCs, oligonucleotides, RNAi payloads)
   - Manufacturing of approved commercial molecules (14 as of Nov 2025)
   - Late-stage development support for Phase 2/3 clinical molecules

2. **Specialty Ingredients Business (17% of revenue, Rs. 352 Cr in FY26)**
   - Fermentation-based ingredients (probiotics, enzymes, vitamin K2-7, Serratiopeptidase)
   - Nutrition and health supplements for domestic and RoW markets
   - India-focused B2B supply to generic pharma and nutraceutical companies

### Customer Profile

- Primarily large US and European biotech startups
- Emerging relationships with top-10 global pharma (added 2–3 large pharma customers in FY26–FY27)
- Approximately 130–140 early-stage programs in development pipeline
- 14 commercial molecules launched (growth from 10 at IPO time July 2025)
- Highly regulated customer base (FDA, EMA markets dominant)

### Competitive Advantages

- **Backward Integration:** Exceptional vertical integration in raw materials, intermediates, and fermentation fragments (notably in GLP-1/semaglutide)
- **Technology Platform:** Multi-modality capability (small molecules, peptides, ADCs, oligonucleotides, biosimilars, fermentation-based products)
- **Regulatory Expertise:** FDA-approved Unit-2; pursuing approvals for Unit-3 (Neo Anthem)
- **Cost Competitiveness:** Competitive against China on cost of goods, particularly in peptides and fermentation-based APIs
- **Culture & Talent:** 40% of employees on ESOP scheme; young, high-engagement workforce

## 3. Capacity Roadmap & Capex Timeline

### Current Capacity Status (As of Q1 FY27 - Jun 2026)

**Unit-1 (Bommasandra, Bangalore)**
- Custom synthesis facility: 25 KL
- Utilization: ~78%
- Status: Fully built out; no further expansion planned

**Unit-2 (Bommasandra, Bangalore - Expanded)**
- Custom synthesis capacity: 376 KL (expanded from 246 KL in FY26)
- Fermentation capacity: 140 KL
- Recent Expansion: CP6 (commissioned Q1 FY26, ~60 KL) and CP7 (commissioned Q2 FY26, ~70 KL), total ~130 KL added
- Custom synthesis utilization (existing base): ~75% as of end-FY26; Q1 FY27: ~50% (softer quarter, recovering in Q2–Q4)
- Fermentation utilization: ~46–47% as of end-FY26; Q1 FY27: ~50%
- Status: Fully built out; no further brownfield expansion scope

**Unit-3 (Neo Anthem - Bangalore)**
- Multi-modality facility (pilot scale + commercial scale)
- Facilities:
  - Peptide synthesis: 16 KL commercial scale facility (fully operational)
  - Fermentation: 2×200 L (microbial, for biosimilar development)
  - Pilot scale labs for discovery and development
  - Oncology/high-potent compounds manufacturing
  - Fermentation blocks (some still under commissioning)
- Utilization: Started at ~15% in FY26; ramped to 30–35% in Q1 FY27; expected to reach 40%+ by end-FY27
- Capex invested: ~Rs. 350 Cr gross block + additional CWIP; total planned ~Rs. 450 Cr
- Expected revenue potential (long-term): ~Rs. 650 Cr at 1.4x–1.5x asset turnover
- Status: Early ramp-up phase; not yet profitable but trending positive

**Unit-4 (Bommasandra - Greenfield Expansion)**
- Land: 30-acre parcel
- Phase 1 Scope:
  - Custom synthesis: 365 KL (vs. current 425 KL across Units 1–3 combined)
  - Fermentation: 100 KL (vs. current 180 KL across Units 1–3)
  - Specialty ingredients plant (nutraceuticals, probiotics)
  - Peptide expansion capability
- Capex: Rs. 1,000–1,200 Cr (split 50–50 between FY27 and FY28)
- Timeline: Groundbreaking commenced; civil work ongoing; target commissioning by end of FY28 (Mar 2028)
- FY27 Capex: ~Rs. 700 Cr (Unit-4 primary driver)
- FY28 Capex: ~Rs. 500 Cr (Unit-4 completion + residual)
- Future Phases: Phase 2 & 3 planned post-Phase 1 completion (could potentially triple capacity)

### Capex Outlook (Multi-Year)

- **FY27:** ~Rs. 700 Cr (primarily Unit-4 Phase 1)
- **FY28:** ~Rs. 500 Cr (Unit-4 Phase 1 completion)
- **FY29+:** Maintenance capex only; possible Unit-4 Phase 2 if order book warrants

### Asset Turnover & Revenue Potential

- Current asset turnover (Units 1–3): ~1.6x–1.7x
- Unit-2 expansion (CP6, CP7): Expected to generate ~Rs. 300+ Cr revenue on Rs. 182 Cr capex base
- Unit-3 (Neo Anthem): Expected long-term revenue of ~Rs. 650 Cr on ~Rs. 450 Cr capex base (1.4x–1.5x turnover)
- Unit-4 Phase 1: Could add ~Rs. 600–700 Cr revenue potential

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### Near-Term Catalysts (Next 12–24 months)

1. **Commercial Molecule Ramp-Up**
   - 4 new commercial molecules approved in FY26 (now 14 total, up from 10 at IPO)
   - Peak sales estimates: ~$10 billion across 4 new molecules (per analyst consensus cited by management)
   - Current contribution: ~8–9% of revenue
   - Growth runway: 2–3 years to blockbuster status; multiple indications and geographies still to be launched
   - *Key trigger:* Accelerated market adoption as innovators expand approvals to new indications and geographies

2. **Semaglutide (GLP-1) API Opportunity**
   - Status: Process validation complete; awaiting CDSCO approval for commercial supply in India
   - Timeline: Expected CDSCO approval within Q1–Q2 of FY27
   - Backward integration: Anthem is only Indian company fully integrated in fermentation + synthesis (vs. Chinese competitors sourcing P29 fragment)
   - Customers sampled: All major Indian generic manufacturers; significant deal flow expected post-approval
   - Market size: Massive; estimated to grow 30%+ CAGR over next 3–5 years
   - *Key trigger:* CDSCO approval; customer offtakes post-approval

3. **Biosimilar Manufacturing**
   - Asset: Repurposed facility with 2×200 L microbial fermentation
   - Product: Microbial biosimilar (off-patent, already marketed globally; Anthem to be second source shifting production from US to India)
   - Market: Already has established revenue stream; peak sales estimated at $500M+
   - Timeline: Expected to contribute to revenue in FY27–FY28
   - *Key trigger:* Regulatory approvals; supply agreement formalization with pharma partner

4. **Large Pharma Traction**
   - Current: 2–3 of top-5 global pharma as customers; added 2–3 new large pharma in FY26–FY27
   - Recent addition: One new major pharma with "multi-dimensional engagement" (R&D, development, lateral projects)
   - Lateral projects: Existing commercial molecules being diversified across new large pharma customers
   - Timeline: Deals in pipeline; first meaningful revenue expected H2 FY27–FY28
   - *Key trigger:* Large pharma supply agreements; lateral project scaling

5. **Unit-3 (Neo Anthem) Ramp-Up**
   - Current WIP: Rs. 55 Cr as of end-FY26; expected to grow to Rs. 100–150 Cr by FY27-end
   - Utilization: 15% (FY26) → 30–35% (Q1 FY27) → 40%+ (FY27-end)
   - Peptide and high-potent compounds driving growth
   - Profitability: Expected to break-even/turn profitable by FY27-end
   - *Key trigger:* Peptide program commercialization; biotech customer growth

6. **Peptide Platform Expansion**
   - Current: 16 KL commercial facility; 10 early-stage peptide programs (non-GLP)
   - Capability: Full integration from fermentation (P29 for GLP-1) to synthesis to finished formulation
   - Modalities: GLP-1 (immediate), peptide drug conjugates, and novel peptides (2–5 year horizon)
   - Margin profile: Confident of maintaining 35%+ EBITDA margins on peptides even at $100/gram pricing
   - *Key trigger:* GLP-1 approval; commercial peptide program scaling

7. **Biotech Funding Recovery**
   - Current environment: Improving; +50% YoY biotech funding flow (as of April 2026)
   - Impact: Increased RFQ flow for early-stage programs (100+ currently in pipeline)
   - Geopolitical tailwinds: US–India relationship normalization; EU–India trade deal favorable
   - *Key trigger:* Continued biotech funding availability; geopolitical stability

### Structural Growth Drivers (Long-Term DNA Shift)

1. **Industry Secular Growth:** CRDMO market growing 12–15% CAGR globally; India gaining share as cost-advantaged, regulated-compliant hub
2. **Regulatory Tailwinds:** India government positioning biotech as strategic sector; PLI scheme support expected
3. **Destocking Normalization:** FY26 destocking (customer inventory reduction) has largely normalized; restocking cycle in FY27 expected to provide +2–3% tailwind
4. **Capacity Constraint Resolution:** Unit-4 commissioned by FY28 enables Anthem to capture 50%+ more business volume vs. current capacity ceiling
5. **Technology Moat:** Backward integration (peptides, fermentation) and advanced modalities (ADC, oligos) create differentiation vs. competitors
6. **Customer Stickiness:** Highly regulated business with long-term contracts (3–5+ years) creates predictable, recurring revenue

### Valuation: Probability-Weighted Fair Value

#### Step 1: Assign Justified P/E Multiples by Scenario

| Scenario | Growth Rate | Risk Profile | Justified P/E Multiple |
|----------|-------------|--------------|------------------------|
| Bull | 23% CAGR FY27–FY29 | Low | 28–30x |
| Base | 18% CAGR FY27–FY29 | Moderate | 22–24x |
| Bear | 11% CAGR FY27–FY29 | High | 16–18x |

**Methodology:** P/E multiples derived from growth rate + industry risk premium. CRDMO industry median P/E: 20–24x. Anthem's multi-modality capability + regulatory approval status warrant 2–4x premium to pure small-molecule CRDMO peers.

#### Step 3: Probability-Weighted Fair Value

**Formula:** (Bull Price × 25%) + (Base Price × 50%) + (Bear Price × 25%)
