## 1. Business Model Overview

**Background.** Airfloa (formerly Airflow Equipments India Pvt Ltd; CIN dates to 1998) is a Chennai-based railway-interiors and precision-components maker with **27 years in rolling stock** *(Nov2025 concall)*.
- **IPO:** BSE SME IPO of ₹91.1 Cr at ₹140/share (65.07 lakh fresh shares). It was subscribed ~211x and listed on 18-Sep-2025 *(chittorgarh)*.
- **Management:** Mr. Dakshinamoorthy Venkatesan (MD), Mr. D. Manikandan (JMD) and Mr. V. Sathishkumar (WTD).
- **Advisors:** Mr. Sudhanshu Mani (ex-GM ICF, the "father of Vande Bharat") and Mr. Tilak Seth (ex-Siemens Mobility) *(Jun2026 concall)*.

**What it sells**

| Vertical | Products / scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Indian Railways: coach interiors (core)** | Turnkey interior furnishing (panelling, flooring, roller blinds, seating, end-walls, nose cones, SS parts) for **Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat, LHB, Vistadome, EMU**. It supplies ICF Chennai, MCF Rae Bareli, RCF Kapurthala and the zonal railways | ~75–80% of the railway order book is from Indian Railways *(Nov2025)*; ₹415–420 Cr of the ₹469 Cr order book is government *(Jun2026)* |
| **Metro / RRTS / OEMs** | Interiors and components for Kolkata Metro, RRTS and Siemens, Alstom, BEML and TMH. Small exports (Sri Lanka, Mozambique) | ~₹70 Cr of metro orders in hand, plus ~₹120 Cr in the bid pipeline *(Jun2026)* |
| **Refurbishment (new)** | Zonal-railway coach refurbishment: a **₹26,000 Cr sanctioned programme for ~25,000 coaches over 3–4 years**. Executed through regional "channel partners" | Target is ≥₹100 Cr of orders in FY27 *(Jun2026)* |
| **New rail systems (development)** | Vande Bharat **door systems** and **vacuum toilets** (development orders); an "integrated train security system" (AI/remote, *not* a Kavach clone); a subsidiary for **electroluminescent / flexible-electronics display boards** | Pre-revenue. The security-system exhibit to RDSO/Railway Board is due in FY27 *(Jun2026)* |
| **Defence & aerospace (entered 2020)** | **Aircraft simulators for HAL** (prototype approved), hulls for MBT and auxiliary tanks, tether drones; with the **Big Bang Boom Solutions JV**, autonomous drones, high-power-laser anti-drone systems and EW. Holds approvals from ~52 DRDO labs | **₹29 Cr in the order book** (Jun-26), down from ~₹65 Cr in Nov-25. HAL orders of ₹60–70 Cr are expected *(Jun2026)* |

**Business-model shift.** Management describes the move from "component supplier" to an **integrated turnkey interiors platform**: design, manufacture, supply, install and commission, all under one roof (design, tooling and manufacturing). It is now adding an **asset-light "channel partner" model**: Airfloa makes the goods in Chennai, and regional railway vendors bid and execute at zonal workshops. Airfloa bills the partner, and the partner bills the railway. That way Airfloa saves EMD/PBG/security deposits and avoids building local infrastructure *(Jun2026)*.

**Competitors:** Kineco, Hindustan Fiberglass Works, DTL Ancillaries, Vibgyor and Universal Engineers. In seating: Tata (with Fainsa of Spain) and a Korean player in Bengaluru *(Jun2026)*.

**Seasonality:** heavily H2- and March-weighted. Q4 typically brings **40–45% of annual revenue** *(Nov2025)*, and H2FY26 was 72% of FY26.

**Takeaways**
- **FY26 delivered on revenue but not on margin.** Revenue grew 66%, but EBITDA margin fell **~480 bps** (24.9% → 20.1%), and H2 alone was 18.2%. Management blames aluminium (**+80%**) and stainless steel (**+60–65%**) inflation. Price-variation clauses cover only ~50–60% of the raw-material hit, and the 30–35% of the bill of materials that is accessories (glass, rubber, composites, fasteners) has no cover at all *(Jun2026)*. Falling finance cost and a lower tax rate (27% → 26%) protected PAT margin at 12.2%.
- **FY23 is a reminder of cyclicality.** Revenue fell 31% to ₹95 Cr and PAT was ~₹1 Cr. The step-change only began in FY24.
- **Q1FY27 revenue of ₹100.7 Cr (+200%) is a genuinely strong start.** It beat all of H1FY26. Profitability is unknown until 14-Nov.

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Vande Bharat sleeper platform.** An Airfloa-led consortium with **Blue Engineering (Italy)** won a **₹34.85 Cr ICF order to design the interiors and build mock-ups for Vande Bharat sleeper trainsets** (12-month scope). Designing the interior puts Airfloa in a strong position for the much larger serial furnishing contracts *(exchange filing via constructionworld/sahi)*. This is management's #1 three-year focus *(Jun2026)*.
2. **Amrit Bharat turnkey and LHB.** The Nov-25 order wins from ICF and MCF covered Amrit Bharat / LHB turnkey interiors *(Nov2025)*. Amrit Bharat turnkey contracts are named as a key three-year opportunity *(Jun2026)*.
3. **Kolkata Metro turnkey interiors.** This is the third major target. Small orders keep flowing (₹6.26 Cr ICF Furnishing, Aug-26; ₹4.12 Cr Kolkata Metro interiors, 05-Nov-2026).
4. **Coach refurbishment (₹26,000 Cr programme, ~25,000 coaches).** This is a new addressable market through zonal workshops, reached via the channel-partner model. Management targets ≥₹100 Cr of orders in FY27 at current margins *(Jun2026)*.
5. **Bid pipeline of ₹1,200 Cr** (~₹900 Cr railways, ~₹120 Cr metro, ~₹60–70 Cr HAL simulators). At the historical 20–25% hit rate that implies **₹240–300 Cr of wins**. Management says 30–40% of what is won can be executed within FY27 *(Jun2026)*.
6. **Capacity doubling (FY28).** The 14-acre campus plus rent savings bring operating leverage once it is live.
7. **Defence optionality.** HAL simulators (prototype approved), tank hulls, and the Big Bang Boom JV (drones, laser anti-drone, EW; ₹25 Cr) with Airfloa holding the manufacturing rights. A flexible-electronics display subsidiary. **This is all optionality, not yet earnings.** We put it at zero in the base case.
8. **Q1FY27 proof point.** Revenue of ₹100.7 Cr (+200% YoY) shows the double-shift capacity is real. A strong H1FY27 print (14-Nov) with recovered margins would be the next catalyst.

**DNA shift (thesis):** from a ~₹100–140 Cr lumpy component vendor (FY22–24) to a ₹300–500 Cr turnkey-interiors integrator riding Vande Bharat / Amrit Bharat volumes. The **unproven** part is whether it can do this *profitably in cash terms*.

## 7. Investment Verdict & Key Metrics to Monitor

### Weighted Fair Value & Entry Recommendation
**Weighted fair value: ₹478/share** (FY27E basis). At **CMP ₹519 (11-Nov-2026)** the stock is **~8.6% above fair value**, with an expected return of **−7.9%**, so it is in the **REDUCE** band (HOLD ceiling ₹502). Fresh money is better deployed at **₹430–478 (ACCUMULATE)** and aggressively **below ₹430 (BUY)**. The **H1FY27 results on 14-Nov-2026** should be read before acting. They are the single biggest near-term swing factor in either direction.

### Bull Case (20%): What Needs to Go Right → ₹829
- Q1's ₹100.7 Cr run-rate holds, and H2 seasonality delivers FY27 revenue of ~₹520 Cr (beats the ₹500 Cr guide).
- The VB-sleeper design win converts into serial furnishing, refurbishment adds ≥₹100 Cr of orders, and HAL simulators come in.
- Commodity relief and turnkey mix lift EBITDA back above 20.5%.
- The receivable cycle falls toward management's 60–70-day target, so growth no longer eats cash.
- The new campus doubles capacity in FY28. The market pays 30x FY27E (~21x FY28E).

### Base Case (55%): Most Likely Path → ₹471
- FY27 revenue is ~₹460 Cr (+44%), slightly below guidance. PAT is ₹51 Cr (11.2%), helped by capitalised R&D.
- Growth moderates to 21–26% in FY28–29 as the order book only partly replenishes. PAT reaches ~₹81 Cr by FY29.
- Working capital improves a little but stays at ~200+ days. Net debt rises with capex and the JV.
- Defence stays a sideshow. 22x FY27E (~17x FY28E).

### Bear Case (25%): What Could Go Wrong → ₹214
- Pipeline conversion disappoints and FY27 revenue is only ~₹400 Cr. RM inflation and L1 tenders hold EBITDA at ~17.5%.
- Receivables stay at 240+ days. Debt-funded capex, the JV and capitalised R&D push net debt above ₹150 Cr, and an equity raise follows at a discount.
- Credibility erodes further (another guidance cut, compliance issues) and the stock de-rates to 14x.

### Rating: **REDUCE** (weighted FV ₹478 vs CMP ₹519)

**What is genuinely good:**
- Revenue delivery is strong: FY26 +66%, Q1FY27 +200%.
- It is moving up the value chain into turnkey interiors on Vande Bharat / Amrit Bharat.
- The government spend pipeline is large.

**Why the price is still too high:**
- At ~24x FY27E base the stock already prices most of the ₹500 Cr guidance.
- The quality of the earnings is weak: FY26 FCF was −₹75 Cr, ~244 receivable days, and FY27 PAT will be boosted by capitalising ~₹40 Cr of R&D.
- Management over-promises on everything beyond 12 months.

**What to do now:**
- Existing holders can trim toward ₹560+.
- New buyers should wait for ₹430–478, or for an H1FY27 balance sheet showing receivables ≤ ₹150 Cr and positive operating cash flow. That evidence would justify moving 5–10 pts of probability from bear to bull and revisiting this rating.
