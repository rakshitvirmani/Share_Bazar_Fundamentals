## 1. Business Model Overview

### What Rashi Peripherals Does

Rashi Peripherals Limited is a pan-India ICT (Information & Communication Technology) distributor, established in 1989. The company operates as an intermediary between global technology brands and retailers/resellers, with 30+ years of distribution expertise. **Crucially, Rashi is NOT a direct-to-consumer retailer** — it is a B2B platform aggregating demand across 700+ towns through 10,000+ channel partners.

### Business Segments

The company operates primarily through two revenue segments:

1. **PES (Personal Computing & Enterprise Solutions)** — 58% of FY26 revenue
   - Branded notebooks, desktops, workstations (HP, Dell, Lenovo, etc.)
   - Enterprise servers and storage solutions
   - AI-capable computing devices and high-performance systems
   - AI data center solutions (NVIDIA DGX, Supermicro, etc.)

2. **LIT (Lifestyle IT & Embedded Systems)** — 42% of FY26 revenue
   - Components: RAM, SSDs, motherboards, power supplies, peripherals
   - Embedded systems for industrial, automotive, robotics applications
   - Lifestyle tech: headphones, IoT devices, health monitoring wearables
   - Software and cloud services (emerging)

### Geographic & Channel Reach

- **55 branch offices** across India (expanded from 54 in Q4 FY26)
- Coverage in 700+ towns with presence in Tier 1, 2, 3, and D-class cities
- Distribution channels: General Trade (GT), Modern Trade (MT), LFR (Large Format Retailers), and Online
- International subsidiary in Singapore for SAARC region operations
- UAE subsidiary established for regional material pickup and execution

### Revenue Mix & Customer Base

- **B2B Distribution Model**: 100% sales through partners (no direct consumer sales)
- **Consumer vs. Commercial Split** (per Q1 FY27 concall): 30-35% consumer-centric, 60-65% commercial (split between SMB and enterprise)
- **OEM Relationships**: 80+ global brands including Intel, AMD, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Dell, HP, Lenovo, and Samsung
- **Channel Partner Base**: 10,000+ registered partners generating revenue across geographies

### Strategic Positioning

Rashi positions itself as a **"technology adoption enabler"** — not just moving boxes, but:
- Creating localized solutions adapted to regional needs
- Providing pre-sales consulting, design services, and implementation support
- Building AI bootcamps and developer communities for ecosystem engagement
- Integrating upward to include system integration, managed services, and embedded design

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### Structural Forces Driving Growth (Multi-Year Super Cycle)

**1. PC Refresh Cycle — Multi-Year, Not One-Quarter Event**
- **Windows 10 End-of-Support**: October 2025 triggered enterprise refresh cycle
- **Reality**: Large corporates are reversing extended refresh timelines (3yr→4yr, 4yr→5yr delays) because they realize that waiting = 30-40% higher costs in 6 months
- **Result**: Calendar 2025 saw record 15.9 Mn PC shipments in India (+10.2% YoY) — highest ever including COVID peaks
- **Impact on Rashi**: Q1 FY27 revenue +61.9% driven by this cycle; expected to sustain through FY27-FY28

**2. AI PC Inflection — Accelerating ASP Uplift**
- **Current Mix**: 25% of PCs sold have NPU (AI processor); Gartner projects 50%+ by 2026
- **Price Impact**: AI notebooks command 20-30% ASP premium vs. standard; margins benefit even if volumes flat
- **Volume Trajectory**: Not the growth driver (volumes may be flat-to-negative as prices rise); **pricing is the tailwind**
- **Rashi Advantage**: 80+ brand relationships ensure allocation when supply constrained; first-mover to stock AI inventories
- **2-3 Year Runway**: As AI features cascade to mid-range and budget PCs, ASP uplift sustains

**3. Component Economics Shifted to Distributors' Favor**
- **Root Cause**: AI data center demand absorbed 60-70% of global DRAM/GPU capacity
- **Impact**: NAND flash, RAM prices up 100-300%, GPU/CPU prices firm (no discounting)
- **Who Benefits**: Distributors with scale, relationships, and working capital to pre-buy inventory
- **Risk Window**: Prices expected to remain elevated through 2028 (per management); after that, normalization risk
- **Rashi's Play**: Locked supply allocations with OEMs; building inventory during upswing to gain share when others can't source

**4. India's Structural Digitization — 1.5L Cr Market, 15-20% PC Penetration**
- **Opportunity Scale**: Indian ICT distribution market ~1.5 lakh Crore; top players hold ~70% (direct and indirect)
- **Penetration Runway**: PC penetration 15-20% vs. 60%+ in developed markets = multi-decade runway
- **Tier 2/3/4 Tailwind**: As SMBs, government, education adopt digitization, demand cascades down value chain
- **Rashi's DNA Fit**: 55 branches in C/D-class cities uniquely position the company to capture this wave
- **Timeline**: 8-10 years of above-GDP growth opportunity

**5. Strategic Pivot: From Distribution to Solution Partnership**
- **Old DNA**: Box-moving, high volume, thin margins
- **New DNA**: System integration, managed services, embedded design, AI consulting
- **Execution Evidence**:
  - VDA Infosolutions (system integration, cybersecurity, managed services)
  - Restar JV (design services, image sensing, IoT for manufacturing)
  - AI bootcamps (2,500 developers, 300+ CXOs trained in Q4 FY26)
  - NVIDIA DGX Spark launch (solution bundling for SMB inference)
- **Margin Impact**: Incremental 2-3 years to material contribution, but 30-50% higher margins than core distribution
- **ROCE Multiplier**: $100 Mn (Rs. 830 Cr) semicond JV at 20%+ ROCE >> Rs. 500 Cr at 10% from distribution

### Probability Shifters for Scenarios (See Section 6 for detailed framework)

**Bull Case Probability Increases If:**
- Semiconductor JV scales to $50+ Mn revenue within 24 months (vs. $0 baseline)
- VDA contribution reaches 5%+ of overall revenue by FY28 (acquisition integrating well)
- AI data center pipeline converts to 10%+ of revenue opportunity

- VDA acquisition integration disappoints; no margin contribution by FY28
- Competitive intensity increases; smaller distributors exit but rationalize margins
- Customer affordability pressure intensifies; 50%+ unit degrowth (vs. current 5-10% guides)

- Pricing normalization (structural, not temporary)
- Volume volatility and consumer affordability cliff

- Semiconductor JV scaling challenges

- Working capital stretch if growth sustains
- Competitive pressure and share loss

**Lower Risk (4-5/10):**

**Mitigation Strategy:** Diversification across segments (PES 58% + LIT 42%), geographies (55 branches), channels (GT, MT, LFR, Online), and business models (distribution + services + projects) reduces single-point-of-failure risk. 20% CAGR guidance is achievable if ANY combination of these drivers persists (not ALL needed).
