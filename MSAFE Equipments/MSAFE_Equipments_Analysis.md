## 1. Business Model Overview

MSAFE Equipments Limited (incorporated Aug 2019) manufactures, sells, and **rents access & height-safety equipment** across India. Products: aluminium scaffoldings, mild steel (MS) scaffoldings, aluminium ladders, FRP ladders, and Aerial Work Platform (AWP) scissor lifts.

**Revenue Model — Dual Engine (Sales + Rental):**

| Revenue Stream | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | H1 FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rental | 62.97% | 52.30% | 51.65% | 43.07% |
| Sales | 37.03% | 47.70% | 48.35% | 56.93% |

The business is shifting slightly toward outright sales as manufacturing capacity came online in FY24, but rental remains the sticky, recurring-revenue core.

**Product Mix (FY25 revenue):** Aluminium scaffolding (sale + rental) = 75.72% of revenues. MS scaffolding ~4%, ladders ~2%, others ~18%.

**Key end-markets:** Civil construction, infrastructure (metro/highway/airports), facility management, MEP & HVAC contractors, industrial maintenance, warehousing, firefighting systems.

**Geographic footprint:** 97.77% of revenue from 22 states + 3 union territories. Key markets: Maharashtra, UP, Delhi, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Chandigarh.

**Manufacturing:** 3 operating units in Gautam Buddha Nagar, Greater Noida, UP (in-house since FY24; previously 3PL). Unit-III commenced May 2025. Proposed Unit-IV in Kosi Kotwan, Mathura, UP (funded via IPO proceeds).

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

**1. Infrastructure supercycle tailwind:**
India Scaffolding Market growing at **10.06% CAGR** — ₹7,209 Cr (2024) → ₹12,812 Cr (2030E). Government capex grew at 27% CAGR over past 5 years; FY26 budget allocated ₹11.21 Trillion to infrastructure. MSAFE is directly positioned in equipment required for every large construction site.

**2. In-house manufacturing flywheel (FY24 shift):**
Pre-FY24 the company was a trader/rental operator. Commissioning its own manufacturing (FY24) is the structural margin inflection — EBITDA margins expanded from ~31% (FY23) to ~37%+ (FY25). Gross margin control is now internal. *This is the single biggest DNA change.*

**3. Rental-to-ownership secular trend playing out:**
~67% of construction equipment in India is now procured via rental. MSAFE's dual-model (sell + rent) lets it capture customers at both ends of the buying decision. Recurring rental revenue provides base-load revenue even in down-cycles.

**4. Capacity step-change with Unit-III + Unit-IV:**
Unit-III (commissioned May 2025) adds 32.85 lakh kg MS capacity alone — larger than Units I & II combined. Unit-IV adds another 24 lakh kg aluminium + MS. Revenue absorption of this capacity is the next 2-year growth story.

**5. Expanding into hanging/suspended scaffolding:**
New product category targeted at high-rise building renovation — an underpenetrated and faster-growing vertical vs ground-level construction scaffolding.

## 5. Red Flags & Risk Analysis

| # | Risk | Severity (1–10) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No long-term customer contracts | **8/10** | Orders are short-term (monthly/weekly rental or spot sales). Revenue visibility is weak. *Per RHP risk factors.* |
| 2 | High employee attrition (>50% in FY23 & FY25) | **7/10** | Operational continuity risk; training & recruitment costs recurring. *Per RHP.* |
| 3 | Working capital intensity | **7/10** | Inventory days jumped to 123 (FY25) vs 36 (FY23) due to manufacturing ramp-up. Trade receivables 22 Cr vs 13 Cr PAT. Cash is thin (₹0.06 Cr on books). |
| 4 | Expansion execution risk | **7/10** | ₹37 Cr Mathura facility: land acquisition, construction, ramp-up all at-risk. Delays would extend cash burn on IPO proceeds. |
| 5 | Debt-to-Equity ~1.12x, rising | **6/10** | ₹40.36 Cr total debt (Dec 2025) vs ₹36 Cr net worth. Promoters have given personal guarantees on loans. |
| 6 | Product liability / safety risk | **6/10** | Height-safety equipment — any site accident causing injury can trigger legal liability, brand damage, loss of repeat business. 94 criminal cases filed *by* the company; 101 indirect tax disputes. |
| 7 | Supplier concentration | **6/10** | Top 10 suppliers = 34–44% of COGS. Aluminium & steel price volatility cannot be easily passed through to customers. |
| 8 | Rental fleet utilization drag | **5/10** | Underutilized fleet generates depreciation without revenue. Fleet condition deteriorates with each deployment, driving refurbishment costs. |
| 9 | SME listing liquidity risk | **5/10** | BSE SME listing — lower trading volumes, wider bid-ask spreads, institutional participation limited. Exit may be difficult for large investors. |
| 10 | Short operating history as manufacturer | **5/10** | Manufacturing began FY24. Only 2 full years of in-house production history — operational surprises possible at scale. |
