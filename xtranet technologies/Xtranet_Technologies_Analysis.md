## 1. Business Model Overview

XtraNet Technologies Limited (formerly XtraNet Technologies Private Limited; CIN U72200MP2002PLC014956), headquartered in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, is an **integrated technology solutions provider with over 24 years of experience** delivering digital infrastructure, enterprise applications, managed services, digital transformation and proprietary digital-trust platforms to government, PSU and private-sector clients. *per AR FY26, "About XtraNet"*

**Four business verticals** (FY26 revenue mix, consolidated, *per AR FY26 "Financial Highlights"/infographic*):

| Vertical | FY26 Revenue Share | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Managed Services / Data Centre Infrastructure & IT Operations | 40.53% (+39% YoY) | 24x7 monitoring, data centre & DR infrastructure, infrastructure modernization, cloud infra, NOC/SOC, IT operations |
| Enterprise Applications | 33.22% (+25% YoY) | ERP implementation & support (finance, HR/HCM, procurement, billing, BPM/workflow), application development & maintenance, system integration |
| Digital Services | 15.93% | Cloud, digital transformation, analytics, AI/ML, IoT, automation, blockchain — legacy-to-digital migration |
| Proprietary Platforms & Products | 10.33% | **Synergy** (low-code, AI-native digital transformation/workflow platform) and **XtraTrust** (digital trust, PKI-based authentication) |

*Note on segment naming:* the AR's "About" infographic labels the largest vertical "Managed Services," while both the MD&A and the Q1 FY27 concall call the same vertical "Data Centre Infrastructure & IT Operations" — these are the same underlying segment. In the Q1 FY27 concall, this segment's quarterly share was reported at ~48% of revenue (up from ~40.53% for full-year FY26), with Enterprise Applications 26%, Proprietary Platforms 14%, Digital Services 12%. *per Q1 FY27 concall, 24-Aug-2026*

**XtraTrust** is one of ~11 privately licensed Certifying Authorities in India (MeitY-approved) issuing Digital Signature Certificates and e-Sign services, with ~850,000 subscribers onboarded via 10,000+ channel partners, 24x7 support and a ~30-minute issuance SLA; management names **eMudhra** as the main comparable competitor. **Synergy** underpins newer proprietary products launched in FY26 — X-ERP, X-Sign, SeDMS (self-service smart lockers), XtraSupport, Synergy SecureDocInfra, and a BFSI-specific Data Protection Solution. *per AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 FY27 concall*

**Customer mix:** Government/PSU customers ≈ 45–47% of revenue, private/non-government ≈ 53–55%; order book split ~45% direct / ~55% indirect (channel/OEM-routed). Presence across 15+ Indian states, 10+ industries, and (per the "About" infographic) 5 countries. *per AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 FY27 concall*

**Revenue model — two structures running in parallel:**
1. **CAPEX-OPEX (deployment + O&M) model:** ~50–60% of contract value billed upfront as CAPEX during a 6–18 month deployment phase, with the balance (35–40%) recognized as O&M/managed-services revenue over the following 3–7 years, billed quarterly, straight-line (no step-up).
2. **Annuity/subscription model:** revenue spread over 5–10 years, billed quarterly (used for XtraTrust-type platform/subscription business). Management applies an internal IRR benchmark of ≥17–18% before bidding on annuity-style projects. *per Q1 FY27 concall Q&A*

**Certifications & moat:** ISO 9001:2015, ISO/IEC 27001:2013, ISO/IEC 20000-1:2011, ISO 14001:2015 and **CMMI Level 5** — regulatory/quality certifications that function as bid-eligibility barriers in government/PSU tenders. XtraTrust's CA license is itself a high-barrier, MeitY-regulated asset.

**Corporate structure:** Three consolidated subsidiaries — **Xtranet BPO Private Limited** (90.24% stake, up from 62.36% in FY25), **Xtratrust Digisign Private Limited** (95.00%, up from 75.00%), **Xtrasynergy Solutions Private Limited** (51.00%, unchanged) — plus one Associate, **Extranet Technology Solutions LLC, Dubai**, positioned as the platform for international expansion. *per AR FY26, Board's Report & Note 40*

**Listing history:** XtraNet completed a **pure fresh-issue Mainboard IPO of ₹166.80 Cr (₹16,680.18 Lakhs)** — price band ₹120–127, final price ₹127/share, no offer-for-sale component — and commenced trading on NSE and BSE on **30 July 2026** (IPO open 23–27 Jul 2026). Pre-IPO paid-up capital was 3,91,51,700 shares (as at 31-Mar-2026); the fresh issue added an estimated ~1,31,34,000 shares, taking post-IPO shares outstanding to an **estimated ~5,22,85,700 (~5.23 Cr)** — this is a derived estimate (pre-IPO AR share count + IPO fresh-issue share count), not a figure stated verbatim in either document read for this analysis, and should be cross-checked against the post-IPO shareholding pattern filing. A 4:1 bonus issue (3,13,21,360 shares) was allotted in September 2025, ahead of the IPO. Promoter holding stood at **83.63%** as at 31-Mar-2026 (pre-IPO); given the fresh-issue-only structure, post-IPO promoter holding is estimated to dilute to **~62–63%**. *per AR FY26, Board's Report — Share Capital & Promoter Shareholding*

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Services-mix shift is actively re-rating profitability.** Services carried ~60% of FY26 revenue (vs. materially more product-heavy ~20% services / ~80% product mix "three years back," per management) and rose further to ~65–68% in Q1 FY27. Since services/O&M revenue earns ~20–22% EBITDA margin vs. ~6–8% for product/CAPEX deployment, this single mix shift explains most of the 855 bps YoY EBITDA-margin expansion seen in Q1 FY27. *per Q1 FY27 concall*

2. **Explicit medium-term guidance:** management targets **₹500+ Cr FY27 revenue (35–40% growth)** and a **35–40% 3-year revenue CAGR**, with Data Centre/IT Ops targeted to grow 35–40% annually and held at ~50–55% of the overall mix, Enterprise Applications at ~20–25%, and Digital Services + Proprietary Platforms combined at ~30%+. A further 50–75 bps of EBITDA-margin improvement is guided for FY27. *per Q1 FY27 concall*

3. **Recurring/annuity revenue base building customer stickiness.** ~50% of the current order book and ~40% of pipeline submissions are structured as multi-year (4–10 year) recurring/annuity revenue — a shift from one-off project billing toward SLA-driven, predictable cash flows, with an internal IRR hurdle of ≥17–18% applied to annuity bids.

4. **Digital-trust platform (XtraTrust) has structural regulatory moat and cross-sell optionality.** One of only ~11 licensed private CAs in India, ~850,000 subscribers, 10,000+ partners, and active B2B expansion into BFSI, aviation, banking and large-enterprise/PSU/railway accounts — plus capacity investment underway to scale further.

5. **Proprietary IP portfolio (Synergy) is expanding the product surface area.** FY26 saw multiple new proprietary launches (X-ERP, X-Sign, SeDMS, XtraSupport, Synergy SecureDocInfra, a BFSI Data Protection Solution) — increasing wallet-share potential with existing customers via cross-sell across the four verticals.

6. **Hardware-inventory strategy as a near-term margin/competitive lever.** Management describes having pre-built inventory ahead of a sharp (3–4x, per management) run-up in hardware/component costs over the past year, avoiding price escalation on government contracts with no escalation clauses; new bids now embed price-escalation clauses (USD-linked, OEM policy-linked). No cancellations reported to date, though management flags product availability itself — not just price — as an emerging constraint.

7. **First year as a listed entity — capital and governance reset.** The ₹170 Cr (fresh-issue-only) IPO funds working-capital, debt repayment/pre-payment, and infrastructure capex, while FY26 also saw the Board add Independent Directors, form dedicated committees, and centralize compliance processes ahead of listing.

8. **Industry tailwind:** India IT spend ~US$176.3 Bn in 2026 (+10.6% YoY, Gartner), with data-centre systems spend growing 20.5%, and the India data-centre market projected to grow from ~US$5.5 Bn (2025) to US$13 Bn+ by 2034 (~10%+ CAGR) — directly aligned with XtraNet's largest and fastest-growing vertical. *per AR FY26 MD&A, Industry Overview*

## Investment Verdict

### Fair Value Summary
- **Probability-Weighted Fair Value (FY27E basis): ~₹305/share**
- **FY30+ Terminal Value (discounted to present): ~₹223/share** (a more conservative long-run anchor, reflecting eventual multiple normalization)
- **CMP (~20-Sep-2026): ~₹312/share** → **~-2% expected return to weighted fair value (near fair value, slight premium)**

### Rating & Recommendation

**RATING: HOLD**

XtraNet is a genuinely differentiated, high-ROE (30%+ historically), CMMI Level 5-certified integrated technology solutions provider with 24+ years of execution history, a regulated digital-trust platform (XtraTrust) with real entry barriers, and a clear, management-articulated strategy of shifting revenue mix toward higher-margin services and recurring/annuity contracts. FY26 results and the Q1 FY27 print both validate this thesis directionally — EBITDA margin +855 bps and PAT +77% YoY in the first quarter as a listed company. However, the stock has **already re-rated ~145% from its ₹127 IPO price to ~₹312 in under two months**, is trading at roughly the forward multiple its own Base Case guidance would justify, and carries a cluster of real, currently-unresolved compliance and disclosure items (MSME interest/litigation, disputed tax demands, FEMA regularization, a large headcount-reporting discrepancy) that a clean audit opinion does not fully offset.

**Bull Case (25% probability):** Pipeline conversion outpaces guidance, services mix holds above 65%, data-centre segment sustains 35-40%+ growth, and XtraTrust scales meaningfully. **Fair Value: ₹477/share** (+53% from CMP). *Key trigger: two consecutive quarters of EBITDA margin above ~19-20% alongside revenue growth at/above 40%.*

**Base Case (50% probability):** FY27 revenue lands near the guided ₹500 Cr (~37% growth), margin improves by the guided 50-75bps, no compliance/tax overhang crystallizes materially. **Fair Value: ₹305/share** (-2% from CMP). *Key trigger: FY27 revenue growth in the 30-40% band with EBITDA margin sustained near 18%.*

**Bear Case (25% probability):** Hardware-cost inflation compresses margins on new/repeat orders, order-book execution slips, services mix reverts, and at least one compliance/tax overhang results in a cash outflow. **Fair Value: ₹132/share** (-58% from CMP). *Key trigger: revenue growth falling below 20% with EBITDA margin below 15.5%.*

### What Would Change This Rating

- **Upgrade to ACCUMULATE/BUY** if the price falls to/below ~₹305 (fair value) or ~₹274.5 (>10% discount) without a change in fundamentals, or if Q2/Q3 FY27 results confirm the services-mix and margin-expansion trajectory is durable rather than a one-quarter phenomenon.
- **Downgrade to REDUCE** if the price rises materially above ~₹320 without commensurate upward guidance revisions, or if any of the MSME-interest, disputed-tax, or FEMA/FLA items crystallizes into a material adverse cash/P&L outcome, or if the headcount-disclosure discrepancy turns out to reflect a governance/reporting-quality issue rather than a scope difference.
