> **Verdict: REDUCE** — Weighted Fair Value **₹605** vs CMP ₹656 (~8% premium).
>
> **The positives:** Exato is a profitable, fast-growing CX/AI systems integrator with blue-chip BPO and banking clients. FY26 revenue rose 35% to ₹168 Cr and PAT rose 67% to ₹16.1 Cr. Q1 FY27 PAT doubled.
>
> **What keeps it a REDUCE:**
> - **Credibility:** the Q4 FY26 guidance was badly missed.
> - **Inconsistent disclosures:** order book, ARR and export share were each stated differently across calls.
> - **Concentration:** the top 10 customers are 85–87% of revenue, with heavy dependence on one OEM (NiCE).

>
> The stock trades at ~41x FY26 and ~28x FY27E EPS. That assumes management's aggressive guidance is largely delivered.

## 1. Business Model Overview

Exato (founded May 2016, Noida) is a **customer-experience (CX), AI and analytics systems integrator and managed-services provider**. It resells and implements OEM platforms (mainly **NiCE**, plus Mitel, Acumatica, Microsoft, Cisco, and newly HPE, AWS and Fortinet) and layers its own consulting, customisation and IP on top.

### Lines of Business (*per Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 concalls*)

| LoB | What | Economics |
|---|---|---|
| CX & Analytics ("CX-as-a-service") | Contact-centre platforms, workforce management, interaction analytics, conversational and agentic AI, managed services | Core business; licences are the biggest revenue item |
| Unified Communication & Infrastructure | Trading-floor telephony for banks, UC, and the **new AI-infrastructure vertical** (HPE "LLM-in-a-box", networking, security) | AI infra margins **~10–12%** initially (*Q1 FY27*) |
| Cloud ERP | Acumatica partner (gold) | Two deals closed in FY26 |
| ExatoIQ (own IP) | Dialer, compliance call monitoring (Compli-call), UAM; deployed at HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, TaskUs | **2–3% of revenue**; targeted 15–30% in 3–4 years; **40–50% margins** |

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### 4.1 International hybrid model (US/UK/Singapore/Australia)
- The aim is to sell at global prices and deliver from India. Management says a $10M deal closed from India would have been worth about $25M if priced onshore (*Q4 FY26*).
- International gross margin is 30–35% vs 22–24% domestic. Every 10 pp shift in mix adds about 1 pp of gross margin.
- Wins so far: iQor (₹60–65 Cr), Diligenta (UK) expansion, agentic-AI orders in the US and UK.

### 4.2 Large multi-year BPO/healthcare contracts
- IKS (₹130 Cr) and BLS (₹95 Cr) give 5-year visibility and top-up potential. ₹120–125 Cr a year of billing is "secured for the next three years" (*Q4 FY26*).

### 4.3 Own IP (ExatoIQ) and agentic AI
- 40–50% margin IP is 2–3% of revenue today, targeted at 15–30% in 3–4 years. A Chief AI Officer (ex-TCS) has been hired, and a large AI-company partnership is "under NDA".

### 4.4 AI-infrastructure vertical
- HPE-based private-AI appliances and data-centre deals are targeted at **25–30% of FY27 revenue** (*Q1 FY27*). This adds top line at the cost of margin.

### 4.5 Inorganic growth and mainboard migration
- Management aspires to the mainboard within ~3 years and has floated targets of "₹500 Cr" and even "₹1,000 Cr" revenue and a "NASDAQ" listing. An M&A pipeline of 3–4 companies is being evaluated.

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### Investment Verdict

**Weighted Fair Value: ₹605 | CMP ~₹656 | Rating: REDUCE.** ACCUMULATE between ₹545 and ₹605; BUY below ₹545.

- **Bull (20%, ₹1,192):** guidance is met, and the international hybrid model, IP and managed services transform the mix. EPS roughly triples by FY28 and the stock re-rates toward the mainboard.
- **Base (50%, ₹614):** a solid 30–40% grower whose margin is held back by hardware. The stock is fairly valued to slightly rich.
- **Bear (30%, ₹201):** a lumpy, concentrated SME integrator misses again, margins slip and the multiple compresses.

**Bottom line:** the business and client base are real, and Q1 FY27 was strong. But management has missed its own near-term guidance, then raised the next guidance. Key metrics have been restated across calls. Revenue depends on ~10 customers and one OEM, and the newest growth engine (AI hardware) carries low margins. At ~28x FY27E for an SME with these traits, there is no margin of safety. Wait for H1 FY27 delivery against the 60–70% guide, for consistent order-book and ARR disclosure, and for a price nearer ₹545–600.
