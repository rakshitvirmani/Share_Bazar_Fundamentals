## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** Emmforce Autotech is a precision engineering and automotive components manufacturer specializing in:
- **4WD locking hubs** (core product, established customer base)
- **Spindles and drivetrain assemblies** (expanding OEM customer base)
- **Hydraulic gear pumps** (new high-margin product line)
- **Rotavator components and blades** (Agri segment, growing distributor network)
- **Forged shafts, gears, and differential parts**
- **Specialized precision engineering products** serving 20+ countries

**Integrated Manufacturing:**
- **Baddi, Himachal Pradesh facility:** ~150,000 sq ft, 600+ skilled workforce
- **In-house capabilities:** R&D, 3D/3M scanning, metallurgical lab, forging, machining, gear cutting, heat treatment, laser cutting, assembly, end-of-line testing
- **Quality standards:** ISO-certified, serves OEM and aftermarket channels
- **Asset base (FY26):** PP&E ₹50.05 Cr (vs ₹26.67 Cr FY25) — significant capex for new orders

**Revenue Mix (FY26):**
- **Top 1 customer:** 47% of revenue
- **Top 3 customers:** 71%
- **Top 5 customers:** 83%
- **Top 10 customers:** 93%
- **Mix shift underway:** OEM orders ramping as new customers enter production (U.S. export order started Feb 2026)

**Financial Model:**
- **Revenue FY26:** ₹104.47 Cr (+19.5% YoY)
- **Net margin FY26:** 11.53% (improving as new capacity utilizes)
- **Asset turnover:** 3x FY26 (expected 3.5–4x FY27 per management)
- **Cash generation:** OCF ₹11.58 Cr FY26 (vs negative ₹3.43 Cr FY25 due to WC buildup for new orders)

## 4. Key Catalysts & DNA Shift

### Major Secured Orders (Provides Revenue Visibility)

1. **U.S. OEM Export Order — ₹470 Cr multiyear** *(MAJOR CATALYST — game changer)*
   - **Start:** Commercial production began Feb 2026 (full ramp by Mar 2026)
   - **FY27E Contribution:** ~₹60 Cr annual run-rate
   - **Margin profile:** 18–20% EBITDA (niche, specialized product)
   - **Timeline:** 8-year contract duration
   - **Status:** Samples approved (PPAP complete), now executing at full throttle
   - **Risk:** Execution at scale; customer satisfaction critical for retention

2. **Drivetrain Supply Orders** *(Emerging OEM customer base)*
   - Long-term contracts with Tier-1 OEM customers (Asia, Europe expansion)
   - Initial revenue: ₹5 Cr already started in FY26
   - Pipeline: ₹10–12 Cr of new orders with PPAP samples at customers
   - Expected start: FY27
   - **Implication:** OEM revenue mix shifting from 10% (FY26) to 25–30% (FY27E)

3. **Rotavator & Agri Business — ₹10.5+ Cr order** *(Seasonal, growth potential)*
   - **TAFE partnership:** Core order, off-season inventory rationalization ending
   - **Own distributor network:** Maharashtra, MP, Karnataka launched (own-brand rotavator blades)
   - **Market opportunity:** 70–80% market share potential (per mgmt)
   - **Seasonality:** Peak Oct–Feb; off-season ramp-up starting Jun 2026
   - **Implication:** FY27 agri revenue ₹20–25 Cr (vs ₹12–15 Cr FY26)

4. **Hydraulic Gear Pumps — Emerging Division** *(New product category)*
   - Positioned as standalone division long-term
   - Currently not quantified by management (conservative guidance)
   - **Niche positioning:** Specialized applications, high-margin
   - **Ramp timeline:** FY27–28

### DNA Shift: From Supplier to OEM Tier-1 Partner

**FY26 reality:** 93% revenue from top 10 customers (legacy replacement/aftermarket focus)
**FY27–28 trajectory:** Shift to OEM-heavy model via:
- Large-scale U.S. export orders (structural, long-term contracts)
- Tier-1 OEM partnerships across Asia, Europe
- Improved margins (OEM niche > replacement generic)
- Reduced customer concentration risk (once new OEMs ramp)

**Implication for multiples:** If OEM revenue reaches 40–50% by FY28, P/E could re-rate 15–18x (vs current 10–12x), given lower execution risk and contract visibility.

## Investment Verdict

The **U.S. export order (₹470 Cr, 8-year contract, ₹60 Cr annual revenue FY27E) is the pivotal catalyst** unlocking 50%+ revenue CAGR over FY26–28E.

- Secured, long-term OEM contracts with visible revenue ramps
