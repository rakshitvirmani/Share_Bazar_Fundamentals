## 1. Business Model Overview

B C C Fuba India (est. 1985) was one of India's first **professional-grade printed circuit board (PCB) makers**. It began as a JV with Fuba Hans Kolbe & Co. (Germany, then Europe's largest PCB maker) and DEG. That technology has been fully absorbed, and the company now makes **double-sided and multilayer PCBs with SMT pads** without foreign assistance (*AR FY26*).

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Segment | Single reportable segment: PCBs (*AR FY26, note 36*) |
| Facility | One plant at Swarghat Road, Nalagarh, HP |
| Production (derived) | ~1.10 lakh sq m in FY26 vs ~0.72 lakh sq m in FY25 (+53%), estimated from electricity units ÷ units per sq m (*AR FY26 Form A*) |
| Realisation (derived) | ~₹6,550/sq m, flat YoY. Growth is volume-led, not price-led |
| Geography | **100% domestic.** No foreign exchange earned in FY25 or FY26 |
| Raw material | **~78% imported**: ₹34.8 Cr of FX raw-material outgo against ₹44.8 Cr cost of materials (*AR FY26*). Copper-clad laminates are mostly imported |
| End-markets (per MD&A) | Automotive electronics, e-mobility, industrial electronics, telecom |
| **Customer concentration** | Customers each above 10% of sales totalled **₹61.9 Cr = 85% of FY26 revenue** (FY25: ₹39.2 Cr = 83%) (*AR FY26, note 36*) |
| People | 139 employees plus 173 contract workers |
| Forward integration | **51% of ioGems Technologies Pvt Ltd** (EMS / PCB assembly), acquired 23 Jan 2026. ioGems was incorporated in Oct 2025; FY26 revenue ₹0.12 Cr, loss ₹0.19 Cr |

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

### 4.1 India PCB import-substitution wave
- The **Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS)** was expanded to ₹40,000 Cr in the 2026–27 Budget and has drawn more than ₹1.15 lakh Cr of investment proposals (*AR FY26 MD&A*).
- India's PCB market is about $6.3 B and could reach about $24.7 B by 2033, a 15–18% CAGR (*AR FY25 MD&A*).
- Domestic professional-grade PCB makers with an established track record are scarce, and BCC Fuba has a 40-year qualification history.

### 4.2 Volume-led growth with operating leverage
- FY26 revenue grew 54%, H1 FY27 about 68%. EBITDA margin rose from about 15% to about 20% as fixed costs (employees, power) were spread over more area.

### 4.3 Pricing power (price revisions)
- Q1 FY27 profit was helped by ₹1.67 Cr of finalised **selling-price revisions** with customers. That suggests the company can pass through laminate and copper inflation, at least with its anchor customers.

### 4.4 Forward integration into EMS (ioGems)
- Moving from bare boards to assembled boards (PCBA) raises value per customer. The subsidiary is tiny today; any traction would be upside.

### 4.5 Capacity addition from rights proceeds
- New machinery in FY27, funded by the ₹17.2 Cr already raised plus ₹17.2 Cr of call money still to come, should support the next leg of growth without leverage.

- Quarterly revenue goes above ₹35 Cr by Q4 FY27 with EBITDA margin at or above 19%, excluding one-offs.

## 7. Key Metrics to Monitor

### Investment Verdict

**Weighted Fair Value: ₹236 | CMP ₹325 | Rating: REDUCE.** ACCUMULATE between ₹212 and ₹236; BUY below ₹212.

- **Bull (25%, ₹407):** the PCB localisation boom plus new machinery keep growth above 50% into FY28. The anchor customers keep scaling, margins stay at about 20%, and the scarcity premium persists.
- **Base (50%, ₹229):** a good small business growing about 30% with about 18% EBITDA margin. Even this outcome is about 30% below today's price.
- **Bear (25%, ₹76):** customer concentration or capital-allocation missteps (subsidiary, related parties) break the story, and the stock falls toward a de-rated multiple on ₹5 EPS.

**Bottom line:** the operating story is real. Volumes are up more than 50%, margins are up about 500 bps and the balance sheet is now net cash. But the stock has run to a price that already exceeds the Bull case, while disclosure is minimal and governance needs watching. Holders should trim into strength. A new position only makes sense at ₹210–235, or after customer diversification and capex deployment become visible.
