## 1. Business Model Overview

Taurian MPS makes **crushing, screening, washing, material-handling and mineral-processing equipment**. Its customers are aggregate producers, stone crushers, miners, infra contractors and recyclers. It was incorporated in 2010 to trade sand, aggregates and spare parts. It started manufacturing in 2011–13 (jaw crushers, mobile equipment) and moved up to integrated plants (200 TPH in 2014–15, 800 TPH capability by 2022–24) *(per H1FY27 investor presentation, Nov 2026)*. It is headquartered in Mumbai and has **one plant at Roorkee, Uttarakhand** (1,10,000 sq ft). It listed on NSE Emerge on 16-Sep-2025 (IPO at ₹171, ~₹42 Cr raised *(per Nov2025 concall; chittorgarh)*).

**Product portfolio** *(per H1FY27 presentation)*
| Category | Products | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Crushing & screening plants (flagship) | Jaw, CG/CB cone, T-Series VSI, roll crushers; inclined/horizontal/high-frequency dry screens; Terra Track hybrid, track-, wheel-mounted and modular plants | Core revenue driver. Track-mounted crushers go to iron-ore mines in the Northeast *(Nov2025 concall)* |
| Beneficiation / washing | Washing systems, sand-washing plants, classification (CycloWash, thickeners) | Higher-value systems. They won the first South India order (manufactured sand) *(Nov2025 concall)* |
| Spares, wear parts & services | Crusher and screen spares, AMC, refurbishment, commissioning | Annuity element. Spares consumption rises from ~5% to 15–20% of machine value over a 10–20 year life *(Nov2025 concall)* |

**Customers**: L&T and JSW (ordering "on a monthly basis"), Tata Steel and Jindal Stainless *(Nov2025 concall)*, plus many small private crusher owners. Installations include Jammu, Haridwar, Guwahati, Kota, Jabalpur, Pune and Chittorgarh, and a waste-recycling plant in Kolkata *(H1FY27 presentation)*.

**Revenue mix**
- **Domestic vs export (FY26):** domestic ₹81.58 Cr. Export ≈ ₹17.8 Cr (~18%; *derived*), up from ~2% (~₹2.5 Cr) in FY25 *(H1FY27 presentation; Nov2025 concall)*. West India is the largest domestic region; South and East are the targeted growth regions.
- **Export markets:** South America (Chile, Dominican Republic, Mexico, Brazil), Saudi Arabia, Oman, Russia (repeat enquiries), Africa (Ghana, Kenya, Congo, Angola), Nepal, Thailand, Portugal. In the US there is a "Taurian Americas" dealership plus warehouse in Texas, on hold since the US tariffs *(Nov2025 concall; H1FY27 presentation)*.
- **Order-book nature:** short-cycle. Industry order books are 2–3 months long, delivery takes ~45 days, and mine owners order only once leases are in hand *(Nov2025 concall)*. **The business is strongly H2-weighted**: the monsoon stops deliveries and commissioning in H1.

**Competitive position**: Taurian prices ~10% below Propel Industries (the market leader, ~₹1,000–1,500 Cr revenue) and claims a wider range than Propel apart from EV trucks *(Nov2025 concall)*. Other competitors are Puzzolana, Terex, Astec and Metso, plus Turkish and Chinese imports. In Nov-2025 Taurian hired Propel's former sales head, **Mr. Anand Mohan**. He is now Director – Global Sales *(Nov2025 concall; H1FY27 presentation)*.

**Management**: Mr. Yashvardhan Bajla is MD (promoter since 2021, Purdue Industrial Engineering). Mr. Sumit Bajla is Founder. Mr. Atul Hirawat is Director – Operations. Mr. Vinod Modi is CFO. Mr. Dushyant Baniya, Chief Manufacturing Officer, came from Tega/Metso/Tata Hitachi *(H1FY27 presentation)*.

**Our read on capacity:**
- FY26 revenue per installed plant was ~₹1.2 Cr (₹99 Cr / 80+ plants, incl. spares).
- At today's mix, 155 plants is therefore worth roughly **₹190–200 Cr**. Management's ₹250 Cr figure needs a richer mix (washing/beneficiation, larger plants) or more output per shift.
- **There is room until ~FY28E (base ₹197 Cr). FY29E base (₹256 Cr) and the bull case need more capacity** beyond the ₹10 Cr in-sourcing capex. No such plan has been announced.
- **The binding constraint so far has been working capital, not capacity.** That is why the ₹42 Cr WC allocation matters more than the capex.

**Funding: preferential issue + warrants (Aug–Oct 2026)**
- **Terms:** issue price ₹419 (₹10 FV + ₹409 premium). The board approved 5.94 lakh shares plus 8.49 lakh warrants on 21-Aug-2026, and the EGM was on 20-Sep-2026 *(NSE filings)*.
- **Funds raised: ₹59.97 Cr ≈ 14.31 lakh units at ₹419.** Promoters took ₹20.94 Cr (= 5 lakh warrants) and non-promoters ₹39.03 Cr *(H1FY27 presentation)*.
- **Payment:** warrants are normally 25% paid up front, with the balance due on conversion within 18 months. So not all ₹59.97 Cr is in hand yet. **We assume this; it is not confirmed.**

## 4. Key Catalysts / DNA Shift

1. **Order book roughly 2.7x a year ago.** It stood at **₹94.23 Cr (all with advance received), to be executed in 4–5 months**. ₹71.91 Cr is domestic and ₹22.32 Cr export (24%) *(H1FY27 presentation)*. That compares with **₹35 Cr in Nov-2025** *(Nov2025 concall)*. For a business with 2–3 month order cycles, this is the strongest forward indicator in the filing. It covers most of a ~₹90 Cr H2FY27.
2. **Working-capital bottleneck removed.** The ₹42 Cr WC infusion lets the company pre-buy components at a discount, shorten execution and take larger orders. Purchase costs already fell ~3–4% after the IPO money arrived *(Nov2025 concall)*, which shows in the gross margin rising from 39% to 44%.
3. **Exports: from ~2% to 18% to a 35% target.** FY26 export share was 18% against the guided ~20% *(Nov2025 concall; H1FY27 presentation)*. Exports earn better margins, buyers pay 50% advance plus LC, and the dealer network is being built out. **A US re-opening is a free option**: the Taurian Americas dealership and warehouse are still in place and not in guidance *(Nov2025 concall)*.
4. **Mix shift to beneficiation and washing.** CycloWash, sand washing and beneficiation are bigger tickets with more engineering content. The silica beneficiation work in Jaipur and pitching through national tenders to Tata Steel Mines are early steps toward a critical-minerals and rare-earths angle *(Nov2025 concall)*.
5. **Professionalised leadership.** The company hired the ex-Propel sales head (Mr. Anand Mohan, who helped take Propel from ₹300 Cr to ~₹1,500 Cr), a CMO from Tega/Metso and a 30-year CFO *(Nov2025 concall; H1FY27 presentation)*. There are new sales teams in South and East India, with first orders won in Hyderabad and the Northeast.
6. **Backward integration.** ₹10.22 Cr capex should save ₹2.1 Cr+ a year, ≈ 100–150 bps of EBITDA margin at a ₹150–200 Cr revenue base.
7. **Industry tailwinds.** Mining reforms, aggregates demand from infra, the shift from natural sand to manufactured sand, and Indian OEMs taking share from MNCs.
8. **Optionality (not valued):** plastic-waste and sewage-waste processing equipment, mentioned in the MD's letter *(H1FY27 presentation)*.

**Why these multiples:**
- **Bull 35x:** close to PEG 1 on a ~45–50% EPS CAGR. That is equal to ~25x FY28E bull EPS. This is the multiple the market grants SME capital-goods names that prove they can grow while funding themselves.
- **Base 28x:** ~0.8 PEG on a ~35% FY26–29E PAT CAGR (₹16.2 → ₹39.7 Cr), which equals **~20.6x FY28E base EPS**. It is discounted for SME liquidity, negative FCF and the pre-IPO guidance miss.
- **Bear 16x:** de-rating to a cyclical small-engineering multiple if growth stalls below 20%, cash burn continues and more dilution follows.
- **Probabilities:** the bull case gets 25% (above bear) because the ₹94 Cr advance-backed order book and the 73% H1 growth are hard evidence. The bear case stays at 20% because of the cash-flow record.

### 6d. Probability-Weighted Fair Value

`(Bull ₹904 × 25%) + (Base ₹603 × 55%) + (Bear ₹259 × 20%)`
`= ₹226.1 + ₹331.6 + ₹51.9`

### 6f. Probability Shifters

**Bull probability ↑ if:**
- H2FY27 revenue ≥ ₹95 Cr (FY27 ≥ ₹150 Cr) with an H2 EBITDA margin ≥ 26% (matching H2FY26's 26.9%).
- The order book stays ≥ ₹90 Cr at the next disclosure *despite* H2 execution (i.e. inflows ≥ ₹90 Cr in 5 months).
- Export share reaches ≥ 25% of FY27 revenue, or the US market reopens (Taurian Americas starts shipping).
- **Operating cash flow turns positive in FY27** and NWC days fall below 200. Short-term debt is cut using the preferential money.
- Backward-integration capex is commissioned on time and gross margin holds ≥ 44%.
- A further capacity expansion is announced and funded from internal accruals.

**Bear probability ↑ if:**
- H2FY27 revenue < ₹80 Cr (FY27 growth < 35%), or the order book drops below ₹60 Cr.
- EBITDA margin < 21% for the full year (the fixed-cost hires do not pay back).
- Receivables or inventory keep rising faster than revenue, net debt goes above ₹40 Cr even after the preferential money, or yet another equity raise is announced.
- Short-term provisions turn out to be overdue statutory dues, or the auditor qualifies / adds an emphasis of matter on the FY27 accounts.
- Warrants are not converted (non-promoter holders walk away at a lower share price), leaving a WC funding hole.
- Management diverts capital to plastic/sewage diversification before the core business generates cash.

### 6g. FY30+ Terminal Value

- **FY30E (base) assumptions:** revenue ₹320 Cr (+25%; needs a second capacity phase). PAT margin 15.5%.
- **FY30E PAT** = ₹49.6 Cr, so **EPS = ₹48.1** (1.031 Cr fully diluted shares).
- **Terminal multiple:** **18x**, a mature small-cap capital-goods multiple once growth normalises to ~15%.
- **FY30 value** = 18 × ₹48.1 = **₹866/share**.
- **Present value**, discounted ~3.4 years (Nov-2026 → Mar-2030):
  - at a 15% cost of equity (SME risk premium): ₹866 / 1.15^3.4 = **₹538/share**
  - at 13%: **₹572/share**

**Interpretation:** the terminal-value PV of ₹538–572 is **12–17% below CMP**. At ₹652 the market is pricing in more than the base path, with growth staying near management's 45–50% CAGR through FY30. Upside from here needs the bull case to play out, especially self-funded growth.

## 7. Investment Verdict & Key Metrics to Monitor

### Weighted Fair Value & Entry Recommendation
**Weighted fair value: ₹610/share** (fully diluted; FY27E basis). At **CMP ₹652** (06-Nov-2026), the stock is **~7% above fair value**, with an expected return of **−6.4%**. That puts it just inside the **REDUCE** band (HOLD up to ₹640). **Fresh buying makes sense below ₹610 (ACCUMULATE), and strongly below ₹549 (BUY).**

### Bull Case (25%) – What Needs to Go Right → ₹904
- The ₹94 Cr order book converts in full and FY27 revenue reaches ~₹160 Cr (+61%).
- Exports rise toward the 35% target. The US reopens through the existing dealership.
- Beneficiation and washing lift ticket sizes. In-sourcing savings and procurement discounts push EBITDA to 25%+.
- The new sales leadership (ex-Propel) wins share in South and East India.
- The preferential money breaks the WC constraint, so growth becomes self-funding.
- The market then pays 35x FY27E (25x FY28E).

### Base Case (55%) – Most Likely Path → ₹603
- FY27 revenue is ~₹146 Cr (+47%), right in management's 40–50% band. PAT is ₹22 Cr (15.2% margin).
- Growth moderates to 30–35% in FY28–29 as the 155-plant capacity fills up. PAT reaches ~₹40 Cr by FY29.
- Working capital stays heavy (~200–250 days) but stops getting worse. No further equity raise is needed before FY28.
- 28x FY27E (≈ 20.6x FY28E).

### Bear Case (20%) – What Could Go Wrong → ₹259
- H2FY27 disappoints (~₹75 Cr) because of LC and export delays, monsoon carry-over or competitive pricing.
- The much larger employee and S&M base squeezes EBITDA to ~20–21%.
- Cash burn continues. Net debt climbs, warrants are not converted, and the company needs to raise equity again.
- Credibility problems return (the pre-IPO ₹140–150 Cr was missed by a third) and the SME-liquidity discount widens.
- The stock de-rates to 16x FY27E.

### Rating: **REDUCE** (weighted FV ₹610 vs CMP ₹652; marginal, as the HOLD ceiling is ₹640)

The operating turnaround is real:
- H1FY27 revenue +73% and PAT +109%.
- Gross margin up ~490 bps.
- An advance-backed order book of ₹94 Cr, 2.7x a year ago.
- Post-IPO margin guidance met.

**The price already reflects most of this.** The stock has run 56% past a preferential price fixed only in August. It also trades above the PV of our base-case FY30 value. The core unresolved issue is **cash conversion**: −₹40 Cr TTM FCF and a second equity raise within 12 months of listing. Existing holders can trim toward ₹700+. New money should wait for **₹550–610**, or for proof of positive operating cash flow in FY27, which would justify moving the base multiple higher.
