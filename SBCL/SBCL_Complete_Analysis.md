## 1. Business Model Overview

**Core Business:** Precision engineering company manufacturing thermal sensing bimetals, electrical contacts, shunts (current-sensing resistors), and related assemblies for automotive, smart metering, switchgear, and industrial applications.

**Strategic DNA:** Transitioning from low-value commodity strip supply to high-value precision components and integrated assemblies. Building integrated solutions platform across thermal, electrical, and mechanical domains.

**Primary Segments:**

1. **Shunts (Current-Sensing Resistors)**
   - Market applications: Smart metering, renewable energy, EV battery management, power distribution
   - Geography: India (largest, ~50% revenue), Europe (traction), Americas (US tariff-exposed)
   - Revenue contribution: ~₹150-180 Cr annually (31-32% of total)
   - Margin profile: 18-20% EBITDA (lower-margin volume business)
   - Key customers: Tier-1 meter manufacturers, renewable companies

2. **Thermostat Bimetals (Core, Historic Anchor)**
   - Applications: Automotive thermostats, switchgear protection, thermostatic control devices
   - Geography: India (largest), Europe, Americas
   - Revenue contribution: ~₹120-150 Cr annually (21-27% of total)
   - Margin profile: 20-22% EBITDA (stable, mature)
   - Key challenge: India market flat (switchgear cycle weak, real estate sluggish)
   - Key opportunity: Export markets (US, EU) growing

3. **Electric Contacts (Relays, Switching Components)**
   - Market driver: Smart meter relays for energy management, demand response
   - Geography: India (smart metering wave), Europe (traditional relays)
   - Revenue contribution: ~₹150-180 Cr annually (26-32% of total)
   - Margin profile: 22-24% EBITDA (higher-margin; beneficiary of tariff shift)
   - Key trend: Converting from commodity strip input to precision-engineered relays
   - Growth: 31-32% contribution to YoY growth in FY26 (tariff-driven component shift)

4. **Components & Assemblies (Emerging, Strategic)**
   - Busbar assemblies for two-wheeler/four-wheeler EVs (Current Collector System — CCS)
   - PCBA (Printed Circuit Board Assemblies) for automotive/industrial
   - EB-welded (electron beam welded) components for precision applications
   - Revenue contribution: Still nascent (<₹5 Cr in FY26)
   - Margin profile: 18-20% EBITDA (initially lower, improving with scale)
   - Strategic importance: Future growth engine; Pune facility being built
   - Expected ramp: ₹250-350 Cr revenue potential over 3-year period

**Geographic Distribution (FY26, ~₹571 Cr revenue):**
- India: 50-55% (₹285-315 Cr) — smart metering, automotive, switchgear
- Europe: 25-30% (₹145-170 Cr) — strong bimetal/relay traction
- Americas: 10-15% (₹55-85 Cr) — shunts exposed; tariff pressure but strategic reset underway
- Others: 5-10% (₹30-60 Cr)

**Competitive Positioning:**
- Largest global bimetal manufacturer with precision capability
- One of few global players in EB-welded components (high technical moat)
- No Indian competitor in EB welding/CCS applications (moat from government "Make in India" push)
- Established customer relationships with Tier-1 OEMs (Maruti, Hero, Bajaj, Siemens, ABB, etc.)
